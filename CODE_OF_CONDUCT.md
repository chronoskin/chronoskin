# Code of conduct

This is a small project about the look of old and new web pages. People come to it to add a layout, fix a pack or ask how something works. Keep it a place where that is pleasant.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique the work, not the person.
- Be specific. "The navigation wraps at 390px in layout 04" helps; "this is broken" does not.
- No harassment, insults, or remarks about who someone is.
- Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads and block accounts that do not keep to this.

If something happens that you think breaks these rules, write to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting (the **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**, and say it is about conduct).
