About this album

Two weeks in the house above the harbour. The pictures are in the order they were taken, so the slideshow runs from the first morning on the hill to the last boat coming in.

How the slideshow plays

Each picture stays for five seconds and the two films play in full where they fall. Captions come from the file names unless you write your own.

  • Press space to pause, and the arrow keys to step.
  • Pictures marked hidden stay in the album but are skipped.

Sending it to the living room

Choose a screen under Settings and the album is resized for it once, then kept ready.
