How do I move my library to a new laptop?

Asked today at 9:12 by Tavish Lorne. 3 answers, 148 views.

AnsweredWrite an answer
TL

Tavish Lorne

New member
4 posts

Today, 9:12Question

Moving everything across

The old laptop is on its last legs and the new one arrived this morning. I have about 9,000 photos and four years of music in chronoskin Sync and would rather not upload it all again.

What I have tried

I installed version 3.2 on the new machine and signed in. It shows an empty library and offers to start a first sync, which the status window says will take two days.

  • Both laptops are on the same home network.
  • The old one still starts, slowly.

The setting I am unsure about

There is a box called use existing folder in the first window. Is that the one I want?

Odile Fenwick

Accepted answer

Today, 9:31Answer 1 of 3

Yes, that box is the one. Copy the whole Library folder from the old laptop to the new one over the network or on a memory card, put it where you want it to live, then tick the box and point it at the copy. The program compares instead of uploading, and that takes minutes.
