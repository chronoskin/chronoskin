Before you buy

What happens after the trial?

The book stays readable for good. Filing new pages waits until you choose an edition, and nothing you scanned is lost.

Can I change edition later?

Yes. You pay the difference for the rest of the year and your binders stay where they are. See binder settings for moving a library.
