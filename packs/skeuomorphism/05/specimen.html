Version 2.1

12 May, 9 MB

The year, counted for you

Open the new Year page and the book has already done the sums.

Every sighting you have written down now feeds a list for the year: how many kinds, the first date and the last. Nothing needs setting up. The questions page says what is counted and what is left out.

Also in this version

  • Forty birds of the north coast, with their winter names
  • A larger count key for cold fingers
  • The weather line now notes wind as well as rain

For the careful

The book you send yourself is one plain file named sightings.csv, a line to a bird, readable by any table program.

Asked often

Not here? Write to us and it soon will be.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. The bird lists live on the phone, and the place is filled in when a signal comes back.

Which birds does it know?

612 of them, across 31 countries. Each version adds a region; the north coast came in 2.0.2.

Can I get my sightings out again?

Whenever you like, as one plain file. The version notes describe it.

Do I pay again for updates?

No. You pay 1.99 once.
