Harbour at six

Gulls and a ferry horn, recorded today at 06:04 from the east pier.

Share Play
00:0003:1206:2409:3612:48

Notes on this take

A still morning with the tide going out. The microphones stood on the east pier, about a metre above the water, pointing at the harbour mouth. Nothing was cut and nothing was levelled afterwards.

What you can hear

  • Gulls over the fish quay from the start to about 04:10.
  • The first ferry sounds its horn twice at 07:32.
  • Ropes and fenders working against the pier at the end.

File

Stored as harbour-at-six.wav, two channels, 24 bit. See how recordings are stored to change this for new takes.
