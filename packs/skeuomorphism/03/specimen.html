A log that reads like one

chronoskin keeps the shape of the paper book it replaces.

Each day opens on a ruled page with the date at the head. Nothing is hidden in menus; the column you need is the column you see. The first passage guide walks through a weekend afloat.

What the page records

  • Time, course and distance run
  • Wind, sea state and the glass
  • Remarks, as long as you like

For the careful

A copy goes wherever you say. Set backup = ~/Logs once and forget about it.
