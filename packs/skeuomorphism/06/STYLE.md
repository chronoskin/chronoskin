# Skeuomorphism app (open ledger on a desk)

## Summary

A small web application dressed as the object it replaces, from the years when screens imitated physical materials: a toolbar across the window, and under it a desk on which one bound book lies open, two paper pages side by side with divider tabs standing on the upper edge. It was a common dress for to-do lists, notebooks, address books and ledgers in the browser and on tablets between about 2010 and 2013. The working parts are ordinary (a table, a list, a form) but each is drawn as stationery: ruled lines, index cards held by a clip, labels pasted on bound volumes, sheets that show the pages lying under them.

## Layout

- Fixed width: `--size-page` is 1000px, the width of the book and of the toolbar's content. The toolbar (`.ds-nav`) and the desk (`.ds-page__desk`, the `--fill-inverse` band) run the full window width; under the desk the page texture shows, with the footer on it.
- Navigation is the application toolbar, `--size-nav` (58px) high: the `.ds-brand` at the left, then one key per part of the application, each a glyph over a word, the current one pressed into the bar; at the right a search field, a small key and the name of the ledger. It is written once, above the desk, and the footer once below it. There is no other navigation except the divider tabs.
- Every view is one `.ds-book` on the desk: a cover (`--space-3` of it shows round the paper) holding a `.ds-book__spread` of two `.ds-book__page` halves of equal width and height, the right one `.ds-book__page--right`; a shade down the middle makes the fold. Each page is a column with `--space-5` of margin: its blocks follow one another, a `.ds-book__title` above each block that needs a name, and what is left of the page is filled with ruled lines (`.ds-book__rest`) above the page number (`.ds-book__folio`). Nothing is placed outside the book and no block spans both pages.
- The home view puts the hero card, the `.ds-stats` totals and the soonest `.ds-list` on the left page, and the `.ds-grid` of shelves, the `.ds-sidebar` of people and one notice on the right page.
- Inner pages keep the toolbar, the desk and the book. The left page opens with the breadcrumb, then `.ds-page-header` (title and one line, keys at the right, a double rule under it), then the working block: the table with its pagination in a `.ds-book__foot`, the form, an empty well, or running text. The right page holds what belongs to the selection on the left: a `.ds-panel` card of facts with its keys, a list of events or labels, the open dialog. Where a subject has several pages, `.ds-tabs` stand on the top edge of the right page as dividers (the book is then `.ds-book--tabbed`); the current tab is the paper's own colour and a little taller.
- Views. The specimen is a five-screen application, one `.ds-view` showing at a time. `desk` is the home view: today's card, the totals, what is due soonest, the shelves and who has what. `loans` is the Out now divider: the table of loans with one line marked, pagination, and on the facing page the marked loan's card and the key to the labels. `returned` is the Returned divider: an empty month on the left, last month's list on the right. `loan` is the Open entry divider: one entry as running text with its links, its facts and its history. `settings` is the settings form with its notices and keys, and on the facing page the dialog that the destructive key opens. The toolbar leads to `desk`, `loans` and `settings`; the dividers join `loans`, `returned` and `loan`, and all three keep Loans pressed in the toolbar. Each view needs one `:has()` selector in the navigation rule of `components.css`.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 3px (badge padding, between page keys and tabs), `--space-2` 6px (cell and row padding, between keys), `--space-3` 10px (the cover's rim, form rows, between shelves), `--space-4` 14px (control side padding, card padding), `--space-5` 22px (page margin, above a block title, the pitch of the ruled lines), `--space-6` 30px (above the book), `--space-7` 44px (below the book).
- Control heights: `--size-pill` 20px (small keys, badges), `--size-control` 28px (keys, fields), `--size-control-large` 38px (the keys of the hero card), `--size-tab` 30px (the current divider). `--size-thumb` 34px is the tile of a list row; `--size-hair` 1px the offset of every highlight.

## Typography and colour roles

- Two families and a monospace. `--font-body` and `--font-ui` (Tahoma, falling back to Lucida Grande and Verdana) for text, tables, fields, keys and the toolbar; `--font-heading` (Rockwell, falling back to Roboto Slab) for the brand, page titles, block titles, figures and the dialog's question; `--font-mono` (Courier New) for code. The references set headings in a commercial slab serif; Rockwell is the nearest system face.
- Sizes: `--text-base` 12px at `--line-body` 1.5; `--text-small` 11px for meta, badges, table heads, toolbar words and the footer; `--text-ui` 12px bold for keys and tabs; `--text-large` 15px for the hero lead and large keys; `--text-h3` 15px, `--text-h2` 18px (block titles), `--text-h1` 24px bold (page titles, figures); `--text-display` 32px bold for the hero title only.
- `--color-page` is the desk top that shows under the band; `--color-inverse` with `--fill-inverse` the desk mat the book lies on and, darkened toward `--color-shadow`, the book's cover; `--color-canvas` the paper of both pages; `--color-surface` the cards, table cells and labels pasted on the paper; `--color-surface-alt` alternate table rows and wells; `--color-surface-strong` the table head.
- `--color-bar` with `--fill-bar` and `--color-bar-text`: the toolbar and the current page number. `--color-bar-alt` with `--fill-bar-alt` and `--color-bar-alt-text`: divider tabs that are not current, card and dialog title strips, the date strip of the hero card.
- Text on paper is `--color-text`, headings `--color-heading`, sidebar titles `--color-heading-alt` over a rule of the same colour. Links are `--color-link`, underlined in running text and in the people list; titles in lists, tables and shelves are plain until hovered.
- `--color-button` is the primary key and the brand tile; `--color-accent` the rule along the top of the hero card and plain badges; `--color-accent-alt` "new" markers. `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are the cloth of the four shelves, with `--color-inverse-text` for the figures on them. `--color-notice` is the note pasted on a page and the marked line of the table. `--color-success`, `--color-warning` and `--color-danger` are for status labels and the destructive key.
- Surface: paper, not gloss. Fills are nearly flat (`--fill-button` lightens by a fifth toward its top edge). `--shadow-panel` is not a soft shadow but two further sheets showing under the card. Corners are small: `--radius-panel` 2px for paper, `--radius-control` 4px for keys and fields, `--radius-page` 7px for the cover, the tabs and the dialog, `--radius-pill` 9px for badges. `--border-width-strong` (2px) is the heavy ledger rule above a table and the totals, the rule under a sidebar title, the accent rule of the hero card, and the frame of the dialog.
- One-off values built in `components.css` from tokens: the cover colour (`--color-inverse` darkened toward `--color-shadow`); the fold (a gradient of `--color-shadow` at 12% over `--space-5`); the ruled lines of `.ds-book__rest` (`--color-border` every `--space-5`); the double rule under a page header; the spine and sheen of a shelf; the paper clip of the hero card; the gloss of the danger key and the status badges; border colours darkened toward `--color-shadow`.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Paints the desk top. `.ds-page__desk` is the band the book lies on and wraps all views; `.ds-page__wrap` a 1000px column for anything else placed on the page texture.
- `.ds-view`: one screen, a `<section>` with an id; only the one named in the address shows, and `.ds-view--home` when none is named.
- `.ds-book`: the open book. `.ds-book--tabbed` when dividers stand on it. `.ds-book__spread` holds the two `.ds-book__page` halves (`.ds-book__page--right` for the second). On a page: `.ds-book__title` names a block (`is-first` when it opens the page), `.ds-book__block` adds the usual gap above a block that has no title, `.ds-book__foot` is the line under a block (pagination or a link left, a count right), `.ds-book__rest` the ruled remainder and `.ds-book__folio` the page number.
- `.ds-nav`: the toolbar. `.ds-nav__inner` centres it; `.ds-nav__items` holds `.ds-nav__item` keys, each a `.ds-nav__glyph` over a word; the current one is pressed in, by `is-current` or, in the specimen, by one selector per view. `.ds-nav__tools` at the right holds the search field (`.ds-form__input` with `.ds-nav__search`), a small secondary key and `.ds-nav__user`.
- `.ds-brand`: at the left of the toolbar. `.ds-brand__mark` is a 28px tile dressed as the primary key (`--fill-button`, darkened border, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark in `--color-button-text`; `.ds-brand__name` the name in the heading font at 18px, always `--weight-bold`. Both are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-hero`: an index card at the head of the home view's first page, with an accent rule along its top and a clip: `.ds-hero__kicker` (the date, on a strip in `--fill-bar-alt` across the head of the card), `.ds-hero__title`, `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__actions` with one large primary key and at most one large secondary key, which wrap under each other when the type is wide. Once, and only on the home view.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page, on the left page under the breadcrumb: `.ds-page-header__copy` with `.ds-page-header__title` (24px bold) and one `.ds-page-header__text` line, `.ds-page-header__actions` with at most two keys, and a double rule beneath.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, paragraphs, lists, `code`, links. `.ds-prose__lead` is a larger opening line.
- `.ds-link`: text link; states `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` for breadcrumbs, footer and meta links. `.ds-links` is a wrapping row.
- `.ds-button`: a matte key, 28px high. `.ds-button--secondary` the paper-coloured key; `.ds-button--danger` the key in `--color-danger` for a destructive action; `.ds-button--large` the 38px key of the hero card; `.ds-button--small` a 20px key. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-button__icon` is a 16px glyph. `.ds-buttons` is a row.
- `.ds-form`: labels above fields. `.ds-form__row` holds a `.ds-form__label` and one control; `.ds-form__pair` sets two rows side by side. `.ds-form__input` and `.ds-form__textarea` are inset fields, `.ds-form__select` a paper key. `is-error` with `.ds-form__error`; `.ds-form__hint`; `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`; `.ds-form__actions` under a rule, where `.ds-form__actions-end` pushes the destructive key to the right edge.
- `.ds-table`: ledger ruling without a frame: a heavy rule above the `.ds-table__head`, hairlines between rows. `.ds-table__row` with `is-first`, `is-alt` and `is-current` (the marked line, in the notice colour); `.ds-table__cell`, `.ds-table__cell--name` for the bold leading column with a `.ds-table__link`, `is-numeric` for right-aligned figures.
- `.ds-list`: ruled lines: `.ds-list__item` with an optional `.ds-list__icon` tile, `.ds-list__body` (`.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__meta`) and a badge at the right.
- `.ds-panel`: a card pasted on the page: `.ds-panel__title` strip, `.ds-panel__body` with `.ds-panel__text` or a `.ds-panel__facts` list of `.ds-panel__fact` lines (`.ds-panel__term` left, `.ds-panel__value` right), then a row of keys.
- `.ds-stat`: one total: `.ds-stat__value` (24px bold slab) over a `.ds-stat__label`. Four of them make the `.ds-stats` row, under a heavy rule and over a hairline, parted by hairlines; `is-first` on the first.
- `.ds-grid`: four `.ds-grid__cell` shelves, two to a line, each a bound volume in `--color-fill-1` (`.ds-grid__cell--2`, `--3`, `--4` for the other fills) with a darker spine at its left. On it a pasted `.ds-grid__label` (`.ds-grid__title` link, `.ds-grid__text`), then `.ds-grid__count` and `.ds-grid__note` in `--color-inverse-text`.
- `.ds-tabs`: divider tabs, right-aligned on the top edge of the right page: `.ds-tabs__tab`, the `is-current` one in the paper colour.
- `.ds-badge`: a small label for a count or a word. `.ds-badge--count` is pressed in and grey, `.ds-badge--new` the marker in `--color-accent-alt`. Status labels: `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning` (dark text) and `.ds-badge--danger`. `.ds-badges` is a row.
- `.ds-sidebar`: an address-book block on a page: `.ds-sidebar__title` over a heavy rule, `.ds-sidebar__list` of ruled `.ds-sidebar__item` lines, each a link at the left and `.ds-sidebar__meta` at the right.
- `.ds-notice`: a note pasted on the page; `.ds-notice__title` the bold lead-in; `.ds-notice--error` for errors. A link inside a notice takes the notice's own text colour and an underline. `.ds-notices` wraps several.
- `.ds-pagination`: separate small paper keys, `.ds-pagination__link`; `is-current` takes the bar colour, `is-disabled` is flat.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a small trail at the head of the left page, with `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` and `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a sheet with a heavy frame: `.ds-dialog__title` strip, `.ds-dialog__body` with `.ds-dialog__heading` (the question, in the slab face) and `.ds-dialog__text`, and `.ds-dialog__actions` with the confirming key last. `.ds-dialog__backdrop` is the dimmed part of the page it lies on.
- `.ds-empty`: a well sunk into the paper with `.ds-empty__icon`, `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one secondary key.
- `.ds-footer`: one line on the page texture under the desk: `.ds-footer__links` at the left, `.ds-footer__note` at the right.
- `.ds-switch`: glossy sliding ON/OFF switch over a checkbox (`.ds-switch__track`, inset, with a raised knob), set as rows of a `.ds-switches` list (`.ds-switches__row`, `.ds-switches__name`). Here: the Reminders row of the settings form.
- `.ds-progress`: inset `<progress>` bar with a glossy fill and a `.ds-progress__label` line. Here: the facts panel of a loan.
- `.ds-tooltip`: `?` key with a dark glossy bubble and arrow (`.ds-tooltip__trigger`, `.ds-tooltip__bubble`) shown on hover or focus. Here: on the "Was due" term.
- `.ds-accordion`: `<details>` help list with glossy bar headings (`.ds-accordion__item`, `__head`, `__body`). Here: the right page of the settings spread.
- `.ds-menu`: popover list with an arrow, hanging from a toolbar button (`<details>`, `.ds-menu__button`, `.ds-menu__list`, `.ds-menu__item`). Here: the header toolbar of the loans page.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 9px`: the badge is the roundest thing; paper is 2px, keys 4px, the cover 7px.
- `border-width <= 2px`: hairlines everywhere, and a 2px ledger rule above tables and totals.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 7px`: paper casts no soft shadow; only the book and the dialog cast 7px.
- `font-size <= 32px`: the title of the hero card is the largest text.
- `font-size >= 11px`: meta text is the smallest.
- `font-weight >= 400`: no light weights.
- `font-families <= 3`: Tahoma, a slab serif for headings and a monospace.
- `line-height <= 1.55`: body text is set at 1.5.
- `uppercase-text <= 5%`: nothing is set in capitals.
- `letter-spacing <= 1px`: nothing is tracked out.
- `transition = none`: states change at once.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves; pages do not turn.
- `content-width <= 1000px`: everything stays inside the book.
- `row-gap <= 10px`: lines of a list or a table touch.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. New content is a block on one of the two pages, under a `.ds-book__title`; if a page is full, the subject gets another divider and another view, never a third column or a block outside the book. A thing pasted on the paper (a card, a label, a note) takes `--color-surface` or `--color-notice`, `--radius-panel` and `--shadow-panel`; a thing ruled on the paper (a list, a table, totals) takes hairlines in `--color-border` and one heavy rule above. Keys stay matte. Do not add glossy two-tone gradients, wide soft shadows, large radii, a third type family or new colours.
