Arc 64 memory player

Portable players | item 20641 | rated 4.6 of 5 by 212 buyers

Ten albums on a player with no moving parts: nothing skips, nothing wears out, and one battery lasts the working week.

What it does

Music is copied from a computer over the cable in the box and kept in 64 MB of built-in memory. A card slot takes it further.

  • Plays for twelve hours on one AA battery
  • Backlit screen with track name and time
  • Five sound settings and a hold switch

Before you order

The transfer program needs a computer with a spare port and 16 MB of memory. It installs a folder named arc64 and nothing else.
