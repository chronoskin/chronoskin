Running a station from a spare room

By the gear desk, 31 May

One machine, one line in and a steady connection will carry a programme to a few hundred listeners.

What you need

The encoder does the hard work, so give it the faster machine. Keep the bit rate low: most of your audience is still on a modem.

  • A sound card with line in
  • A mixer or a single deck
  • An upstream of 128 kbps or better

Checking the signal

Start the encoder with chronoskin --meter and watch the level. It should peak just under the top mark.

Getting listed

Run the programme for a week at the same hours, then send the stream address to the relay crew. They listen in before the station goes on the dial.
