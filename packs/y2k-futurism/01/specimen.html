Building a jukebox from an old desktop

By the hardware desk, 31 May

A retired machine, a sound card and one free evening are enough to put every disc you own behind a single remote.

What you need

Any processor from the last four years will do. Spend the money on disk space instead: a full shelf of discs needs about twenty gigabytes.

  • A desktop with a spare drive bay
  • A sound card with line out
  • A network card or a long cable

Setting it up

Copy the discs one shelf at a time and let the player name the tracks. Keep the machine out of sight: it only needs power, the network and a cable to the amplifier.

Checking the output

Start the player from a prompt with chronoskin --test-tone. A steady tone on both speakers means the card is set up.
