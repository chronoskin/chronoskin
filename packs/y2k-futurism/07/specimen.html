case study 12 / web / 2001

Harbour radio: a station in one small window

A local station wanted listeners to keep it open all day. We made the site the size of a radio.

The brief

Most visitors arrive on a shared line at work. The whole site had to load in under ten seconds and never need a second window.

  • One window, 640 by 480, no scrolling on the front
  • The programme guide readable at a glance
  • Requests sent without leaving the page

What we built

The schedule runs along the foot of the window like a dial. Every page is drawn from four colours and one typeface, so the pictures can be spent on the presenters.

Keeping it light

Each page is checked against a budget before it ships. The build stops when weigh --max 40k reports a page over the line.
