Setting up a home network

By the technology desk, March 11 | 26 comments

Two computers and one fast connection is now an ordinary household. Sharing that connection takes an afternoon.

What you need

A router sits between the modem and your machines and hands each one an address. Most will work as shipped, but change the administrator password before anything else.

  • A cable or DSL modem from your provider
  • A four-port router
  • One network cable per computer

Where to put it

Keep the router near the modem and off the floor. Run the longest cable first: it decides where everything else has to sit. Do not staple cable to the skirting board; clips cost little and do not cut the wires inside.

Checking the connection

Open a command prompt and type ping 192.168.0.1. Four replies mean the router is answering.

Sharing a printer

Plug the printer into the machine that is switched on most often, then share it from that computer's printer settings.
