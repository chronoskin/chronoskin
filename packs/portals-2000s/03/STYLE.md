# Fluid news portal, 2000 to 2004

## Summary

A full-window news and community front page of the kind technology, hobby and games sites ran between 2000 and 2004: the sheet stretches with the browser, folder tabs stand on a coloured bar for navigation, a dark feature band runs across the top, and below it a wide column of stories sits beside one fixed column of small boxes on the right. Every story opens with its headline on a solid bar, and everything else is a ruled or outlined rectangle set in 10px to 12px Arial with hairline borders. It was replaced from about 2005 by fixed, centred layouts with gradients and rounded corners.

## Layout

- The page is fluid. `.ds-page__frame` has no fixed width: it fills the window inside the `--space-4` (6px) padding of `.ds-page`, where `--color-page` shows as a thin frame, and it never gets narrower than `--size-page` (760px). Do not centre a fixed column and do not cap the width.
- Order from the top: `.ds-nav`, the `.ds-hero` band, `.ds-page__columns`, `.ds-footer`. Navigation is at the top in three parts: `.ds-nav__masthead` (wordmark on the left, search on the right, on the sheet colour), `.ds-nav__links` (a row of folder tabs, the open one in the bar colour and 3px taller) and directly under it `.ds-nav__sub`, a full-width bar in the same colour carrying a short row of links (those of the open tab on a large site; in the specimen the site's shortcuts) and, at the right, the date.
- `.ds-page__columns` is two columns: a fluid main column (`.ds-page__main`) on the left and `--size-rail` 200px (`.ds-page__rail`) on the right, `--space-5` (10px) apart and padded `--space-5` from the sheet edge. There is no left column. The rail is a stack of boxes: `.ds-panel` and the boxed menu `.ds-sidebar`.
- In the main column each block is headed by a `.ds-section` (marker, title, rule), and blocks are `--space-5` apart. Stories are a `.ds-list`; a row of four categories is a `.ds-grid`; a short ruled `.ds-table` of forum topics or figures closes the column.
- Inner pages keep the masthead, the tabs, the bar under them, both columns and the footer; they have no hero. The main column opens with `.ds-breadcrumb` as a plain line, then `.ds-page-header` (an outlined band with the title, one line of text and the page's button at the right) with the `.ds-stats` line attached under it, then `.ds-tabs` standing on the `.ds-table` it switches, then the `.ds-form` box and further blocks under `.ds-section` headings. The rail holds the section's `.ds-sidebar` menu first. An article page puts a `.ds-section` heading and the article, with its own h1, in place of the page header.
- Views. The specimen is one example site of five screens, one showing at a time, with the header and footer written once around them. `home` is the front page: hero band, latest notes, the category strip, the forum table and the rail. `notes` is the story list: breadcrumb, page header, the stories and pagination. `note` is one story: the article, related links and comments set as list items. `submissions` is the logged-in page: page header with the stats line, tabs on the table, then the buttons for the returned note, the delete message box and the empty drafts band. `submit` is the form page: page header, notices, the form box and the guidelines. The tabs mark Workshop for `notes` and `note` and My chronoskin for `submissions` and `submit`; the bar under them marks the open view's own link.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 1px is the vertical padding inside buttons and inputs; `--space-2` 2px is the vertical padding of bars, title strips, story headlines and menu rows; `--space-3` 3px is the vertical padding of tabs, table cells, form rows and notices; `--space-4` 6px is the page margin, the side padding of boxes and cells, and the gap between a section heading and its content; `--space-5` 10px is the column gap, the gap between blocks and between stories, and the side padding of bars; `--space-6` 16px is list indent and the padding around a dialog. There is no larger step.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-figure` 120px by `--size-figure-height` 90px is the hero picture slot; `--size-mark` 26px the brand tile; `--size-search` 160px a search or nickname input; `--size-label` 130px the label column of a form; `--size-field` 240px a form input; `--size-area` a textarea; `--size-dialog` 340px a message box.
- Things are separated by 1px lines: boxes are outlined, tables are fully ruled, form rows and menu rows are ruled, sections end in a rule. The only solid blocks are the bars, story headlines, the hero band and the tinted grid strip.

## Typography and colour roles

- Blues on a dark window. `--color-page` #003366 (navy) frames a white sheet (`--color-canvas`, `--color-surface` #ffffff); `--color-surface-alt` #eeeee3 (warm stone) fills the page header, the menu box, form action rows, inactive in-page tabs, the dialog and the empty state; `--color-surface-strong` #989acd (light periwinkle) with black text is inactive navigation tabs, menu title strips and picture slots.
- `--color-bar` #666699 (periwinkle) with white text is the open navigation tab and the bar under it, story headlines, panel and dialog titles, table heads and section markers; `--color-bar-alt` #4d86bc (mid blue) with white text is the form title strip and alternate panel titles. `--color-inverse` #003366 with white text is the hero band, the one dark block of a page.
- `--color-text` and `--color-heading` #000000; `--color-text-muted` #666666 for posting lines, counts and captions; `--color-heading-alt` #666699 for section titles, h3 and positive figures.
- `--color-link` #003366, `--color-link-quiet` #000000 for tabs, menu and breadcrumb links, visited #666699, hover #cc0000, active #ff0000. `--color-accent` #fdfe96 (pale yellow) with black text is the badge and the current page number; as a text colour it is the current link on the bar and every link on the hero band. `--color-accent-alt` #cc0000 marks promoted entries.
- `--color-fill-1` #f1f0ff lilac, `--color-fill-2` #e4f7e4 green, `--color-fill-3` #fff0e0 peach, `--color-fill-4` #eeeee3 stone tint the four cells of the grid strip; lilac is also the stats line. Text on a fill is `--color-text`, `--color-heading` or a link colour. Rules and box outlines are `--color-border` #989acd; the tab outline, dialog outline and footer rule are `--color-border-strong` #003366; rules between form rows and menu rows are `--color-border-muted` #eeeee3.
- `--color-button` #eeeee3 with black label; the secondary button is white with a #003366 label. `--color-danger` #cc0000 on #fff0e0; `--color-success` #3f8640 and `--color-warning` #fff8c0 are badge fills; `--color-notice` #fffbee.
- One family: `--font-body`, `--font-heading` and `--font-ui` are all Arial; `--font-mono` Courier for code.
- Small and tight: `--text-base` and `--text-ui` 11px, `--text-small` 10px for posting lines, the bar under the tabs, the breadcrumb and captions, `--text-large` 12px for a lead paragraph, `--text-h3` 12px for story headlines and hero side titles, `--text-h2` 13px for section and grid titles, `--text-h1` and `--text-display` 16px for the wordmark, the hero title and the page title. Hierarchy comes from bold, not from size.
- `--line-body`, `--line-heading` and `--line-display` are 1.09 (12px lines for 11px text). `--weight-ui` 700: tabs and both buttons are bold. No uppercase, no tracking.
- Links in running text are underlined (`--link-decoration`); tabs, menu, breadcrumb and bar links are not (`--link-decoration-quiet` none) until hovered.
- Hairlines: `--border-width` and `--border-width-strong` are both 1px, solid. Buttons and inputs have a 1px bevel. `--shadow-panel` is a 1px ring in `--color-border-strong` with no offset and no blur around each panel, outside the 1px outline of its body, so a panel reads as a double-ruled box. Controls and the dialog carry the same kind of ring: `--shadow-control` is a 1px ring in `--color-border-strong` around every button and input, outside its bevel (`--color-focus` when hovered, an inset ring when pressed), `--shadow-dialog` a 1px ring in `--color-border-strong`. `--shadow-text` is none. A hovered button and the secondary button take `--color-surface`, the sheet colour. Fills are flat and all radii are 0. `--focus-ring` is 1px solid in `--color-focus`; `--transition` none.
- One-off values built in `components.css` without a token: `border-style: outset` and `inset` on buttons and inputs, `solid` for tab outlines and the 1px dividers in link rows, and `calc()` sums of spacing steps for the bullet indent and the form action offset.

## Components

- `.ds-page` on `<body>`: page colour, 6px margin and base type. Wrap everything in `.ds-page__frame`. `.ds-page__columns` holds `.ds-page__main` and `.ds-page__rail`. `.ds-page__legal` is the centred 10px copyright line. `.ds-block` groups a section heading with its content 6px apart; `.ds-block--tight` makes its children touch (page header with stats, tabs with table).
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, at the left of the masthead and a link to the front page, once per page. `.ds-brand__mark` is a `--size-mark` (26px) tile dressed like the primary button: `--fill-button` with the mark drawn in `--color-button-text`, the outset bevel in `--color-border-muted`, `--radius-control` and `--shadow-control`, so every palette and surface set reskins it. The mark is inline SVG with square line caps and a 2.8 stroke. `.ds-brand__name` is the 16px bold wordmark in `--font-heading` with the heading tracking and transform, in `--color-heading` because the masthead sits on the sheet. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-nav`: `.ds-nav__masthead` holds `.ds-nav__brand` (the `.ds-brand` over a 10px `.ds-nav__tagline`) and the `.ds-nav__search` form (`.ds-nav__search-label`, a small input, a button). `.ds-nav__links` is the row of `.ds-nav__link` tabs; `is-current` is the open one (in the specimen `:has()` rules, one per view, mark the tab and the bar link of the open view). `.ds-nav__sub` is the bar under them with `.ds-nav__sub-links` (`.ds-nav__sub-item`, `.ds-nav__sub-link`, `is-current` in the accent colour) and a note at the right.
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the specimen (`.ds-view--home` is the front page); only the view named in the address shows. A real multi-page site does not need it.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: inner pages only, the first line of the main column: quiet links, `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` (`>`), bold `.ds-breadcrumb__current`. No strip behind it.
- `.ds-hero`: the dark band across the sheet under the navigation, front page only. `.ds-hero__lead` holds the `.ds-hero__figure` picture slot, the 16px `.ds-hero__title`, `.ds-hero__text` and one `.ds-button`, the main action; then three `.ds-hero__cell` columns divided by 1px lines, each a bold `.ds-hero__subtitle` link and a `.ds-hero__note`. Links inside take the accent colour.
- `.ds-section`: heading of a block in the main column: `.ds-section__marker` (a solid 6px block), `.ds-section__title` (13px) and an optional `.ds-section__more` link at the right, over a 1px rule.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page: an outlined stone band with `.ds-page-header__title` (16px, the only h1), one line of `.ds-page-header__text`, and `.ds-page-header__action` holding one `.ds-button` at the right.
- `.ds-stat`: one figure, written as a typed pair: `.ds-stat__label` then the bold `.ds-stat__value`. The row is `.ds-stats`, a tinted line attached under the page header in which figures are divided by 1px bars. Three to five figures; never enlarge the number.
- `.ds-prose`: article text on the sheet: h1, h2, h3, p, ul, ol, `code`, strong; `.ds-prose__lead` for the italic first paragraph and `.ds-prose__byline` for the 10px credit.
- `.ds-link`: underlined link with `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` is black and not underlined until hover; `.ds-link--hot` is the promoted entry. `.ds-links` lays a row of links out.
- `.ds-button`: bevelled button, bold, for the main action. `.ds-button--secondary` is the lighter one. `.ds-button--danger` keeps the bevel and sets the label in `--color-danger`; never fill a button red. `disabled` or `is-disabled` greys it; `is-active` presses the bevel in. Group with `.ds-buttons`. A link that leads to another page and looks like a button is an `<a>` with the same classes.
- `.ds-form`: an outlined box. `.ds-form__title` is its strip in the second bar colour; each `.ds-form__row` is a ruled row with a right-aligned bold `.ds-form__label` ending in a colon (optional `.ds-form__hint` under it) and a control. Controls take `.ds-form__input` (`--small` 160px, `--auto` for selects, `--area` for textareas); `is-invalid` plus `.ds-form__error` marks a bad field. `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox` for checkboxes and radios; `.ds-form__actions` is the stone button row aligned under the controls.
- `.ds-table`: a fully ruled table: every cell has a 1px line, `th` in the bar colour, cells white, `.ds-table__num` right-aligned, `is-up` (in `--color-success`) and `is-down` (in `--color-danger`) to colour a change. `.ds-table__caption` is the 10px muted line under it.
- `.ds-tabs`: outlined folder tabs on a 1px line, directly above the table or box they switch. `.ds-tabs__tab` is stone; `is-current` takes the sheet colour, is 3px taller and breaks the line.
- `.ds-list`: the story list. Each `.ds-list__item` opens with `.ds-list__title`, the headline as a link on a full-width bar, then a 10px `.ds-list__meta` posting line, the `.ds-list__text` and a bold `.ds-list__tools` line of links in parentheses. Stories are 10px apart. `.ds-list--bullets` is the one-line square-bullet variant for rail boxes, with ordinary underlined links.
- `.ds-panel`: a box of the rail: `.ds-panel__title` bar over an outlined `.ds-panel__body`. Body helpers: `.ds-panel__text`, `.ds-panel__field` with `.ds-panel__label`, `.ds-panel__option` (poll choice), `.ds-panel__actions`, `.ds-panel__small`. `.ds-panel--alt` is stone with the second bar colour.
- `.ds-grid`: one outlined strip cut into four `.ds-grid__cell` columns by 1px lines, tinted `--1`, `--2`, `--3` or the base fill; each has a 13px `.ds-grid__title` link and a 10px `.ds-grid__text` line of sub-links. Cells touch.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the boxed menu of the rail: `.ds-sidebar__title` strip over a `.ds-sidebar__list` of ruled `.ds-sidebar__item` rows, each a quiet link with an optional count or mark at the right; `is-current` makes the open page bold.
- `.ds-badge`: square 10px bold label on the accent. `.ds-badge--alt` is bare red text; `.ds-badge--count` a muted count in parentheses. Status labels change the fill: `.ds-badge--success` green with white text, `.ds-badge--warning` pale yellow with black text, `.ds-badge--danger` red with white text. One or two words each, in a table's status column.
- `.ds-notice`: one-line outlined message starting with a bold `.ds-notice__title`. `.ds-notice--error` uses the danger colour for text and border. Stack several in `.ds-notices`.
- `.ds-pagination`: a right-aligned line under a rule at the end of a list: a muted `.ds-pagination__label`, underlined links, and the current page as `.ds-pagination__current` on the accent.
- `.ds-dialog`: the era's message box, in the page flow: 340px stone box with a 1px dark outline, bar-coloured `.ds-dialog__title`, `.ds-dialog__body` with `.ds-dialog__text` and right-aligned `.ds-dialog__actions`. `.ds-dialog__backdrop` is the flat area it sits on.
- `.ds-empty`: an outlined stone band with the muted `.ds-empty__text` on the left and one button on the right.
- `.ds-footer`: a rule across the sheet, then `.ds-footer__bar`, a centred line of `.ds-footer__item` entries holding `.ds-link` links and divided by 1px bars, then `.ds-page__legal`. No bar behind it.
- `.ds-menu`: on a navigation item that has sub-pages; its `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__link` entries drops under the item on hover or keyboard focus (`.is-open` shows it statically).
- `.ds-progress`: a poll result, quota or elapsed-time bar: `.ds-progress__label` above, `.ds-progress__track` holding `.ds-progress__fill` (width by a `--NN` percent class), `.ds-progress__value` at the right.
- `.ds-accordion`: a list of questions or help topics as `<details class="ds-accordion__item">` with `.ds-accordion__summary` (a plus or minus box before it) and `.ds-accordion__body`. Use it for FAQs and help, not for navigation.

## Never

- `palette-colours <= 16`: navy, periwinkle, mid blue, stone and four pale tints; no further hue.
- `font-size <= 16px`: the largest text is 16px bold.
- `font-size >= 10px`: nothing is set below 10px.
- `font-weight <= 700`: regular and bold only.
- `font-families <= 2`: Arial and a monospace for code.
- `line-height <= 1.2`: lines are set tight, 12px for 11px text.
- `letter-spacing <= 0px`: no text is tracked.
- `uppercase-text <= 0%`: nothing is uppercased by CSS.
- `border-radius <= 0px`: every box is square.
- `border-width <= 1px`: every rule, outline and bevel is a hairline.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 0px`: the only shadows are 1px outline rings around panels, controls and the dialog; nothing is soft.
- `text-shadow = none`: text is flat.
- `gradient-fills <= 0%`: fills are flat colour.
- `row-gap <= 10px`: table and menu rows touch; stories are 10px apart.
- `block-gap <= 16px`: blocks sit 10px apart; there is no empty band between sections.
- `content-width >= 90%`: the sheet fills the window; it is never a narrow centred column.
- `transition = none`: states change instantly.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen: a new rail box copies `.ds-panel` (title bar, outlined body padded `--space-4`) or, for a menu, `.ds-sidebar`; a new block of the main column starts with a `.ds-section` heading and puts ruled or outlined content under it; a new kind of story copies a `.ds-list__item` with its headline bar; a new inner page copies the specimen's second page (breadcrumb line, `.ds-page-header` with `.ds-stats`, tabs on a table, a form box); a new link row copies `.ds-nav__sub-links` with 1px bar dividers. Use only the tokens in `tokens.css` and the `--size-*` and `--space-*` tokens in `components.css`, and take the nearest spacing step instead of a new number. Keep the sheet fluid with the 200px rail on the right, keep text between 10px and 13px apart from one 16px title, and separate things with a 1px line or a bar, never with whitespace, cards, soft shadows or rounded containers.
