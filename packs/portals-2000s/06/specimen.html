Getting more from your mailbox

A short guide from the chronoskin Mail team. You receive it once, in your first month.

Three settings are worth five minutes of your time: folders, filters and your signature.

Keep the Inbox short

Make a folder for anything you will want again, and let a filter put new mail there as it arrives. Bulk mail and Trash empty themselves after seven days.

  • Folders: up to 20 of your own
  • Filters: up to 15, checked in order
  • Attachments: 1 MB a message

Read your mail elsewhere

A mail program can collect from this mailbox as well as the web page can.

Settings for a mail program

Enter pop.chronoskin.example as the incoming server and your sign-in name as the account.

A signature

Up to four lines of plain text, added to every message you write.
