# Web mail start page, 2000 to 2004

## Summary

A signed-in mail and "my account" page of the kind portals and Internet providers gave their members between 2000 and 2004: a grey sheet that fills the browser window like a program, with a bar of services across the top, a row of small buttons under it, a fixed column of folders on the left and the message list, an open message or a form filling the rest. Lists have raised column heads and ruled white rows, every block is an outlined box with a title strip, and text is 11px Tahoma with 10px small print. It was replaced from about 2005 by mail pages that loaded messages without reloading, with rounded panels and soft gradients.

## Layout

- The page is fluid. `.ds-page__frame` has no fixed width: it fills the window inside the `--space-5` (8px) padding of `.ds-page`, is outlined like a window in `--color-border-strong`, and never gets narrower than `--size-page` (760px). Do not centre a fixed column and do not cap the width.
- Order from the top: `.ds-nav`, `.ds-page__body`, `.ds-footer`. Navigation is at the top in three parts: `.ds-nav__top` (the brand on the left, the account line on the right, on the sheet), `.ds-nav__links` (the service bar on `--fill-bar`; the open service is a cell cut out of the bar in the sheet colour) and `.ds-nav__tools` (a row of small secondary buttons on the left and the search form on the right, closed by a rule).
- `.ds-page__body` is two columns `--space-5` apart and padded `--space-5`: `--size-side` (156px) for `.ds-page__side`, and the rest of the window for the page content (in the specimen the open `.ds-view`). `.ds-page__side` is the same on every page, the home page included: the `.ds-sidebar` folder box, the space meter in a `.ds-panel--alt` and one `.ds-panel--inverse`. It is a stack of boxes, not a tinted column, and it does not run the height of the page.
- The content column is `.ds-page__main`, a stack of full-width blocks `--space-5` apart. There is no right rail. Where two blocks stand abreast they are a `.ds-page__pair` (two equal halves that end level) or a `.ds-page__pair--aside` (the work and one `--size-aside`, 210px, box).
- Home page: `.ds-hero` (the greeting), the `.ds-stat` line, a pair (newest messages as a `.ds-list` beside the day's `.ds-panel`), then `.ds-grid`, four service cells across.
- Inner pages keep the header, the folder column and the footer and have no hero. The content column opens with `.ds-breadcrumb` as a plain 10px line, then `.ds-page-header` (title in the display weight, one line of text, the page's button at the right, closed by a rule), then the working block: `.ds-tabs` standing on a `.ds-buttons` toolbar, which is joined to the `.ds-table` it acts on, which is closed by `.ds-pagination`. A form sits under a `.ds-section` strip. An open message has no page header: a toolbar, the `.ds-message` head and the `.ds-prose` body, joined into one box, head the page.
- Views. The specimen is one example site of five screens, one showing at a time; the header, folder column and footer are written once around them. `home` is the start page: greeting, status line, newest messages, calendar and service cells. `inbox` is the message list: page header, tabs, toolbar, table and pagination. `message` is one open message: toolbar, message head, the text and a row of links. `compose` is the form page: page header, notices, the form beside a box of addresses. `folders` is the management page: page header, status line, tabs and toolbar on the folder table, the empty-trash message box beside the empty Drafts block. The service bar marks Mail for `inbox` and `message`; the folder box marks Inbox for the same two.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 1px is the vertical padding inside buttons, inputs and folder rows; `--space-2` 2px is the vertical padding of title strips, table cells, tabs and form rows; `--space-3` 3px is the vertical padding of toolbars, the status line, list rows and notices; `--space-4` 5px is the side padding of table cells and folder rows and the gap between buttons; `--space-5` 8px is the page margin, the gap between columns and between blocks, and the side padding of strips and boxes; `--space-6` 14px is the padding of the greeting, an open message, a dialog and an empty state, and list indent. There is no larger step.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-mark` 24px the brand tile; `--size-search` 140px the search input; `--size-label` 84px the label column of forms, message heads and calendar rows; `--size-field` 260px a form input; `--size-area` a textarea; `--size-dialog` 310px a message box; `--size-check` 22px the checkbox column of a list; `--size-meter` 9px the height of the space meter.
- Blocks are outlined boxes in `--color-border` under a title strip or a toolbar, and parts of one working block are joined, not spaced. Rows inside a box are ruled with `--color-border-muted`.

## Typography and colour roles

- One family: `--font-body`, `--font-heading` and `--font-ui` are Tahoma (Geneva and Arial behind it), the interface face of the period's desktop. `--font-mono` is Courier New for code and for the text of a message being written.
- `--text-base` and `--text-ui` 11px for everything; `--text-small` 10px for the account line, breadcrumb, counts, dates, hints and the footer; `--text-large` 12px for the greeting text and a lead paragraph; `--text-h3` 11px, `--text-h2` 14px (status figures, grid titles, the date in the greeting), `--text-h1` and `--text-display` 18px. Page titles, the greeting and the product name beside the wordmark are set in `--weight-display` 400: large and regular, not bold. Strip, box and column titles are 11px bold.
- `--line-body` 1.3, `--line-heading` and `--line-display` 1.25. `--weight-ui` 400: secondary buttons and tabs are regular, the primary button and the open tab bold. No uppercase, no tracking.
- Links are underlined (`--link-decoration`), folder and subject links included; only breadcrumb links are quiet.
- `--color-page` #e8e8e8 shows as a thin frame round the sheet; `--color-canvas` #d0d0d0 is the grey sheet itself, on which the header, breadcrumb, page header and footer sit directly. `--color-surface` #ffffff is the inside of boxes: list rows, the folder box, an open message, the greeting. `--color-surface-alt` #eeeeee is toolbars, the status line, forms, message heads, the pagination strip, the open tab and the dialog. `--color-surface-strong` #a8a8a8 with `--color-heading` text is section and folder title strips and closed tabs.
- `--color-bar` #000099 with white text is the service bar and panel and dialog titles; `--color-bar-alt` #686868 with white text is the title of a `.ds-panel--alt`. `--color-inverse` #082058 with white text is the one dark box; its title and links take `--color-accent`.
- `--color-text` and `--color-heading` #000000; `--color-text-muted` #484848; `--color-heading-alt` #000099 for the product name, the date in the greeting and h3.
- `--color-link` #000099, quiet #000000, visited #686898, hover #0033cc, active #ff0000. `--color-accent` #ffd000 with black text is the badge, the meter fill and the current page number; `--color-accent-alt` #d00000 marks promoted links and bare reminders.
- `--color-fill-1` #f0f8ff, `--color-fill-2` #ffffd0, `--color-fill-3` #d0d0ff, `--color-fill-4` #e8e8e8 tint the four service cells; `--color-fill-2` is also a selected row. Text on a fill is `--color-text`, `--color-heading` or a link colour.
- `--color-border` #989898 outlines boxes; `--color-border-strong` #303030 outlines the sheet and the dark box; `--color-border-muted` #e0e0e0 rules rows and is the light side of bevels.
- `--color-button` #d0d0d0 with black label (`--color-button-hover-text` #000099), secondary #e8e8e8; column heads of a table are dressed as buttons (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, outset bevel). `--color-danger` #d00000 on `--color-danger-surface` #fff0e0; `--color-success` #3f8640 and `--color-warning` #ffd000 fill status labels; `--color-notice` #ffffd0.
- Surface: the chrome of a desktop program. `--border-width` 2px with `--border-style` groove: every outline and rule between blocks is an etched line. `--shadow-panel` is a 1px light inner edge at the top left and a dark one at the bottom right, so boxes look raised; `--shadow-control` gives buttons and inputs a second dark inner edge, lighter at the top left when hovered and reversed when pressed; `--shadow-dialog` is a hard 2px offset. `--fill-bar` and `--fill-bar-alt` fade from the bar colour on the left to a lighter mix on the right, like a window title bar. No blur, no rounding, no text shadow.
- One-off values built in `components.css` without a token: `border-style: outset` and `inset` on buttons, inputs, column heads, the meter and the dialog frame; `solid` for the hairline rules between rows, tabs and link-row dividers, which must stay single lines whatever `--border-style` is; `underline` on hovered bar links and tabs; the meter fill's width in percent; `color-mix()` of `--color-overlay` with `transparent` behind a dialog; and `calc()` over spacing and border tokens for indents and for letting the open tab overlap the toolbar's outline.
- Tahoma shipped with the period's desktop systems; the references used it and Arial for the same role.

## Components

- `.ds-page` on `<body>`: page colour, 8px margin and base type. Wrap everything in `.ds-page__frame`. `.ds-page__body` holds `.ds-page__side` and the content; `.ds-page__main` stacks the content's blocks; `.ds-page__pair` and `.ds-page__pair--aside` set two blocks abreast. `.ds-page__legal` is the centred 10px copyright line. `.ds-block` joins the parts of one working block (tabs, toolbar, table, pagination, or a section strip and its content) so they touch.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name at the left of the top line, a link to the start page, once per page. `.ds-brand__mark` is a `--size-mark` (24px) tile dressed like the primary button: `--fill-button`, the mark in `--color-button-text`, the 2px outset bevel, `--radius-control` and `--shadow-control`. The mark is inline SVG with butt caps and a 2.6 stroke. `.ds-brand__name` is the 18px bold wordmark in `--color-heading`, because the top line sits on the sheet; `.ds-brand__product` names the service after it in regular weight and `--color-heading-alt`. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-nav`: the header. `.ds-nav__top` holds the `.ds-brand` and `.ds-nav__account` (`.ds-nav__account-item` entries divided by 1px bars). `.ds-nav__links` is the service bar of `.ds-nav__item` cells; `.ds-nav__link` with `is-current` is cut out in the sheet colour with heading-coloured text; in the specimen the open view marks its cell through `:has()` rules, one per view. `.ds-nav__tools` holds `.ds-nav__shortcuts` (secondary buttons) and the `.ds-nav__search` form (`.ds-nav__search-label`, an input, a button).
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the specimen (`.ds-view--home` is the start page); only the view named in the address shows. A real multi-page site does not need it, but its content takes the same grid column.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: inner pages only, the first line of the content column. 10px trail on the sheet, quiet links, `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` (`>`), ending in bold `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the folder box in the left column. `.ds-sidebar__title` strips over `.ds-sidebar__list` lists; each `.ds-sidebar__item` is a link with a `.ds-sidebar__count` at the right; `is-current` tints the open folder's row and sets it bold. Use `.ds-link--hot` for the one action row ("Add a folder").
- `.ds-progress`: a sunken track with `.ds-progress__fill` in the accent, for space used; set the fill's width for the value shown.
- `.ds-section`: title strip of a block on the strong surface, outlined with the block: `.ds-section__title` and an optional right-hand `.ds-section__more` link.
- `.ds-hero`: the greeting, first on the start page: a raised white box with `.ds-hero__title` (18px regular), `.ds-hero__text` and `.ds-hero__actions` (one primary and one secondary button), and at the right `.ds-hero__aside`, a ruled-off note headed by `.ds-hero__date`. No picture, no colour band.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page, once per page. `.ds-page-header__title` (18px regular, the page's only h1) over one line of `.ds-page-header__text`, with `.ds-page-header__action` at the right holding one `.ds-button`; an etched rule closes it.
- `.ds-stat`: one outlined status line of three to five `.ds-stat__item` cells, each a muted `.ds-stat__label` on the left and a 14px bold `.ds-stat__value` on the right, divided by etched lines. No icons.
- `.ds-message`: the head of an open message, joined under its toolbar: `.ds-message__row` lines of a right-aligned `.ds-message__label` and a `.ds-message__value`.
- `.ds-prose`: the text of a message or a help page in a white outlined box: h1, h2, h3, p, ul, ol, `code`, strong; `.ds-prose__lead` for a 12px first paragraph and `.ds-prose__byline` for a 10px credit.
- `.ds-link`: underlined blue link with `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` is text-coloured and not underlined until hover; `.ds-link--hot` is the red action entry. `.ds-links` is a white outlined row of links joined under the box they belong to.
- `.ds-button`: bevelled grey button, bold, for the main action. `.ds-button--secondary` is lighter and regular weight. `.ds-button--danger` is the same bevel with the label in `--color-danger`; never fill a button red. `disabled` or `is-disabled` greys it; `is-active` presses the bevel in. `.ds-buttons` is a toolbar: a tinted outlined strip joined to the table or message it acts on; `.ds-buttons__gap` pushes what follows it to the right end. A link that looks like a button is an `<a>` with the same classes.
- `.ds-form`: tinted outlined block under a `.ds-section` strip, of `.ds-form__row` pairs: a right-aligned bold `.ds-form__label` (optional `.ds-form__hint`) and a control. Controls take `.ds-form__input` (`--auto` for selects, `--wide` for a full-width line, `--area` for the monospaced textarea); `is-invalid` plus a `.ds-form__error` line marks a bad field. `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox` for checkboxes and radios, set in a row; `.ds-form__actions` is the button row under the controls.
- `.ds-table`: an outlined list: column heads dressed as raised buttons, white rows ruled apart, `.ds-table__num` right-aligned for figures, `.ds-table__check` for the checkbox column. A row takes `is-unread` (bold) or `is-selected` (tinted); `is-up` and `is-down` colour a figure. `.ds-table__caption` is the 10px line under it.
- `.ds-tabs`: property-sheet tabs standing on a toolbar or box: outlined `.ds-tabs__tab` cells on the strong surface; `is-current` is taller, bold, in the toolbar's tint and open at the bottom.
- `.ds-list`: an outlined white list under a `.ds-section` strip. Each `.ds-list__item` is one ruled line: `.ds-list__body` with a bold `.ds-list__title` and a muted `.ds-list__text`, and a `.ds-list__meta` date at the right.
- `.ds-panel`: a small window: `.ds-panel__title` bar over `.ds-panel__body`. Body helpers: `.ds-panel__text`, `.ds-panel__list`, `.ds-panel__row` (a `.ds-panel__when` time and a line of text, ruled), `.ds-panel__small`. `.ds-panel--alt` is tinted under the secondary bar; `.ds-panel--inverse` is the dark box with an accent title, at most one per page.
- `.ds-grid`: four service cells across the content column, outlined and tinted with `.ds-grid__cell--1`, `--2`, `--3` or the base fill; each `.ds-grid__cell` has a 14px `.ds-grid__title`, a line of `.ds-grid__text` and one link.
- `.ds-badge`: square yellow 10px bold label ("New"). `.ds-badge--alt` is bare red text; `.ds-badge--count` is muted text in parentheses. Status labels keep the block and change the fill: `.ds-badge--success` green with white text (in order, delivered), `.ds-badge--warning` amber with black text (due to be emptied, pending), `.ds-badge--danger` red with white text (large, over the limit, failed).
- `.ds-notice`: one-line outlined message on pale yellow, starting with a bold `.ds-notice__title`. `.ds-notice--error` uses the danger colour for text and border. Stack several in `.ds-notices`.
- `.ds-pagination`: the tinted foot of a list, joined to it: `.ds-pagination__info` (the range shown) on the left and `.ds-pagination__item` links divided by 1px bars on the right, the current page as `.ds-pagination__current` on the accent.
- `.ds-dialog`: the era's message box, in the page flow and drawn as a small window: a 310px tinted box with a 2px raised frame and a hard offset shadow, `.ds-dialog__title` bar, `.ds-dialog__body` with `.ds-dialog__text` and centred `.ds-dialog__actions`. `.ds-dialog__backdrop` is the dimmed outlined area it sits on.
- `.ds-empty`: white outlined block under a `.ds-section` strip with a centred muted `.ds-empty__text` and one button.
- `.ds-footer`: an etched rule, then `.ds-footer__bar`, a centred row of `.ds-footer__item` entries with `.ds-footer__link`, followed by `.ds-page__legal`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a help hint beside a field: `.ds-tooltip__trigger` is a boxed question mark, `.ds-tooltip__text` shows under it on hover or focus. Everywhere else use the browser's own `title`.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: no reference has a rounded box in CSS; every block is a square rectangle.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 0px`: shadows are 1px bevel edges and one hard offset; nothing is soft.
- `text-shadow = none`: text is flat, on bars too.
- `gradient-fills <= 10%`: only title bars fade, left to right; boxes, rows and the sheet are flat.
- `font-size <= 18px`: the largest text is an 18px page title.
- `font-size >= 10px`: nothing is set below 10px.
- `font-weight <= 700`: regular and bold only.
- `font-families <= 2`: Tahoma for everything and a monospace for code and message text.
- `line-height <= 1.4`: text runs on 14px lines.
- `letter-spacing <= 0px`: no text is tracked.
- `uppercase-text <= 0%`: nothing is uppercased by CSS.
- `underlined-links >= 60%`: links are underlined; only bar, tab and breadcrumb links are not.
- `border-width <= 2px`: 2px etched outlines and bevels, 1px rules between rows.
- `row-gap <= 8px`: list, table and folder rows touch.
- `block-gap <= 14px`: blocks are joined or sit 8px apart.
- `content-width >= 90%`: the sheet fills the window; it is never a narrow centred column.
- `transition = none`: states change instantly.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen: a new box copies `.ds-panel` (title bar, etched outline, white body padded `--space-5`); a new working block copies the inbox (tabs, a `.ds-buttons` toolbar, the `.ds-table`, a tinted foot) and keeps its parts joined; a new settings page copies the `compose` or `folders` view (breadcrumb, `.ds-page-header`, then a form under a `.ds-section` strip or a toolbar on a table); a new entry in the left column is a `.ds-sidebar__item` row or a small `.ds-panel--alt`; a new control copies `.ds-button` or `.ds-form__input` with its 2px bevel. Use only the tokens in `tokens.css` and the `--size-*` and `--space-*` tokens in `components.css`, and take the nearest spacing step instead of a new number. Keep text at 10px and 11px apart from 14px figures and one 18px regular-weight title, keep corners square, let the page fill the window, and separate things with an outline, a title strip or a ruled row, never with whitespace, cards, soft shadows or rounded containers.
