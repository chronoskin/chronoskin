# Travel booking, 2000 to 2004

## Summary

A flight-booking site of the kind travel agents, airlines and portals' travel channels ran between 2000 and 2004: a 760px sheet hanging from the top of a 1024px window under a band of solid colour, with five large two-line tabs for the kinds of travel and a numbered step line under them. The home page sets a boxed search desk beside the week's offers; every page after it is a step of a booking, with a narrowing column or a price column beside ruled tables and forms. Titles are a bold serif up to 26px over 12px sans-serif text and 10px Verdana small print, and it gave way from about 2005 to wider pages with calendars that opened in place, photographs and rounded panels.

## Layout

- The page is not fluid. `--size-page` is 760px; `.ds-page__frame` is a sheet of that width centred in the window (`margin: 0 auto`) and touching its top edge, closed at the bottom by a `--border-width-strong` rule. `.ds-page` pads the window at the sides and bottom only and paints `--fill-page`. No text sits on the page colour.
- Navigation is three stacked parts of the header, all full width: `.ds-nav__top`, the band in the bar colour holding the wordmark on the left and the account links on the right; `.ds-nav__tabs`, five equal two-line tabs (a serif label over a 10px caption), of which the open one takes the secondary bar colour; and `.ds-nav__steps`, the strip in that same colour which the open tab runs into, listing the numbered steps of a booking with the current one in bold.
- Everything below the header sits in `.ds-page__body`, padded `--space-5` (10px), and each view is one `.ds-page__flow`, a column of blocks `--space-5` apart.
- The home page is `.ds-page__desk`: the `.ds-desk` search box in a `--size-desk` (252px) column on the left, and on the right a `.ds-page__flow` of the `.ds-hero` and the two-by-two `.ds-grid`. Under it `.ds-page__lower` mirrors the split: a `.ds-section` over a `.ds-list` on the left and one `.ds-panel` in a `--size-desk` column on the right. Columns stretch so both end level.
- Inner pages keep the header and footer and have no hero and no search desk. Each opens with `.ds-breadcrumb`, then `.ds-page-header`, then the work. A list of results is `.ds-page__columns`: a `--size-filter` (168px) `.ds-sidebar` on the left and the `.ds-tabs`, `.ds-table` and `.ds-pagination` touching each other on the right. A page about one fare or one booking is `.ds-page__columns--summary`: the work on the left and a `--size-summary` (224px) column of `.ds-panel` boxes on the right. An account page uses the whole width: `.ds-stat`, tabs on the table with its `.ds-buttons` row, then `.ds-page__pair` for two half-width blocks.
- Views. The specimen is one example site of five screens, one showing at a time; the header and footer are written once around them. `home` is step 1: the search desk, the offer of the week, four offers, fares that fell today and a box of advice. `results` is step 2: breadcrumb, page header, the narrowing column, a sort line on the fares table, pagination. `fare` is step 3: the two legs of the journey, the fare conditions as article text, a row of links, and the price column. `book` is step 4: page header, notices, the travellers form and the booking so far. `trips` is the signed-in page: page header, stat cells, tabs on the bookings table with its buttons, the cancel message box beside the empty past-trips block. The Flights tab marks the four steps and My trips marks `trips`; the step line marks the open step.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 1px is the padding between checkbox rows; `--space-2` 2px is the vertical padding of buttons and inputs and of form rows; `--space-3` 4px is the vertical padding of table cells, list rows, tabs and title strips; `--space-4` 7px is the side padding of cells and boxes and the gap between fields; `--space-5` 10px is the padding of the sheet, the gutter between columns and the gap between blocks; `--space-6` 14px is the gap between links in a row and the padding round a dialog. There is no step above 14px.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-price` 84px sets the price block of the hero; `--size-step` 16px the numbered square of a step; `--size-mark` 28px the brand tile; `--size-search` 112px a short input; `--size-field` 210px a text input; `--size-label` 116px the label column of a form; `--size-area` the height of a textarea; `--size-dialog` 340px a message box.
- Blocks are separated by 10px of sheet and closed by rules, not boxed: only the search desk, panels, the form, notices, the hero and the dialog carry an outline.

## Typography and colour roles

- `--font-heading` (a serif in this style's own type set) is the wordmark, tab labels, page and section titles, prices and figures. `--font-body` is running text and table cells. `--font-ui` is buttons, inputs, the step line, table heads and all 10px small print. `--font-mono` is fare codes. One reference set its headings in a commercial serif; Georgia, then Times New Roman, is the stand-in.
- `--text-base` 12px is body text; `--text-small` and `--text-ui` 10px are meta lines, captions, buttons and table heads; `--text-large` 13px is the hero text, table prices and large buttons. `--text-h3` 12px is panel and legend titles, `--text-h2` 15px section titles, tab labels, grid titles and list prices, `--text-h1` 21px page titles, the hero title, stat figures, leg times and grid prices, `--text-display` 26px the wordmark and the hero price, set in `--weight-display`.
- `--line-body` is 1.3; headings are 1.05. Headings are not uppercased and not tracked.
- `--color-page` shows outside the sheet; `--color-canvas` is the sheet and `--color-surface` the table rows, panels and forms. `--color-surface-alt` fills the search desk, the tab strips, the sidebar, stat cells, alternate list rows and form action rows; `--color-surface-strong` fills resting navigation tabs, table heads, form legends, the dialog title and the current page number. Text on both is `--color-text`, `--color-heading` and `--color-link-quiet` or `--color-link`.
- `--color-bar` (through `--fill-bar`) with `--color-bar-text` is the top band, the title of the search desk and of panels. `--color-bar-alt` with `--color-bar-alt-text` is the open navigation tab, the step line and the title of `.ds-panel--alt`. `--color-inverse` (through `--fill-inverse`) with `--color-inverse-text` is the price block of the hero and `.ds-panel--inverse`, where links take `--color-accent`.
- `--color-accent` with `--color-accent-text` is the badge, the number of the current step and the small square markers before section titles and link rows. `--color-accent-alt` is bare emphasis text such as a price drop.
- `--color-fill-2` tints the hero and the chosen row of a table; `--color-fill-1` the legs of a journey and the first grid cell; `--color-fill-2`, `--color-fill-3` and `--color-fill-4` the other grid cells. Text on a fill is `--color-text`, `--color-link`, `--color-heading` and `--color-heading-alt`.
- `--color-link` underlined is every link in the work, the sort line included; `--color-link-quiet` is the breadcrumb, resting tab labels and footer links. `--color-heading` is page titles; `--color-heading-alt` is section titles, prices in lists and grid cells, and h2 in articles.
- `--color-border` is outlines and the rules between table rows; `--color-border-strong` the heavy rules (under the page header and table head, over stat cells and the sidebar, the foot of the sheet) and the outline of buttons, the search desk and the dialog; `--color-border-muted` the rules between list rows.
- The primary button is `--fill-button` with `--color-button-text`, outlined in `--color-border-strong` with a `--border-width-strong` foot, `--radius-control` and `--shadow-control`. The destructive button is filled with `--color-danger` and lettered in `--color-bar-text`. `--color-success` and `--color-warning` are badge fills only.
- One-off values built in `components.css` without a token: the square markers are a `linear-gradient()` of `--color-accent` sized `--space-3` or `--space-4`; a transparent top border reserves the height of the current tab's edge; `calc()` sums of spacing steps set a few paddings.
- Visited, hover, active and focus colours cannot be measured on a static capture; they are colours measured elsewhere in the references assigned to those roles.

## Components

- `.ds-page` on `<body>`: page fill and base type. Wrap everything in `.ds-page__frame`; put the views in `.ds-page__body`. `.ds-page__flow` is a column of blocks; `.ds-page__desk`, `.ds-page__lower`, `.ds-page__columns` (with `--summary`) and `.ds-page__pair` split it into columns. `.ds-block` groups things that must touch. `.ds-page__legal` is the 10px copyright line.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name at the left of the top band, a link to the home page, once per page. `.ds-brand__mark` is a `--size-mark` (28px) tile dressed like the primary button: `--fill-button`, the mark in `--color-button-text`, a 1px `--color-border-strong` outline, `--radius-control` and `--shadow-control`. The mark is inline SVG with round caps and a 2.2 stroke. `.ds-brand__name` is the 26px wordmark in `--font-heading` and `--weight-display`, in `--color-bar-text` because it sits on the bar. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-nav`: the header. `.ds-nav__top` holds the `.ds-brand` and `.ds-nav__account` (a list of `.ds-nav__account-link`). `.ds-nav__tabs` is the row of five `.ds-nav__item` entries, each a `.ds-nav__link` holding a `.ds-nav__label` and a `.ds-nav__caption`; `is-current` gives it the secondary bar colour. `.ds-nav__steps` lists `.ds-nav__step` links, each starting with a `.ds-nav__step-number` square, `is-current` on the open step, and may end with a `.ds-nav__steps-note` at the right.
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the specimen (`.ds-view--home` is the start page). A real multi-page site does not need it.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: inner pages only, first thing in the view: a 10px trail over a thin rule, `.ds-link--quiet` entries, `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` slashes and a bold `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-desk`: the search box of the home page: `.ds-desk__title` strip over `.ds-desk__body`, a form of `.ds-desk__field` labels (`.ds-desk__label` over a full-width `.ds-form__input`), `.ds-desk__row` to set two or three fields abreast (`.ds-desk__field--wide` gives one of them more room), and `.ds-desk__foot` with a link and the large search button.
- `.ds-hero`: the offer of the week, home page only. `.ds-hero__body` holds `.ds-hero__kicker`, the 21px `.ds-hero__title`, `.ds-hero__text` and `.ds-hero__actions` (one `.ds-button`, then a link); `.ds-hero__price` is the dark block at its right with the 26px `.ds-hero__figure` between two `.ds-hero__from` lines.
- `.ds-section`: a serif title behind a small accent square, over a thin rule, with `.ds-section__title` and an optional `.ds-section__more` link at the right.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page, once per page: `.ds-page-header__title` (the page's h1) and one line of `.ds-page-header__text` over a heavy rule, with `.ds-page-header__action` holding one button at the right end.
- `.ds-stat`: the row. Three to five `.ds-stat__item` cells 10px apart, each tinted under a heavy top edge, with a 21px `.ds-stat__value` over a 10px `.ds-stat__label`.
- `.ds-leg`: one leg of a journey: departure on the left and arrival on the right (`.ds-leg__end`), each a `.ds-leg__time` over a `.ds-leg__place` and the date, with `.ds-leg__facts` ruled above and below between them.
- `.ds-prose`: article text: h1, h2, h3, p, ul, ol, `code` (outlined, for fare codes), strong; `.ds-prose__byline` for the 10px line of facts and `.ds-prose__lead` for the 13px first paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: underlined link with `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` is the navigation colour without underline. `.ds-links` is a row of links under an article, each behind a small accent square.
- `.ds-button`: the coloured key for the main action; `.ds-button--large` for the one button that moves the booking on. `.ds-button--secondary` is the pale key with a lighter outline; `.ds-button--danger` is filled with the danger colour, for cancelling a booking; `disabled` or `is-disabled` greys it; `is-hover` and `is-active` use the surface set's hover and pressed shadows. `.ds-buttons` is the row under a table, with `.ds-buttons__label` saying what the buttons act on.
- `.ds-form`: an outlined block of groups, each a `.ds-form__legend` strip over a `.ds-form__group` of `.ds-form__row` pairs: a right-aligned bold `.ds-form__label` (optional `.ds-form__hint`) and a `.ds-form__field` holding the control. Controls take `.ds-form__input` (`--small`, `--auto` for selects, `--area` for textareas); `is-invalid` with a `.ds-form__error` line marks a bad field. `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox` for checkboxes and radios; `.ds-form__actions` is the tinted row with the large button.
- `.ds-table`: a fares table without an outline: tinted head over a heavy rule, a hairline under every row, `is-current` to tint the chosen row, `.ds-table__num` for right-aligned figures, `.ds-table__price` for the bold price, `.ds-table__keep` to stop a cell wrapping, `.ds-table__note` for the 10px line under a cell. `.ds-table__caption` is the small line under it.
- `.ds-tabs`: the sort line: an outlined tinted strip standing on the table, opened by a muted `.ds-tabs__label`, with `.ds-tabs__tab` links; `is-current` is a cut-out in the sheet colour under a heavy top edge.
- `.ds-list`: offers one to a line. Each `.ds-list__item` has a `.ds-list__body` (a bold `.ds-list__title` link over a `.ds-list__meta` line) and a serif `.ds-list__price` at the right; rows alternate two surfaces and are ruled apart.
- `.ds-panel`: an outlined box: `.ds-panel__title` strip over `.ds-panel__body`, optional `.ds-panel__actions` foot with a button. Body helpers: `.ds-panel__text`, `.ds-panel__list` of `.ds-panel__row` (label left, figure right; `--total` is the ruled bold sum), `.ds-panel__small`. `.ds-panel--alt` is tinted under the secondary bar; `.ds-panel--inverse` is the one dark box of a page.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the narrowing column: a tinted block under a heavy top edge, with ruled `.ds-sidebar__title` headings over `.ds-sidebar__list` lists whose `.ds-sidebar__item` rows hold a link and a `.ds-sidebar__count`.
- `.ds-grid`: four `.ds-grid__cell` offers two by two, tinted `--color-fill-1` or with `--2`, `--3`, `--4`, each with a 15px `.ds-grid__title` link, a `.ds-grid__text` line and a 21px `.ds-grid__price` under a small `.ds-grid__from`.
- `.ds-badge`: a small accent label ("BEST PRICE"). `.ds-badge--alt` is bare text in `--color-accent-alt`. Status labels change the fill: `.ds-badge--success` (ticketed, confirmed), `.ds-badge--warning` (pay by), `.ds-badge--danger` (card refused).
- `.ds-notice`: an outlined message that opens with a bold `.ds-notice__title` on its own line. `.ds-notice--error` uses the danger colours and a heavier outline. Stack several in `.ds-notices`.
- `.ds-pagination`: a typed line under the table: `.ds-pagination__count` on the left, then underlined page links with the current page as `.ds-pagination__current` on a tinted square.
- `.ds-dialog`: the era's message box, in the page flow: a `--size-dialog` box with a heavy outline, a tinted serif `.ds-dialog__title`, `.ds-dialog__body` with `.ds-dialog__text` and left-aligned `.ds-dialog__actions`, on the flat `.ds-dialog__backdrop`.
- `.ds-empty`: an outlined block with a centred muted `.ds-empty__text` and one button; it fills the height of its row.
- `.ds-footer`: a thin rule, then `.ds-footer__bar`, a row of underlined `.ds-footer__link` entries on the sheet, followed by `.ds-page__legal`.
- `.ds-carousel`: marks the lead block as a rotator; `.ds-carousel__dots` is its row of numbered `.ds-carousel__dot` buttons, the showing one `.is-current`. Static: one slide is in the page.
- `.ds-accordion`: a list of questions or help topics as `<details class="ds-accordion__item">` with `.ds-accordion__summary` (a plus or minus box before it) and `.ds-accordion__body`. Use it for FAQs and help, not for navigation.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a help hint beside a field: `.ds-tooltip__trigger` is a boxed question mark, `.ds-tooltip__text` shows under it on hover or focus. Everywhere else use the browser's own `title`.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 4px`: only buttons, inputs and badges are eased, by 4px; boxes are square.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 0px`: the only shadows are hard 1px offsets under buttons and under the dialog.
- `text-shadow = none`: text is flat, including on bars.
- `font-size <= 26px`: the largest text is the 26px wordmark and hero price.
- `font-size >= 10px`: nothing is set below 10px.
- `font-weight <= 700`: regular and bold only.
- `font-families <= 4`: a serif for titles, two sans-serifs, a monospace for codes.
- `letter-spacing <= 0px`: no text is tracked.
- `uppercase-text <= 0%`: nothing is uppercased by CSS.
- `border-width <= 2px`: hairlines, and 2px for heavy rules.
- `row-gap <= 6px`: rows of tables and lists touch.
- `block-gap <= 14px`: blocks sit 10px apart.
- `content-width <= 770px`: the sheet is fixed at 760px and does not stretch.
- `transition = none`: states change instantly.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen: a new box copies `.ds-panel` (hairline outline, title strip in `--fill-bar`, body padded `--space-4`); a new search form copies `.ds-desk`; a new list of priced things copies `.ds-list` or `.ds-table` with the price at the right in the heading font; a new step of a booking copies the `fare` or `book` view (breadcrumb, `.ds-page-header`, `.ds-page__columns--summary` with the running price on the right) and adds its link to `.ds-nav__steps`. Use only the tokens in `tokens.css` and the `--size-*` and `--space-*` tokens in `components.css`, and take the nearest spacing step instead of a new number. Keep body text at 12px with 10px small print, set titles and prices in the heading font, close blocks with rules instead of boxing them, and never add soft shadows, rounded containers, photographs as backgrounds or whitespace wider than 14px.
