A sun the size of a coin

Painted in forty minutes, standing in the water, before the light had a colour.

The horizon is drawn with the edge of a knife. Everything above it was laid in one wet layer and never touched again.

Where to stand

Two metres back, a little to the left. The catalogue number is Cat. 01; the audio guide begins here.

Look for

  • The pencil line still showing under the sea
  • A thumbprint in the lower right corner
  • The date scratched into wet paint
  • The wall text
  • The audio stop (heard)
