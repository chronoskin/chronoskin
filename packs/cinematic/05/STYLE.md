# Cinematic exhibition

## Summary

This is the site of a museum's exhibition between 2015 and 2026: a deep green page, a masthead with the house's name in the middle, and one picture edge to edge, a row of dark rooms seen through their doorways with a single lit painting at the end, carrying nothing but its captions. The title stands under the picture in a large display serif, works hang as framed canvases under their own lamps, and the one colour is brass, on rules, frames and buttons. It was a common look for exhibition and collection sites of museums and galleries in the late 2010s and the 2020s.

## Layout

- The page is fluid and designed at 1440px. Content sits in `.ds-wrap`, centred, at most `--size-page` (1200px) wide with `--space-6` side padding (`--space-4` on a phone). Sections pad themselves with `--space-8` (96px), `--tight` with `--space-7`.
- Navigation is `.ds-nav`, a masthead on the page colour in two lines: on the first the day's hours at the left, the brand in the middle with its name in the heading face, and the word Menu (a `.ds-menu` sheet) and a small ticket button at the right; on the second, under a hairline, four label links centred, `--size-nav` (56px) tall. A strong rule closes it and a strong accent mark stands under the current link.
- The home view stacks: `.ds-hero`, which is `.ds-hero__picture`, the scene `--size-scene` (640px) tall from edge to edge with a hairline frame and four captions and no other text, then `.ds-hero__copy` on the page: the kicker and the title across the whole column, and under a hairline one row with the lead, the run of the exhibition as a `.ds-progress` and two buttons; a `.ds-split` section whose head stays at the left while the `.ds-list` of rooms runs at the right; `.ds-wall`, a stretch of gallery wall holding the `.ds-carousel` of framed works; the `.ds-stat` row; the `.ds-grid` of four cards for the visit; the `.ds-band`.
- Inner pages have no hero and no picture at their head: type alone, centred. Each opens inside `.ds-wrap` with `.ds-top` (the breadcrumb) and the `.ds-page-header`: the title, one line of text and at most two buttons, all centred, on a strong rule. Then `.ds-main` with `.ds-cols`: a wide column and the `.ds-sidebar` `--size-side` (300px) wide at the right; `--work` and `--even` are two halves. Tabs sit at the top of the wide column, above the notices.
- Views. The specimen is the site of one exhibition. `home` is the exhibition. `rooms` shows one room: tabs, two notices, six works in a grid of three with badges and a tooltip, and the pagination; the rooms as a sidebar and an empty state at the right. `work` shows one work: `.ds-hang`, the work on its wall under a lamp, and the curator as an avatar at the left; a panel of facts, notes as prose, links and an accordion at the right. `visit` has the table of hours, a panel with the day's places as a progress line and two switches at the left, and the booking form with its error and all four buttons and the open dialog at the right.
- Below 1100px the row under the title, the split section and the columns stack and the grid and the figures go two by two; below 800px the masthead loses the hours and its links scroll sideways, the picture is lower, works go two by two and a wide table scrolls inside `.ds-table__wrap`; below 520px the grid is one column. The page never scrolls sideways.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px, `--space-2` 8px (label to text, sidebar rows), `--space-3` 14px (between buttons, a work's mount), `--space-4` 20px (rows, panel padding, grid gap), `--space-5` 28px (column gaps in rows, cell padding), `--space-6` 40px (page gutter, stack gap, columns), `--space-7` 60px (section head to content, split columns, between hung works), `--space-8` 96px (sections, the wall), `--space-9` 150px. `--space-track` 2.5px is the tracking of labels.
- Other sizes: `--size-mark` 30px, `--size-control` 44px, `--size-dialog` 500px, `--size-work` 340px (the width of a hung work).
- On a phone (800px and below) `.ds-nav__hours` is not shown and `.ds-nav__links`, the second line of the masthead, is one row that scrolls sideways without a scrollbar. At 520px and below the button in `.ds-nav__actions` is not shown, the `.ds-menu` stays, the links spread over the row, and a tooltip in a work opens as a full-width note above its line.

## Typography and colour roles

- These are the conventions of the era, kept from its first layout. A block stands on one of five grounds, and each ground has its own text token. The page (`--fill-page`), panels (`--fill-panel`), `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` and the four fills carry `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading` and `--color-link`. A scene (a picture) stands on the bar, `--fill-bar` or `--color-bar`, and carries only `--color-bar-text`. The footer stands on `--fill-bar-alt` and carries `--color-bar-alt-text`. The inverted block (`--fill-inverse`, `.ds-band`) carries only `--color-inverse-text`. Accent elements (`--fill-accent`: badges) carry `--color-accent-text`.
- Pictures are drawn, never loaded. In a picture `--color-bar` is the ground and every solid shape is a silhouette in it; `--color-accent` is the light; `--color-accent-alt` is the second light; `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are the tones between; `--color-bar-text` is the hottest point of a light. The texture of a picture comes from `--fill-bar`: `.ds-scene__grain` takes that fill less its own colour (two background layers blended by difference) and lays it over the finished picture, so a surface set changes the texture of every picture and it shows on a dark and on a pale ground alike.
- Nothing assumes that a palette is dark. A picture is as dark as `--color-bar`, the page as dark as `--color-page`; `--color-inverse` is the opposite of the page.
- The fills are also the grounds of grid cells and carry `--color-text` and `--color-heading`, never `--color-bar-text` or `--color-inverse-text` as running text.
- Buttons by ground. On the page, panels and fills: `.ds-button` (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`) and `.ds-button--secondary` (`--fill-button-secondary`, text and outline in `--color-button-secondary-text`). On a scene only `.ds-button--onscene` (fill `--color-bar-text`, text `--color-bar`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. On the inverted block only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost`. The destructive button is `--color-danger` with `--color-button-text`.
- `--color-heading-alt` is the colour of kickers, numbers used as labels, panel and sidebar titles and h3 in prose. The label is the second voice: `--font-ui` at `--text-small` or `--text-ui`, `--ui-transform`, tracked by `--space-track` (a layout token; the type vocabulary has no positive tracking for labels). Dates, times, prices and counts are labels in `--font-mono`.
- The default badge is `--fill-accent` with `--color-accent-text`. Status badges are never fills: the status colour is text, outline and a small dot on a transparent ground. `.ds-badge--quiet` is an outline in `--color-border`.
- `--color-notice` with `--color-notice-text` is the information notice, with a left edge of `--border-width-strong` in `--color-accent`; the error notice is `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`.
- Borders: `--border-width` in `--color-border` between rows and around panels, `--color-border-strong` for the rules that open a table, a list or a page, `--color-border-muted` inside boxes, `--color-input-border` around fields. `--border-width-strong` marks the current item (navigation, tabs, pagination), the progress line and the notice edge. Every border takes `--border-style`.
- Radii: `--radius-control` for buttons, fields and carousel arrows; `--radius-panel` for panels, notices, the dialog, the empty state and tooltips; `--radius-pill` for badges, switches and avatars; `--radius-page` for the largest boxes (pictures, grid cells). Shapes inside a drawing use percentages and `clip-path`, never a radius token.
- Shadows: `--shadow-panel` on panels and grid cells, `--shadow-control` on buttons and the brand tile, `--shadow-control-hover` on hovered buttons and cells, `--shadow-dialog` on the dialog and an open menu. `--backdrop-blur` blurs what lies behind the dialog's veil.
- One-off values made with `calc()` and `color-mix()` because the vocabulary has no token: lights and haze are a light token mixed with `transparent` or with a fill; hairlines on a picture are `currentColor` at 24 to 40%; the rim of light on a figure is a `drop-shadow` of twice `--border-width` in the accent; the largest type steps down as a share of its own token at 1100px, 800px and 520px, so every type set shrinks in proportion to itself; the hero title is also held to 13vw, so a wider type set cannot push it out of the column.
- Here: the page is a deep green-black `#0b1410`, pictures are darker still, text is ivory and grey green, and the one colour is brass, `--color-accent`, with a pale jade `--color-accent-alt` as the daylight of a skylight. The fills are three greens and one bronze brown. The primary button is brass with dark text; the inverted block is parchment.
- Four families. `--font-heading` is a display serif (Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond, Big Caslon, Hoefler Text) for titles and figures, in upper and lower case at weight 400 (`--heading-transform` is `none`); `--font-body` is a text serif (Source Serif, Iowan Old Style, Palatino) at `--text-base` 16px and `--line-body` 1.65; `--font-ui` is a flared sans (Optima, Marcellus) for labels in capitals; `--font-mono` (Fira Mono, Andale Mono, Menlo) sets counts and prices.
- `--text-display` 150px with `--line-display` 0.92 and `--display-tracking` -4px (the hero title only; its `em` is italic in `--color-heading-alt`); `--text-h1` 76px (section and page titles, figures); `--text-h2` 38px (room names and numerals, cell titles, the band); `--text-h3` 23px (work titles, sidebar rows, table names, menu, dialog title); `--text-large` 21px (leads).
- Surface: `--border-style` is `double`, so a rule of `--border-width-strong` (3px) is a pair of fine lines (under the masthead, the page title, the current link and tab, around a work and the dialog) while a hairline of 1px stays single. Corners are eased by 2px, the largest boxes are square. Buttons and badges are short gradients of metal with an inner highlight; panels carry an inner line of light; the dialog and a hung work cast a soft shadow. `--fill-bar` is a weave of fine lines like canvas that lies over every picture and every canvas; `--fill-page` is lighter at the top. Links in running text are underlined. No blur.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets font, text colour and `--fill-page`.
- `.ds-wrap`: the centred content column, `--size-page` wide.
- `.ds-label`: the small tracked line. `--muted`, `--accent` (in `--color-heading-alt`), `--mono` for figures.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, in the navigation and again in the footer. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark as inline SVG; `.ds-brand__name` is the name. Name and mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, p, lists, `code`, `strong`. `.ds-prose__lead` for the first paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: text link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `--quiet` for meta; `--arrow` is a label led by a hairline that grows on hover. `.ds-linkrow` lays links in a row.
- `.ds-button`: a label in a box. Variants: `--secondary`, `--danger` (destructive actions only), `--onscene` (pictures), `--inverse` (the inverted block), `--ghost` (both), `--small`, `--wide`. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttonrow` spaces buttons.
- `.ds-form`: vertical form with labels above the fields. `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side; `.ds-form__field` wraps `.ds-form__label` and one of `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`. Error: `is-error` on the input plus `.ds-form__error`. `.ds-form__hint`, `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`, `.ds-form__actions`.
- `.ds-badge`: small tracked label on `--fill-accent`. `--quiet` outlined. Status variants: `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, each an outline with a dot. `.ds-badgerow` for several.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with a strong left edge; `--error` in the danger colours. `.ds-notice__title` is the tracked lead-in. `.ds-noticerow` stacks notices.
- `.ds-pagination`: `.ds-pagination__link` labels; `is-current` is underlined with `--color-accent`, `is-disabled` is grey.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a label trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` parted by slashes; the last is `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a block of `--color-overlay` (blurring by `--backdrop-blur`) centring `.ds-dialog__box`, a panel with a strong outline and `--shadow-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__head` (`.ds-dialog__title`, a drawn cross `.ds-dialog__close`), `.ds-dialog__body` and `.ds-dialog__actions` with the main action first.
- `.ds-empty`: a hairline box with a short accent rule (`.ds-empty__icon`), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small secondary button.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input` (invisible, covers the row), `.ds-switch__track` with its knob, `.ds-switch__label` with an optional `small` line. On is `is-on` or `:checked`: outline and knob in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a dotted-underlined trigger holding `.ds-tooltip__tip`, a small inverted box shown on hover, focus or `is-open`; `--end` aligns it to the right edge.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person drawn as a backlit head and shoulders from the picture colours; `--2` lit from the left with `--color-accent-alt`, `--3` from above; `--large` doubles it. `.ds-person` sets one beside `.ds-person__name` and `.ds-person__role`.
- `.ds-progress`: `.ds-progress__head` (a label and a `.ds-progress__value` in mono) over `.ds-progress__bar`, a `<progress>` drawn as a line of `--border-width-strong` with the value in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-accordion`: ruled `<details>`: `.ds-accordion__item` with a `.ds-accordion__head` label that ends in a plus (a cross when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-band`: the inverted block, `.ds-band__inner` inside. Only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost` on it.
- `.ds-stack`: stacks blocks one step apart; `--tight` closer. `.ds-main` is the body of an inner page. `.ds-facts` is a `dl` of label and value pairs inside a panel.
- `.ds-table`: a ruled table: a label head under a strong rule, hairlines between rows, no vertical lines; `is-hover` tints a row. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures in mono; `.ds-table__title` is the row's name in the heading face. Wrap it in `.ds-table__wrap`; `.ds-table__foot` holds a count and the pagination.
- `.ds-panel`: a hairline box: `.ds-panel__head` with a label `.ds-panel__title` and an optional badge, and `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__text` is a paragraph.
- `.ds-section`: a block of the page; `--tight`. `.ds-section__head` holds a `.ds-section__kicker`, the `.ds-section__title` and an arrow link at the right.
- `.ds-figure`: one standing person in a long coat seen from behind, a silhouette in `--color-bar` with a rim of light; it stands inside a picture and is placed by that picture.
- `.ds-scene`: the picture of the enfilade, a positioned box on `--fill-bar` in `--color-bar-text` whose first children are empty layers, in this order: `.ds-scene__far` (the far room: wall, floor and the daylight from above), `.ds-scene__spot` (the lamp's cone), `.ds-scene__art` (the lit painting), `.ds-figure` (one visitor), `.ds-scene__wall` and `.ds-scene__wall--2` (two walls, each with its doorway cut out, darker the nearer), `.ds-scene__floor` (the light running out along the floor), `.ds-scene__bench`, `.ds-scene__wall--1` (the nearest doorway, in the bar colour), `.ds-scene__jambs` (the lit thickness of each doorway), `.ds-scene__grain`, `.ds-scene__frame` (a hairline inside the edge) and two `.ds-scene__caption` lines of labels (`--foot` for the lower one). One per site.
- `.ds-work`: a painting in its frame: the strong border is the frame, the padding the mount, `.ds-work__canvas` the canvas (4 to 5; `--wide` 5 to 4), one box of gradients with the weave over it. Canvases: the default is a small sun over a dark sea; `--2` two soft fields; `--3` an arch with a lit door and a moon; `--4` rain as fine lines; `--5` a disc on a turning ground; `--6` a diagonal front. `.ds-work__title` is its name in the heading face.
- `.ds-hang`: a `.ds-scene` used as a stretch of wall for one work: `.ds-hang__light` (wall, skirting and the lamp's cone), `.ds-scene__grain`, the `.ds-work` and a label.
- `.ds-nav`: the masthead. `.ds-nav__inner` holds `.ds-nav__hours` (a label led by a dot in `--color-success`), the brand and `.ds-nav__actions`; `.ds-nav__links` is the second line of `.ds-nav__link` (the current one in the heading colour on a strong accent mark, `is-current`). In the specimen the showing view marks its link with one `:has()` rule per view; a project sets `is-current` itself.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` in the masthead: `.ds-menu__toggle` (a small lozenge and the word) and `.ds-menu__list` of centred `.ds-menu__item` in the heading face, a sheet with the strong outline. Closed until clicked.
- `.ds-hero`: `.ds-hero__picture` (the `.ds-scene`) and `.ds-hero__copy`: `.ds-hero__kicker` (a badge and a label), `.ds-hero__title` with an `em`, and `.ds-hero__side`, a row with `.ds-hero__lead`, a progress line and `.ds-hero__actions`. One per site.
- `.ds-split`: a section in two columns, 4 to 8: `.ds-split__head` (kicker, title, `.ds-split__text` and an arrow link) stays in the window while the content at the right scrolls.
- `.ds-list`: the rooms as ruled rows under a strong rule: each `.ds-list__item` is an italic `.ds-list__number`, a `.ds-list__title` with a `.ds-list__text` under it, and a `.ds-list__meta` label; a hovered row is washed from the middle.
- `.ds-wall`: a `.ds-scene` used as a section: `.ds-wall__light` (wall and skirting) and `.ds-scene__grain`, then the content in `--color-bar-text`.
- `.ds-carousel`: works hung on one line: `.ds-carousel__track` of `.ds-carousel__slide` (`--wide`, `--small`), each a `.ds-work` under the cone of its own lamp with a `.ds-carousel__caption` beneath, then `.ds-carousel__nav` with a count, `.ds-carousel__dots` of lozenge `.ds-carousel__dot` and two round `.ds-carousel__arrow`.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of four centred figures between two strong rules; each `.ds-stat__item` is a `.ds-stat__figure` over a `.ds-stat__label`, parted by hairlines.
- `.ds-grid`: four cards, one on each fill and each lit from above; a `.ds-grid__cell` is a centred link with a label at the top and `.ds-grid__title` and `.ds-grid__text` at the bottom.
- `.ds-band`: here centred, `.ds-band__title` beside one button.
- `.ds-top`: the line that opens an inner page, with the breadcrumb centred.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page, centred: `.ds-page-header__main` with the `.ds-page-header__title` and one line of `.ds-page-header__text`; `.ds-page-header__actions` under it with ordinary buttons, since it stands on the page.
- `.ds-cols`: wide column and sidebar; `--work`, `--even`. `.ds-works` is the grid of three `.ds-works__item` (a work over its title and a badge or label).
- `.ds-sidebar`: a panel with a centred `.ds-sidebar__title` and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` in the heading face with a `.ds-sidebar__count`; the current one is italic on an accent line.
- `.ds-tabs`: labels on a hairline; the current `.ds-tabs__tab` (`is-current`) stands on the strong accent rule.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing block on `--fill-bar-alt`, centred: `.ds-footer__head` with the brand and a `.ds-footer__about` line, `.ds-footer__cols` of three `.ds-footer__title` and `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link`; `.ds-footer__legal`.

## Never

- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat, also on pictures.
- `border-radius <= 2px`: corners are barely eased; only circles are round.
- `border-width <= 3px`: a hairline is 1px and the strong rule, a pair of lines, 3px.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 70px`: the widest shadow, under the dialog, ends at 70px.
- `font-size >= 12px`: labels are the smallest text.
- `font-size <= 150px`: the exhibition's title is the largest text.
- `font-weight <= 700`: the display face is set at regular weight.
- `line-height <= 1.65`: body text is set at 1.65.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. First decide which ground it stands on (page, panel or fill; picture; inverted block; footer) and take the text token and the buttons of that ground; never put a primary button on a picture or `--color-bar-text` on a fill. A new picture is built the way the existing ones are: it needs a subject that reduces to light and silhouette (a room, a doorway, a horizon, a figure against light, a lamp on an object), several layers of depth, one clear source of light and a frame or caption that says it is a picture; the bar colour for the ground and every solid shape, the accent for the light, the alternate accent for a second light, the fills for the tones between, shapes from percentages, hard gradient stops and `clip-path`, and `.ds-scene__grain` last. An inner page gets a real picture or none: type alone is right, a wash of colour is not. Do not load an image and do not write a colour. If the installing project has photographs or film, they replace the drawn layers and the captions, labels and type stay. Keep type on the `--text-*` tokens, the largest sizes stepped down with the same factors as the specimen, labels tracked by `--space-track`, spacing on the `--space-*` scale, and note any one-off `color-mix()` shade next to its rule.
