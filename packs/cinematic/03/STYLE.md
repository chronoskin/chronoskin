# Cinematic object house shop

## Summary

This is the shop of a fashion or object house between 2015 and 2026: a lookbook first and a shop second, opening on a dark room in which a few objects stand in the light of one lamp, with the collection's name in very large wide capitals. Navigation is a single thin line with the mark in the middle and the bag at the right, the product grid is sparse, each object alone on a lit plate, and prices, piece numbers and counts are small monospaced labels. It was the common look of fashion, design and game-studio shops in the second half of the 2010s and the first half of the 2020s.

## Layout

- The page is fluid and designed at 1440px, and runs edge to edge: content is inset only by `--space-5` (28px) through `.ds-edge` (`--space-4` on a phone); `--size-page` (1360px) is the width of `.ds-wrap` for text that must not grow wider. Sections pad themselves with `--space-8` (96px), `--tight` with `--space-7`; `--edge` adds the side inset.
- Navigation is `.ds-nav`, one line `--size-nav` (60px) tall on the page colour that stays at the top of the window: label links at the left (one of them a `.ds-menu` that drops the collections), the brand in the middle, a product link and the bag with its count at the right. A hairline runs under it and an accent mark under the current link.
- The home view stacks: `.ds-hero`, a room `--size-scene` (780px) tall with the title at the lower left and lead and buttons along the foot; a section with the `.ds-carousel` of looks; the `.ds-grid` of four objects; the `.ds-stat` line; the `.ds-band`.
- Inner pages have no hero. Each opens inside `.ds-edge` with `.ds-top` (the breadcrumb) and the `.ds-page-header`: a very large title on a strong hairline with one line of text under it and, at the right on the baseline, a count or price and one button. Then `.ds-main` with `.ds-cols`: a `.ds-sidebar` `--size-side` (220px) wide at the left that stays in the window, and the working column; `--object` and `--bag` are 7 to 5. Tabs sit in a `.ds-toolbar` at the top of the working column with a switch at its right end.
- Views. The specimen is the shop of a house that makes objects in numbered runs. `home` is the lookbook. `shop` has the kinds of object as a sidebar, tabs and a switch, two notices, six objects in a grid of three with badges and a tooltip, and the pagination. `object` shows one lamp: a gallery, a table of sizes, a progress line for the run, the description as prose, the maker as an avatar, links and an accordion. `bag` has the three lines of the bag as a list, the open remove dialog and an empty state at the left, and the summary panel and checkout form with all four buttons at the right.
- Below 1100px columns stack, the sidebar becomes two columns of links and grids go two by two; below 800px the navigation becomes two rows (brand and bag, then the links), the carousel's caption drops under its plate and a wide table scrolls inside `.ds-table__wrap`; below 520px figures go two by two. The page never scrolls sideways.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px, `--space-2` 8px (label to text, tab gaps), `--space-3` 12px (between buttons, grid columns, gallery gaps), `--space-4` 20px (rows, panel padding), `--space-5` 28px (page inset, stack gap, grid rows), `--space-6` 40px (columns), `--space-7` 64px (tight sections), `--space-8` 96px (sections), `--space-9` 144px. `--space-track` 2px is the tracking of labels.
- Other sizes: `--size-mark` 26px, `--size-control` 46px, `--size-dialog` 440px, `--size-thumb` 96px (gallery thumbnails and the pictures in the bag).
- On a phone (800px and below) `.ds-nav__inner` has two rows: the `.ds-brand` and `.ds-nav__actions` above, `.ds-nav__links` below on a hairline, and the first link of the actions is not shown. At 520px and below that link is shown again, the links spread over the row, the Collections `.ds-menu` opens as a full row under the bar instead of a box under its toggle, and a tooltip in a grid cell opens as a full-width note above its line.

## Typography and colour roles

These are the conventions of the era; every layout and every token set keeps them.

- A block stands on one of five grounds, and each ground has its own text token. The page (`--fill-page`), panels (`--fill-panel`), `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` and the four fills carry `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading` and `--color-link`. A scene (the picture of a screen) stands on the bar: `--fill-bar` or `--color-bar`, and carries only `--color-bar-text`. The footer and other quiet strips stand on `--fill-bar-alt` and carry `--color-bar-alt-text`. The inverted block (`--fill-inverse`, class `.ds-band`) carries only `--color-inverse-text`. Accent elements (`--fill-accent`: badges) carry `--color-accent-text`.
- Pictures are drawn, never loaded. In a scene `--color-bar` is the darkness and every solid shape (a crowd, a ridge, a figure, an object) is a silhouette in it; `--color-accent` is the light (a beam, the sun, a lamp); `--color-accent-alt` is the second light (a flare, a rim, a far lamp); `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are the tones between dark and light (haze, sky, walls, far hills); `--color-bar-text` is the hottest point of a light and the colour of an outlined word. The grain or scan lines of a scene come from `--fill-bar`, so a surface set changes the texture of every picture and a palette changes its light.
- Nothing assumes that a palette is dark. The scene is as dark as `--color-bar`, the page as dark as `--color-page`; `--color-inverse` is the opposite of the page.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are also the grounds of grid cells and carry `--color-text` and `--color-heading`; they never carry `--color-inverse-text` or `--color-bar-text` as running text.
- Buttons by ground. On the page, panels and fills: `.ds-button` (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`) and `.ds-button--secondary` (`--fill-button-secondary`, text and outline in `--color-button-secondary-text`). On a scene only `.ds-button--onscene` (fill `--color-bar-text`, text `--color-bar`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. On the inverted block only `.ds-button--inverse` (fill `--color-inverse-text`, text `--color-inverse`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. The ghost button takes outline and text from the block it stands on. The destructive button is `--color-danger` with `--color-button-text`.
- `--color-heading-alt` is the colour of kickers, numbers used as labels, panel and sidebar titles and h3 in prose. On a scene a kicker inherits `--color-bar-text`.
- The label is the second voice of the era: `--font-ui` at `--text-small` or `--text-ui`, `--ui-transform`, tracked by `--space-track` (a layout token, 2px; the type vocabulary has no positive tracking for labels). Dates, timecodes, coordinates, prices and counts are labels in `--font-mono`.
- The default badge is `--fill-accent` with `--color-accent-text`. Status badges are never fills: the status colour (`--color-success`, `--color-warning`, `--color-danger`) is text, outline and a small dot on a transparent ground, so they hold on any palette. `.ds-badge--quiet` is an outline in `--color-border`.
- `--color-notice` with `--color-notice-text` is the information notice, with a left edge of `--border-width-strong` in `--color-accent`; the error notice is `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`.
- Borders are hairlines: `--border-width` in `--color-border` between rows and around panels, `--color-border-strong` above a table or list and around the dialog, `--color-border-muted` inside boxes, `--color-input-border` around fields. `--border-width-strong` is the mark of the current item (navigation, tabs, pagination), the progress line and the notice edge.
- Radii: `--radius-control` for buttons, fields, tabs and carousel arrows; `--radius-panel` for panels, notices, the dialog, the empty state, textareas and tooltips; `--radius-pill` for badges, switches and small avatars; `--radius-page` for the largest boxes (stills, plates, sleeves, grid cells). Shapes inside a drawing use percentages and `clip-path`, never a radius token, so a surface set cannot bend a silhouette.
- Shadows: `--shadow-panel` on panels and grid cells, `--shadow-control` on buttons and the brand tile, `--shadow-control-hover` on hovered buttons and cells, `--shadow-dialog` on the dialog and an open menu. `--backdrop-blur` blurs what lies behind the dialog's veil and behind an open overlay menu.
- One-off values made with `calc()` and `color-mix()` because the vocabulary has no token: lights and haze are a light token mixed with `transparent` at 20 to 90%; hairlines on a scene are `--color-bar-text` at 20 to 30%; outlined words are a text stroke of `--border-width` in the block's text colour at 26 to 70%; the display size steps down as `--text-display` times 0.7, 0.46 and 0.3 at 1100px, 800px and 520px, and `--text-h1` times 0.6 and 0.5, so every type set shrinks in proportion to itself. Opacity 0.6 to 0.86 dims secondary text on scenes.
- Here: the page is a cold blue-black `#05090f`, rooms are darker still, text is ice white and blue grey, and the one colour is the acid yellow-green `--color-accent`, with a cyan `--color-accent-alt` as the second light and the strong outline. The fills are four deep blues and teals. The primary button is the accent with dark text and a faint glow; the inverted block is ice white.
- Three families. `--font-heading` is a wide, heavy sans (Unbounded, Archivo Black, Arial Black; the references used commercial extended faces) for titles and figures, in capitals at weight 900 with tight tracking. `--font-body` and `--font-ui` are one grotesque (Space Grotesk, Helvetica Neue): text at `--text-base` 15px and `--line-body` 1.55, labels at 12px and weight 700. `--font-mono` (Space Mono, SF Mono) sets prices, piece numbers and counts.
- `--text-display` 128px with `--line-display` 0.94 and `--display-tracking` -5px (the hero title and the outlined word on a plate; the room's outlined word is 1.4 times that); `--text-h1` 60px (section titles, figures, the band; page titles are 1.3 times that); `--text-h2` 32px (carousel titles); `--text-h3` 18px (panel, list and dialog titles); `--text-large` 19px (leads, prices).
- Surface: square corners (badges 2px), hairlines of 1px and marks of 3px, and light instead of shadow: `--shadow-control` and `--shadow-dialog` are glows in the cyan `--color-shadow`, `--shadow-panel` an inner line. `--fill-page` carries faint vertical guide lines, `--fill-bar` fine scan lines that show in every room and plate, and the button and accent fills are short gradients. Links in running text are underlined. No blur.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets font, text colour and `--fill-page`.
- `.ds-wrap`: the centred content column, `--size-page` wide.
- `.ds-label`: the small tracked line. `--muted`, `--accent` (in `--color-heading-alt`), `--mono` for figures.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, in the navigation and again in the footer. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark as inline SVG; `.ds-brand__name` is the name, set as a label. Name and mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, p, lists, `code`, `strong`. `.ds-prose__lead` for the first paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: text link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `--quiet` for meta; `--arrow` is a label led by a hairline that grows on hover, used for "all dates" and "read". `.ds-linkrow` lays links in a row.
- `.ds-button`: a flat box with a tracked label. Variants: `--secondary`, `--danger` (destructive actions only), `--onscene` (scenes), `--inverse` (the inverted block), `--ghost` (both), `--small`, `--wide`. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttonrow` spaces buttons.
- `.ds-form`: vertical form with labels above the fields. `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side; `.ds-form__field` wraps `.ds-form__label` and one of `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`. Error: `is-error` on the input plus `.ds-form__error`. `.ds-form__hint`, `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`, `.ds-form__actions`.
- `.ds-badge`: small tracked label on `--fill-accent`. `--quiet` outlined. Status variants: `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, each an outline with a dot. `.ds-badgerow` for several.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with a strong left edge; `--error` in the danger colours. `.ds-notice__title` is the tracked lead-in. `.ds-noticerow` stacks notices.
- `.ds-pagination`: `.ds-pagination__link` labels; `is-current` is underlined with `--color-accent`, `is-disabled` is grey. Page numbers are written with two digits.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a label trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` parted by slashes; the last is `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a block of `--color-overlay` (blurring by `--backdrop-blur`) centring `.ds-dialog__box`, a panel with a strong outline and `--shadow-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__head` (`.ds-dialog__title`, a drawn cross `.ds-dialog__close`), `.ds-dialog__body` and `.ds-dialog__actions` with the main action first.
- `.ds-empty`: a hairline box with a short accent rule (`.ds-empty__icon`), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small secondary button.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input` (invisible, covers the row), `.ds-switch__track` with its knob, `.ds-switch__label` with an optional `small` line. On is `is-on` or `:checked`: outline and knob in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a dotted-underlined trigger holding `.ds-tooltip__tip`, a small inverted box shown on hover, focus or `is-open`; `--end` aligns it to the right edge.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person drawn as a backlit head and shoulders: dark shapes in `--color-bar` on a lit copy of themselves, a light in the corner, a fill behind. `--2` is lit from the left with `--color-accent-alt`, `--3` from above with `--color-bar-text`; `--large` doubles it. `.ds-person` sets one beside `.ds-person__name` and `.ds-person__role`.
- `.ds-progress`: `.ds-progress__head` (a label and a `.ds-progress__value` in mono) over `.ds-progress__bar`, a `<progress>` drawn as a hairline of `--border-width-strong` with the value in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-accordion`: ruled `<details>`: `.ds-accordion__item` with a `.ds-accordion__head` label that ends in a plus (a cross when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-band`: the inverted block. Only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost` inside.
- `.ds-stack`: stacks blocks one step apart; `--tight` closer. `.ds-main` is the body of an inner page. `.ds-facts` is a `dl` of label and value pairs inside a panel.
- `.ds-object`: a thing of the house, drawn as a dark body in `--color-bar` whose edge nearest the light is the accent. The default is a flask with a stopper and a label; `--vase`, `--bowl`, `--candle` (with a flame in the accent) and `--lamp` (a dome on a stem with the accent glowing under it). It stands inside a `.ds-plinth`, a `.ds-grid__plate` or the `.ds-hero`, sized in percent of that box.
- `.ds-plinth`: the studio a single object stands in: a box on `--fill-bar` whose wall is washed with one fill, with a floor line and a pool of light where the object stands. `--2` is crossed by a slanted bar of the second light, `--3` has a disc of light behind the object, `--4` a cone from above. `--wide` (1.5 to 1) for lookbook slides, `--square` for galleries and the bag. `.ds-plinth__label` sets two mono labels across the top; `.ds-plinth__word` is an outlined word behind the object.
- `.ds-nav`: the thin line. `.ds-nav__inner` holds `.ds-nav__links` of `.ds-nav__link` (the current one bright with an accent mark under it, `is-current`), the brand and `.ds-nav__actions`; `.ds-nav__count` is the number in the bag. In the specimen the showing view marks its link with one `:has()` rule per view; a project sets `is-current` itself.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` among the links: `.ds-menu__toggle` (the word and a plus) and `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__item` rows with counts, a hairline box that drops under it. Closed until clicked.
- `.ds-hero`: the room of the home view, on `--fill-bar`: `.ds-hero__room` (the wall, the table's edge and the pool of light on it), `.ds-hero__cone` (the lamp's cone), `.ds-hero__word` (an outlined word on the wall), a `.ds-object--lamp` hanging from the top edge and three objects under it; then `.ds-hero__kicker`, `.ds-hero__title` and `.ds-hero__foot` with `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__actions`. One per site.
- `.ds-section`: a block of the page; `--tight`, `--edge`. `.ds-section__head` holds a `.ds-section__kicker`, the `.ds-section__title` and an arrow link at the right.
- `.ds-carousel`: one look to a slide: `.ds-carousel__track` of `.ds-carousel__slide` (a `.ds-plinth--wide` beside `.ds-carousel__copy` with a label, `.ds-carousel__title`, `.ds-carousel__text` and a secondary button), then `.ds-carousel__nav` with a count, `.ds-carousel__dots` of hairline `.ds-carousel__dot` and two word `.ds-carousel__arrow`.
- `.ds-grid`: a sparse grid of objects, four across; `--three` three across. A `.ds-grid__cell` is a link: `.ds-grid__plate` (a lit plate on one of the four fills, in turn, holding the object and an optional badge), a `.ds-grid__row` with `.ds-grid__title` and `.ds-grid__price`, and `.ds-grid__text`.
- `.ds-stat`: one line of four figures between hairlines; each `.ds-stat__item` is a `.ds-stat__figure` with a narrow `.ds-stat__label` beside it.
- `.ds-band`: here `.ds-band__inner` sets `.ds-band__text`, one sentence in the heading face, beside `.ds-band__side` with a paragraph and a button.
- `.ds-edge`: the side inset of full-width content. `.ds-top` holds the breadcrumb of an inner page.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page: `.ds-page-header__main` with the `.ds-page-header__title` and one line of `.ds-page-header__text`; `.ds-page-header__actions` at the right with a count or `.ds-price` and one button; a strong hairline closes it.
- `.ds-cols`: sidebar and working column; `--object`, `--bag` 7 to 5. `.ds-toolbar` puts tabs and a switch, or a count and the pagination, on one line.
- `.ds-sidebar`: `.ds-sidebar__title` label and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` labels with a `.ds-sidebar__count`; the current one is bright and led by a short accent rule.
- `.ds-tabs`: words in a row; the current `.ds-tabs__tab` (`is-current`) is boxed with `--color-border-strong`.
- `.ds-gallery`: `.ds-gallery__thumbs`, a column of three small plinths (the current one outlined with the accent), beside the large one. `.ds-price` is a price in mono at `--text-large`.
- `.ds-table`: a full grid of hairlines with a label head on `--color-surface-alt`; `is-hover` tints a row. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures in mono; `.ds-table__title` is the row's link. Wrap it in `.ds-table__wrap`.
- `.ds-panel`: a hairline box with `.ds-panel__head` (a `.ds-panel__title` in the heading face and a label) and `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__text` is a paragraph. Inside, `.ds-facts` rules each pair and `.ds-facts__total` enlarges the last value.
- `.ds-list`: the lines of the bag: each `.ds-list__item` is a small square plinth, a `.ds-list__title` with a `.ds-list__meta` label under it, and `.ds-list__end` with the price and a quiet link.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing strip on `--fill-bar-alt`: `.ds-footer__cols` with the brand and a `.ds-footer__about` line and three columns of `.ds-footer__title` and `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link`; `.ds-footer__legal`.

## Never

- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat, also on pictures.
- `border-radius <= 12px`: boxes are square and badges eased by 2px; the only rounder corners are the shoulders of a drawn flask.
- `border-width <= 3px`: rules are hairlines; 3px marks the current item and the progress line.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 80px`: shadows are glows, and the widest, around the dialog, ends at 80px.
- `font-size >= 12px`: labels are the smallest text.
- `font-size <= 180px`: the outlined word in the room is the largest, at 1.4 times the display size.
- `font-families <= 3`: a wide display face, one sans-serif for reading and labels, one monospace for figures.
- `line-height <= 1.55`: body text is set at 1.55.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. First decide which ground it stands on (page, panel or fill; scene; inverted block; footer strip) and take the text token and the buttons of that ground; never put a primary button on a scene or `--color-bar-text` on a fill. A new picture is built the way the existing ones are: the bar colour for darkness and every solid shape, the accent for the light, the alternate accent for a second light, the fills for the tones between, shapes from percentages, gradients with hard stops and `clip-path`, and the grain from `--fill-bar`; do not load an image and do not write a colour. If the installing project has photographs or film, they replace the drawn layers of a scene and everything else (the shade under the text, the labels, the type) stays. Keep type on the `--text-*` tokens, the largest sizes stepped down with the same factors as the specimen, labels tracked by `--space-track`, spacing on the `--space-*` scale, and note any one-off `color-mix()` shade next to its rule.
