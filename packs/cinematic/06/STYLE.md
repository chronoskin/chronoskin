# Cinematic festival

## Summary

This is the site of a music festival or club night between 2015 and 2026: a violet-black page, a bar of ruled cells across the top, and one rounded frame holding the night scene, a ring of magenta light standing on the water with beams fanning out of it, the festival's name in huge lowercase geometric letters low at the left. Under it the names run as one poster, the nights stand side by side as lists of times, and everything that is not type is a rounded cell with a faint glow. It was the common look of festival, club and line-up sites in the late 2010s and the 2020s.

## Layout

- The page is fluid and designed at 1440px. Content sits in `.ds-wrap`, centred, at most `--size-page` (1400px) wide with `--space-6` side padding (`--space-4` on a phone). Sections pad themselves with `--space-8` (84px), `--tight` with `--space-7`.
- Navigation is `.ds-nav`, a bar of ruled cells `--size-nav` (58px) tall on the page colour: the brand, then one equal cell to each of four links, then the word Menu (a `.ds-menu` sheet) and a small button. The current cell is tinted and stands on an accent mark.
- The home view stacks: `.ds-hero`, the scene as one rounded frame `--size-scene` (800px) tall, inset from the window by `--space-4`, with a badge and a mono label across its head (`.ds-hero__top`), the two-line title low at the left and `.ds-hero__foot`, three cells of glass: the lead, the passes sold as a `.ds-progress`, and two buttons; a section with the `.ds-poster` of names in two sizes; a section with `.ds-days`, three `.ds-day` boxes side by side, each a `.ds-list` of times; a section with the `.ds-carousel` of wide `.ds-plate` pictures, one and a part of the next to a screen; then the `.ds-stat` box, the `.ds-grid` of four stages and the `.ds-band` as a rounded slab.
- Inner pages have no hero. Each opens inside `.ds-wrap` with `.ds-top` (the breadcrumb) and the `.ds-page-header`, a box of two cells, 7 to 5: the title alone at the left, one line of text and at most two buttons at the right. Type alone; where a page has a picture (the night's) it is a full `.ds-plate--wide` at the top of `.ds-main`. Then `.ds-cols`: a column `--size-side` (280px) with the `.ds-sidebar` and a wide one; `--night` is 7 to 5, `--even` two halves. Tabs sit in a `.ds-toolbar` at the top of the wide column with a switch at its right end.
- Views. The specimen is the site of a festival of three nights. `home` is the festival. `lineup` has the stages as a sidebar and an empty state at the left, and tabs with a switch, two notices, the timetable as a table with badges and a tooltip, and the pagination at the right. `night` shows one night: the plate of its stage, notes as prose and links at the left; a panel of facts, the two hosts as avatars and an accordion at the right. `tickets` has three passes as panels, each with a price and a progress line, then the form with its error and all four buttons beside a panel of three switches and the open dialog.
- Below 1100px the days, the passes and the columns stack, the hero's foot goes to two rows and the grid and the figures go two by two; below 800px the navigation becomes two rows (brand and menu, then the link cells), the page header stacks, the ring grows against the narrower frame and a wide table scrolls inside `.ds-table__wrap`; below 520px grids are one column and plates are 4 to 3. The page never scrolls sideways.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (stripes of the water, tab inset), `--space-2` 8px (label to text), `--space-3` 12px (between buttons, list rows, hero cells), `--space-4` 16px (panel and cell padding, grid gaps, the hero's inset), `--space-5` 22px (column gap, poster gap), `--space-6` 32px (page gutter, stack gap, the hero's padding), `--space-7` 52px (section head to content, tight sections), `--space-8` 84px (sections, the beacon's glow), `--space-9` 120px. `--space-track` 1px is the tracking of labels.
- Other sizes: `--size-mark` 28px, `--size-control` 44px, `--size-dialog` 460px.
- On a phone (800px and below) `.ds-nav__inner` has two rows: the `.ds-brand` and `.ds-nav__actions` (the `.ds-menu` and a button) above, `.ds-nav__links` below on a hairline, one row of cells that scrolls sideways without a scrollbar; the current cell stays tinted on its accent mark. At 520px and below the button in `.ds-nav__actions` is not shown and the `.ds-menu` stays.

## Typography and colour roles

- These are the conventions of the era, kept from its first layout. A block stands on one of five grounds, and each ground has its own text token. The page (`--fill-page`), panels (`--fill-panel`), `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` and the four fills carry `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading` and `--color-link`. A scene (a picture) stands on the bar, `--fill-bar` or `--color-bar`, and carries only `--color-bar-text`. The footer stands on `--fill-bar-alt` and carries `--color-bar-alt-text`. The inverted block (`--fill-inverse`, `.ds-band`) carries only `--color-inverse-text`. Accent elements (`--fill-accent`: badges) carry `--color-accent-text`.
- Pictures are drawn, never loaded. In a picture `--color-bar` is the ground and every solid shape is a silhouette in it; `--color-accent` is the light; `--color-accent-alt` is the second light; `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are the tones between; `--color-bar-text` is the hottest point of a light. The texture of a picture comes from `--fill-bar`: `.ds-scene__grain` takes that fill less its own colour (two background layers blended by difference) and lays it over the finished picture, so a surface set changes the texture of every picture and it shows on a dark and on a pale ground alike.
- Nothing assumes that a palette is dark. A picture is as dark as `--color-bar`, the page as dark as `--color-page`; `--color-inverse` is the opposite of the page.
- The fills are also the grounds of grid cells and carry `--color-text` and `--color-heading`, never `--color-bar-text` or `--color-inverse-text` as running text.
- Buttons by ground. On the page, panels and fills: `.ds-button` (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`) and `.ds-button--secondary` (`--fill-button-secondary`, text and outline in `--color-button-secondary-text`). On a scene only `.ds-button--onscene` (fill `--color-bar-text`, text `--color-bar`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. On the inverted block only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost`. The destructive button is `--color-danger` with `--color-button-text`.
- `--color-heading-alt` is the colour of kickers, numbers used as labels, panel and sidebar titles and h3 in prose. The label is the second voice: `--font-ui` at `--text-small` or `--text-ui`, `--ui-transform`, tracked by `--space-track` (a layout token; the type vocabulary has no positive tracking for labels). Dates, times, prices and counts are labels in `--font-mono`.
- The default badge is `--fill-accent` with `--color-accent-text`. Status badges are never fills: the status colour is text, outline and a small dot on a transparent ground. `.ds-badge--quiet` is an outline in `--color-border`.
- `--color-notice` with `--color-notice-text` is the information notice, with a left edge of `--border-width-strong` in `--color-accent`; the error notice is `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`.
- Borders: `--border-width` in `--color-border` between rows and around panels, `--color-border-strong` for the rules that open a table, a list or a page, `--color-border-muted` inside boxes, `--color-input-border` around fields. `--border-width-strong` marks the current item (navigation, tabs, pagination), the progress line and the notice edge. Every border takes `--border-style`.
- Radii: `--radius-control` for buttons, fields and carousel arrows; `--radius-panel` for panels, notices, the dialog, the empty state and tooltips; `--radius-pill` for badges, switches and avatars; `--radius-page` for the largest boxes (pictures, grid cells). Shapes inside a drawing use percentages and `clip-path`, never a radius token.
- Shadows: `--shadow-panel` on panels and grid cells, `--shadow-control` on buttons and the brand tile, `--shadow-control-hover` on hovered buttons and cells, `--shadow-dialog` on the dialog and an open menu. `--backdrop-blur` blurs what lies behind the dialog's veil.
- One-off values made with `calc()` and `color-mix()` because the vocabulary has no token: lights and haze are a light token mixed with `transparent` or with a fill; hairlines on a picture are `currentColor` at 24 to 40%; the rim of light on a figure is a `drop-shadow` of twice `--border-width` in the accent; the largest type steps down as a share of its own token at 1100px, 800px and 520px, so every type set shrinks in proportion to itself.
- Here: the page is a violet-black `#0d0618`, pictures are darker still, text is pale lilac, and the one colour is the magenta `--color-accent`, with a violet `--color-accent-alt` as the second light (the beams, the beacon). The fills are four deep violets, plum and indigo. The primary button is magenta with dark text and a glow; the inverted block is pale lilac.
- Three families. `--font-heading` and `--font-ui` are one geometric sans (Futura, Jost, Poppins, Century Gothic): titles in lowercase at weight 700 with tight tracking (`--heading-transform` is `lowercase`), labels in capitals at 500; `--font-body` is a grotesque (Inter, Helvetica Neue) at `--text-base` 15px and `--line-body` 1.45; `--font-mono` (DM Mono, Roboto Mono, Menlo) sets times, prices and counts.
- `--text-display` 176px with `--line-display` 0.8 and `--display-tracking` -4px (the hero title and the pale word in the footer); `--text-h1` 80px (section and page titles, the poster's first names, figures, prices, the band); `--text-h2` 36px (day names, the poster's second names, cell and plate titles, the menu); `--text-h3` 20px (acts in lists and tables, sidebar rows, dialog title); `--text-large` 20px (leads).
- Surface: everything is rounded, controls by 10px, panels by 14px, the largest boxes by 22px, badges and switches as pills; hairlines of 1px and marks of 2px. Light replaces shadow: buttons and the dialog glow in the magenta `--color-shadow`, panels float on a dark soft shadow. Panels, fields, the footer and the tabs are translucent (`--fill-panel`, `--fill-input`, `--fill-bar-alt`), and `--backdrop-blur` is 18px: the cells at the foot of the hero and the dialog's veil blur the picture behind them. `--fill-bar` is a fine diagonal hatch that lies over every picture; `--fill-page` glows faintly at the top right. Links are not underlined until hovered.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets font, text colour and `--fill-page`.
- `.ds-wrap`: the centred content column, `--size-page` wide.
- `.ds-label`: the small tracked line. `--muted`, `--accent` (in `--color-heading-alt`), `--mono` for figures.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, in the navigation and again in the footer. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark as inline SVG; `.ds-brand__name` is the name. Name and mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, p, lists, `code`, `strong`. `.ds-prose__lead` for the first paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: text link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `--quiet` for meta; `--arrow` is a label led by a hairline that grows on hover. `.ds-linkrow` lays links in a row.
- `.ds-button`: a label in a box. Variants: `--secondary`, `--danger` (destructive actions only), `--onscene` (pictures), `--inverse` (the inverted block), `--ghost` (both), `--small`, `--wide`. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttonrow` spaces buttons.
- `.ds-form`: vertical form with labels above the fields. `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side; `.ds-form__field` wraps `.ds-form__label` and one of `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`. Error: `is-error` on the input plus `.ds-form__error`. `.ds-form__hint`, `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`, `.ds-form__actions`.
- `.ds-badge`: small tracked label on `--fill-accent`. `--quiet` outlined. Status variants: `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, each an outline with a dot. `.ds-badgerow` for several.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with a strong left edge; `--error` in the danger colours. `.ds-notice__title` is the tracked lead-in. `.ds-noticerow` stacks notices.
- `.ds-pagination`: `.ds-pagination__link` labels; `is-current` is underlined with `--color-accent`, `is-disabled` is grey.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a label trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` parted by slashes; the last is `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a block of `--color-overlay` (blurring by `--backdrop-blur`) centring `.ds-dialog__box`, a panel with a strong outline and `--shadow-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__head` (`.ds-dialog__title`, a drawn cross `.ds-dialog__close`), `.ds-dialog__body` and `.ds-dialog__actions` with the main action first.
- `.ds-empty`: a hairline box with a short accent rule (`.ds-empty__icon`), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small secondary button.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input` (invisible, covers the row), `.ds-switch__track` with its knob, `.ds-switch__label` with an optional `small` line. On is `is-on` or `:checked`: outline and knob in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a dotted-underlined trigger holding `.ds-tooltip__tip`, a small inverted box shown on hover, focus or `is-open`; `--end` aligns it to the right edge.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person drawn as a backlit head and shoulders from the picture colours; `--2` lit from the left with `--color-accent-alt`, `--3` from above; `--large` doubles it. `.ds-person` sets one beside `.ds-person__name` and `.ds-person__role`.
- `.ds-progress`: `.ds-progress__head` (a label and a `.ds-progress__value` in mono) over `.ds-progress__bar`, a `<progress>` drawn as a line of `--border-width-strong` with the value in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-accordion`: ruled `<details>`: `.ds-accordion__item` with a `.ds-accordion__head` label that ends in a plus (a cross when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-band`: the inverted block, `.ds-band__inner` inside. Only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost` on it.
- `.ds-stack`: stacks blocks one step apart; `--tight` closer. `.ds-main` is the body of an inner page. `.ds-facts` is a `dl` of label and value pairs inside a panel.
- `.ds-table`: a ruled table: a label head under a strong rule, hairlines between rows, no vertical lines; `is-hover` tints a row. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures in mono; `.ds-table__title` is the row's name in the heading face. Wrap it in `.ds-table__wrap`; `.ds-table__foot` holds a count and the pagination.
- `.ds-panel`: a hairline box: `.ds-panel__head` with a label `.ds-panel__title` and an optional badge, and `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__text` is a paragraph.
- `.ds-section`: a block of the page; `--tight`. `.ds-section__head` holds a `.ds-section__kicker`, the `.ds-section__title` and an arrow link at the right.
- `.ds-figure`: one standing person in a long coat seen from behind, a silhouette in `--color-bar` with a rim of light; it stands inside a picture and is placed by that picture.
- `.ds-scene`: the picture of the shore, a positioned box on `--fill-bar` in `--color-bar-text` whose first children are empty layers, in this order: `.ds-scene__sky` (night sky with a few stars, lit at the horizon), `.ds-scene__beams` (thin beams fanning out of the ring), `.ds-scene__skyline` (a far town), `.ds-scene__tower` and `.ds-scene__lamp` (a beacon and its light), `.ds-scene__water` (the water, the ring's broken reflection and the two paths of light), `.ds-scene__ring` (the ring and its glow), `.ds-scene__pier` (the near pier and its posts), `.ds-scene__shade` (darkness under the text) and `.ds-scene__grain`. One per site.
- `.ds-plate`: one wide picture (16 to 9; `--wide` 21 to 8), a single box of gradients in `.ds-plate__set` with `.ds-scene__grain` over it, `.ds-plate__label` (two mono labels across the head) and `.ds-plate__caption` (a `.ds-plate__title` and a label along the foot). Plates: the default is a lamp on a mast throwing two beams over the stacks of a stage; `--2` the ring far off over a wide water; `--3` three tents against a glow under a string of bulbs.
- `.ds-nav`: the bar of cells. `.ds-nav__inner` holds the brand, `.ds-nav__links` of `.ds-nav__link` cells (the current one tinted on an accent mark, `is-current`) and `.ds-nav__actions`. In the specimen the showing view marks its link with one `:has()` rule per view; a project sets `is-current` itself.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` in the bar: `.ds-menu__toggle` (an accent dot and the word) and `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__item` in the heading face with a mono number, a rounded sheet with a strong outline and a glow. Closed until clicked.
- `.ds-hero`: the full scene of the home view: `.ds-hero__top`, `.ds-hero__title` of two lines, and `.ds-hero__foot` of three `.ds-hero__cell` (translucent, blurred): `.ds-hero__lead`, a progress line, `.ds-hero__actions` with `--onscene` and `--ghost` buttons. One per site.
- `.ds-poster`: the names as one running block in the heading face at `--text-h1`: `.ds-poster__name` items parted by accent dots, every other one `--outline` (a text stroke); `.ds-poster--second` is the smaller second block.
- `.ds-days`: three `.ds-day` boxes side by side; each has a `.ds-day__head` (the `.ds-day__name` and a date or badge) and a `.ds-list`.
- `.ds-list`: the acts of one night: each `.ds-list__item` is a mono `.ds-list__time`, a `.ds-list__title` and a `.ds-list__meta` label for the stage; a hovered row is tinted.
- `.ds-carousel`: `.ds-carousel__track` of `.ds-carousel__slide`, each one `.ds-plate`, the next showing at the edge; then `.ds-carousel__nav` with a count, `.ds-carousel__dots` of round `.ds-carousel__dot` (the current one a short pill) and two `.ds-carousel__arrow`.
- `.ds-stat`: four cells in one rounded ruled box; each `.ds-stat__item` is a `.ds-stat__figure` over a `.ds-stat__label`.
- `.ds-grid`: four rounded cells, one on each fill and each with a glow in a different corner; a `.ds-grid__cell` is a link with a mono label at the top and `.ds-grid__title` and `.ds-grid__text` at the bottom.
- `.ds-band`: here a rounded slab inside the column, `.ds-band__title` beside one button.
- `.ds-top`: the line that opens an inner page, with the breadcrumb.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page, a rounded box of two cells: `.ds-page-header__main` with the `.ds-page-header__title`; `.ds-page-header__side` with one line of `.ds-page-header__text` and `.ds-page-header__actions` (ordinary buttons, since it stands on a panel).
- `.ds-cols`: narrow and wide column; `--night`, `--even`. `.ds-toolbar` sets the tabs and a switch on one line. `.ds-passes` lays three panels side by side; `.ds-price` is a pass's price in the heading face. `.ds-people` lays two `.ds-person` side by side.
- `.ds-sidebar`: a rounded panel with a `.ds-sidebar__title` label and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` in the heading face with a `.ds-sidebar__count`; the current one is a tinted rounded row.
- `.ds-tabs`: one rounded strip of segments; the current `.ds-tabs__tab` (`is-current`) is filled with `--fill-accent`.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing block on `--fill-bar-alt`: `.ds-footer__cols` of four ruled `.ds-footer__col` (the brand with a `.ds-footer__about` line, then three of `.ds-footer__title` and `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link`), `.ds-footer__word`, the site's name as one large pale word, and `.ds-footer__legal`.

## Never

- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat, also on pictures; only boxes glow.
- `border-radius <= 22px`: the largest boxes are rounded by 22px; only pills and circles are rounder.
- `border-width <= 2px`: rules are hairlines; 2px marks the current item.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 90px`: the widest glow, around the dialog, ends at 90px.
- `font-size >= 12px`: labels are the smallest text.
- `font-size <= 176px`: the festival's name is the largest text.
- `font-weight <= 700`: titles are set at 700 and nothing is heavier.
- `line-height <= 1.45`: body text is set at 1.45.
- `underlined-links <= 10%`: links are told apart by colour; only a hovered link is underlined.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. First decide which ground it stands on (page, panel or fill; picture; inverted block; footer) and take the text token and the buttons of that ground; never put a primary button on a picture or `--color-bar-text` on a fill. A new picture is built the way the existing ones are: it needs a subject that reduces to light and silhouette (a room, a doorway, a horizon, a figure against light, a lamp on an object), several layers of depth, one clear source of light and a frame or caption that says it is a picture; the bar colour for the ground and every solid shape, the accent for the light, the alternate accent for a second light, the fills for the tones between, shapes from percentages, hard gradient stops and `clip-path`, and `.ds-scene__grain` last. An inner page gets a real picture or none: type alone is right, a wash of colour is not. Do not load an image and do not write a colour. If the installing project has photographs or film, they replace the drawn layers and the captions, labels and type stay. Keep type on the `--text-*` tokens, the largest sizes stepped down with the same factors as the specimen, labels tracked by `--space-track`, spacing on the `--space-*` scale, and note any one-off `color-mix()` shade next to its rule.
