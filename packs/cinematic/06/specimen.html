saturday night

Fourteen acts from six in the evening until the ferry. The hangar runs latest.

Saturday pass Timetable
The hangar stage18.07, 22:40one lamp, two beamsPlate 01

how the night runs

It starts with brass on the pier while the light goes, and it ends in the hangar with the doors open to the water.

Nothing overlaps on the ring. Between sets the stage goes dark for exactly ten minutes and the only light on the site is the lamp on the mast.

getting between stages

Four minutes on foot from the ring to the hangar. Your pass carries the code SAT 18 and opens every gate.
