# Cinematic tour site

## Summary

This is the site of a band on tour between 2015 and 2026: every screen opens with a dark stage drawn edge to edge, the tour's name in enormous condensed capitals over it, half of it only an outline, and almost no chrome, only a thin line of small tracked labels across the top. Below the picture the page is black with a fine grain, tour dates are a huge ruled list, numbers are set as graphics and one hot colour is used like a stage lamp: in the beams, under the current navigation item, on the one badge. It was the common look of official artist, tour and festival sites in the second half of the 2010s and the first half of the 2020s.

## Layout

- The page is fluid and designed at 1440px. Content sits in `.ds-wrap`, centred, at most `--size-page` (1320px) wide with `--space-6` side padding (`--space-4` on a phone). Sections pad themselves with `--space-9` (128px), `--tight` and scene sections with `--space-8`.
- Navigation is `.ds-nav`, one line `--size-nav` (72px) tall laid over the top of the scene that opens every view: brand at the left, four label links at the right, then the word Menu (a `.ds-menu` that drops a list in the corner) and a small ghost button. A hairline of the scene's text colour runs under it.
- The home view stacks: `.ds-hero`, a scene `--size-scene` (860px) tall with the kicker, the two-line title and a foot of three columns (lead, two buttons, a countdown `.ds-progress`); a `.ds-ticker` of city names; a section with the `.ds-list` of the next five nights; the `.ds-stat` row; a scene section holding the `.ds-carousel` of records; the `.ds-grid` of four cells; the `.ds-band`; the footer with the site's name as one outlined word.
- Inner pages have no hero. Each opens with `.ds-strip`, a low block on the bar fill `--size-strip` (420px) tall that carries the breadcrumb and the `.ds-page-header` (large title, one line of text, at most two buttons at the right, all in the scene's text colour). A strip holds either a real small picture (the tour's is a bank of lamps, `.ds-scene--lamps`) or type alone on the grain with one outlined word, never a wash of colour; then `.ds-main` with `.ds-cols`: a narrow column with the `.ds-sidebar` and a wide one (1 to 3), `--wide` (5 to 7) or `--even`. Tabs sit at the top of the wide column, above the notices and the table.
- Views. The specimen is the site of a band on tour. `home` is the landing page. `tour` has the legs of the tour as a sidebar with two progress lines and an empty state for festivals, then tabs, two notices, the table of dates with badges and a tooltip, and the pagination. `records` shows one record: its sleeve, a panel of details, the four players as avatars, the running order, notes as prose, links and an accordion of formats. `club` is the sign-up: the form with its error and all four buttons, a panel of three switches and the open dialog.
- Below 1100px the columns stack and the grid and the figures go two by two; below 800px the navigation becomes two rows (links scroll sideways), the hero's foot stacks, a list row keeps only date, city and badge, and a wide table scrolls inside `.ds-table__wrap`; below 520px the grid is one column. The page never scrolls sideways.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (dots of the LED screen, hairline gaps), `--space-2` 8px (label to text), `--space-3` 12px (between buttons, table cells), `--space-4` 16px (list rows, panel padding), `--space-5` 24px (cell padding, column gaps in rows), `--space-6` 32px (page gutter, stack gap), `--space-7` 48px (section head to content, columns), `--space-8` 80px (tight sections), `--space-9` 128px (sections). `--space-track` 2px is the tracking of labels.
- Other sizes: `--size-mark` 28px, `--size-control` 44px, `--size-dialog` 480px, `--size-sleeve` 420px (a record cover, and the least height of a grid cell less `--space-7`), `--size-date` 132px (the date column of the list).
- On a phone (800px and below) `.ds-nav__inner` has two rows: the `.ds-brand` and `.ds-nav__actions` (the `.ds-menu` and the button) above, `.ds-nav__links` below on a hairline, one row that scrolls sideways without a scrollbar; the current link keeps its accent mark. At 520px and below the button in `.ds-nav__actions` is not shown, the `.ds-menu` stays, and the links spread over the row instead of scrolling.

## Typography and colour roles

These are the conventions of the era; every layout and every token set keeps them.

- A block stands on one of five grounds, and each ground has its own text token. The page (`--fill-page`), panels (`--fill-panel`), `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` and the four fills carry `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading` and `--color-link`. A scene (the picture of a screen) stands on the bar: `--fill-bar` or `--color-bar`, and carries only `--color-bar-text`. The footer and other quiet strips stand on `--fill-bar-alt` and carry `--color-bar-alt-text`. The inverted block (`--fill-inverse`, class `.ds-band`) carries only `--color-inverse-text`. Accent elements (`--fill-accent`: badges) carry `--color-accent-text`.
- Pictures are drawn, never loaded. In a scene `--color-bar` is the darkness and every solid shape (stage gear, a ridge, a figure, an object) is a silhouette in it; `--color-accent` is the light (a beam, the sun, a lamp); `--color-accent-alt` is the second light (a flare, a rim, a far lamp); `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are the tones between dark and light (haze, sky, walls, far hills); `--color-bar-text` is the hottest point of a light and the colour of an outlined word. The grain or scan lines of a scene come from `--fill-bar`, so a surface set changes the texture of every picture and a palette changes its light.
- Nothing assumes that a palette is dark. The scene is as dark as `--color-bar`, the page as dark as `--color-page`; `--color-inverse` is the opposite of the page.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are also the grounds of grid cells and carry `--color-text` and `--color-heading`; they never carry `--color-inverse-text` or `--color-bar-text` as running text.
- Buttons by ground. On the page, panels and fills: `.ds-button` (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`) and `.ds-button--secondary` (`--fill-button-secondary`, text and outline in `--color-button-secondary-text`). On a scene only `.ds-button--onscene` (fill `--color-bar-text`, text `--color-bar`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. On the inverted block only `.ds-button--inverse` (fill `--color-inverse-text`, text `--color-inverse`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. The ghost button takes outline and text from the block it stands on. The destructive button is `--color-danger` with `--color-button-text`.
- `--color-heading-alt` is the colour of kickers, numbers used as labels, panel and sidebar titles and h3 in prose. On a scene a kicker inherits `--color-bar-text`.
- The label is the second voice of the era: `--font-ui` at `--text-small` or `--text-ui`, `--ui-transform`, tracked by `--space-track` (a layout token, 2px; the type vocabulary has no positive tracking for labels). Dates, timecodes, coordinates, prices and counts are labels in `--font-mono`.
- The default badge is `--fill-accent` with `--color-accent-text`. Status badges are never fills: the status colour (`--color-success`, `--color-warning`, `--color-danger`) is text, outline and a small dot on a transparent ground, so they hold on any palette. `.ds-badge--quiet` is an outline in `--color-border`.
- `--color-notice` with `--color-notice-text` is the information notice, with a left edge of `--border-width-strong` in `--color-accent`; the error notice is `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`.
- Borders are hairlines: `--border-width` in `--color-border` between rows and around panels, `--color-border-strong` above a table or list and around the dialog, `--color-border-muted` inside boxes, `--color-input-border` around fields. `--border-width-strong` is the mark of the current item (navigation, tabs, pagination), the progress line and the notice edge.
- Radii: `--radius-control` for buttons, fields, tabs and carousel arrows; `--radius-panel` for panels, notices, the dialog, the empty state, textareas and tooltips; `--radius-pill` for badges, switches and small avatars; `--radius-page` for the largest boxes (stills, plates, sleeves, grid cells). Shapes inside a drawing use percentages and `clip-path`, never a radius token, so a surface set cannot bend a silhouette.
- Shadows: `--shadow-panel` on panels and grid cells, `--shadow-control` on buttons and the brand tile, `--shadow-control-hover` on hovered buttons and cells, `--shadow-dialog` on the dialog and an open menu. `--backdrop-blur` blurs what lies behind the dialog's veil and behind an open overlay menu.
- One-off values made with `calc()` and `color-mix()` because the vocabulary has no token: lights and haze are a light token mixed with `transparent` at 20 to 90%; hairlines on a scene are `--color-bar-text` at 20 to 30%; outlined words are a text stroke of `--border-width` in the block's text colour at 26 to 70%; the display size steps down as `--text-display` times 0.7, 0.46 and 0.3 at 1100px, 800px and 520px, and `--text-h1` times 0.6 and 0.5, so every type set shrinks in proportion to itself. Opacity 0.6 to 0.86 dims secondary text on scenes.
- Here: the page and every scene are black, text is white and grey, and the one colour is the red `--color-accent`, with the orange `--color-accent-alt` as the second lamp. The fills are two near-blacks and two dried reds. The primary button is white with black text and turns red when hovered; the inverted block is pale grey.
- Three families. `--font-heading` is a condensed poster grotesque (Bebas Neue, League Gothic, Oswald, Impact; the references used commercial condensed faces) for every title, date and figure, always in capitals. `--font-body` is Helvetica Neue at `--text-base` 15px and `--line-body` 1.6. `--font-ui` and `--font-mono` are one monospace for all labels at `--text-small` 11px and `--text-ui` 12px.
- `--text-display` 220px with `--line-display` 0.84 and `--display-tracking` -2px (hero title, the outlined footer word, carousel numbers; the outlined scene word is 1.5 times that); `--text-h1` 96px (section and page titles, figures); `--text-h2` 48px (list rows, cell titles); `--text-h3` 26px (track titles, sidebar rows, table cities, dialog title); `--text-large` 20px (leads).
- Surface: no radius anywhere, no shadows, hairlines of 1px and marks of 2px, a speckle of grain in `--fill-page` and a denser one in `--fill-bar` (both a tiled pattern of single specks, with no filter), a fast linear `--transition`, no blur. Links in running text are underlined; navigation and titles are not.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets font, text colour and `--fill-page`.
- `.ds-wrap`: the centred content column, `--size-page` wide.
- `.ds-label`: the small tracked line. `--muted`, `--accent` (in `--color-heading-alt`), `--mono` for figures.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, in the navigation and again in the footer. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark as inline SVG; `.ds-brand__name` is the name, set as a label. Name and mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, p, lists, `code`, `strong`. `.ds-prose__lead` for the first paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: text link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `--quiet` for meta; `--arrow` is a label led by a hairline that grows on hover, used for "all dates" and "read". `.ds-linkrow` lays links in a row.
- `.ds-button`: a flat box with a tracked label. Variants: `--secondary`, `--danger` (destructive actions only), `--onscene` (scenes), `--inverse` (the inverted block), `--ghost` (both), `--small`, `--wide`. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttonrow` spaces buttons.
- `.ds-form`: vertical form with labels above the fields. `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side; `.ds-form__field` wraps `.ds-form__label` and one of `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`. Error: `is-error` on the input plus `.ds-form__error`. `.ds-form__hint`, `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`, `.ds-form__actions`.
- `.ds-badge`: small tracked label on `--fill-accent`. `--quiet` outlined. Status variants: `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, each an outline with a dot. `.ds-badgerow` for several.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with a strong left edge; `--error` in the danger colours. `.ds-notice__title` is the tracked lead-in. `.ds-noticerow` stacks notices.
- `.ds-pagination`: `.ds-pagination__link` labels; `is-current` is underlined with `--color-accent`, `is-disabled` is grey. Page numbers are written with two digits.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a label trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` parted by slashes; the last is `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a block of `--color-overlay` (blurring by `--backdrop-blur`) centring `.ds-dialog__box`, a panel with a strong outline and `--shadow-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__head` (`.ds-dialog__title`, a drawn cross `.ds-dialog__close`), `.ds-dialog__body` and `.ds-dialog__actions` with the main action first.
- `.ds-empty`: a hairline box with a short accent rule (`.ds-empty__icon`), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small secondary button.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input` (invisible, covers the row), `.ds-switch__track` with its knob, `.ds-switch__label` with an optional `small` line. On is `is-on` or `:checked`: outline and knob in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a dotted-underlined trigger holding `.ds-tooltip__tip`, a small inverted box shown on hover, focus or `is-open`; `--end` aligns it to the right edge.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person drawn as a backlit head and shoulders: dark shapes in `--color-bar` on a lit copy of themselves, a light in the corner, a fill behind. `--2` is lit from the left with `--color-accent-alt`, `--3` from above with `--color-bar-text`; `--large` doubles it. `.ds-person` sets one beside `.ds-person__name` and `.ds-person__role`.
- `.ds-progress`: `.ds-progress__head` (a label and a `.ds-progress__value` in mono) over `.ds-progress__bar`, a `<progress>` drawn as a hairline of `--border-width-strong` with the value in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-accordion`: ruled `<details>`: `.ds-accordion__item` with a `.ds-accordion__head` label that ends in a plus (a cross when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-band`: the inverted block. Only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost` inside.
- `.ds-stack`: stacks blocks one step apart; `--tight` closer. `.ds-main` is the body of an inner page. `.ds-facts` is a `dl` of label and value pairs inside a panel.
- `.ds-scene`: the picture. A positioned box on `--fill-bar` in `--color-bar-text` whose first children are empty layers: `.ds-scene__light` (four beams from lamps above the frame and the glow of the stage), `.ds-scene__haze` (smoke in two fills), `.ds-scene__rig` (the truss under the navigation and two towers of lamps), `.ds-scene__stage` (the edge of the stage and the gear on it in silhouette: stacks, microphones, monitors, drums, a keyboard), `.ds-scene__screen` (a dot matrix over everything, like a LED wall), `.ds-scene__shade` (a vignette and the dark foot that carries text) and `.ds-scene__word` (one enormous outlined word). Variants are other pictures: `--back` (a column of light from below, behind the records) and `--lamps` (a framed bank of eighteen lamps on its stand, for a strip; it needs only the light layer). Use all layers for the hero. A scene with no layers but `.ds-scene__word` is type alone on the grain.
- `.ds-nav`: the thin line over the scene. `.ds-nav__inner` holds the brand, `.ds-nav__links` of `.ds-nav__link` (the current one fully bright with an accent mark under it, `is-current`) and `.ds-nav__actions`. In the specimen the showing view marks its link with one `:has()` rule per view; a project sets `is-current` itself.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` in the navigation: `.ds-menu__toggle` (two short lines and the word) and `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__item`, a hairline box that drops in the corner. Closed until clicked.
- `.ds-hero`: the full scene of the home view: `.ds-hero__side` (a vertical label on the left edge), `.ds-hero__kicker` (a badge and two mono labels), `.ds-hero__title` of two lines, the second with `.ds-hero__outline`, and `.ds-hero__foot` with `.ds-hero__lead`, `.ds-hero__actions` and `.ds-hero__next`. One per site.
- `.ds-ticker`: a strip of names on `--fill-bar-alt`, each `.ds-ticker__item` followed by a small accent square; it is cut off at the right edge.
- `.ds-section`: a block of the page; `--tight`, `--scene` (padding for a scene used as a section). `.ds-section__head` holds a `.ds-section__kicker`, the `.ds-section__title` and an arrow link at the right.
- `.ds-list`: the huge ruled list of dates. Each `.ds-list__item` is a row of `.ds-list__date` (day in the heading face, month as a `small` label), `.ds-list__title` (the city), `.ds-list__meta`, a status badge and an arrow link; a hovered row is washed with the accent from the left.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of four figures between two hairlines; each `.ds-stat__item` is a `.ds-stat__figure` over a `.ds-stat__label`, parted by vertical hairlines.
- `.ds-carousel`: one record to a slide: `.ds-carousel__track` of `.ds-carousel__slide` (a `.ds-sleeve` beside `.ds-carousel__copy` with an outlined `.ds-carousel__number`, `.ds-carousel__title`, `.ds-carousel__text` and a button), then `.ds-carousel__nav` with a count, `.ds-carousel__dots` of `.ds-carousel__dot` and two `.ds-carousel__arrow`.
- `.ds-sleeve`: a square record cover drawn from the scene colours (grooves around an accent disc); `--2` a sun over lines, `--3` diagonal bars, `--4` a turning cone. `.ds-sleeve__label` sets two mono labels across its top.
- `.ds-grid`: four tall cells joined by hairlines, one on each fill and each lit from a different corner; a `.ds-grid__cell` is a link with a number label at the top and `.ds-grid__title` and `.ds-grid__text` at the bottom.
- `.ds-strip`: the low block that opens an inner page; it holds a `.ds-wrap` with the breadcrumb and the page header, over one small picture or over the grain and an outlined word.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page, inside `.ds-strip`: `.ds-page-header__main` with the `.ds-page-header__title` and one line of `.ds-page-header__text`; `.ds-page-header__actions` at the right with `--onscene` and `--ghost` buttons.
- `.ds-cols`: narrow and wide column; `--wide`, `--even`.
- `.ds-sidebar`: `.ds-sidebar__title` label and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of ruled `.ds-sidebar__item` rows in the heading face with a `.ds-sidebar__count`; the current row is bright with an accent rule under it.
- `.ds-tabs`: labels on a hairline; the current `.ds-tabs__tab` (`is-current`) is underlined with the accent.
- `.ds-table`: a ruled table: a label head under a strong rule, hairlines between rows, no vertical lines; `is-hover` tints a row. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures in mono; `.ds-table__title` is the row's link in the heading face. Wrap it in `.ds-table__wrap`; `.ds-table__foot` holds a count and the pagination.
- `.ds-panel`: a hairline box: `.ds-panel__head` with a label `.ds-panel__title` and an optional badge, and `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__text` is a paragraph.
- `.ds-tracks`: a numbered running order: `.ds-tracks__item` rows of `.ds-tracks__number`, `.ds-tracks__title` and `.ds-tracks__time`. `.ds-people` lays four `.ds-person` two by two.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing strip on `--fill-bar-alt`: `.ds-footer__cols` with the brand and a `.ds-footer__about` line, three columns of `.ds-footer__title` and `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link`; `.ds-footer__word`, the site's name as one outlined word in display size; `.ds-footer__legal`.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: every box is square.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 0px`: nothing casts a soft shadow; the only box shadow is the hard inset line under the current navigation link.
- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat, also on pictures.
- `border-width <= 2px`: rules are hairlines; 2px marks the current item.
- `font-size >= 11px`: labels are the smallest text.
- `font-size <= 330px`: the outlined word of a scene is the largest, at one and a half times the display size.
- `font-families <= 3`: a condensed display face, one sans-serif for reading, one monospace for labels.
- `font-weight <= 700`: weight comes from the condensed face, not from bold.
- `line-height <= 1.6`: body text is set at 1.6.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. First decide which ground it stands on (page, panel or fill; scene; inverted block; footer strip) and take the text token and the buttons of that ground; never put a primary button on a scene or `--color-bar-text` on a fill. A new picture is built the way the existing ones are: the bar colour for darkness and every solid shape, the accent for the light, the alternate accent for a second light, the fills for the tones between, shapes from percentages, gradients with hard stops and `clip-path`, and the grain from `--fill-bar`; do not load an image and do not write a colour. If the installing project has photographs or film, they replace the drawn layers of a scene and everything else (the shade under the text, the labels, the type) stays. Keep type on the `--text-*` tokens, the largest sizes stepped down with the same factors as the specimen, labels tracked by `--space-track`, spacing on the `--space-*` scale, and note any one-off `color-mix()` shade next to its rule.
