# Cinematic documentary player

## Summary

This is the site of a documentary released in chapters, as film and album microsites were built between 2015 and 2026: the whole site is a player, with the navigation as a bar fixed to the foot of the window that also shows what is playing and how far it has run. The home view opens on one scene with the title high in its sky and then hangs the chapters from a single line down the middle of the page, a frame on one side and the text on the other, turn and turn about. The page is cold white and the pictures are ink-dark, set in a heavy typewriter face with a book serif for reading, and one green, the northern lights, is the only colour.

## Layout

- The page is fluid and designed at 1440px. Content sits in `.ds-wrap`, centred, at most `--size-page` (1240px) wide with `--space-6` side padding (`--space-4` on a phone). Sections pad themselves with `--space-9` (120px), `--tight` with `--space-8`; `--alt` is a section on `--color-surface-alt` between two hairlines. Section heads, figures, the voices and the footer are centred on the middle line of the page.
- Navigation is `.ds-nav`, the player: a bar `--size-nav` (68px) tall on `--fill-bar-alt`, fixed to the foot of the window, with the brand at the left, four label links in the middle (the current one stands on an accent mark) and at the right a round play button, the chapter and time now playing, and the word Chapters (a `.ds-menu` that opens upwards). A `.ds-progress__bar` runs along its upper edge as the scrubber. Nothing stands at the top of the page.
- The home view stacks: `.ds-hero`, a scene `--size-scene` (800px) tall from the top edge of the window, with a line of labels across its head, the two-line title under it, the six chapters as a rail of ticks at the right edge and lead, two buttons and a badge in the dark foot; a section with the `.ds-list`, the spine; the `.ds-stat` row; a tight section with the `.ds-carousel` of voices; an `--alt` section with the `.ds-grid` of four ways to watch; the `.ds-band`; the footer, which keeps `--size-nav` clear at its foot for the player.
- Inner pages have no hero and no picture at their head. Each opens inside `.ds-wrap` with `.ds-top` (under a strong rule, the breadcrumb at the left and the page's number such as `02 / 04` at the right) and the `.ds-page-header`: the title, one line of text, at most two buttons at the right. Then `.ds-main` with `.ds-cols`: a wide column and at the right a narrow one `--size-side` (320px) with the `.ds-sidebar`; `--wide` is 7 to 5, `--even` two halves. A chapter page puts its `.ds-player` across the whole column first. Tabs sit in a `.ds-toolbar` at the top of the wide column with a switch at its right end, above the notices and the table.
- Views. The specimen is the film's site. `home` is the landing page. `chapters` lists the six: tabs and a subtitles switch, two notices, the table with badges and a tooltip, the pagination, and at the right a sidebar, two progress lines and an empty state. `chapter` is one chapter: the player with its scrubber, notes as prose, links, an accordion, a panel of facts, two people as avatars and what plays next. `watch` asks for a screening: the form with its error and all four buttons, a panel of three switches and the open dialog.
- Below 1100px the columns stack, the figures go two by two and the player drops the title of what is playing; below 800px the player is two rows (brand and controls, then the links, which scroll sideways), the hero loses its rail and stacks its foot, the spine moves to the left edge with each frame above its text, and the grid is one column; a wide table scrolls inside `.ds-table__wrap`. The page never scrolls sideways.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (hairline gaps), `--space-2` 8px (label to text, between tabs), `--space-3` 12px (between buttons, table cells), `--space-4` 16px (panel padding, grid gap), `--space-5` 22px (cell padding, gaps in rows), `--space-6` 32px (page gutter, stack gap, spine to frame), `--space-7` 52px (section head to content, columns, between chapters), `--space-8` 76px (tight sections, the band), `--space-9` 120px (sections). `--space-track` 1px is the tracking of labels.
- Other sizes: `--size-mark` 30px, `--size-control` 46px, `--size-dialog` 500px, `--size-node` 64px (a node of the spine and the play ring).
- On a phone (800px and below) the player has two rows: the `.ds-brand` and its controls above, `.ds-nav__links` below on a hairline, one row that scrolls sideways without a scrollbar; `.ds-nav__now` is not shown from 1100px down. The `.ds-menu` stays in the player and opens upwards. At 520px and below the chapters table lets its cells and titles wrap.

## Typography and colour roles

These are the conventions of the era; every layout and every token set keeps them.

- A block stands on one of five grounds, and each ground has its own text token. The page (`--fill-page`), panels (`--fill-panel`), `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` and the four fills carry `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading` and `--color-link`. A scene (a picture) stands on the bar: `--fill-bar` or `--color-bar`, and carries only `--color-bar-text`. The player and the footer stand on `--fill-bar-alt` and carry `--color-bar-alt-text`. The inverted block (`--fill-inverse`, class `.ds-band`) carries only `--color-inverse-text`. Accent elements (`--fill-accent`: badges, a hovered node) carry `--color-accent-text`.
- Pictures are drawn, never loaded. In a scene `--color-bar` is the darkness and every solid shape (a hut, a mast, a stake, a window bar) is a silhouette in it; `--color-accent` is the light (the northern lights, a lamp, a lit window, the low sun); `--color-accent-alt` is the second, small light (a window far off, a flag); `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are the tones between (the plain, snow on a ledge, drifts); `--color-bar-text` is the hottest point of a light. The grain of a scene comes from `--fill-bar`, which shows wherever the sky or a wall is left open.
- Nothing assumes that a palette is dark, or that page and pictures are alike. A scene is as dark as `--color-bar`, the page as dark as `--color-page`.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are also the grounds of grid cells and carry `--color-text` and `--color-heading`.
- Buttons by ground. On the page, panels and fills: `.ds-button` and `.ds-button--secondary`. On a scene only `.ds-button--onscene` (fill `--color-bar-text`, text `--color-bar`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. On the inverted block only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost`. The destructive button is `--color-danger` with `--color-button-text`.
- `--color-heading-alt` is the colour of kickers, panel and sidebar titles and h3 in prose.
- The label is the second voice: `--font-ui` at `--text-small` or `--text-ui`, `--ui-transform`, tracked by `--space-track` (a layout token, 1px). Times, counts, dates and coordinates are labels in `--font-mono`.
- The default badge is `--fill-accent` with `--color-accent-text`. Status badges are never fills: the status colour is text, outline and a small dot on a transparent ground. `.ds-badge--quiet` is an outline in `--color-border`.
- `--color-notice` with `--color-notice-text` is the information notice, with a left edge of `--border-width-strong` in `--color-accent`; the error notice is `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`.
- Borders: `--border-width` in `--color-border` around panels, cells, tabs and the table, `--color-border-strong` for the spine, its nodes, the rule above the figures and the current tab, `--color-border-muted` between rows inside a box, `--color-input-border` around fields. `--border-width-strong` is the rule above an inner page and above the figures, the mark of the current item (navigation, tabs, sidebar, pagination), the progress line and the notice edge.
- Radii: `--radius-control` for buttons, fields and tabs; `--radius-panel` for panels, the table, the sidebar, notices, the dialog, the menu and tooltips; `--radius-pill` for badges, switches and avatars; `--radius-page` for framed scenes and grid cells. Nodes, the play ring, carousel arrows and dots are circles. Shapes inside a drawing use percentages and `clip-path`, never a radius token.
- Shadows: `--shadow-panel` on panels, the table, the sidebar, framed scenes and grid cells, `--shadow-control` on buttons and the brand tile, `--shadow-control-hover` on hovered buttons and cells, `--shadow-dialog` on the dialog and an open menu. `--backdrop-blur` blurs what passes behind the player and behind the dialog's veil.
- One-off values made with `calc()` and `color-mix()` because the vocabulary has no token: lights are a light token mixed with `transparent` at 16 to 90%; the plain is a fill mixed into `--color-bar` at 40 to 84%; hairlines on a scene are `--color-bar-text` at 22 to 44%; the shade under text on a scene is `--color-bar` at 70 to 74%; the display size steps down as `--text-display` times 0.7, 0.46 and 0.3 at 1100px, 800px and 520px, with `--display-tracking` scaled by the same factors, `--text-h1` times 0.6 and 0.5 and `--text-h2` times 0.7; a quote is `--text-large` times 1.7. Opacity 0.6 to 0.74 dims links that are not current.
- Here: a cold white page with white panels and soft shadows; the pictures, the buttons and the inverted block are ink blue-black. The light `--color-accent` is the green of the northern lights and `--color-accent-alt` a pale ice blue; the fills are four snow tones, so in a scene the plain and the drifts are pale under a dark sky. Links and kickers are a deep green that reads on white.
- Two families. `--font-heading`, `--font-ui` and `--font-mono` are one typewriter face (Courier Prime, Courier New, Courier), bold and in capitals for titles and labels, regular for figures. `--font-body` is a book serif (Charter, Iowan Old Style, Georgia) at `--text-base` 17px and `--line-body` 1.6.
- `--text-display` 132px with `--line-display` 0.9 and `--display-tracking` -8px (the hero title); `--text-h1` 68px (section and page titles, figures, the band); `--text-h2` 36px (chapter titles on the spine, cell titles); `--text-h3` 21px (table titles, short rows, the queue, dialog title); `--text-large` 21px (leads; quotes at 1.7 times).
- Surface: hairlines of 1px and marks of 3px; soft corners of 6px on controls, 10px on panels and 14px on pictures and cells; soft shadows under panels and pictures; the page lightens towards its top and `--fill-bar` is strewn with single specks, which read as falling snow in every picture; a quick eased `--transition`; a wide soft focus ring; no blur. Links in running text are underlined; navigation and titles are not.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets font, text colour and `--fill-page`.
- `.ds-wrap`: the centred content column, `--size-page` wide.
- `.ds-label`: the small tracked line. `--muted`, `--accent` (in `--color-heading-alt`), `--mono` for figures.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, in the player and again in the footer. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small tile dressed like the primary button holding the mark as inline SVG; `.ds-brand__name` is the name, set as a label. Name and mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, p, lists, `code`, `strong`. `.ds-prose__lead` for the first paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: text link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `--quiet` for meta; `--arrow` is a label led by a hairline that grows on hover. `.ds-linkrow` lays links in a row.
- `.ds-button`: a box with a tracked label. Variants: `--secondary`, `--danger` (destructive actions only), `--onscene` (scenes), `--inverse` (the inverted block), `--ghost` (both), `--small`, `--wide`. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttonrow` spaces buttons.
- `.ds-form`: vertical form with labels above the fields. `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side; `.ds-form__field` wraps `.ds-form__label` and one of `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`. Error: `is-error` on the input plus `.ds-form__error`. `.ds-form__hint`, `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`, `.ds-form__actions`.
- `.ds-badge`: small tracked label on `--fill-accent`. `--quiet` outlined. Status variants: `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, each an outline with a dot. `.ds-badgerow` for several.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with a strong left edge; `--error` in the danger colours. `.ds-notice__title` is the tracked lead-in. `.ds-noticerow` stacks notices.
- `.ds-pagination`: `.ds-pagination__link` labels; `is-current` is underlined with `--color-accent`, `is-disabled` is grey. Page numbers are written with two digits.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a label trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` parted by slashes; the last is `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a block of `--color-overlay` centring `.ds-dialog__box`, a panel with a strong outline and `--shadow-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__head` (`.ds-dialog__title`, a drawn cross `.ds-dialog__close`), `.ds-dialog__body` and `.ds-dialog__actions` with the main action first.
- `.ds-empty`: a hairline box with a short accent rule (`.ds-empty__icon`), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small secondary button.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input`, `.ds-switch__track` with its knob, `.ds-switch__label` with an optional `small` line. On is `is-on` or `:checked`: outline and knob in `--color-accent`. In a `.ds-toolbar` it is one line with a label.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a dotted-underlined trigger holding `.ds-tooltip__tip`, a small inverted box shown on hover, focus or `is-open`; `--end` aligns it to the right edge.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person drawn as a backlit head and shoulders from the scene colours. `--2` is lit from the left with `--color-accent-alt`, `--3` from above with `--color-bar-text`; `--large` doubles it. `.ds-person` sets one beside `.ds-person__name` and `.ds-person__role`; `.ds-people` stacks them.
- `.ds-progress`: `.ds-progress__head` (a label and a `.ds-progress__value` in mono) over `.ds-progress__bar`, a `<progress>` drawn as a line of `--border-width-strong` with the value in `--color-accent`. The bar alone, with `.ds-nav__scrub`, is the scrubber on the player's upper edge.
- `.ds-accordion`: ruled `<details>`: `.ds-accordion__item` with a `.ds-accordion__head` label that ends in a plus (a cross when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-scene`: the picture. A positioned box on `--fill-bar` in `--color-bar-text` whose first children are three empty layers, `.ds-scene__sky`, `.ds-scene__far` and `.ds-scene__near`, and where text lies on it a fourth, `.ds-scene__shade` (a dark head and foot). The variant is the picture: `--aurora` (two arcs of northern lights over the plain, a hut with a lit window beside its mast; it holds at any shape and is the hero), `--mast` (a radio mast in ice fog, its lamp with a halo, four stays), `--hut` (the hut's window from outside, with a curtain, a pot, snow, icicles and its light on the drift), `--ice` (the low sun on the sea ice, a line of stakes with two flags). `--frame` makes a scene a picture of 16 to 9 with a hairline, `--radius-page` and `--shadow-panel`; `.ds-scene__play` is a ring with a triangle in its middle.
- `.ds-nav`: the player at the foot of the window. `.ds-nav__scrub` on a `.ds-progress__bar`, then `.ds-nav__inner` with the brand, `.ds-nav__links` of `.ds-nav__link` (`is-current` fully bright on an accent mark) and `.ds-nav__player`: `.ds-nav__play`, `.ds-nav__now` (two labels) and the menu. In the specimen the showing view marks its link with one `:has()` rule per view; a project sets `is-current` itself.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` in the player: `.ds-menu__toggle` (three short lines and the word) and `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__item`, each with a time in a `span`; the sheet opens upwards. Closed until clicked.
- `.ds-hero`: the scene of the home view: `.ds-hero__head` (a line of labels on a hairline), `.ds-hero__title` of two `span` lines, `.ds-hero__rail` (an ordered list of `.ds-hero__tick` links, the current one with a long accent mark) and `.ds-hero__foot` with `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__actions`. One per site.
- `.ds-section`: a block of the page; `--tight`, `--alt`. `.ds-section__head` is centred: a `.ds-section__kicker`, the `.ds-section__title` and an optional `.ds-section__text`.
- `.ds-list`: the spine, an ordered list on one vertical line. Each `.ds-list__item` has a `.ds-list__node` (the number in a circle on the line), a `.ds-list__picture` (a framed scene, a link) and `.ds-list__copy` with `.ds-list__meta` (a status badge and a time), the `.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__text` and an arrow link; even items put the picture at the right. `.ds-list__item--short` is a chapter not yet out: a small node and one line of title and date, no picture. A hovered item (`is-hover`) fills its node with the accent.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of four figures under a strong rule; each `.ds-stat__item` centres a `.ds-stat__figure` over a `.ds-stat__label`, parted by vertical hairlines.
- `.ds-carousel`: voices from the film, one to a slide: `.ds-carousel__track` of `.ds-carousel__slide` (a `.ds-carousel__quote` in the reading face and a `.ds-person`), then `.ds-carousel__nav` with two round `.ds-carousel__arrow` either side of `.ds-carousel__dots` of `.ds-carousel__dot`.
- `.ds-grid`: four low, wide cells two by two, one on each fill, each lit from a different corner; a `.ds-grid__cell` is a link with a `.ds-grid__number`, `.ds-grid__title`, `.ds-grid__text` and `.ds-grid__more`, an arrow at the right.
- `.ds-band`: the inverted block, centred: `.ds-band__inner` with a mono label, `.ds-band__title` and one `--inverse` button.
- `.ds-top`: the first line of an inner page: breadcrumb and page number under a strong rule.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page: `.ds-page-header__main` with the `.ds-page-header__title` and one line of `.ds-page-header__text`; `.ds-page-header__actions` at the right.
- `.ds-main`: the body of an inner page. `.ds-cols`: wide and narrow column; `--wide`, `--even`. `.ds-stack` stacks blocks one step apart; `--tight` closer.
- `.ds-player`: a chapter as one framed scene across the column, with `.ds-player__bar` in its dark foot: a `.ds-progress` as the scrubber and a label.
- `.ds-sidebar`: `.ds-sidebar__title` label and a `.ds-sidebar__list`, a rounded box of `.ds-sidebar__item` labels with a `.ds-sidebar__count`; the current row is tinted and marked at its left.
- `.ds-toolbar`: the line above a table: `.ds-tabs` at the left, one `.ds-switch` at the right.
- `.ds-tabs`: a row of small boxes; the current `.ds-tabs__tab` (`is-current`) is filled and stands on an accent mark.
- `.ds-table`: a sheet in a rounded box: a label head on `--color-surface-alt` under a strong rule, faint lines between rows, no vertical lines; `is-hover` tints a row. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures in mono; `.ds-table__title` is the row's link in the heading face. Wrap it in `.ds-table__wrap`, which draws the box; `.ds-table__foot` holds a count and the pagination.
- `.ds-panel`: a rounded box: `.ds-panel__head` with a label `.ds-panel__title` and an optional badge, and `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__text` is a paragraph. `.ds-facts` is a `dl` of label and value pairs inside it.
- `.ds-queue`: what plays next: `.ds-queue__item` links, each a small framed scene beside `.ds-queue__title` and a mono label.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing strip on `--fill-bar-alt`, centred: `.ds-footer__inner` with the brand, `.ds-footer__about` and a `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link`; `.ds-footer__legal`. It leaves room for the player below it.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 14px`: corners are soft, not round; circles are kept for nodes, the play ring and the carousel's controls.
- `border-width <= 3px`: rules are hairlines; 3px marks the current item and heads a page.
- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat, also on pictures.
- `font-size >= 12px`: labels are the smallest text.
- `font-size <= 132px`: the hero title is the largest.
- `font-families <= 2`: one typewriter face for titles, labels and figures, one serif for reading.
- `line-height <= 1.6`: body text is set at 1.6.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves by itself.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. First decide which ground it stands on (page, panel or fill; scene; inverted block; player or footer strip) and take the text token and the buttons of that ground; never put a primary button on a scene or `--color-bar-text` on a fill. A new picture is a new variant of `.ds-scene` built the way the existing ones are: the bar colour for darkness and every solid shape, the accent for the one light, the alternate accent for a small second light, the fills for the ground between, positions and sizes in percentages, gradients with hard stops and `clip-path`, and the sky left open so the grain of `--fill-bar` shows; do not load an image and do not write a colour. Where text lies on a picture, add `.ds-scene__shade`. If the installing project has film or photographs, they replace the three layers of a scene and everything around them (the shade, the play ring, the scrubber, the spine) stays. Keep type on the `--text-*` tokens, the largest sizes stepped down with the same factors as the specimen, labels tracked by `--space-track`, spacing on the `--space-*` scale, and note any one-off `color-mix()` shade next to its rule.
