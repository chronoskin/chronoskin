Notes on the coat

It began as a blanket thrown over a chair, and it still wants to be one.

The wool is felted twice so that it can be cut and left raw. There is one seam, down the centre of the back, and it is sewn by hand.

How it is worn

Open, with the collar up. Pattern CS 12 07 comes in four lengths and is shortened only at the shoulder.

Care

  • Brush it; do not wash it
  • Hang it wet, away from heat
  • Send it back to us every third winter
