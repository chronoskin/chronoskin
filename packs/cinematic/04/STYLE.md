# Cinematic collection

## Summary

This is the site of a fashion house showing one season between 2015 and 2026: a pale page of bone paper, the season's name in enormous tight grotesque capitals at the left and one tall framed picture of the show's hall at the right, then the looks as a strip of tall frames in the order they walk. Pictures are printed rather than lit, paper and one deep blue like a cyanotype, and everything else is near-black type, hairlines and a few heavy bars. It was the common look of collection, lookbook and show sites of fashion houses in the second half of the 2010s and the first half of the 2020s.

## Layout

- The page is fluid and designed at 1440px. Content sits in `.ds-wrap`, centred, at most `--size-page` (1360px) wide with `--space-6` side padding (`--space-4` on a phone). Sections pad themselves with `--space-8` (88px), `--tight` with `--space-7`; `--ruled` opens with a strong hairline.
- Navigation is `.ds-nav`, one line `--size-nav` (64px) tall on the page colour, not over a picture: the brand at the left, four links in the middle, each led by its number, and at the right the word Index (a `.ds-menu` that drops a sheet in the corner) and a small secondary button. A strong hairline runs under it and a heavy accent mark under the current link.
- The home view stacks: `.ds-hero`, split 7 to 5: at the left `.ds-hero__copy` on the page (kicker, the two-line title, and a foot with the lead, the show's countdown as a `.ds-progress` and two buttons), at the right `.ds-hero__frame`, one tall picture `--size-scene` (720px) high with a hairline frame and four captions; a section with the `.ds-carousel`, the runway, five tall `.ds-look` frames to a screen; the `.ds-band` with the show's date; the `.ds-stat` row under heavy bars; the `.ds-grid` of four cloths; the `.ds-list` of notes.
- Inner pages have no hero and no picture at their head: type alone. Each opens inside `.ds-wrap` with `.ds-top` (the breadcrumb at the left, the page's number such as `02 / 04` at the right) and the `.ds-page-header`: a very large title with one line of text under it and at most two buttons at the right, on a rule of `--border-width-strong`. Then `.ds-main` with `.ds-cols`: a narrow column `--size-side` (240px) with the `.ds-sidebar` and a wide one; `--look` is 5 to 7, `--even` two halves. Tabs sit in a `.ds-toolbar` at the top of the wide column with a switch at its right end.
- Views. The specimen is the site of a house showing its autumn collection. `home` is the season. `looks` has the parts of the show as a sidebar, two notices and an empty state at the left, and tabs with a switch, six looks in a grid of three with badges and a tooltip, and the pagination at the right. `look` shows one look: its frame and the cutter as an avatar at the left; a panel of facts, the table of pieces, notes as prose, links and an accordion at the right. `show` is the request for a seat: the form with its error and all four buttons, a panel with the seats held as a progress line and two switches, and the open dialog.
- Below 1100px the hero and the columns stack, the runway shows three frames and the grid and the figures go two by two; below 800px the navigation becomes two rows (the links scroll sideways), the page header stacks, the runway shows two frames and a part of the third and the looks go two by two; below 520px the grid is one column. The page never scrolls sideways.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px, `--space-2` 8px (label to text), `--space-3` 12px (between buttons, caption to frame), `--space-4` 18px (gaps between frames and cells, panel padding), `--space-5` 26px (rows of the list, column gaps in rows), `--space-6` 36px (page gutter, stack gap, the hero's padding), `--space-7` 56px (section head to content, columns, tight sections), `--space-8` 88px (sections, the indent of the title's second line), `--space-9` 136px. `--space-track` 1.5px is the tracking of labels.
- Other sizes: `--size-mark` 26px, `--size-control` 46px, `--size-dialog` 460px.
- On a phone (800px and below) `.ds-nav__inner` has two rows: the `.ds-brand` and `.ds-nav__actions` above, `.ds-nav__links` below on a hairline, one row that scrolls sideways. At 520px and below `.ds-nav__links` and the button in `.ds-nav__actions` are not shown: the Index `.ds-menu` stays and is the navigation, its list opening as a full row under the bar with every `.ds-menu__item` set at `--text-h2` and the current view marked by a `--color-accent` rule under it.

## Typography and colour roles

- These are the conventions of the era, kept from its first layout. A block stands on one of five grounds, and each ground has its own text token. The page (`--fill-page`), panels (`--fill-panel`), `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` and the four fills carry `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading` and `--color-link`. A scene (a picture) stands on the bar, `--fill-bar` or `--color-bar`, and carries only `--color-bar-text`. The footer stands on `--fill-bar-alt` and carries `--color-bar-alt-text`. The inverted block (`--fill-inverse`, `.ds-band`) carries only `--color-inverse-text`. Accent elements (`--fill-accent`: badges) carry `--color-accent-text`.
- Pictures are drawn, never loaded. In a picture `--color-bar` is the ground and every solid shape is a silhouette in it; `--color-accent` is the light; `--color-accent-alt` is the second light; `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are the tones between; `--color-bar-text` is the hottest point of a light. The texture of a picture comes from `--fill-bar`: `.ds-scene__grain` takes that fill less its own colour (two background layers blended by difference) and lays it over the finished picture, so a surface set changes the texture of every picture and it shows on a dark and on a pale ground alike.
- Nothing assumes that a palette is dark. A picture is as dark as `--color-bar`, the page as dark as `--color-page`; `--color-inverse` is the opposite of the page.
- The fills are also the grounds of grid cells and carry `--color-text` and `--color-heading`, never `--color-bar-text` or `--color-inverse-text` as running text.
- Buttons by ground. On the page, panels and fills: `.ds-button` (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`) and `.ds-button--secondary` (`--fill-button-secondary`, text and outline in `--color-button-secondary-text`). On a scene only `.ds-button--onscene` (fill `--color-bar-text`, text `--color-bar`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. On the inverted block only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost`. The destructive button is `--color-danger` with `--color-button-text`.
- `--color-heading-alt` is the colour of kickers, numbers used as labels, panel and sidebar titles and h3 in prose. The label is the second voice: `--font-ui` at `--text-small` or `--text-ui`, `--ui-transform`, tracked by `--space-track` (a layout token; the type vocabulary has no positive tracking for labels). Dates, times, prices and counts are labels in `--font-mono`.
- The default badge is `--fill-accent` with `--color-accent-text`. Status badges are never fills: the status colour is text, outline and a small dot on a transparent ground. `.ds-badge--quiet` is an outline in `--color-border`.
- `--color-notice` with `--color-notice-text` is the information notice, with a left edge of `--border-width-strong` in `--color-accent`; the error notice is `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`.
- Borders: `--border-width` in `--color-border` between rows and around panels, `--color-border-strong` for the rules that open a table, a list or a page, `--color-border-muted` inside boxes, `--color-input-border` around fields. `--border-width-strong` marks the current item (navigation, tabs, pagination), the progress line and the notice edge. Every border takes `--border-style`.
- Radii: `--radius-control` for buttons, fields and carousel arrows; `--radius-panel` for panels, notices, the dialog, the empty state and tooltips; `--radius-pill` for badges, switches and avatars; `--radius-page` for the largest boxes (pictures, grid cells). Shapes inside a drawing use percentages and `clip-path`, never a radius token.
- Shadows: `--shadow-panel` on panels and grid cells, `--shadow-control` on buttons and the brand tile, `--shadow-control-hover` on hovered buttons and cells, `--shadow-dialog` on the dialog and an open menu. `--backdrop-blur` blurs what lies behind the dialog's veil.
- One-off values made with `calc()` and `color-mix()` because the vocabulary has no token: lights and haze are a light token mixed with `transparent` or with a fill; hairlines on a picture are `currentColor` at 24 to 40%; the rim of light on a figure is a `drop-shadow` of twice `--border-width` in the accent; the largest type steps down as a share of its own token at 1100px, 800px and 520px, so every type set shrinks in proportion to itself.
- Here: the page is bone `#ece9e2`, pictures stand on a slightly darker paper and are printed in one blue, text is near-black, and the one colour is the blue `--color-accent`, with a navy `--color-accent-alt` as its deeper shade. Because the bar is pale here, a picture reads as a print: the shapes are paper, the light is ink. The fills are four tints of paper and blue grey. The primary button is black with bone text and turns blue when hovered; the inverted block is black.
- Three families. `--font-heading` and `--font-ui` are one tight grotesque (Inter Tight, Neue Haas Grotesk, Helvetica Neue) for titles in capitals at weight 500 and for labels; `--font-body` is a text serif (Newsreader, Source Serif, Times New Roman) at `--text-base` 17px and `--line-body` 1.5; `--font-mono` (IBM Plex Mono, Menlo) sets numbers and dates.
- `--text-display` 200px with `--line-display` 0.82 and `--display-tracking` -9px (the hero title only); `--text-h1` 104px (section and page titles, figures, the show's date); `--text-h2` 40px (list and cell titles); `--text-h3` 22px (look names, table names, the index menu, dialog title); `--text-large` 24px (leads).
- Surface: no radius except pills (badges, switches, avatars), hairlines of 1px and heavy bars of 4px (`--border-width-strong`: over the figures, under the page title, under the current link and tab, the progress line), no resting shadows, and a hard offset block in `--color-shadow` behind a hovered button, a hovered cell and the dialog. `--fill-bar` is a halftone of fine dots that lies over every picture; `--fill-bar-alt` darkens the footer's top edge. Links in running text are underlined. No blur.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets font, text colour and `--fill-page`.
- `.ds-wrap`: the centred content column, `--size-page` wide.
- `.ds-label`: the small tracked line. `--muted`, `--accent` (in `--color-heading-alt`), `--mono` for figures.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, in the navigation and again in the footer. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark as inline SVG; `.ds-brand__name` is the name. Name and mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, p, lists, `code`, `strong`. `.ds-prose__lead` for the first paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: text link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `--quiet` for meta; `--arrow` is a label led by a hairline that grows on hover. `.ds-linkrow` lays links in a row.
- `.ds-button`: a label in a box. Variants: `--secondary`, `--danger` (destructive actions only), `--onscene` (pictures), `--inverse` (the inverted block), `--ghost` (both), `--small`, `--wide`. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttonrow` spaces buttons.
- `.ds-form`: vertical form with labels above the fields. `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side; `.ds-form__field` wraps `.ds-form__label` and one of `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`. Error: `is-error` on the input plus `.ds-form__error`. `.ds-form__hint`, `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`, `.ds-form__actions`.
- `.ds-badge`: small tracked label on `--fill-accent`. `--quiet` outlined. Status variants: `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, each an outline with a dot. `.ds-badgerow` for several.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with a strong left edge; `--error` in the danger colours. `.ds-notice__title` is the tracked lead-in. `.ds-noticerow` stacks notices.
- `.ds-pagination`: `.ds-pagination__link` labels; `is-current` is underlined with `--color-accent`, `is-disabled` is grey.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a label trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` parted by slashes; the last is `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a block of `--color-overlay` (blurring by `--backdrop-blur`) centring `.ds-dialog__box`, a panel with a strong outline and `--shadow-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__head` (`.ds-dialog__title`, a drawn cross `.ds-dialog__close`), `.ds-dialog__body` and `.ds-dialog__actions` with the main action first.
- `.ds-empty`: a hairline box with a short accent rule (`.ds-empty__icon`), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small secondary button.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input` (invisible, covers the row), `.ds-switch__track` with its knob, `.ds-switch__label` with an optional `small` line. On is `is-on` or `:checked`: outline and knob in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a dotted-underlined trigger holding `.ds-tooltip__tip`, a small inverted box shown on hover, focus or `is-open`; `--end` aligns it to the right edge.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person drawn as a backlit head and shoulders from the picture colours; `--2` lit from the left with `--color-accent-alt`, `--3` from above; `--large` doubles it. `.ds-person` sets one beside `.ds-person__name` and `.ds-person__role`.
- `.ds-progress`: `.ds-progress__head` (a label and a `.ds-progress__value` in mono) over `.ds-progress__bar`, a `<progress>` drawn as a line of `--border-width-strong` with the value in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-accordion`: ruled `<details>`: `.ds-accordion__item` with a `.ds-accordion__head` label that ends in a plus (a cross when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-band`: the inverted block, `.ds-band__inner` inside. Only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost` on it.
- `.ds-stack`: stacks blocks one step apart; `--tight` closer. `.ds-main` is the body of an inner page. `.ds-facts` is a `dl` of label and value pairs inside a panel.
- `.ds-table`: a ruled table: a label head under a strong rule, hairlines between rows, no vertical lines; `is-hover` tints a row. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures in mono; `.ds-table__title` is the row's name in the heading face. Wrap it in `.ds-table__wrap`; `.ds-table__foot` holds a count and the pagination.
- `.ds-panel`: a hairline box: `.ds-panel__head` with a label `.ds-panel__title` and an optional badge, and `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__text` is a paragraph.
- `.ds-section`: a block of the page; `--tight`. `.ds-section__head` holds a `.ds-section__kicker`, the `.ds-section__title` and an arrow link at the right.
- `.ds-figure`: one standing person in a long coat seen from behind, a silhouette in `--color-bar` with a rim of light; it stands inside a picture and is placed by that picture.
- `.ds-scene`: the picture of the hall, a positioned box on `--fill-bar` in `--color-bar-text` whose first children are empty layers, in this order: `.ds-scene__wall` (the back wall), `.ds-scene__side` and `.ds-scene__side--right` (the two side walls: windows between piers, cut to the lines of the perspective), `.ds-scene__lines` (floorboards and ceiling beams running to the vanishing point), `.ds-scene__floor` (the light of the door on the floor), `.ds-scene__door` (the door of light and its glow), `.ds-scene__cast` (the figure's shadow), `.ds-figure`, `.ds-scene__shade` (a vignette), `.ds-scene__grain`, `.ds-scene__frame` (a hairline inside the edge) and two `.ds-scene__caption` lines of mono labels (`--foot` for the lower one). One per site.
- `.ds-look`: one tall frame (3 to 5) of the lookbook: `.ds-look__set` (the set behind the figure, one box of gradients), `.ds-figure`, `.ds-scene__grain` and `.ds-look__label` (two mono labels across the head); a `.ds-badge` placed inside sits at the lower left. Sets: the default is a door of light; `--2` the light of a slatted window across a wall; `--3` one lamp overhead and its pool; `--4` a flight of stairs under a slit of window; `--5` a lit curtain.
- `.ds-nav`: the line on the page. `.ds-nav__inner` holds the brand, `.ds-nav__links` of numbered `.ds-nav__link` (the current one in the heading colour on a heavy accent mark, `is-current`) and `.ds-nav__actions`. In the specimen the showing view marks its link with one `:has()` rule per view; a project sets `is-current` itself.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` in the navigation: `.ds-menu__toggle` (the word and a plus) and `.ds-menu__list` of numbered `.ds-menu__item` in the heading face, a sheet with a strong outline that drops in the corner. Closed until clicked.
- `.ds-hero`: the split head of the home view: `.ds-hero__copy` with `.ds-hero__kicker` (a badge and two mono labels), `.ds-hero__title` of two lines, the second indented, and `.ds-hero__foot` with `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__side` (the countdown and `.ds-hero__actions`); `.ds-hero__frame` is the `.ds-scene` at the right. One per site.
- `.ds-carousel`: the runway: `.ds-carousel__track` of `.ds-carousel__slide` (a `.ds-look` over `.ds-carousel__title` and a label), five to a screen, then `.ds-carousel__nav` with a count, `.ds-carousel__dots` of `.ds-carousel__dot` drawn as segments of a heavy line, and two `.ds-carousel__arrow`.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of four figures; each `.ds-stat__item` is a `.ds-stat__figure` over a `.ds-stat__label`, under a heavy bar.
- `.ds-grid`: four cloths in one row, the outer two twice as wide, one on each fill and each with its own weave of fine lines; a `.ds-grid__cell` is a link with a mono label at the top and `.ds-grid__title` and `.ds-grid__text` at the bottom.
- `.ds-list`: notes as ruled rows: each `.ds-list__item` is a mono `.ds-list__meta` (date and subject), a `.ds-list__title` with a `.ds-list__text` under it, and an arrow link; a hovered row is tinted.
- `.ds-band`: here it holds the show: a label over `.ds-band__date` at the left, `.ds-band__side` with a label and the button at the right.
- `.ds-top`: the line that opens an inner page: breadcrumb and page number.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page: `.ds-page-header__main` with the `.ds-page-header__title` and one line of `.ds-page-header__text`; `.ds-page-header__actions` at the right with ordinary buttons, since it stands on the page.
- `.ds-cols`: narrow and wide column; `--look`, `--even`. `.ds-toolbar` sets the tabs and a switch on one line. `.ds-looks` is the grid of three `.ds-looks__item` (a look over its name and a badge or label).
- `.ds-sidebar`: `.ds-sidebar__title` label and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of ruled `.ds-sidebar__item` labels with a `.ds-sidebar__count`; the current one has a heavy accent mark at its left.
- `.ds-tabs`: labels on a hairline; the current `.ds-tabs__tab` (`is-current`) stands on a heavy accent mark.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing block on `--fill-bar-alt`: `.ds-footer__cols` with the brand and a `.ds-footer__about` line, three columns of `.ds-footer__title` and `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link`; `.ds-footer__legal`.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: every box is square; only pills and circles are round.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 0px`: a shadow is a hard offset block, never soft.
- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat, also on pictures.
- `border-width <= 4px`: rules are hairlines; 4px is the heavy bar.
- `font-size >= 11px`: labels are the smallest text.
- `font-size <= 200px`: the season's name is the largest text.
- `font-weight <= 700`: titles are set at 500; bold is for emphasis in text only.
- `line-height <= 1.5`: body text is set at 1.5.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. First decide which ground it stands on (page, panel or fill; picture; inverted block; footer) and take the text token and the buttons of that ground; never put a primary button on a picture or `--color-bar-text` on a fill. A new picture is built the way the existing ones are: it needs a subject that reduces to light and silhouette (a room, a doorway, a horizon, a figure against light, a lamp on an object), several layers of depth, one clear source of light and a frame or caption that says it is a picture; the bar colour for the ground and every solid shape, the accent for the light, the alternate accent for a second light, the fills for the tones between, shapes from percentages, hard gradient stops and `clip-path`, and `.ds-scene__grain` last. An inner page gets a real picture or none: type alone is right, a wash of colour is not. Do not load an image and do not write a colour. If the installing project has photographs or film, they replace the drawn layers and the captions, labels and type stay. Keep type on the `--text-*` tokens, the largest sizes stepped down with the same factors as the specimen, labels tracked by `--space-track`, spacing on the `--space-*` scale, and note any one-off `color-mix()` shade next to its rule.
