# Cinematic film site

## Summary

This is the official site of a film between 2015 and 2026: a letterboxed picture between two dark bars, the title in very large high-contrast serif capitals centred on it, a billing block of tiny tracked credits beneath, and the navigation reduced to a dark rail of vertical labels at the left edge and the word Menu in the corner. Below it the page is a warm near-black with bone-white type, stills in film proportion, cast as backlit portraits and screenings as a quiet ruled table. It was the common look of film, series and studio sites in the second half of the 2010s and the first half of the 2020s.

## Layout

- The page is fluid and designed at 1440px. A rail `--size-rail` (72px) wide runs down the left edge for the whole height of the page; views and footer stand to its right. Content sits in `.ds-wrap`, centred, at most `--size-page` (1240px) wide with `--space-6` side padding. Sections pad themselves with `--space-9` (136px), `--tight` with `--space-8`; `--flush` drops the top padding.
- Navigation is `.ds-nav`, the rail on `--fill-bar`: the brand at the top (mark, then the name set vertically), five vertical label links that stay in the window as the page scrolls, the current one marked by a short accent line, and a frame-ratio label at the foot. The `.ds-menu` is the word Menu in the top right corner; opened, it is a veil the height of the window at the right edge with the five views in the heading face.
- The home view stacks: `.ds-hero` (a dark bar of labels, the letterboxed scene `--size-scene` (600px) tall with kicker, title, lead and two buttons centred low in the frame, the trailer's `.ds-progress` and the billing block); a `.ds-statement` beside the `.ds-stat` figures; the `.ds-grid` of four stills; the `.ds-cast` row of four portraits; the `.ds-list` of production notes; the `.ds-band`.
- Inner pages have no hero. Each opens with `.ds-top` inside `.ds-wrap`: a line with the breadcrumb at the left and the page's index (`02 / 05`) at the right, the `.ds-page-header` (large serif title, one line of text, one button at the right) and a sliver of the film, a `.ds-still--sliver` `--size-sliver` (150px) tall and as wide as the column. Then `.ds-main` with `.ds-cols` (8 to 4, the `.ds-sidebar` at the right), `--even`, or one full-width `.ds-stack`. Tabs sit in a `.ds-toolbar` above the table, with a switch at its right end.
- Views. The specimen is the site of one film. `home` is the film. `cast` has four people as panels with portraits, a panel of facts about the crew and two more portraits. `screenings` has the tabs and a switch, two notices, the table of screenings with badges and a tooltip, and the pagination. `stills` has the carousel of frames, four thumbnails, the open download dialog and an empty state. `press` has the notes as prose with links and an accordion of questions, the kit as a sidebar and the request form with all four buttons.
- Below 1100px the columns stack and the stills go two by two; below 800px the rail becomes a bar across the top (brand, Menu, and the links in a row that scrolls sideways), portraits go two by two and a wide table scrolls inside `.ds-table__wrap`; below 520px grids are one column. The page never scrolls sideways.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px, `--space-2` 8px (label to text, list gaps), `--space-3` 12px (between buttons, thumbnails), `--space-4` 18px (rows, panel padding, grid gap), `--space-5` 24px (column gaps in rows, the camera marks' inset), `--space-6` 36px (page gutter, stack gap), `--space-7` 56px (columns, rail groups), `--space-8` 88px (tight sections, the sun's size), `--space-9` 136px (sections). `--space-track` 2px is the tracking of labels.
- Other sizes: `--size-nav` 64px (the hero's bar and the corner Menu), `--size-mark` 32px, `--size-control` 42px, `--size-dialog` 520px, `--size-menu` 380px (the overlay menu).
- On a phone (800px and below) the rail becomes a bar across the top: the `.ds-brand` and the `.ds-menu` toggle on the first row, `.ds-nav__links` on a second row that scrolls sideways, the current link marked by an accent line under it; `.ds-nav__index` is not shown. At 520px and below `.ds-nav__links` is not shown and the `.ds-menu` veil is the only navigation: its `.ds-menu__item` rows list every view at a smaller size, the current one with the accent line, and a tooltip in a table opens as a full row under its line.

## Typography and colour roles

These are the conventions of the era; every layout and every token set keeps them.

- A block stands on one of five grounds, and each ground has its own text token. The page (`--fill-page`), panels (`--fill-panel`), `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` and the four fills carry `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading` and `--color-link`. A scene (the picture of a screen) stands on the bar: `--fill-bar` or `--color-bar`, and carries only `--color-bar-text`. The footer and other quiet strips stand on `--fill-bar-alt` and carry `--color-bar-alt-text`. The inverted block (`--fill-inverse`, class `.ds-band`) carries only `--color-inverse-text`. Accent elements (`--fill-accent`: badges) carry `--color-accent-text`.
- Pictures are drawn, never loaded. In a scene `--color-bar` is the darkness and every solid shape (a crowd, a ridge, a figure, an object) is a silhouette in it; `--color-accent` is the light (a beam, the sun, a lamp); `--color-accent-alt` is the second light (a flare, a rim, a far lamp); `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are the tones between dark and light (haze, sky, walls, far hills); `--color-bar-text` is the hottest point of a light and the colour of an outlined word. The grain or scan lines of a scene come from `--fill-bar`, so a surface set changes the texture of every picture and a palette changes its light.
- Nothing assumes that a palette is dark. The scene is as dark as `--color-bar`, the page as dark as `--color-page`; `--color-inverse` is the opposite of the page.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are also the grounds of grid cells and carry `--color-text` and `--color-heading`; they never carry `--color-inverse-text` or `--color-bar-text` as running text.
- Buttons by ground. On the page, panels and fills: `.ds-button` (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`) and `.ds-button--secondary` (`--fill-button-secondary`, text and outline in `--color-button-secondary-text`). On a scene only `.ds-button--onscene` (fill `--color-bar-text`, text `--color-bar`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. On the inverted block only `.ds-button--inverse` (fill `--color-inverse-text`, text `--color-inverse`) and `.ds-button--ghost`. The ghost button takes outline and text from the block it stands on. The destructive button is `--color-danger` with `--color-button-text`.
- `--color-heading-alt` is the colour of kickers, numbers used as labels, panel and sidebar titles and h3 in prose. On a scene a kicker inherits `--color-bar-text`.
- The label is the second voice of the era: `--font-ui` at `--text-small` or `--text-ui`, `--ui-transform`, tracked by `--space-track` (a layout token, 2px; the type vocabulary has no positive tracking for labels). Dates, timecodes, coordinates, prices and counts are labels in `--font-mono`.
- The default badge is `--fill-accent` with `--color-accent-text`. Status badges are never fills: the status colour (`--color-success`, `--color-warning`, `--color-danger`) is text, outline and a small dot on a transparent ground, so they hold on any palette. `.ds-badge--quiet` is an outline in `--color-border`.
- `--color-notice` with `--color-notice-text` is the information notice, with a left edge of `--border-width-strong` in `--color-accent`; the error notice is `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`.
- Borders are hairlines: `--border-width` in `--color-border` between rows and around panels, `--color-border-strong` above a table or list and around the dialog, `--color-border-muted` inside boxes, `--color-input-border` around fields. `--border-width-strong` is the mark of the current item (navigation, tabs, pagination), the progress line and the notice edge.
- Radii: `--radius-control` for buttons, fields, tabs and carousel arrows; `--radius-panel` for panels, notices, the dialog, the empty state, textareas and tooltips; `--radius-pill` for badges, switches and small avatars; `--radius-page` for the largest boxes (stills, plates, sleeves, grid cells). Shapes inside a drawing use percentages and `clip-path`, never a radius token, so a surface set cannot bend a silhouette.
- Shadows: `--shadow-panel` on panels and grid cells, `--shadow-control` on buttons and the brand tile, `--shadow-control-hover` on hovered buttons and cells, `--shadow-dialog` on the dialog and an open menu. `--backdrop-blur` blurs what lies behind the dialog's veil and behind an open overlay menu.
- One-off values made with `calc()` and `color-mix()` because the vocabulary has no token: lights and haze are a light token mixed with `transparent` at 20 to 90%; hairlines on a scene are `--color-bar-text` at 20 to 30%; outlined words are a text stroke of `--border-width` in the block's text colour at 26 to 70%; the display size steps down as `--text-display` times 0.7, 0.46 and 0.3 at 1100px, 800px and 520px, and `--text-h1` times 0.6 and 0.5, so every type set shrinks in proportion to itself. Opacity 0.6 to 0.86 dims secondary text on scenes.
- Here: the page is a warm near-black `#12100d`, scenes are darker still, text is bone white, and the one colour is the sand gold `--color-accent`, with an ember orange `--color-accent-alt` for flares and far lamps. The fills are four browns from umber to dry earth. The primary button is bone with dark text; the inverted block is bone.
- Three families. `--font-heading` is a high-contrast serif (Playfair Display, Bodoni Moda, Didot, Bodoni 72; the references used commercial display faces) for titles and figures, in capitals at weight 400. `--font-body` and `--font-ui` are one geometric sans (Avenir Next, Jost, Century Gothic): text at `--text-base` 16px and `--line-body` 1.7, labels at 11px and weight 500. `--font-mono` (Courier Prime, Courier New) sets dates, timecodes and counts.
- `--text-display` 168px with `--line-display` 0.9 and `--display-tracking` -3px (the film's title); `--text-h1` 84px (page titles, figures, the band's sentence); `--text-h2` 44px (section titles, the statement, the overlay menu); `--text-h3` 22px (titles of notes, panels, table rows, the dialog); `--text-large` 22px (leads).
- Surface: pill buttons, fields and tabs (`--radius-control` 999px), panels almost square (2px), stills with 4px corners, hairlines only (both border widths are 1px). No resting shadows; the dialog and the open menu cast a wide soft one, a hovered button a small one. `--fill-page` is a slow vignette, `--fill-bar` carries a fine film grain that lies over every still, `--fill-bar-alt` and `--fill-panel` are translucent and `--backdrop-blur` is 14px, so the dialog's veil and the overlay menu blur what is behind them. Links are not underlined until hovered. `--transition` is slow, 0.5s.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets font, text colour and `--fill-page`.
- `.ds-wrap`: the centred content column, `--size-page` wide.
- `.ds-label`: the small tracked line. `--muted`, `--accent` (in `--color-heading-alt`), `--mono` for figures.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, in the navigation and again in the footer. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark as inline SVG; `.ds-brand__name` is the name, set as a label. Name and mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, p, lists, `code`, `strong`. `.ds-prose__lead` for the first paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: text link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `--quiet` for meta; `--arrow` is a label led by a hairline that grows on hover, used for "all dates" and "read". `.ds-linkrow` lays links in a row.
- `.ds-button`: a flat box with a tracked label. Variants: `--secondary`, `--danger` (destructive actions only), `--onscene` (scenes), `--inverse` (the inverted block), `--ghost` (both), `--small`, `--wide`. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttonrow` spaces buttons.
- `.ds-form`: vertical form with labels above the fields. `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side; `.ds-form__field` wraps `.ds-form__label` and one of `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`. Error: `is-error` on the input plus `.ds-form__error`. `.ds-form__hint`, `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`, `.ds-form__actions`.
- `.ds-badge`: small tracked label on `--fill-accent`. `--quiet` outlined. Status variants: `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, each an outline with a dot. `.ds-badgerow` for several.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with a strong left edge; `--error` in the danger colours. `.ds-notice__title` is the tracked lead-in. `.ds-noticerow` stacks notices.
- `.ds-pagination`: `.ds-pagination__link` labels; `is-current` is underlined with `--color-accent`, `is-disabled` is grey. Page numbers are written with two digits.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a label trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` parted by slashes; the last is `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a block of `--color-overlay` (blurring by `--backdrop-blur`) centring `.ds-dialog__box`, a panel with a strong outline and `--shadow-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__head` (`.ds-dialog__title`, a drawn cross `.ds-dialog__close`), `.ds-dialog__body` and `.ds-dialog__actions` with the main action first.
- `.ds-empty`: a hairline box with a short accent rule (`.ds-empty__icon`), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small secondary button.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input` (invisible, covers the row), `.ds-switch__track` with its knob, `.ds-switch__label` with an optional `small` line. On is `is-on` or `:checked`: outline and knob in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a dotted-underlined trigger holding `.ds-tooltip__tip`, a small inverted box shown on hover, focus or `is-open`; `--end` aligns it to the right edge.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person drawn as a backlit head and shoulders: dark shapes in `--color-bar` on a lit copy of themselves, a light in the corner, a fill behind. `--2` is lit from the left with `--color-accent-alt`, `--3` from above with `--color-bar-text`; `--large` doubles it. `.ds-person` sets one beside `.ds-person__name` and `.ds-person__role`.
- `.ds-progress`: `.ds-progress__head` (a label and a `.ds-progress__value` in mono) over `.ds-progress__bar`, a `<progress>` drawn as a hairline of `--border-width-strong` with the value in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-accordion`: ruled `<details>`: `.ds-accordion__item` with a `.ds-accordion__head` label that ends in a plus (a cross when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-band`: the inverted block. Only `.ds-button--inverse` and `.ds-button--ghost` inside.
- `.ds-stack`: stacks blocks one step apart; `--tight` closer. `.ds-main` is the body of an inner page. `.ds-facts` is a `dl` of label and value pairs inside a panel.
- `.ds-scene`: the large picture, a landscape. A positioned box on `--color-bar` in `--color-bar-text` whose first children are empty layers, in this order: `.ds-scene__sky` (darkness running through the fills to the accent at the horizon), `.ds-scene__sun` (a small hot disc with a long horizontal flare and a halo), `.ds-scene__ridge--far` (jagged peaks, a `clip-path`), `.ds-scene__ridge--mid` (two dunes), `.ds-scene__figure` (one cloaked walker), `.ds-scene__ridge--near` (the black foreground), `.ds-scene__grain` (the texture of `--fill-bar` alone, the fill less its own colour, laid over the picture by difference, so its grain shows on a dark and on a pale bar alike) and `.ds-scene__marks` (the corners of the camera gate).
- `.ds-still`: one frame of the film in 2.39 to 1, drawn as a single box of gradients with the grain over it: the default is a low sun over dunes; `--night` a moon, stars and a range; `--door` a slab of light with someone standing in it; `--road` two lamps and a white line; `--sea` a sun and its column on water. `--tall` (1.6 to 1) for grid cells, `--sliver` for the strip under a page header. `.ds-still__label` sets two mono labels along its foot.
- `.ds-nav`: the rail. `.ds-nav__rail` holds the brand, `.ds-nav__links` of vertical `.ds-nav__link` (the current one bright with an accent line, `is-current`) and `.ds-nav__index`. In the specimen the showing view marks its link with one `:has()` rule per view; a project sets `is-current` itself.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` in the top right corner: `.ds-menu__toggle` (the word and two lines) and `.ds-menu__list`, a full-height veil on `--fill-bar-alt` with `.ds-menu__item` rows in the heading face, each with its number. Closed until clicked.
- `.ds-hero`: the letterboxed picture of the home view: `.ds-hero__bar` (labels in the upper bar), `.ds-hero__frame` (the `.ds-scene`, with `.ds-hero__kicker`, `.ds-hero__title`, `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__actions` centred low), `.ds-hero__scrub` (the trailer's progress line) and `.ds-hero__credits` (the billing block; the title inside it is a `b` in the heading face). One per site.
- `.ds-section`: a block of the page; `--tight`, `--flush`. `.ds-section__head` is a ruled line with a `.ds-section__kicker` above the `.ds-section__title` and an arrow link at the right.
- `.ds-statement`: the synopsis in the heading face (`.ds-statement__text`, with `em` in the accent) beside the figures.
- `.ds-stat`: four figures two by two; each `.ds-stat__item` is a label over a `.ds-stat__figure`, under a strong hairline.
- `.ds-grid`: four cells, one on each fill; a `.ds-grid__cell` is a link holding a `.ds-still--tall`, a timecode label, `.ds-grid__title` and `.ds-grid__text`.
- `.ds-cast`: four large square portraits in a row, each a `.ds-cast__item` link with a `.ds-avatar`, a name and a part.
- `.ds-list`: dated notes as ruled rows of four columns: `.ds-list__meta` (the date), `.ds-list__title` in the heading face, `.ds-list__text` and an arrow link.
- `.ds-top`: the opening of an inner page: `.ds-top__line` (breadcrumb and index), the page header and a sliver still.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page: `.ds-page-header__main` with the `.ds-page-header__title` and one line of `.ds-page-header__text`; `.ds-page-header__actions` at the right.
- `.ds-cols`: main column and side column, 8 to 4; `--even`. `.ds-toolbar` puts tabs and a switch on one line.
- `.ds-tabs`: joined pills in one outline; the current `.ds-tabs__tab` (`is-current`) is filled with `--color-surface-strong`.
- `.ds-table`: a ruled table: a head of accent labels between a strong and a plain hairline, faint rules between rows; `is-hover` tints a row. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures in mono; `.ds-table__title` is the row's link in the heading face. Wrap it in `.ds-table__wrap`; `.ds-table__foot` holds a count and the pagination.
- `.ds-panel`: a hairline box with `.ds-panel__head` (a `.ds-panel__title` in the heading face and an optional badge) and `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__text` is a paragraph. `--person` puts a square portrait at the left. `.ds-panels` lays panels two by two.
- `.ds-sidebar`: a box on `--color-surface-alt`: `.ds-sidebar__title` label and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of ruled `.ds-sidebar__item` rows with a `.ds-sidebar__count`; the current row is bright.
- `.ds-carousel`: one still to a slide: `.ds-carousel__track` of `.ds-carousel__slide`, then `.ds-carousel__nav` with the frame count, round `.ds-carousel__dots` of `.ds-carousel__dot` and two `.ds-carousel__arrow`. `.ds-thumbs` is a row of four small stills under it.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing strip on `--fill-bar-alt`, centred: `.ds-footer__inner` with the brand, `.ds-footer__about` (the billing line), a `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link` and `.ds-footer__legal`.

## Never

- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat, also on pictures.
- `border-width <= 1px`: every rule and outline is a hairline.
- `border-radius <= 4px`: boxes are almost square; only pills and circles are rounder.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 120px`: only the dialog, the open menu and a hovered control cast a shadow, wide and soft.
- `font-size >= 11px`: labels are the smallest text.
- `font-size <= 168px`: the film's title is the largest text.
- `font-families <= 3`: a display serif, one sans-serif for reading and labels, one monospace for figures.
- `font-weight <= 600`: the display face is set at regular weight.
- `line-height <= 1.7`: body text is set at 1.7.
- `underlined-links <= 10%`: links are told apart by colour; only a hovered link is underlined.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. First decide which ground it stands on (page, panel or fill; scene; inverted block; footer strip) and take the text token and the buttons of that ground; never put a primary button on a scene or `--color-bar-text` on a fill. A new picture is built the way the existing ones are: the bar colour for darkness and every solid shape, the accent for the light, the alternate accent for a second light, the fills for the tones between, shapes from percentages, gradients with hard stops and `clip-path`, and the grain from `--fill-bar`; do not load an image and do not write a colour. If the installing project has photographs or film, they replace the drawn layers of a scene and everything else (the shade under the text, the labels, the type) stays. Keep type on the `--text-*` tokens, the largest sizes stepped down with the same factors as the specimen, labels tracked by `--space-track`, spacing on the `--space-*` scale, and note any one-off `color-mix()` shade next to its rule.
