Edition

Plate 14 as a print of 60 by 34 cm, twelve copies.

Notes from the road

Nobody has bought fuel here for seven years, and nobody has told the timer.

The road was moved inland after the cliff went. The station stayed, and so did its supply. At 17:50 in November the roof lights come on, the sign follows a minute later, and the forecourt is the brightest place for twenty kilometres.

How it was shot

One camera on one tripod, moved four times a night. The file names follow the pattern F09_n3_p14: film, night, plate.

What is in the film

  • Dusk to full dark in one take of four minutes
  • Rain on the second night, with the forecourt as a mirror
  • The sign going out at 01:00
