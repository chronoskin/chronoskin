Three tarns before lunch

A half day loop over open fell, shared by Ines Halloran.

Save walk
  • 7.4Miles
  • 1,820Feet of climb
  • 4:10Hours
  • 48Saves

The walk

Start early. The first tarn is in shade until ten and the last one is best with the sun behind you.

Getting there

Park at the chapel or take the valley bus to the bridge. The path leaves the lane by a stone stile marked FP 12 and climbs beside the beck. See the list of bus times before you set off.

  • Boots, not trainers: the middle section is wet all year.
  • No shelter above the second tarn.
  • Dogs on leads from March to July.

On the top

The cairn sits a little north of the true summit. In mist, follow the wall, not the path.

Before you go

The valley bus runs hourly until six. The last one back from the far end leaves the bridge at ten past five.

There is no phone signal between the first tarn and the drove road, so save the walk to read it on the hill.

What to carry

Boots, a waterproof and a map. There is no water on the route.

Dogs and livestock

Sheep graze the fell all year. Keep dogs on a lead from March to July.

Getting back

The last bus leaves the far end at ten past five.
