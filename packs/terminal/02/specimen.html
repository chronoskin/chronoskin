about

chronoskin mail 4.1.3 reads and writes plain mail folders and speaks to two kinds of server. It keeps no copy of your password; it asks the system keyring.

The manual is one text file: read it here or with any pager.

Where is my mail kept?

In plain folders under your home directory, one file per message.

Which servers does it speak to?

The two common kinds, both over an encrypted line only.

How do I leave?

Press q. Changes are written to disk first.
