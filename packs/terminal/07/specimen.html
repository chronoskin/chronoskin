Help

The rules of the place and the commands that get you around it.

Ask in #help

Rules and first steps

chronoskin chat is a small server for people who use and build chronoskin. It speaks a plain line protocol, so any client works; this page is one of them.

Three rules

  • Be kind. Disagree with the idea, not with the person.
  • Paste text, not pictures of text. Long pastes go to a file.
  • Ask your question at once; do not ask whether you may ask.

Getting around

Everything is a command typed into the input line at the foot. A command starts with a slash: /join #help opens a channel, /part leaves it.

Windows

Each channel is a window with a number. The window list above the input line shows them, and the one you are reading is cut out of the bar.

Mentions

A line that names you is set on a stronger ground with a mark at its left edge, and its window gets a count.
