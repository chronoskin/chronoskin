How ordering works

The chronoskin shop is run by two people of the project. There is no warehouse: goods are printed in small batches and sent from one room.

From cart to door

  • Fill the cart and give an address. No account is needed.
  • Pay by card or transfer. The receipt comes by mail.
  • The parcel leaves within a day and carries a tracking number.

When something is wrong

Answer the receipt and say what happened. Quote the order number, which looks like CS-2026-03180, and we take it from there.

Returns

Unworn goods come back within 30 days. Seconds and stickers do not.

Batches

A sold out size returns with the next batch, about every six weeks.

asked most

How long does a parcel take?

Two to four working days inside the union, up to two weeks beyond it.

Do you ship everywhere?

To 41 countries so far. The order form lists them.

Can I change an order?

Until it is packed. Answer the receipt.

Where does the money go?

To the build machines and the people who mind them.
