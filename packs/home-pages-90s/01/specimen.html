The Show

What Starlane Seven is, and why I made this page.

Episode Guide

Seven people, one very old ship

If you have never seen it, start here!

Starlane Seven is a serial about the crew of a cargo ship that should have been scrapped years ago. It is shown late on Saturdays, it has no laser fights at all, and it is the best thing on television.

Why this page?

Because in 1997 I could not find a single page about the show, so I made one. Everything on chronoskin is typed by hand in a text editor and saved as index.html. You will find here:

  • a review of every episode I have on tape;
  • pictures, all small files for slow modems;
  • the news, when there is any.

How the links look

A link you have not followed, one you have visited, one under the pointer, one as you click it, a bold link and a small link.
