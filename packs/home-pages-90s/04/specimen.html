What it does

The clock that Windows forgot to give you.

chronoskin Clock sits in a corner of the desktop and shows the time in figures large enough to read from across the room. It uses less than 1% of the processor and stays out of the way of other windows.

What is new in 2.1

  • an alarm can play any sound file, not only the beep;
  • the on-line counter starts by itself when the modem connects;
  • the window remembers where you left it.

Installing it

Run the file you downloaded and choose a folder. To start the clock with a different colour, add a switch to the shortcut, for example cclock.exe /green. To take it off again:

Start, Settings, Control Panel
Add/Remove Programs, chronoskin Clock, Remove
