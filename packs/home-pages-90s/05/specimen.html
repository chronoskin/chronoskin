Club Guide

How the notice board works, and the three rules we keep.

Join the Club

How to post a message

If you can write a postcard, you can use the board.

Choose a board, press Post a New Message and give your message a title that says what it is about. "Bird?" helps nobody; "Small brown bird with a white eye stripe" brings six answers by tea time.

What to put in a sighting

  • what you saw, and how many;
  • the day and the hour;
  • your town or county, never your street.

Making a word bold

The board does not take tags, but a word between two stars is shown in bold: type *waxwing* to shout about one.

How the links look

A link you have not followed, one you have visited, one under the pointer, one as you click it, a bold link and a small link.

The three rules

  1. Be kind. Everybody once called a dunnock a sparrow.
  2. Never say where a rare bird is nesting.
  3. Nothing for sale, except at the club table in May.
