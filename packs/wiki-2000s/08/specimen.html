A wiki in three steps

Five minutes of reading; then you know everything that matters.

Questions people ask [show]

Can I change the address? Yes, once. Can others edit? Only members you invite.

Create a wiki

  • The basics
  • For teachers
  • For teams
  • Questions

1Name it

Choose a short name. It becomes the address of your wiki, so riverside-choir is found at riverside-choir.chronoskin. You can change the title later; the address stays.

2Write the first page

Every wiki starts with one page called Home. Press "edit" and write what the wiki is for. To start another page, put its name in double square brackets and save:

You typeSee the [[Rehearsal dates]] and the [[Song list]].
You getSee the Rehearsal dates and the Song list.

A red link is a page that does not exist yet. Click it and you are writing it.

Formatting you will use

  • the B and I buttons for bold and italic;
  • a line that starts with * for a list;
  • a line that starts with ! for a heading.

3Invite the others

Send people the address. Whoever has a member account can change pages; everybody else can read them, unless you close the wiki.

How links look

  • Rehearsal dates (a page of your wiki)
  • Home (a page you have been to)
  • Members (under the pointer)
  • Recent changes (while pressed)
  • Song list (not written yet)
  • The town hall's booking page (another site)

Good first pages

  1. Who we are
  2. Dates
  3. Who does what
  4. Things we decided
  5. Useful addresses

What happens next

  1. We send you a link.
  2. You follow it and choose a password.
  3. Your wiki opens on its Home page, ready to edit.

A good address

Short, lowercase, words joined by a hyphen. It cannot be changed, so leave out the year unless it matters.
