[edit]English

Etymology

From an old word for twilight, built on the same root as glow. It dropped out of general use and came back through northern poetry in the 1700s.

Pronunciation

  • Respelled: GLOH-ming
  • Rhymes with: roaming, homing, foaming

Noun

gloaming (plural gloamings)

  1. The half-light after sunset and before full dark; twilight. They walked home in the gloaming, the lamps not yet lit.
  2. (figurative) The closing part of a period; a time of fading. In the gloaming of the old firm, few clerks remained.
  3. (rare, regional) The dim light before sunrise. needs a quotation
