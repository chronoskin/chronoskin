Understand[edit]

Port Varen grew up around its fish quay and still lands a catch every morning. The old town is a tangle of lanes behind the harbour wall; everything a visitor wants is within fifteen minutes on foot of the clock tower. Timetables use the 24-hour clock, written like 15:40.

Get in[edit]

By train

Trains from the Old Capital take about two hours and arrive at Varen Quay, right on the harbour. Buy a return; it costs little more than a single.

  • Six trains a day, four on Sundays.
  • Bicycles travel free outside the morning rush.

See[edit]

  • Varen Light, end of the north mole. The white lighthouse; 96 steps to the gallery. Open 10:00 to 17:00, closed Mondays · allow an hour3.00
  • Smokehouse Row, Quay Lane. Nine working fish smokers in tarred sheds, best before noon. Always open · the lane is cobbled and steepfree
