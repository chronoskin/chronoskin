9 and 10 May 2014

Two days of maps.

chronoskin is a small conference for people who draw, print and publish maps: one room, one track, twenty-four talks and a long walk on Sunday.

Register for $95 See the schedule

Early price until 11 April. Students pay half.

One room, one track

Nobody has to choose between two talks. Everyone hears the same thing and argues about it at lunch.

Workshops with ink

Saturday afternoon is hands-on: relief shading by hand, and printing a two-colour map on a proof press.

Lunch is included

Both days, with coffee from eight. Tell us what you do not eat when you register.

Short talks New

Friday closes with ten talks of five minutes each. The list opens at the door, first come first served.

The Sunday walk

Twelve kilometres along the estuary with a paper map and no phone. Free, and optional.

Recorded and shared

Every talk is filmed and published a month later, with the slides, under an open licence.

Getting there

A brick hall from 1887 with tall windows and a view of the water.

The hall is on the north quay, eight minutes on foot from the central station. Leave by the harbour exit and keep the water on your right.

By train or tram

Trams 4 and 9 stop outside. From the airport, the train to the central station takes twenty minutes and runs every quarter of an hour.

By car or bicycle

  • There is no parking on the quay; use the station car park
  • Eighty bicycle stands in the yard behind the hall
  • Step-free entrance from the yard side

Show the code MAPS14 at the two hotels on the quay for the conference rate.
