A rye loaf that forgives a late start

Mixed after supper, baked whenever the oven is free the next day.

Rye has little of the stretch that wheat has, so there is nothing to gain from kneading it. Stir the dough until no dry flour is left, cover the bowl and leave it alone for twelve to sixteen hours. A cool room is better than a warm one.

What you need

  • 400 g wholemeal rye flour and 100 g strong white flour
  • 10 g salt and a teaspoon of caraway seed
  • 150 g of active starterFed within the last twelve hours and risen to its peak. and 380 g of water

Baking it

Heat the oven to 230 degrees with a lidded pot inside. Bake for thirty minutes with the lid on and twenty with it off. If you keep notes, file this one as rye-overnight-3: it is the third version, and the first two were bricks.
