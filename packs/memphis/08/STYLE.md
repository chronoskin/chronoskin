# Memphis habit board (2016 to 2022)

## Summary

A fluid dashboard in the Memphis revival look of 2016 to 2022: flat loud colours, whole fields of pattern, and discs, hoops and zigzags laid across the edges of the blocks. Nothing is outlined and nothing casts a shadow: depth comes from one flat block or shape overlapping another. This pack is a habit tracker, the playful kind of personal dashboard common in those years, and its charts are made of the era's shapes: the day is a hoop cut into arcs, the week is seven columns of pattern, the month is a calendar of discs and the figures sit on a chain of capsules.

## Layout

- The page is fluid: `--size-page` is 100% and every section (`.ds-nav`, `.ds-hero`, `.ds-page-header`, `.ds-band`, `.ds-footer`) is full-bleed. Content sits in a centred `--size-content` (1180px) column with `--space-gutter` (40px) at the sides. Designed at a 1440px viewport.
- The header is one tall flat bar (`--size-nav` 80px): the brand, then the destinations, each led by a small shape (disc, tilted block, triangle, quarter), then a week tag and the account avatar. The current destination lies on a flat tilted block of `--color-bar-alt` that reaches below the bar.
- Order of the home page: the bar, the hero, a tinted band whose chain of stat capsules climbs over the foot of the hero and which holds the week chart beside today's list, a band on the page ground with a panel and a carousel beside the month, which lies half on a slab of pattern, and the footer.
- The hero is today as a poster. The left 7 of 12 columns hold a tilted badge, the count at display size with its second half on an accent block, a lead and two buttons, on the open ground. The right 5 hold `.ds-dial`, a hoop cut into one arc per fill, with four shapes round it; `.ds-hero__ground` is a disc of pattern that the top right corner of the window cuts off, and the dial lies half on it.
- `.ds-stat` is four capsules of four lengths in one chain, each overlapping the one before, two of them tilted and shifted. The chain is pulled up by `--space-8`.
- `.ds-chart` and `.ds-month` are flat sheets with `--radius-page`. `.ds-grid` is the calendar: seven columns of discs.
- Inner pages keep the bar and footer and drop the hero. In its place comes a `.ds-page-header`: a `--color-surface-alt` band with the breadcrumb, the `--text-h1` title and one line of lead on the left, the actions on a flat tilted capsule at the right, a disc of pattern cut off by the right edge, and a line of dots and dashes in two fills along the bottom. Below it one `.ds-band` holds a `.ds-split` (7 to 5; `--even`, `--flip`, or `--side` with a `--size-sidebar` 270px column first). Tabs are the first row of the main column, then notices, then the table.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (chip padding, gaps inside the tab capsule), `--space-2` 8px (gaps between discs, label to control), `--space-3` 12px (row padding, gaps between columns of the chart), `--space-4` 18px (panel padding, gap between buttons), `--space-5` 24px (gap between stacked blocks, overlap of the capsules, sheet padding), `--space-6` 34px (gap between columns, capsule side padding), `--space-7` 50px (tight band padding, the stripe end of a panel title), `--space-8` 76px (band and hero padding, the climb of the stat chain), `--space-9` 108px (foot of the hero). `--space-nudge` 3px is the pressed offset and the focus outline offset.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-control` 46px, `--size-control-large` 56px, `--size-control-small` 34px, `--size-logo` 40px, `--size-avatar` 42px, `--size-dial` 320px, `--size-art` 170px (unit of the chart height and the capsule radius), `--size-shape` 92px (unit of every ornament), `--size-dot` 18px (pitch of every pattern), `--size-stroke` 5px (weight of drawn lines), `--size-dialog` 470px.
- Below 1000px columns stack, the dial shrinks and goes under the words, the capsules stand two by two, free shapes, pattern slabs and the header disc disappear and the destinations take a row of their own under the brand. Below 600px gutters shrink to `--space-4`, the hero title is half the display size, the capsules stack, the two-week strip breaks into two rows and table columns marked `.ds-table__extra` are hidden, so nothing scrolls sideways. Below 600px the `.ds-nav__links` cells are equal and centred, and the menu list in the row opens from the right edge.
- Views: four, switched by plain `#name` links. `home` is today. `habits` is the list: a filter sidebar and an empty state on the left; tabs, notices, the table with a tooltip, the badges and the pagination on the right. `habit` is one habit: the two-week strip and a progress bar, the prose on its sheet, a small dial, the last check-ins and two avatars; it is reached from every list row and from the More menu, and the bar marks Habits. `new` has the form with a switch and the four buttons beside a preview and the accordion, and the confirmation dialog on its overlay band.

## Typography and colour roles

- Type: headings in the heaviest cut of a humanist sans (`--font-heading`: Gill Sans Ultra Bold, then Gill Sans at weight 900, Lilita One), which gives fat, quirky lower-case words; text and controls in the same family at normal weights (`--font-body`, `--font-ui`: Gill Sans, Lato, Calibri). `--text-base` 18px at 1.45, `--text-small` 15px, `--text-ui` 16px at weight 600, `--text-large` 23px for leads. `--text-display` 92px for the hero title; `--text-h1` 52px (page titles, figures), `--text-h2` 34px (sheet titles), `--text-h3` 21px. Nothing is uppercase. Links in running text are underlined, titles and navigation are not.
- Palette: pink in several depths and one green. A pink-white page `--color-page` #fff0f5, white surfaces, pale pink `--color-surface-alt` #ffd9e6 and stronger pink `--color-surface-strong` #ffb8d2. The four fills are candy pink #ff8fba, the green #2fd39a, orchid #f7a6e4 and deep pink #f0609b. The green is the accent and the primary button; `--color-accent-alt` is the deep pink. Ink, the secondary button and the inverse are a dark wine #3f0a25; `--color-bar-alt` is a deep green; `--color-shadow` (the fifth ornament colour, never a shadow) is cherry red.
- Surface: no outlines and no shadows. `--border-width` is a 1px dotted hairline used only round form controls and between rows; all shadow tokens are `none`. Controls are pills, panels round two opposite corners, tags are drops (`--radius-pill` leaves one corner square) and the largest containers round both lower corners like a cup (`--radius-page`). `--fill-page` draws faint double pinstripes; `--fill-bar` closes the bar with a row of dots; `--fill-bar-alt` is sprinkles on deep green; `--fill-inverse` is hoops and sprinkles on wine.
- Which text sits on which fill (the era's convention; every Memphis layout and token set keeps to it):
  - `--color-page`, `--color-canvas` and the three surfaces carry `--color-text`, headings in `--color-heading` or `--color-heading-alt`, links in the `--color-link-*` tokens and secondary text in `--color-text-muted`.
  - `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4`, `--fill-accent`, `--color-accent-alt`, `--color-success` and `--color-warning` are the loud fills. They carry `--color-accent-text` and nothing else: links on them use `.ds-link--plain`, and there is no muted text on a loud fill.
  - `--color-danger` is readable as text on any surface; as a fill it carries `--color-surface`. `--color-bar` carries `--color-bar-text`, `--color-bar-alt` carries `--color-bar-alt-text`, `--color-inverse` carries `--color-inverse-text`, `--color-notice` carries `--color-notice-text`, buttons carry their own text tokens.
  - A box is a flat block of one colour with no outline and nothing behind it. A block never sits on a ground of its own colour: panels and sheets (`--fill-panel`) go on the page or on a tinted band, tinted blocks on the page or on a surface.
  - `--fill-page` is the quiet pattern and may carry text. The loud ones (`--fill-bar-alt`, `--fill-inverse`, every `.ds-cover`) never do: text goes on a flat block or chip laid over them. `--color-border` and `--color-border-strong` are the ink of drawn ornaments, not of frames.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets the page fill, the base font and colour; sections are its children.
- `.ds-brand`: the mark and name, first in the bar, linking home. `.ds-brand__mark` is a `--size-logo` tile dressed like the primary button and tilted; `.ds-brand__name` is the name in the heading font. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own.
- `.ds-nav`: the header: `.ds-nav__inner` holds the brand, `.ds-nav__links` of `.ds-nav__link`, each starting with an empty `.ds-nav__icon` (the shape), one `.ds-menu`, and `.ds-nav__actions` with a plain badge and an avatar. The current link is marked through `:has()` rules, one per view, or with `is-current`.
- `.ds-hero`: today. `.ds-hero__ground` is a `.ds-cover .ds-cover--ground` cut as a disc. `.ds-hero__inner` is the two-column grid; `.ds-hero__body` has a tilted badge, `.ds-hero__title` with its second half in `.ds-hero__mark`, `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__actions`; `.ds-hero__art` holds four `.ds-shape` and the `.ds-dial`.
- `.ds-dial`: how much is done, as a hoop: `.ds-dial__ring` is cut into arcs of the fills with the rest in `--color-disabled` (`.ds-dial--25`, none for a half, `--66`, `--75`), and `.ds-dial__hub` is a flat disc with `.ds-dial__value` and `.ds-dial__label`. `.ds-dial--small` for a panel. The ring is drawn with a conic gradient and a mask, not with a border.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page: `.ds-page-header__disc` (a ground cover cut as a disc), `.ds-page-header__inner`, the breadcrumb, then `.ds-page-header__row` with `.ds-page-header__title` and `.ds-page-header__lead` on the left and `.ds-page-header__actions`, a flat tilted capsule holding the buttons, on the right.
- `.ds-band`: any full-width section. `.ds-band--surface`, `--alt`, `--strong` set the fill; `--tight` less padding; `--lift` for a band whose content climbs into the section above; `--overlay` is the dialog backdrop. `.ds-band__slab` is a ground cover over the right 42% of a band, with `--radius-page`; lay a sheet or flat blocks half on it. `.ds-band__inner` is the content column; `.ds-band__label` is a small heading over one block. `.ds-split`, `.ds-stack` (`--wide`) and `.ds-row` arrange blocks.
- `.ds-stat`: the chain of figures; `.ds-stat` is the row. Each `.ds-stat__cell` is a capsule in the next fill with a `.ds-stat__value` and a `.ds-stat__label`.
- `.ds-chart`: a week on a flat sheet: `.ds-chart__head` with `.ds-chart__title`, then `.ds-chart__bars` of seven `.ds-chart__bar`, each a `.ds-chart__value` over a `.ds-cover` column whose height comes from `.ds-chart__bar--1` to `--6`; every second column has a round head and `is-current` tilts one. `.ds-chart__ground` is the strip of `--fill-bar-alt` they stand on and `.ds-chart__days` the row of `.ds-chart__day` chips under it.
- `.ds-grid`: the calendar: seven columns of `.ds-grid__cell` discs under a row of `.ds-grid__head`. `is-done` fills a disc with the next fill, `is-missed` draws a hoop, `is-current` is a tilted inverted block; other days are pale. `.ds-grid--strip` is fourteen days in one row. It lies on `.ds-month`, a flat sheet that also takes a `.ds-chart__head` and a line of text.
- `.ds-list`: rows on one flat `--color-surface` block: `.ds-list__item` with a `.ds-list__check` (a drawn hoop; with `is-done` on the row a filled disc with a tick), `.ds-list__main` (`.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__text`) and a muted `.ds-list__meta`; hairlines between rows.
- `.ds-panel`: a flat `--fill-panel` block with a `.ds-panel__title` strip on `--color-surface-strong` that ends in a block of accent stripes, and a `.ds-panel__body`. `.ds-panel--pick` tilts it and gives the strip the accent fill. `.ds-sheet` is a larger flat block with `--radius-page` for prose.
- `.ds-sidebar`: a flat `--color-surface-strong` block of filters: `.ds-sidebar__title`, `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` rows divided by hairlines (`is-current` is bold), standing on a foot of pattern, one `.ds-cover` with two small shapes.
- `.ds-footer`: a field of `--fill-inverse` pattern with one shape; `.ds-footer__inner` is one flat inverted capsule with `.ds-footer__brand`, `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link` and `.ds-footer__legal`.
- `.ds-shape`: an ornament: an empty `aria-hidden` span, absolutely placed in a section, behind the content or across an edge. Filled kinds: `--disc`, `--half`, `--quarter`, `--tri`, `--block`. Line-only kinds: `--ring`, `--hoop`, `--line` (holds an SVG squiggle or zigzag), `--draw` (holds an SVG triangle, cross or arc). Pattern kinds: `--dots`, `--grid`, `--bars`, `--stripes`. Colour `--c1` to `--c4`, `--c5`, `--ink`, `--paper`; size `--s`, `--l`, `--xl`, `--xxl`; rotation `--tilt`, `--turn`, `--flip`, `--side`; place `--tl`, `--tr`, `--bl`, `--br`, `--edge-l`, `--edge-r`. A shape is either filled or a line, never a filled shape with an outline. Free shapes are hidden on narrow screens.
- `.ds-cover`: a field of pattern: a fill with a pattern in a clashing colour. Pairs `.ds-cover--1` to `--6`; patterns `--dots`, `--stripes`, `--grid`, `--zig`, `--dash`, `--rings`, `--terrazzo`, `--confetti`. `--ground` makes it a layer behind a section's content. `.ds-cover__chip` is a label on a solid surface.
- `.ds-squiggle`: a short wavy rule, an inline SVG in `--color-accent`, for a section title that needs one.
- `.ds-prose`: long text: h1, h2, h3, paragraphs, lists, strong, inline code on a flat chip, links. `.ds-prose__lead` is the first, larger paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: a link in content. `.ds-link--quiet` for titles and lists; `.ds-link--plain` inherits its colour and is the only link allowed on a loud fill. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-links` is a wrapping row.
- `.ds-button`: one flat block of `--fill-button` with `--radius-control` corners. `.ds-button--secondary`, `.ds-button--danger` (once per view), `--large`, `--small`. `is-hover` swaps the fill and tilts the block, `is-active` drops it, `is-disabled`, `is-focus`. `.ds-buttons` is a row.
- `.ds-badge`: a small flat tag; `--alt`, `--plain`, the status variants `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning`, `.ds-badge--danger`, and `--tilt`. `.ds-badges` is a row.
- `.ds-form`: stacked `.ds-form__field` blocks: `.ds-form__label`, `.ds-form__control` (`--area` for a textarea), `.ds-form__hint` or `.ds-form__error`; `.ds-form__row` puts two side by side; `is-focus`, `is-invalid`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-form__check` wraps a `.ds-form__checkbox`.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input`, `.ds-switch__track`, `.ds-switch__label`, `.ds-switch__hint`; `--end` puts the track at the right; `is-on`.
- `.ds-table`: one flat block; the head row is `--color-bar`; rows alternate and are divided by hairlines; `is-current` marks a row. `.ds-table__name` wraps the first cell's text and leads it with a small shape in the next fill; `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures, `.ds-table__extra` is dropped on a phone, `.ds-table__meta` is a muted second line.
- `.ds-tabs`: one flat `--color-surface-strong` capsule that holds the choices (`.ds-tabs__tab`); the current one (`is-current`) is an accent capsule inside it, tilted.
- `.ds-notice`: a flat `--color-notice` block with a half disc standing on its left edge; `.ds-notice__title`, `.ds-notice__text`. `.ds-notice--error` is `--color-danger-surface` with a `--color-danger` half disc.
- `.ds-pagination`: flat discs and capsules (`.ds-pagination__link`); `is-current` is a tilted accent block, `is-disabled`, `is-hover`.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a small trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` joined by short accent dashes; the last has `is-current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a flat `--fill-panel` block on the overlay band: `.ds-dialog__bar` with `.ds-dialog__title` and `.ds-dialog__close`, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__actions`.
- `.ds-empty`: a flat `--color-surface-alt` block, centred: `.ds-empty__art` (three small shapes), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small button.
- `.ds-menu`: a drop-down, a `<details>` with a `.ds-menu__toggle` summary and a `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__link`, each with an optional `.ds-menu__note`; a flat `--color-inverse` block, tilted. `.ds-menu--end` opens it leftwards.
- `.ds-tooltip`: wraps its trigger; `.ds-tooltip__tip` is a small flat inverted chip shown on hover and focus (`is-open` shows it statically). Do not leave it open over other content.
- `.ds-accordion`: `.ds-accordion__item` (`<details>`) with a `.ds-accordion__head` ending in a `.ds-accordion__sign` (a disc that becomes a diamond when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-progress`: a flat track with a striped `.ds-progress__bar`; `--alt` is dotted; width by `--0`, `--25`, `--40`, `--60`, `--75`, `--90`. `.ds-progress__legend` is the line above it.
- `.ds-avatar`: a face (inline SVG) or initials in `.ds-avatar__pic` on a fill (`--1` to `--4`), with `.ds-avatar__text` holding `.ds-avatar__name` and `.ds-avatar__meta`; `--large`.
- `.ds-carousel`: `.ds-carousel__track` is a scroll-snap row of `.ds-carousel__slide` (a `.ds-carousel__quote` and an avatar); `.ds-carousel__foot` holds `.ds-carousel__dots` (`.ds-carousel__dot`, `is-current`) and two `.ds-carousel__arrow`.

## Never

- `box-shadow = none`: no box has anything behind it, neither a soft shadow nor a hard offset block. Depth comes from one shape or block overlapping another.
- `border-width <= 1px`: the only borders are hairlines round form controls and between rows. No card, button, tag, capsule or bar is outlined; a ring is drawn with a gradient, never with a border.
- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat.
- `font-size <= 92px`: the hero title is the largest text.
- `font-size >= 15px`: labels, badges and meta text are the smallest.
- `font-weight >= 400`: no light weights; headings are the heaviest cut.
- `font-families <= 3`: one humanist sans in two cuts and the monospace of inline code.
- `line-height <= 1.45`: body text is set at 1.45.
- `uppercase-text <= 1%`: nothing is set in capitals; the heavy lower case does the shouting.
- `underlined-links <= 15%`: only links in running text are underlined.
- `gradient-fills <= 12%`: gradients only draw patterns, hoops and the dial; no box fades from one colour to another.
- `row-gap <= 8px`: rows of a table or list touch and are divided by hairlines.
- `content-width >= 75%`: bands are full-bleed and their column is 1180px of a 1440px viewport.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves on its own; only hover tilts a control.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. A box is one flat fill with a radius token: no border, no `box-shadow`, no outline. If it would vanish on its ground, change its fill or the ground's, do not frame it. Follow the convention above for its text: `--color-accent-text` on the loud fills, `--color-text` on the surfaces. A new chart is built from the shapes: counts as discs, shares as arcs of a hoop, amounts as columns of pattern on a patterned strip, a row of figures as overlapping capsules; draw each from the fill tokens in turn so that every palette repaints it, and put its numbers on flat ground. Give depth by overlap: pull a chain or a sheet over the edge of the band above, lay a sheet half on a slab of pattern, let a `.ds-shape` cross from one block onto the next. Tilt small things (the current tab, a capsule, a featured panel, today's block) by 1 to 8 degrees; never tilt running text or a form. For a size the vocabulary lacks use `calc()` over an existing token; `--size-stroke` is the weight of every drawn line.
