How counting works

A habit counts once a day, inside the hours you gave it. Reading counts from six in the evening until midnight.

Streaks

A streak is the number of days in a row with a check-in. One missed day breaks the row but not the month: the calendar keeps the hoop and carries on.

  • A check-in after midnight belongs to the new day
  • A paused habit keeps its streak for two weeks
  • Two misses in a row turn the label red

How small is small enough?

If you could do it with a cold, it is small enough. Ten minutes beats an hour you skip.

What happens when I miss a day?

The streak starts again and the month keeps a hoop for that day.

Can a habit end?

Yes. Give it a number of days and it moves to Finished when it gets there.
