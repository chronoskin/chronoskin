Too loud, or not loud enough?

It is a poster for a three-day festival in an old tram depot, and I have printed it twice. I cannot tell any more whether it shouts or just talks fast.

What I tried

Two colours, pink over black, on a sheet that is slightly too small. The file is depot-v9.pdf, which tells you how the week went.

  • The date is the biggest thing on the sheet
  • The stripes run the wrong way on purpose
  • There is no logo, only the name

What I want to hear

The truth. Would you stop walking for it? If not, tell me where your eye gave up.
