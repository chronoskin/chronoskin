# Memphis handbook (2016 to 2022)

## Summary

A fluid documentation site in the Memphis revival look of 2016 to 2022: flat loud colours that clash on purpose, whole fields of pattern, and squiggles, rings and quarter circles laid across the edges of the blocks. Nothing is outlined and nothing casts a shadow: depth comes from one flat block or shape overlapping another. This pack is the handbook of a kit of shapes, the kind of playful style guide and documentation site that agencies and design tools published in those years: chapters hang from the header as index tabs, the front page sets its contents as a poster of staggered stripes, and every inner page reads on one flat sheet between a pattern-edged index and a short list of what is on the page.

## Layout

- The page is fluid: `--size-page` is 100% and every section (`.ds-nav`, `.ds-hero`, `.ds-page-header`, `.ds-band`, `.ds-footer`) is full-bleed. Content sits in a centred `--size-content` (1240px) column with `--space-gutter` (36px) at the sides. Designed at a 1440px viewport.
- The header has two parts. `.ds-nav__bar` is one flat bar (`--size-nav` 64px) with the brand, the search field (`--size-search` 320px), the version menu and a small button. Under it `.ds-nav__rack` is a thin strip of the loud `--fill-bar-alt` pattern, and the chapters hang from it as index tabs: flat blocks in the four fills, each of its own length, the current one longer, inverted and tilted. The tabs reach down over the top of the section below.
- Order of the home page: the header, the hero, a tinted band whose row of stat diamonds climbs over the foot of the hero, a band with the list of changed pages beside a panel and a carousel, a band that is one whole field of pattern with a flat tilted card on it, and the footer.
- The hero is the contents as a poster, not a centred message. The left half holds a tilted badge, the title at display size with one word on an accent block, the lead and two buttons, all on the open ground. The right half is `.ds-grid`: four stripes of colour with a huge number each, of different lengths and heights, pushed left and right of each other and overlapping, each ending in a patterned cut-out (disc, quarter, tilted block, arch). `.ds-hero__ground` is a quarter circle of pattern that fills the top right corner of the window; three shapes lie across the seams.
- `.ds-stat` is four diamonds of four sizes that touch at their points; each label is a flat tilted chip over the lower point. The row is pulled up by `--space-9`.
- Inner pages keep the header and footer and drop the hero. In its place comes a `.ds-page-header`: a `--color-surface-alt` band with the breadcrumb, then the chapter number on a tilted accent block that hangs over the lower edge, the `--text-h1` title and one line of lead, and the actions at the right; a row of pennants in two fills closes it. Below it one `.ds-band` holds `.ds-doc`: the index (`.ds-sidebar`, `--size-sidebar` 250px), the page on its `.ds-sheet`, and `.ds-toc` (`--size-aside` 220px). `.ds-doc--wide` drops the third column. Tabs are the first row inside the sheet. Pages without an index use `.ds-split` (7 to 5 columns; `--even`, `--flip`).
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (chip padding, gaps between tabs), `--space-2` 8px (label to control, rows of chips), `--space-3` 12px (row padding), `--space-4` 16px (panel padding, gap between buttons, the pattern edge of the index), `--space-5` 22px (gap between stacked blocks, height of the rack), `--space-6` 30px (pennant pitch, large padding), `--space-7` 44px (sheet padding, gap between columns), `--space-8` 68px (band padding), `--space-9` 96px (hero padding and the climb of the stat row). `--space-nudge` 2px is the pressed offset and the focus outline offset.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-control` 44px, `--size-control-large` 54px, `--size-control-small` 32px, `--size-logo` 34px, `--size-avatar` 40px, `--size-art` 150px (unit of the diamonds and the stripe cut-outs), `--size-shape` 88px (unit of every ornament), `--size-dot` 16px (pitch of every pattern), `--size-stroke` 4px (weight of drawn lines), `--size-dialog` 480px.
- Below 1100px the list of what is on the page moves under the sheet. Below 1000px columns stack (the index goes last), the hero pattern shrinks to a small quarter in the corner, the stripes line up, free shapes and pattern slabs disappear and the search field takes a row of its own. Below 600px gutters shrink to `--space-4`, the index tabs share the width equally and lose their numbers, the hero title is half the display size, the diamonds stand two by two, and table columns marked `.ds-table__extra` are hidden, so nothing scrolls sideways. Below 600px `.ds-nav__actions` spreads across the row, the menu list `.ds-menu--end` opens from the left edge, and a `.ds-tooltip__tip` opens under its line within the gutters.
- Views: five, switched by plain `#name` links. `home` is the front of the handbook. `guide` is a page of prose with the index, code, notices, a tooltip, links and the pagination. `shapes` is the reference: tabs, the table with a shape in each row, the badges and an empty state. `news` is the changelog with its panels. `ask` has the questions accordion, the form with a switch and the four buttons, and the confirmation dialog on its overlay band.

## Typography and colour roles

- Type: headings, navigation and buttons in a geometric sans set in capitals (`--font-heading`, `--font-ui`: Jost, Josefin Sans, then Futura and Century Gothic) with `--heading-tracking` 0.05em; body in a wide, friendly sans (Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible, then Trebuchet MS). `--text-base` 16px at 1.6, `--text-small` 13px, `--text-ui` 13px, `--text-large` 20px for leads. `--text-display` 84px at weight 700 for the hero title; `--text-h1` 46px (page titles, numbers on stripes, diamonds and the chapter block), `--text-h2` 30px, `--text-h3` 19px. Links in running text carry a dashed underline; titles and navigation carry none.
- Palette: a night ground, deep aubergine `--color-page` #25102f, with three lighter aubergines for surfaces. The four fills are orange #ff8a1f, sky #79c7ff, coral #ff5470 and butter #ffd97a; `--color-accent` is the coral, the primary button the orange, `--color-bar` the sky, `--color-bar-alt` the butter. Cream #f8ecdc is the inverse and the secondary button; `--color-shadow` (the fifth ornament colour, never a shadow) is lilac. Text is near white; text on every loud fill is the aubergine `--color-accent-text`.
- Surface: no outlines and no shadows. `--border-width` is a 1px hairline used only round form controls and between rows; all shadow tokens are `none`. Controls are leaves (`--radius-control` rounds two opposite corners), panels have one soft corner (`--radius-panel`), tags are pills, and the largest containers round their top left corner (`--radius-page`). `--fill-page` scatters small faint triangles; `--fill-bar-alt` is a row of coral quarter discs and sky dots on butter; `--fill-inverse` draws small rainbows on cream.
- Which text sits on which fill (the era's convention; every Memphis layout and token set keeps to it):
  - `--color-page`, `--color-canvas` and the three surfaces carry `--color-text`, headings in `--color-heading` or `--color-heading-alt`, links in the `--color-link-*` tokens and secondary text in `--color-text-muted`.
  - `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4`, `--fill-accent`, `--color-accent-alt`, `--color-success` and `--color-warning` are the loud fills. They carry `--color-accent-text` and nothing else: links on them use `.ds-link--plain`, and there is no muted text on a loud fill.
  - `--color-danger` is readable as text on any surface; as a fill it carries `--color-surface`. `--color-bar` carries `--color-bar-text`, `--color-inverse` carries `--color-inverse-text`, `--color-notice` carries `--color-notice-text`, buttons carry their own text tokens.
  - A box is a flat block of one colour with no outline and nothing behind it. A block never sits on a ground of its own colour: panels and the sheet (`--fill-panel`) go on the page or on a tinted band, tinted blocks on the page or on a surface.
  - `--fill-page` is the quiet pattern and may carry text. The loud ones (`--fill-bar-alt`, `--fill-inverse`, every `.ds-cover`) never do: text goes on a flat block or chip laid over them. `--color-border` and `--color-border-strong` are the ink of drawn ornaments, not of frames.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets the page fill, the base font and colour; sections are its children.
- `.ds-brand`: the mark and name, first in the bar, linking home. `.ds-brand__mark` is a `--size-logo` tile dressed like the primary button and tilted; `.ds-brand__name` is the name in the heading font. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own.
- `.ds-nav`: the header. `.ds-nav__bar` and `.ds-nav__inner` hold the brand, `.ds-nav__search` (one small `.ds-form__control`) and `.ds-nav__actions` with the version `.ds-menu` (its toggle is `.ds-nav__version`, a flat surface chip) and a small button. `.ds-nav__rack` is the pattern strip; `.ds-nav__links` hangs the `.ds-nav__link` tabs from it, each with a `.ds-nav__num`. The current tab is marked through `:has()` rules, one per view, or with `is-current`.
- `.ds-hero`: the front page. `.ds-hero__ground` is a `.ds-cover .ds-cover--ground` cut as a quarter circle. `.ds-hero__inner` is two halves and holds three free `.ds-shape`; `.ds-hero__body` has a badge, `.ds-hero__title` with one word in `.ds-hero__mark`, `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__actions`. The second half is the `.ds-grid`.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page: `.ds-page-header__inner`, the breadcrumb, then `.ds-page-header__row` with `.ds-page-header__num` (the chapter number on a tilted accent block), `.ds-page-header__text` (`.ds-page-header__title`, `.ds-page-header__lead`) and `.ds-page-header__actions`. One or two shapes sit at its right end.
- `.ds-band`: any full-width section. `.ds-band--surface`, `--alt`, `--strong` set the fill; `--tight` less padding; `--lift` for a band whose content climbs into the section above; `--overlay` is the dialog backdrop; `--pattern` is a band that is one field of pattern (`.ds-band__field`, a ground cover) with the flat tilted `.ds-band__card` on it. `.ds-band__slab` is a ground cover down the right 30% of a band. `.ds-band__inner` is the content column; `.ds-band__head` (`--late` when it follows a lifted row) holds `.ds-band__title`, a `.ds-squiggle` and `.ds-band__lead`; `.ds-band__label` is a small heading over one block. `.ds-split`, `.ds-stack` (`--wide`) and `.ds-row` arrange blocks.
- `.ds-doc`: the three columns of an inner page (`--wide` for two). `.ds-doc__main` holds the `.ds-sheet`, a flat `--fill-panel` block with `--radius-page`, and may hold one large shape that shows from behind the sheet's corner. `.ds-toc` lists what is on the page: `.ds-toc__title`, `.ds-toc__list` of `.ds-toc__item` led by small shapes, then free blocks such as a progress bar, a switch or an avatar.
- `.ds-code`: a code sample on a flat `--color-inverse` block in the monospace, with its language on the tilted accent chip `.ds-code__tag`.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of figures; `.ds-stat` is the row. Each `.ds-stat__cell` is a `.ds-stat__value` on a diamond in the next fill and a `.ds-stat__label` chip.
- `.ds-grid`: the contents. Each `.ds-grid__cell` is a stripe in the next fill with a `.ds-grid__num`, a `.ds-grid__title` (its link is `.ds-link--plain`), a `.ds-grid__text` and one `.ds-cover` as the cut-out at its end.
- `.ds-list`: rows on one flat `--color-surface` block: `.ds-list__item` with a `.ds-list__mark` (a disc, a tilted block, a triangle, a quarter, through the fills), `.ds-list__main` (`.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__text`) and a muted `.ds-list__meta`; hairlines between rows.
- `.ds-panel`: a flat `--fill-panel` block whose `.ds-panel__title` is a label on `--color-surface-strong` stuck, tilted, over its top edge; `.ds-panel__body` holds the content. `.ds-panel--pick` gives the label the accent fill; `.ds-panel--flat` is a tinted panel for a surface ground.
- `.ds-release`: one version in the changelog: a flat block with `.ds-release__num` on a tilted fill block hanging off its corner, `.ds-release__title`, `.ds-release__meta` and a `.ds-release__list` of `.ds-release__item`, each a status badge and a line.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the index: a flat `--color-surface-strong` block with an edge of dots down its left side, `.ds-sidebar__title` and `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item`. The current item (`is-current`) is an accent block that reaches out over the edge; its link is `.ds-link--plain`.
- `.ds-footer`: a field of `--fill-inverse` pattern with two shapes; `.ds-footer__inner` holds `.ds-footer__block`, one flat inverted block set to the left with `.ds-footer__brand`, `.ds-footer__text`, columns of `.ds-footer__title` and `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link`, and `.ds-footer__legal`.
- `.ds-shape`: an ornament: an empty `aria-hidden` span, absolutely placed in a section, behind the content or across an edge. Filled kinds: `--disc`, `--half`, `--quarter`, `--tri`, `--block`. Line-only kinds: `--ring`, `--hoop`, `--line` (holds an SVG squiggle or zigzag), `--draw` (holds an SVG triangle, cross or arc). Pattern kinds: `--dots`, `--grid`, `--bars`, `--stripes`. Colour `--c1` to `--c4`, `--c5`, `--ink`, `--paper`; size `--s`, `--l`, `--xl`, `--xxl`; rotation `--tilt`, `--turn`, `--flip`, `--side`; place `--tl`, `--tr`, `--bl`, `--br`, `--edge-l`, `--edge-r`. A shape is either filled or a line, never a filled shape with an outline. Free shapes are hidden on narrow screens.
- `.ds-cover`: a field of pattern: a fill with a pattern in a clashing colour. Pairs `.ds-cover--1` to `--6`; patterns `--dots`, `--stripes`, `--grid`, `--zig`, `--dash`, `--rings`, `--terrazzo`, `--confetti`. `--ground` makes it a layer behind a section's content. `.ds-cover__chip` is a label on a solid surface.
- `.ds-squiggle`: the short wavy rule under a section title, an inline SVG in `--color-accent`.
- `.ds-prose`: long text: h1, h2, h3, paragraphs, lists, strong, inline code on a flat chip, links. `.ds-prose__lead` is the first, larger paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: a link in content. `.ds-link--quiet` for titles and lists; `.ds-link--plain` inherits its colour and is the only link allowed on a loud fill. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-links` is a wrapping row.
- `.ds-button`: one flat block of `--fill-button` with `--radius-control` corners. `.ds-button--secondary`, `.ds-button--danger` (once per view), `--large`, `--small`. `is-hover` swaps the fill and tilts the block, `is-active` drops it, `is-disabled`, `is-focus`. `.ds-buttons` is a row.
- `.ds-badge`: a small flat tag; `--alt`, `--plain`, the status variants `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning`, `.ds-badge--danger`, and `--tilt`. `.ds-badges` is a row.
- `.ds-form`: stacked `.ds-form__field` blocks: `.ds-form__label`, `.ds-form__control` (`--area` for a textarea), `.ds-form__hint` or `.ds-form__error`; `.ds-form__row` puts two side by side; `is-focus`, `is-invalid`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-form__check` wraps a `.ds-form__checkbox`.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input`, `.ds-switch__track`, `.ds-switch__label`, `.ds-switch__hint`; `--end` puts the track at the right; `is-on`.
- `.ds-table`: one flat block; the head row is `--color-bar`; rows alternate and are divided by hairlines; `is-current` marks a row. `.ds-table__art` is a narrow first column holding a small `.ds-shape`, so a row can show the thing it describes; `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures, `.ds-table__extra` is dropped on a phone, `.ds-table__meta` is a muted second line.
- `.ds-tabs`: one flat `--color-surface-strong` bar cut into segments (`.ds-tabs__tab`); the current one (`is-current`) is an accent block, larger than the bar and tilted. First row inside the sheet.
- `.ds-notice`: a flat `--color-notice` block with a diamond set half over its left edge; `.ds-notice__title`, `.ds-notice__text`. `.ds-notice--error` is `--color-danger-surface` with a `--color-danger` diamond.
- `.ds-pagination`: flat counters in the shape of the controls (`.ds-pagination__link`); `is-current` is the accent fill, tilted; `is-disabled`; `is-hover`. The last one may name the next page.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a small trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` with accent triangles between; the last has `is-current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a flat `--fill-panel` block on the overlay band: `.ds-dialog__bar` with `.ds-dialog__title` and `.ds-dialog__close`, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__actions`.
- `.ds-empty`: a flat `--color-surface-alt` block, centred: `.ds-empty__art` (three small shapes), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small button.
- `.ds-menu`: a drop-down, a `<details>` with a `.ds-menu__toggle` summary and a `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__link`, each with an optional `.ds-menu__note`; a flat `--color-inverse` block, tilted. `.ds-menu--end` opens it leftwards.
- `.ds-tooltip`: wraps its trigger; `.ds-tooltip__tip` is a small flat inverted chip shown on hover and focus (`is-open` shows it statically). Do not leave it open over other content.
- `.ds-accordion`: `.ds-accordion__item` (`<details>`) with a `.ds-accordion__head` ending in a `.ds-accordion__sign` (a disc that becomes a diamond when open) and a `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-progress`: a flat track with a striped `.ds-progress__bar`; `--alt` is dotted; width by `--0`, `--25`, `--40`, `--60`, `--75`, `--90`. `.ds-progress__legend` is the line above it.
- `.ds-avatar`: a face (inline SVG) or initials in `.ds-avatar__pic` on a fill (`--1` to `--4`), with `.ds-avatar__text` holding `.ds-avatar__name` and `.ds-avatar__meta`; `--large`.
- `.ds-carousel`: `.ds-carousel__track` is a scroll-snap row of `.ds-carousel__slide` (a `.ds-carousel__quote` and an avatar); `.ds-carousel__foot` holds `.ds-carousel__dots` (`.ds-carousel__dot`, `is-current`) and two `.ds-carousel__arrow`.

## Never

- `box-shadow = none`: no box has anything behind it, neither a soft shadow nor a hard offset block. Depth comes from one shape or block overlapping another.
- `border-width <= 1px`: the only borders are hairlines round form controls and between rows. No card, button, tag, tab or bar is outlined; a thick line is a drawn squiggle or zigzag, never a frame.
- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat.
- `font-size <= 84px`: the hero title is the largest text.
- `font-size >= 13px`: labels, badges and meta text are the smallest.
- `font-weight >= 400`: no light weights.
- `font-families <= 3`: a geometric sans for headings and controls, a plain sans for text and the monospace of code.
- `line-height <= 1.6`: body text is set at 1.6.
- `underlined-links <= 15%`: only links in running text are underlined.
- `gradient-fills <= 8%`: gradients only draw patterns and edges; no box fades from one colour to another.
- `row-gap <= 8px`: rows of a table or list touch and are divided by hairlines.
- `content-width >= 75%`: bands are full-bleed and their column is 1240px of a 1440px viewport.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves on its own; only hover tilts a control.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. A box is one flat fill with a radius token: no border, no `box-shadow`, no outline. If it would vanish on its ground, change its fill or the ground's, do not frame it. Follow the convention above for its text: `--color-accent-text` on the loud fills, `--color-text` on the surfaces. Give depth by overlap: hang a block over the lower edge of its band, stick a label over the top edge of a panel, let a `.ds-shape` show from behind the corner of the sheet, set stripes of different fills against each other and make them unequal. A new page gets its chapter number on the tilted block of the page header and one shape or pattern of its own; keep text on flat blocks, off the loud patterns. Tilt small things (tabs, labels, the current item) by 1 to 6 degrees; never tilt running text or a form. For a size the vocabulary lacks use `calc()` over an existing token; `--size-stroke` is the weight of every drawn line.
