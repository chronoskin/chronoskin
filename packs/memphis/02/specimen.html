Journal

Notes from the studio table, one or two a month.

Why we keep the wobble

Every identity we make starts as a drawing on paper. Somewhere between the paper and the screen, most studios tidy the line. We stopped doing that.

A circle is a decision

Draw a circle by hand and it closes a little late, a little flat on one side. That is the part people remember. In our files the layer is called keep-as-drawn and nobody is allowed to smooth it.

  • Trace once, at the size it was drawn
  • Fix what is unclear, never what is uneven
  • Print it small before anyone approves it

When we do tidy

Signs that are read from a moving tram, and anything smaller than a thumbnail. Everything else waves back.

Rosa Kiviniemidraws the first line, 14 March
