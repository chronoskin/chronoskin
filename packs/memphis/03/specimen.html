Show notes

Ines thinks yellow shouts. Tunde thinks it only shouts next to violet. Forty minutes later nobody has moved.

In this episode

They test the claim on a real poster, swap one colour at a time and read the listener letters. The file they argue over is named poster-final-loud.pdf.

  • Why pink and teal fight and still look friendly
  • What a black outline does to a quiet colour
  • The letter about a yellow kitchen

Mentioned

A book of wrapping paper, a tram seat fabric and one very orange bus.
