What is a risograph?A stencil printer that lays down one colour at a time
Paper and size

Uncoated 170 gram paper, 297 by 420 millimetres. It fits any A3 frame without a mount.

Delivery

Posted flat within two days. Free over 40.

Returns

Send it back within 30 days, flat, and we refund the print and the postage.

About this print

Ines cut the shapes from painted paper on a Monday morning with the radio too loud, and printed them the same afternoon.

How it is printed

Each colour goes through the machine on its own, so the two never land in quite the same place. The number pencilled on the back, such as LM-061, tells you which sheet of the edition is yours.

  • Two soy inks, tomato and cobalt
  • Signed and numbered by hand
  • No two sheets line up the same way

Looking after it

Keep it out of direct sun and do not laminate it. The ink stays a little soft for the first week.
