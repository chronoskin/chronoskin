The echo tunnel

Forty metres of pipe, wide enough to crawl through. Floor 1, from four years.

Book a time

Shout in, and wait

Your voice goes down the pipe, hits the far wall and walks back. It takes about a quarter of a second, which is long enough to be surprised.

What to try

Clap once and count the claps that come back. Then whisper: the tunnel carries a whisper further than a shout. The sign at the entrance, 40 m, is the length your voice travels each way.

  • One clap, and listen for three
  • Two friends, one at each end
  • A drum, if Rosa has left it out

Why it works

Sound is a push that travels through air. A hard wall pushes back. A soft one, such as the curtain halfway down, swallows it.
