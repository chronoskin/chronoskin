# Memphis science centre (2016 to 2022)

## Summary

A fluid visitor site in the Memphis revival look of 2016 to 2022, in its sun-faded register: salmon, turquoise and mustard on a grey-beige ground, whole fields of confetti, scallops and rings, and arches, half discs and zigzags laid across the edges of the blocks. Nothing is outlined and nothing casts a shadow: depth comes from one flat block or shape overlapping another. It was common on the sites of children's museums, science centres, ice-cream brands and schools; this pack is a science centre for children, with a centred brand, a hero block that climbs into a field of pattern under a tilted ticket of opening hours, an arcade of figures, exhibits that zigzag, a day's timetable as ticket stubs and a booking form.

## Layout

- The page is fluid: `--size-page` is 100% and every section (`.ds-nav`, `.ds-hero`, `.ds-page-header`, `.ds-band`, `.ds-footer`) is full-bleed. Content sits in a centred `--size-content` (1120px) column with `--space-gutter` (48px) at the sides. Designed at a 1440px viewport.
- Order of the home page: a strip of `--fill-bar-alt` pattern with today's hours on a flat chip; the navigation bar (`--size-nav` 92px) with the brand stacked in the middle and a list of links on either side; the hero; the arcade of four stat arches standing on the edge of the next band; three exhibits on a surface band; a tinted band with the day's timetable beside a panel and a carousel; a slim band in `--color-fill-4` with the call to action; the footer.
- Sections touch and have no rule between them. Shapes lie over the corners of bands and across their edges.
- The hero starts with `.ds-hero__field`, a full-width field of pattern 1.7 times `--size-art` tall, holding a rising half disc and five loose shapes. `.ds-hero__inner` is pulled up into it by three quarters of `--size-art`: the words stand on `.ds-hero__body`, a flat block with `--radius-page` corners in the first column, and `.ds-ticket`, a tilted arch-topped block of `--color-fill-3` with the opening hours, takes the `--size-ticket` (320px) column, shifted left and up so that it lies over the corner of the body and the edge of the field.
- `.ds-stat` is an arcade: four arches of unequal width (4:3:5:3) and height that touch and stand on the lower edge of `.ds-band--stand`, a band with no padding below.
- `.ds-grid` is three columns that zigzag: odd tiles carry an arch of pattern over a tinted label, even tiles start `--space-6` lower and carry the label over a bowl of pattern; every third picture is taller. `.ds-split` is a main column and a `--size-sidebar` (300px) column, `.ds-split--even` two halves.
- Inner pages keep the strip, the navigation and the footer and drop the hero. In its place comes a `.ds-page-header`: a flat `--color-surface-strong` band with a scalloped lower edge and one large half disc, hoop or quarter at its right end; the breadcrumb, the `--text-h1` title and one line of lead on the left, the actions on the right. Below it one `.ds-band` holds a `.ds-split`: the main column starts with the `.ds-tabs` or the notices, then the grid, text or table; the side column holds the arch-shaped `.ds-sidebar`, panels and the empty state. Inner pages are left-aligned; only band heads on the home page and the footer are centred.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (underline offset, chip padding), `--space-2` 8px (gap between timetable rows, label to control), `--space-3` 12px (row padding, strip padding), `--space-4` 18px (panel padding, gap between buttons, the scallops), `--space-5` 26px (gap between cards and stacked blocks, ticket padding), `--space-6` 36px (hero block padding, drop of the even tiles, pitch of the scallops), `--space-7` 56px (tight band padding, gap between columns, band head to content), `--space-8` 84px (band padding, the space under the curve of an arch), `--space-9` 120px (height of the curve of an arch, the tallest stat). `--space-nudge` 3px is the pressed offset, the focus outline offset and the underline thickness.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-control` 50px, `--size-control-large` 62px, `--size-control-small` 38px, `--size-logo` 48px, `--size-avatar` 48px, `--size-art` 220px (unit of an exhibit picture and of the hero field), `--size-shape` 110px (the unit of every ornament), `--size-dot` 22px (pitch of every pattern), `--size-stroke` 4px (weight of drawn lines and pattern lines), `--size-dialog` 520px.
- Below 1000px the navigation stacks (brand, then the two lists centred), the ticket moves under the hero block, still tilted, the grid and the arcade drop to two columns, splits stack and free shapes in bands disappear. Below 600px gutters shrink to `--space-4`, the grid is one column, the hero title is half the display size, the hero field is lower and its shapes smaller, buttons may wrap and table columns marked `.ds-table__extra` are hidden, so nothing scrolls sideways. On a phone (600px and below) `.ds-nav__links` (both lists) leave the bar and `.ds-nav__fold` (a `.ds-menu` with the `.ds-nav__burger` summary) stands at the right of the brand; it opens a list of every view across the width of the window, the current one on the accent fill, and a `.ds-menu__list` elsewhere is at most 80vw wide.
- Views: four, switched by plain `#name` links. `home` is the poster. `exhibits` is the catalogue: tabs by age, six tiles and the pagination, with the floors and an empty state beside them. `exhibit` is one exhibit: the text and related links, with the status badges, the guide (an avatar and a tooltip) and a facts sidebar beside them. `visit` has the notices, the price table and the questions accordion beside the booking form with a switch and the four buttons, and the confirmation dialog on its overlay band. The navigation marks the current view through `:has()` rules, one per view.

## Typography and colour roles

- Type: headings in a soft, heavy serif (`--font-heading`: Fraunces, Recoleta, Cooper Black, then Rockwell and American Typewriter); body, navigation and buttons in a geometric sans (Outfit, Jost, then Avenir Next and Century Gothic). `--text-base` 18px at 1.58, `--text-small` 15px, `--text-ui` 16px at weight 600, `--text-large` 23px for leads. `--text-display` 88px at weight 700 and line height 0.98 (the hero title is set at 86% of it, so that it fits its block); `--text-h1` 50px (inner page titles, stat figures, the footer name), `--text-h2` 37px (band titles, the ticket), `--text-h3` 23px (tile, panel and sidebar titles, timetable times). Nothing is uppercase. Links in running text carry a dotted underline, titles and navigation none.
- Palette: sun-faded. A grey-beige page `--color-page` #e7dfd2 with paper surfaces #f8f3ea, faded salmon `--color-surface-alt` #f2d8cd and faded turquoise `--color-surface-strong` #cde4de. The four fills are salmon #ee8a74, turquoise #4bbdb1, mustard #e3b23c and cornflower #93a9dc; the text on them and all other text is one slate ink #25383f (`--color-accent-text`, `--color-text`). `--color-accent` and the primary button are the salmon, `--color-accent-alt` the turquoise, the fifth ornament colour `--color-shadow` is sea green #2f8f83. `--color-bar` is the mustard, for the navigation and table heads; the secondary button, the strip and the footer are the ink. Links are deep turquoise; link hover and the second heading colour are brick #a3432f, visited a dusty plum. Danger #a8273a, success #84c48d, warning #f0a35e, notice pale mustard.
- Surface: no outlines and no shadows. `--border-width` and `--border-width-strong` are a 1px dashed hairline, used only round form controls and between rows; all five shadow tokens are `none`. Buttons and inputs are pills with one square corner (`--radius-control`), tags are pills, panels round their lower right corner by 36px (`--radius-panel`) and the largest blocks two opposite corners by 64px (`--radius-page`). `--fill-page` scatters faint flecks over the page; `--fill-bar-alt` is a strip with salmon scallops along its upper edge and turquoise ones along its lower; `--fill-inverse` sets mustard and turquoise half discs and salmon dashes on the ink. `--fill-button-hover` is the turquoise. The focus ring is a double line. No text shadow, no blur; `--transition` moves only `transform` and the fill.
- Which text sits on which fill (the era's convention; every Memphis layout and token set keeps to it):
  - `--color-page`, `--color-canvas`, `--color-surface`, `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` carry `--color-text`, headings in `--color-heading` or `--color-heading-alt`, links in the `--color-link-*` tokens and secondary text in `--color-text-muted`.
  - `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4`, `--color-accent` (`--fill-accent`), `--color-accent-alt`, `--color-success` and `--color-warning` are the loud fills. They carry `--color-accent-text` and nothing else: headings and links on them inherit it (use `.ds-link--plain`), and there is no muted text on a loud fill. The ticket and the stat arches are such fills, so everything written on them inherits.
  - `--color-danger` is readable as text on any surface. Used as a fill (destructive button, danger badge) it carries `--color-surface`. `--color-danger-surface` carries `--color-text`.
  - `--color-bar` carries `--color-bar-text`, `--color-bar-alt` carries `--color-bar-alt-text`, `--color-inverse` carries `--color-inverse-text`, `--color-notice` carries `--color-notice-text`, buttons carry their own text tokens and `--color-disabled` carries `--color-disabled-text`. The bar is a loud colour of its own, so the primary and the secondary button must both read on it.
  - A box is a flat block of one colour. It has no outline and nothing behind it: every `--shadow-*` token is `none` and `components.css` never sets `box-shadow`. Two blocks are told apart by their fills, so a block never sits on a ground of its own colour: panels (`--fill-panel`) go on the page, on `--color-surface-alt` or on a pattern; tinted blocks (tile labels, the sidebar, the empty state) go on the page or on a surface. The secondary button and the menu use the darkest or lightest colour of the palette (`--color-button-secondary`, `--color-inverse`); in a set where that colour is also a fill, keep the secondary button off that fill.
  - The only borders are hairlines: `--border-width` in `--color-input-border` around a form control and in `--color-border-muted` between rows of a table, list or sidebar, drawn in the set's `--border-style`. `--color-border` and `--color-border-strong` are the ink of drawn ornaments (`.ds-shape--ink`, the specks in the terrazzo and confetti patterns), not of frames. `--color-shadow` is the fifth ornament colour (`.ds-shape--c5`, confetti); it is never a shadow.
  - Patterns come in two strengths. `--fill-page` is the quiet one, drawn in a faint tint of the ground, and text may sit on it. The loud ones (`--fill-bar-alt`, `--fill-inverse` and every `.ds-cover`) are drawn in a second, clashing colour and never carry text directly: text goes on a flat block or chip laid over the pattern (`.ds-nav__strip-text`, `.ds-cover__chip`, `.ds-hero__body`, the ticket, the footer block). The only exceptions are the faint tints of `.ds-avatar__pic` and `.ds-progress__bar`, which mix `--color-accent-text` at under 30% into the fill.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Sets the page fill, the base font and colour; sections are its children, there is no wrapper column.
- `.ds-brand`: the mark and name, in the middle of the navigation bar with the name under the mark, linking home. `.ds-brand__mark` is a `--size-logo` tile dressed like the primary button (flat fill, text colour, radius, no outline) and tilted 8 degrees; `.ds-brand__name` is the name in the heading font at `--text-h3`. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own.
- `.ds-nav`: the header. `.ds-nav__strip` is a strip of `--fill-bar-alt` pattern; its words sit in `.ds-nav__strip-text`, a flat `--color-bar-alt` chip, with a `.ds-nav__strip-link`. `.ds-nav__bar` is flat `--fill-bar`, a grid of three: `.ds-nav__links`, the brand, and a second list with `.ds-nav__links--end`, which holds one `.ds-menu` and ends with a small secondary button. A `.ds-nav__link` is a pill; hovered it takes `--color-surface`, and the current one is a `--color-surface` arch.
- `.ds-hero`: the poster. Its first child is `.ds-hero__field`, a `.ds-cover` across the whole width holding six `.ds-shape`, placed by their order (the first is the rising half disc). `.ds-hero__inner` is the grid that climbs into the field: `.ds-hero__body`, the flat block with a tilted badge, `.ds-hero__title` at 86% of the display size with one word in `.ds-hero__mark` (a tilted accent pill), `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__actions`; and the `.ds-ticket`.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page, used instead of the hero: a flat `--color-surface-strong` band whose lower edge is a row of scallops in `--color-page`. `.ds-page-header__inner`, the breadcrumb, then `.ds-page-header__row` with `.ds-page-header__title` and `.ds-page-header__lead` on the left and `.ds-page-header__actions` on the right; one large shape stands at its right end.
- `.ds-band`: any full-width section. Fill by `.ds-band--surface`, `.ds-band--alt`, `.ds-band--strong`, `.ds-band--fill` (`--color-fill-4` with `--color-accent-text`; the only loud band, for a call to action), `.ds-band--overlay` (dialog backdrop); `.ds-band--tight` for less padding; `.ds-band--stand` for a band whose content stands on its lower edge. `.ds-band__inner` is the content column, `.ds-band__head` a centred `.ds-band__title`, a `.ds-squiggle` and a `.ds-band__lead`; `.ds-band__label` is a small heading over one block. `.ds-split`, `.ds-stack` (`.ds-stack--wide`) and `.ds-row` arrange blocks.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of figures as an arcade: four `.ds-stat__cell` arches, each one flat fill, unequal in width and height, touching and standing on one line; a `.ds-stat__value` at `--text-h1` over a short `.ds-stat__label`. `.ds-stat` is the row.
- `.ds-grid`: exhibit tiles. A `.ds-grid__cell` has no frame: a `.ds-cover` with one or two shapes, cut as an arch on odd tiles and as a bowl on even ones, and `.ds-grid__body`, a flat label on `--color-surface-strong` (odd) or `--color-surface-alt` (even) with `.ds-grid__title`, `.ds-grid__text` and an optional badge. On even tiles the label comes first.
- `.ds-list`: the timetable as ticket stubs, `--space-2` apart: each `.ds-list__item` is a flat `--color-surface` block, every second one set in by `--space-5`; `.ds-list__time` stands at its left end on a loud fill with a rounded nose, cycling through the four fills; then `.ds-list__main` (`.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__text`) and a muted `.ds-list__meta`. Put it on a tinted band or the page, not on a surface band.
- `.ds-panel`: a flat `--fill-panel` block with `--radius-panel` corners; its `.ds-panel__title` is led by a disc in the accent fill, then `.ds-panel__body`. `.ds-panel--pick` is `--color-surface-strong` and its title is led by a diamond in `--color-accent-alt`.
- `.ds-sidebar`: a flat `--color-surface-alt` block cut as an arch window: the centred `.ds-sidebar__title` sits under the curve, then a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` rows divided by hairlines; an item may end with a badge.
- `.ds-ticket`: the opening hours in the hero: a `--color-fill-3` block with an arched top, tilted 4 degrees, holding `.ds-ticket__title`, a plain badge, `.ds-ticket__rows` of `.ds-ticket__row` (a day and its hours, divided by hairlines in a tint of the text) and a `.ds-link--plain`.
- `.ds-footer`: a field of `--fill-inverse` pattern with two shapes; on it `.ds-footer__inner`, one flat `--color-inverse` block with `--radius-page` corners, centred: `.ds-footer__sign` with `.ds-footer__brand` and `.ds-footer__text` across the top, three columns of `.ds-footer__title` and `.ds-footer__list` of `.ds-footer__link`, and `.ds-footer__legal` as the last line, over a faint hairline.
- `.ds-shape`: an ornament. An empty `aria-hidden` span, absolutely placed in a section that has `position: relative`, behind the content or across the edge of two blocks. Filled kinds, with no outline: `.ds-shape--disc`, `.ds-shape--half`, `.ds-shape--quarter`, `.ds-shape--tri` (clipped box), `.ds-shape--block`. Line-only kinds: `.ds-shape--ring` (a thick circle) and `.ds-shape--hoop` (a thin one), both drawn with a radial gradient and not with a border; `.ds-shape--line`, which holds an inline SVG squiggle or zigzag, and `.ds-shape--draw`, which holds an inline SVG triangle, cross or arc, all stroked in `currentColor` at one and a half times `--size-stroke`. Pattern kinds: `.ds-shape--dots`, `.ds-shape--grid`, `.ds-shape--bars`, `.ds-shape--stripes`. Colour: `.ds-shape--c1` to `.ds-shape--c4` (the four fills), `.ds-shape--c5` (`--color-shadow`), `.ds-shape--ink` (`--color-border-strong`), `.ds-shape--paper` (`--color-surface`). A shape is either filled or a line, never a filled shape with an outline round it. Size: `.ds-shape--s`, none, `.ds-shape--l`, `.ds-shape--xl`, `.ds-shape--xxl` (0.5, 1, 2, 3 and 4.5 times `--size-shape`). `.ds-shape--tilt`, `.ds-shape--turn`, `.ds-shape--flip` and `.ds-shape--side` rotate it. Place with `.ds-shape--tl`, `--tr`, `--bl`, `--br`, `--edge-l`, `--edge-r`. Free shapes are hidden on narrow screens so they never cover text; keep them out of the way of buttons.
- `.ds-cover`: a field of pattern, for pictures and for whole grounds: a fill with a pattern in a clashing colour. Colour pairs: `.ds-cover--1` to `.ds-cover--4` (each fill with the next one), `.ds-cover--5` (`--color-surface-strong` with the accent), `.ds-cover--6` (`--color-inverse` with `--color-fill-1`). Patterns: `.ds-cover--dots`, `.ds-cover--stripes`, `.ds-cover--grid`, `.ds-cover--zig` (rows of teeth), `.ds-cover--dash` (short strokes in rows), `.ds-cover--rings`, `.ds-cover--terrazzo` and `.ds-cover--confetti`, all at the pitch of `--size-dot`. As a picture it holds one or two `.ds-shape` (in a row, not absolutely) and may carry a `.ds-cover__chip` label on a solid surface. `.ds-cover--ground` makes it an absolutely placed layer behind the content of a section; the layout places it with a class of its own. Use patterns big: a band across the hero, a third of a section, the end of a page header.
- `.ds-squiggle`: the short wavy rule under a section title, an inline SVG in `--color-accent`. It is the style's rule: there are no straight heavy rules.
- `.ds-prose`: long text: h1, h2, h3 (in `--color-heading-alt`), paragraphs, lists with coloured markers, strong, inline code on a flat `--color-surface-alt` chip, and links. `.ds-prose__lead` is the first, larger paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: a link in content, bold, in `--color-link` with the `--link-decoration` underline as thick as `--space-nudge`; on hover it takes `--color-link-hover` and the underline turns wavy. `.ds-link--quiet` for titles, breadcrumbs and lists; `.ds-link--plain` inherits its colour and is the only link allowed on a loud fill. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-links` is a wrapping row of them.
- `.ds-button`: one flat block of `--fill-button` with `--radius-control` corners, no outline and no shadow. `.ds-button--secondary` uses the secondary fill; `.ds-button--danger` is `--color-danger` with `--color-surface` text, once per view; `.ds-button--large` and `.ds-button--small` change height and text size. `is-hover` swaps the fill (`--fill-button-hover`; the secondary button takes the accent) and tilts the block 2 degrees, `is-active` drops it by `--space-nudge`, `is-disabled` is `--color-disabled`, `is-focus` shows `--focus-ring`. On a block of the button's own colour use the other button. `.ds-buttons` is a row.
- `.ds-badge`: a small flat tag in the UI font with `--radius-pill` corners and no outline; `--fill-accent` by default. `.ds-badge--alt` (`--color-accent-alt`), `.ds-badge--plain` (`--color-surface-strong` with `--color-text`, for counts, neutral tags and tags on a product tile), and the status variants `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning` (both with `--color-accent-text`) and `.ds-badge--danger` (`--color-danger` with `--color-surface` text). `.ds-badge--tilt` turns it 4 degrees. `.ds-badges` is a row.
- `.ds-form`: stacked `.ds-form__field` blocks, a bold small `.ds-form__label` over a `.ds-form__control` (input, select; `.ds-form__control--area` for a textarea, which takes `--radius-panel`), then `.ds-form__hint` or `.ds-form__error`. A control is `--fill-input` with the hairline in `--color-input-border`. `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side. `is-focus` adds the focus ring, `is-invalid` turns the control `--color-danger-surface` with a `--color-danger` hairline, `is-disabled` greys it. `.ds-form__check` wraps a `.ds-form__checkbox` (checked: the accent fill with a small square in it) and its text.
- `.ds-switch`: an on/off control over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__input` (invisible, covers the label), a flat `.ds-switch__track` with a flat thumb in `--color-button-secondary`, `.ds-switch__label` and an optional `.ds-switch__hint`. On, the track is `--fill-accent`. `.ds-switch--end` puts the track at the right end of a full-width row. `is-on` shows the on state statically.
- `.ds-table`: one flat `--color-surface` block. The head row is `--color-bar` with `--color-bar-text`; rows alternate `--color-surface` and `--color-surface-alt` and are divided by a hairline in `--color-border-muted`; `is-current` on a row makes it `--color-surface-strong` and bold (the total of the basket). `.ds-table__num` right-aligns figures, `.ds-table__extra` marks a column that is dropped on a phone, `.ds-table__meta` is a muted second line in a cell.
- `.ds-tabs`: a wrapping row of flat `--color-surface-strong` chips (`.ds-tabs__tab`); the current one (`is-current`) is `--fill-accent` and tilted 3 degrees. `is-hover` tints and tilts. Use for two to four views of one block, as the first row of the main column of an inner page.
- `.ds-notice`: a flat `--color-notice` block with a striped end drawn in its own text colour; `.ds-notice__title` and `.ds-notice__text`. `.ds-notice--error` is `--color-danger-surface` with `--color-text` and `--color-danger` stripes.
- `.ds-pagination`: a row of flat `--color-surface-strong` counters (`.ds-pagination__link`, `--radius-pill`); the current page (`is-current`) is `--fill-accent` and tilted, `is-disabled` is grey, `is-hover` takes `--color-accent-alt` and tilts the other way.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a small trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` separated by accent dots; the last has `is-current`. It is the first line of an inner page.
- `.ds-dialog`: a flat `--fill-panel` block on the overlay, with no frame: `.ds-dialog__bar` on `--color-surface-strong` with `.ds-dialog__title` and a `.ds-dialog__close` in the secondary button colours, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__actions` with the buttons at the right.
- `.ds-empty`: a flat `--color-surface-alt` block, centred: `.ds-empty__art` (three small `.ds-shape` in a row), `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one small button.
- `.ds-menu`: a drop-down under a navigation item, a `<details>` with a `.ds-menu__toggle` summary and a `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__link`, each with an optional `.ds-menu__note`. The list is a flat `--color-inverse` block, tilted 1 degree, so it reads on any ground without a frame or a shadow; a hovered link takes the accent fill.
- `.ds-nav__fold`: the phone navigation, a `.ds-menu` that is hidden above 600px. Below it the links leave the bar and its `.ds-nav__burger` summary, a tilted `--color-surface` arch, opens one list of every view (`.ds-nav__fold-link`, the current one on the accent fill) followed by the secondary links.
- `.ds-tooltip`: wraps its trigger; `.ds-tooltip__tip` is a small flat `--color-inverse` chip above it, tilted, shown on hover and focus (`is-open` shows it statically). Do not leave it open over other content.
- `.ds-accordion`: `.ds-accordion__item` (`<details>`) with a `.ds-accordion__head` summary on `--color-surface-alt` ending in a `.ds-accordion__sign`: an accent disc that becomes a diamond in `--color-accent-alt` when open. The open head sits on `--color-surface-strong`; `.ds-accordion__body` holds the answer on the panel fill.
- `.ds-progress`: a flat `--color-disabled` track with a `.ds-progress__bar` in `--fill-accent`, striped; `.ds-progress--alt` is dotted `--color-accent-alt`. Width by `.ds-progress--0`, `--25`, `--40`, `--60`, `--75`, `--90` (50% without). `.ds-progress__legend` is the label and figure above it.
- `.ds-avatar`: a face (inline SVG) or initials in `.ds-avatar__pic`, a tile with `--radius-control` corners on a fill with a faint pattern (`.ds-avatar--1` to `.ds-avatar--4`), optionally with `.ds-avatar__text` holding `.ds-avatar__name` and `.ds-avatar__meta`. `.ds-avatar--large` for a feature or a byline.
- `.ds-carousel`: `.ds-carousel__track` is a scroll-snap row of `.ds-carousel__slide` on one flat `--color-surface-strong` block (a `.ds-carousel__quote` and an avatar in each; a slide may start with a `.ds-carousel__pic`, an arch of pattern); `.ds-carousel__foot` holds `.ds-carousel__dots` (a disc, a diamond and a square in `--color-border`; `is-current` is the accent) and two `.ds-carousel__arrow` buttons in the secondary button colours.

## Never

- `box-shadow = none`: no box has anything behind it, neither a soft shadow nor a hard offset block. A block of colour behind every box is another style; depth here comes from one shape or block overlapping another.
- `border-width <= 1px`: the only borders are hairlines round form controls and between rows. No card, button, tag, tile or bar is outlined; a thick line is a drawn squiggle or zigzag, never a frame.
- `text-shadow = none`: type is flat.
- `font-size <= 76px`: the hero title, at 86% of the display size, is the largest text of this layout.
- `font-size >= 15px`: labels, badges and meta text are the smallest.
- `font-weight >= 400`: no light weights.
- `font-families <= 2`: a soft heavy serif for headings and one geometric sans for the rest (plus the monospace of inline code).
- `line-height <= 1.58`: body text is set at 1.58.
- `uppercase-text <= 1%`: nothing is set in capitals in this type set.
- `underlined-links <= 20%`: only links in running text are underlined.
- `gradient-fills <= 9%`: gradients are used only to draw patterns: dots, stripes, dashes, zigzags, rings and terrazzo. No box fades from one colour to another.
- `row-gap <= 8px`: rows of a table or list touch and are divided by hairlines.
- `block-gap <= 56px`: stacked blocks are 26px apart, columns 56px; sections touch.
- `content-width >= 72%`: bands are full-bleed and their column is 1120px of a 1440px viewport.
- `palette-colours <= 20`: grey-beige, paper, slate ink, four fills, sea green, three pale tints, the status colours and two greys.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves on its own; only hover tilts a control.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. A box is one flat fill with a radius token: no border, no `box-shadow`, no outline. If it would vanish on its ground, change its fill or the ground's, do not frame it. Follow the convention above for its text: `--color-accent-text` on the loud fills, `--color-text` on the surfaces. Give depth by overlap: pull a flat block up into a pattern field with a negative margin, lay a tilted block over its corner, stand arches of different fills and heights side by side on the edge of the next band, let a half disc rise from the lower edge of a band. Decoration is the point of this style: a new section gets a `.ds-cover` pattern as part of its ground or a few `.ds-shape` in its empty corners, mixing filled shapes with line-only ones; draw both from the fill tokens so that every palette repaints them, and keep text on flat blocks, off the loud patterns. Corners are square or fully round (discs, arches, bowls, quarters) and come from the radius tokens, some of which round a single corner; never give boxes a uniform medium radius. Tilt small things (tags, the current tab, one word of the title, the ticket) by 2 to 6 degrees with `transform: rotate()`; never tilt running text or a form. For a size the vocabulary lacks use `calc()` over an existing token; `--size-stroke` is the weight of every drawn line.
