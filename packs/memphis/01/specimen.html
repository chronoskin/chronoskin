Before you ask

Can I give my ticket to a friend?

Yes, until the day before. Change the name on the badge from the link in your receipt.

Is the depot step-free?

Both stages and the long table are. Workshop A has two steps and a ramp.

What if a workshop is full?

Put your name on the list at the desk. Seats that are empty five minutes in are given away.

Do children need a ticket?

Under twelve come free with an adult.

Good to know

Where the depot is, what to bring and how we treat each other.

Getting there

The old tram depot is ten minutes on foot from the central station. Follow the yellow triangles painted on the pavement.

By tram, bike or on foot

Lines 4 and 9 stop at the depot gate. There are racks for four hundred bikes behind the hall, and the map link in your receipt opens at gate-b, the entrance nearest the long table.

  • Doors open at nine, the first talk starts at a quarter past ten
  • Bring a pencil; paper is on every table
  • The cloakroom is free and closes at midnight

House rules

Be kind, share the scissors and ask before you photograph someone's work. The full text is read out once, on Friday morning.
