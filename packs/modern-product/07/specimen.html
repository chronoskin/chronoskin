Top questions

Asked this week.

Why does the music stop after a few minutes?

Usually the router moves the speaker between two bands. Give both bands the same name, or keep the speaker within one room of the router.

Can two people control the same speaker?

Yes. Anyone on the same network with the app can play, pause and queue.

How long is the warranty?

Two years from the day of purchase, three for headphones registered within a month.

Does the speaker work without Wi-Fi?

Portable speakers play over Bluetooth. Room speakers need the network.

Where do I find the serial number?

Under the speaker, and in the app under the name of the room.

Add the speaker in the app

The app finds a new speaker by itself. All you do is confirm that the one it found is yours.

Before you start

Stand within three metres of the speaker with your phone on the home network. The light on top should blink green; if the app asks for a code, it is the eight characters printed as CS-XXXX-XX under the speaker.

  • Open the app and choose Add a product.
  • Wait for the chime, then tap This is mine.
  • Name the room. You can change it later.

If the light stays orange

The speaker is still starting. Give it a minute. If nothing changes, unplug it for ten seconds and begin this step again.

Using a guest network? Speakers and phone must share one network, so switch the phone first.
