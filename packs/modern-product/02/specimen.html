Battery and charging

Eighteen days is the figure for ordinary wear: the display on, sleep tracking each night and an hour of exercise a day.

How we measure

A new watch is charged to full and worn until it switches itself off. With position trackingSatellite positioning, switched on only while a workout runs. on for every workout the figure is nearer eleven days. Low power mode, set with Settings › Battery, stretches it to forty.

Charging

  • Magnetic puck, any 5 W supply.
  • Ten minutes of charge gives about three days.
  • The cell keeps 80% of its capacity after 1,000 full cycles.

After ten minutes on the puckabout 3 of 18 days

Can I swim with it?

Yes, to 100 metres. Rinse it in fresh water after the sea.

Does it work with my phone?

With any phone from the last six years, through the app.

What happens to the battery after years?

It keeps 80% after 1,000 full cycles, and the workshop can change it.
