Guide 3 of 14About 4 minutes left

Add the checkout to your site

The checkout is a page we host. You send the customer there with an amount, and we send them back when they have paid.

Create a payment

Call POST /payments with the amount, the currency and the address to return to. The answer holds a checkout_url.

Before you go live

  • Swap the test key for the live key; they differ only in their first letters.
  • Pay once with a real card and refund it from the dashboard.
  • Tell us where to send the payout, or it waits in your balance.

A payment that is not finished within an hour expires, and the customer can simply start again.
