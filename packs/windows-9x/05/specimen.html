Welcome to the chronoskin Atlas 98 Setup Wizard

This wizard fetches the atlas over your telephone line and installs it on your computer.

It is a good idea to close your other programs before you go on. You can stop at any step and pick up later: parts already fetched are kept.

Download Now 5.2 MB, or order the CD by post.
