okaforHost, 1,402 messages

Saturday 14 March 1998, 19:02Message 1 of 13

No sound since the driver update

The mixer opens, the bars move, and the speakers stay quiet.

I put in the new driver disk on Friday. The card is found at start and nothing is marked with a warning, but no program makes a sound. The line SET BLASTER=A220 I5 D1 is still in the start file.

What I have tried

  • Taking the card out and putting it in the next slot
  • The old driver, from the disk that came in the box
  • Another pair of speakers

What the machine is

A 166 with 32 MB, one sound card and a modem that wants the same interrupt.

reyesHost, 3,077 messages

Saturday 14 March 1998, 19:20Message 2 of 13

That last line is your answer. Move the modem to interrupt 3 and the card will speak again.

See the list of free interrupts, a topic you have read, a link under the pointer, a link as you click it and who the hosts are.
