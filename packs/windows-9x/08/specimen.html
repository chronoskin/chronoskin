One evening, one room

Four players, two microphones and a tide table.

The quartet set up in a boat shed and played the whole disc through twice. What you hear is the second pass, with the door open. The catalogue number is CSK 98014.

Why we chose it

  • It sounds like a room, not like a desk
  • The slow tracks are the best ones
  • It is short enough to play again at once
