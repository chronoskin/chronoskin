Update 7.0.2

A small patch for the raid's second seal and for crews that could not rejoin.

The update is live on every platform. It needs 1.2 GB and does not reset the weekly raid.

Fixed

  • The second seal no longer stays closed when a runner leaves.
  • Crews of three can rejoin after a lost connection.
  • The Ringfall cloak keeps its shader after a fast travel.

For players with their own overlay

The frame counter moved to the launch option -hud.frames 1.
