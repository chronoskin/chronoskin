How we beat the Gate Warden

Three phases, one rule: nobody stands in the embers.

I hold him at the north pillar. When he raises the shield, stop all harm at once; macro it with /stopattack if you must.

Phase one

  • Menders stand on the two marked stones.
  • Rangers kill the cinder hounds as they come.

What drops

The Gatekeeper's Bulwark and, once in a blue moon, Ashfall, the Last Ember. Loot rules are in the charter.
