The hundred doors

The Warden

Might and Lore. A shield, a mace and the patience of a wall.

Play the Warden

She does not step back

The Warden fights where she stands and makes that place safe.

Her skills build resolve each time she blocks, and spend it in slams that stagger everything near. With a relic of the right kind a slam becomes a wall, a banner or a wave of fire.

Three ways to grow

  • Bulwark: more block, and allies behind you share it.
  • Oathkeeper: banners that heal and brand.
  • Breaker: two-handed maces and no shield at all.

For those who plan ahead

Builds can be saved as text and shared. A Warden build starts with WRD and its relics follow in order.

Questions

Will my character be wiped at release

No. Characters, relics and stash carry over. Only the ladders start again.

Can I upgrade my edition later

Yes, for the difference in price.

Does one purchase cover every platform

The save is shared; the game is bought once per platform.

How long will early access last

About a year. The roadmap names the acts in order.
