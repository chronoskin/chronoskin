# Game fantasy sky isles (porcelain, pale sky, thin gold)

## Summary

The site of an anime-styled fantasy role-playing game as such sites have looked since about 2020: a clear top bar on a full-bleed daylight sky with floating islands, the version's title set low and centred above a sea of clouds, and one rounded sheet rising out of the clouds that holds numbered chapters. It is the light, airy end of the era: white and pale sky grounds, thin gold lines and rings, a high-contrast serif for titles over a soft sans, pill buttons and one rose accent, with heroes shown as backlit figures under arches. The page is responsive and every picture is drawn in CSS from the palette.

## Layout

- Full-bleed pictures, one centred sheet. The hero and the mastheads run from edge to edge; their content sits in `.ds-wrap` (at most `--size-page`, 1160px, with `--space-6` gutters). Below them `.ds-sheet` is one column of the same width in `--color-canvas` with `--radius-page` corners and the panel shadow, pulled up by `--space-7` so that it overlaps the clouds; the page ground (`--fill-page`) shows at both sides of it and under it. The sheet is cut into `.ds-chapter` bands, alternately plain and `--alt`, with a small diamond on each dividing rule. Designed at 1440px; under 1380px the rail is hidden, under 1100px two-column blocks become one column, grids two across and the roster three across; under 700px grids become one column. Nothing scrolls sideways at 390px; tables scroll inside themselves.
- Order of the home page: the top bar (`--size-nav`, 72px) lying on the hero; the hero (`--size-hero`, 800px) with a kicker, the title, a lead, the large button beside the round trailer button and a line of platform badges, all centred and set low; then the sheet: chapter 01 the banner carousel beside the news list with its category tabs, chapter 02 the featured hero (figure under an arch, name, traits, the cast of crests), chapter 03 the four regions, chapter 04 figures and two panels, and the closing band. The footer is a centred strip of sky. `.ds-rail`, a pill of diamond dots fixed at the right edge, repeats the navigation.
- Inner pages keep the top bar, the rail and the footer; the hero is left out. Each starts with `.ds-masthead`, a low sky (`--size-masthead`, 300px) that carries the breadcrumb and the `.ds-page-header`, then the sheet with one or two chapters. Working parts sit in `.ds-columns` (a flexible column beside `--size-aside`, 340px) or a `.ds-split` (`--wide` is 3 to 2); `.ds-tabs` go at the top of a chapter.
- Views: `home`; `news` (the version notes, more news, topics, links); `heroes` (tabs by element, the roster of arches, pagination, then one hero: skill crests with their windows, the skill table, traits and badges); `world` (the accordion of regions, the empty fifth region, travel notes); `redeem` (notices, the gift code form with switches and buttons, the dialog that unlinks a device, codes already used).
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (label to bar, rings), `--space-2` 8px (small gaps), `--space-3` 12px (between buttons, tight stacks), `--space-4` 16px (between tiles, inside small boxes), `--space-5` 24px (between blocks, panel padding), `--space-6` 40px (chapter sides, gutters, columns), `--space-7` 64px (chapter top and bottom, the sheet's overlap), `--space-8` 96px (room for the clouds under the hero copy).
- Control heights: `--size-control-small` 32px, `--size-control` 44px, `--size-control-large` 58px. `--size-figure` 400px is the widest arch, `--size-banner` 360px the carousel slide, `--size-vista` 150px a region picture, `--size-ring` 46px a chapter numeral.
- On a phone (1000px and below) `.ds-nav__links` is not shown and `.ds-nav__more`, a `.ds-menu` placed first in the bar, opens a full-width list of every view as centred `.ds-menu__item` rows, the open view marked by an accent bar at its left; its summary is a round button. At 700px and below `.ds-nav__action` is hidden and is reached from the menu, where its row carries `.ds-menu__item--elsewhere` so that it is never marked as the open view. A `.ds-tooltip__tip` opens under its line between the gutters; a table in `.ds-table__wrap` keeps its cells on one line and scrolls inside the wrapper.

## Typography and colour roles

- Three families. `--font-heading` is a high-contrast serif (Playfair Display where installed, else Didot, Bodoni), in mixed case at regular weight, for titles, names and figures. `--font-body` and `--font-ui` are a soft humanist sans (Nunito Sans, Noto Sans, Avenir); labels, navigation, buttons, tabs and badges are small capitals of it, tracked by 1px to 3px. `--font-mono` only appears in inline code.
- Sizes: `--text-base` 16px at `--line-body` 1.7; `--text-small` 13px; `--text-ui` 13px; `--text-large` 19px; `--text-h3` 21px, `--text-h2` 30px, `--text-h1` 44px for chapter titles; `--text-display` 64px for mastheads, hero names, figures and the band. The hero title is 1.3 times the display size, capped by the window width.
- Palette: a pale sky page, a white sheet, three near-white surfaces, pale sky bars with deep blue lettering (`--color-bar`, `--color-bar-alt`), slate text under navy headings, gold for rules, rings and second headings (`--color-border-strong`, `--color-heading-alt`, `--color-accent-alt`), one rose accent, a warm gold primary button with dark lettering, and a twilight blue inverse block. `--color-shadow` is a soft blue, so shadows and distant shapes stay airy.
- Surface: hairline borders, pill buttons and badges, 16px panels and a 28px sheet, a white gap and a gold hairline around every panel (`--shadow-panel`), soft blue drop shadows, a rose ring on hover, a page dotted with white (`--fill-page`), slow 0.25s easing. No glass: `--backdrop-blur` is 0.
- The era's rule for text on fills is kept: on `--color-canvas` and the three surfaces (and `--fill-panel`) text is `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading`, `--color-heading-alt` and the link colours; on `--fill-bar` only `--color-bar-text` (table heads, the dialog title); on `--fill-bar-alt` only `--color-bar-alt-text` (every `.ds-scene` and what lies on it: the top bar, hero copy, mastheads, the carousel title; and the footer); on `--fill-inverse` only `--color-inverse-text`; on `--fill-accent` only `--color-accent-text`; on `--fill-button` `--color-button-text`, on `--fill-button-secondary` `--color-button-secondary-text`; in a form well `--color-input-text`; on `--color-notice` `--color-notice-text` and on `--color-danger-surface` `--color-danger`. No text is written on `--color-page`.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4`, the status colours and `--color-accent-alt` are inks and lights: a hero's colour (the sky in the arch and the crest), region cells as the surface tinted 24% by their fill, status badges on a 16% tint of themselves, progress bars. Never a fill under text.
- The pictures. `.ds-scene`: the sky is `--fill-bar-alt`; the sun, its rings and the falling water are `--color-accent-alt`; a low glow is `--color-accent`; islands, mountains and the traveller are `--color-bar-alt` mixed toward `--color-shadow`, less for what is further away; the clouds are `--color-canvas`, so the sheet seems to rise out of them. `.ds-scene--2` to `--5` wash the sky with `--color-fill-1`, `--color-fill-3`, `--color-fill-4` and `--color-fill-2`. `.ds-figure`: the arch is a fill colour over `--color-surface`, the figure that colour mixed far toward `--color-shadow` and lit at one edge by `--color-accent-alt`. Every palette repaints them.
- One-off values built in `components.css` from tokens: the mixes named above, percentages in `clip-path` polygons, gradients and the arch radius, `50%` radius for avatars, rings and the play disc, 45 degree turns for diamonds, tracking of 1px to 3px built from `--size-hair` and `--space-1`, `min()` of a size token and a share of the window width for the largest titles.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`; paints the page ground. `.ds-wrap` is the content column of the hero, mastheads and footer.
- `.ds-nav`: the clear top bar in three parts: the brand, `.ds-nav__links` of `.ds-nav__link` in the middle with a small diamond between them (the current one underlined in `--color-accent-alt`; `is-current`, or in the specimen one selector per view), and `.ds-nav__end` with a `.ds-nav__action` link and one small primary button. One item is a `.ds-menu`.
- `.ds-nav__more`: the phone navigation, a `.ds-menu` hidden above 1000px. Its summary (`.ds-menu__summary`: `.ds-nav__bars` and a `.ds-nav__label`) opens a sheet of every view, the sub-pages as `.ds-menu__item--sub`.
- `.ds-rail`: the pill of diamond dots fixed at the right edge: `.ds-rail__item` holds a `.ds-rail__dot` link and a `.ds-rail__label` shown on hover; the current dot is filled.
- `.ds-brand`: `.ds-brand__mark`, a 40px round tile dressed as the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, its border, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`), and `.ds-brand__name` in the heading face. Both are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-hero`: the version's picture, a `.ds-scene` whose `.ds-hero__copy` holds a `.ds-kicker`, `.ds-hero__title`, `.ds-hero__lead`, `.ds-hero__actions` (the large button and a `.ds-play`) and `.ds-hero__note`.
- `.ds-scene`: the drawn sky, used for the hero, mastheads, the carousel slide and region pictures. Six empty spans in this order: `.ds-scene__sky`, `.ds-scene__sun`, `.ds-scene__far` (mountains), `.ds-scene__isle` (the great island, a small one, falling water), `.ds-scene__near` (a cliff and a traveller), `.ds-scene__cloud`. Variants `.ds-scene--2` to `--5`.
- `.ds-kicker`: a small tracked line between two fading gold rules. `.ds-star`: a four-pointed gold star, used beside kickers and side box titles.
- `.ds-play`: a round disc with a label, for the trailer; `.ds-play__disc`.
- `.ds-masthead`: the low sky that starts an inner page; holds the breadcrumb and the page header.
- `.ds-page-header`: `.ds-page-header__title`, `.ds-page-header__text`, `.ds-page-header__actions`; written in the scene's text colour.
- `.ds-sheet`: the column that holds the chapters. `.ds-chapter` one band (`--alt` in `--color-surface-alt` with a faint gold light) with a `.ds-chapter__head`: `.ds-chapter__index` (a numeral in a double ring), `.ds-chapter__title`, `.ds-chapter__line` and `.ds-chapter__more`.
- `.ds-newsroom`: the carousel beside the news column. `.ds-carousel`: one slide showing: `.ds-carousel__stage` holds a `.ds-carousel__slide` (a scene with a badge, `.ds-carousel__title` and `.ds-carousel__meta`) and two round `.ds-carousel__arrow`s (`--next`); `.ds-carousel__foot` a caption and `.ds-carousel__dots` of `.ds-carousel__dot`.
- `.ds-figure`: a hero as a backlit figure under an arch, three empty spans: `.ds-figure__arch`, `.ds-figure__body`, `.ds-figure__prop`. Plain is a mage with a great hat and a staff in `--color-fill-1`; `.ds-figure--2` an archer in `--color-fill-4`, `--3` a knight with a lit sword in `--color-fill-3`, `--4` a winged herald in `--color-fill-2`. It scales with its column.
- `.ds-banner`: the featured hero: a figure beside `.ds-banner__copy` with `.ds-banner__role`, `.ds-banner__name`, `.ds-banner__epithet`, `.ds-banner__text`, `.ds-banner__traits` (two progress bars) and a button. `.ds-cast`: the row of crests under it, `.ds-cast__item`, the current one ringed.
- `.ds-roster`: six arches across, each a `.ds-roster__item` link with a figure, `.ds-roster__name` and `.ds-roster__role`; `is-current` rings the arch.
- `.ds-skills`: a row of round skill crests, each a `.ds-tooltip` trigger.
- `.ds-who`: an avatar beside `.ds-who__name` and `.ds-who__meta`.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2 (ruled underneath), h3 (in `--color-heading-alt`), paragraphs, lists, `code`, links. `.ds-prose__lead` is the opening line.
- `.ds-link`: text link; states `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet`. `.ds-links` is a wrapping row.
- `.ds-button`: a pill, 44px high, with a thin line inside its edge. `.ds-button--secondary` (white with a gold edge); `.ds-button--danger` (secondary pill, danger ink and edge); `.ds-button--large` (58px); `.ds-button--small` (32px). States `is-hover` (ring from `--shadow-control-hover`), `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttons` is a row.
- `.ds-form`: rows (`.ds-form__row`) of a `.ds-form__label` (170px; above the field on a phone) and a `.ds-form__field`. `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`; `.ds-form__input--short`; `is-error` with `.ds-form__error`; `.ds-form__hint`; `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`; `.ds-form__actions`, `.ds-form__actions-end`. `.ds-form--inline` is one field and one button in a line.
- `.ds-switch`: an on and off row over a checkbox: `.ds-switch__label`, `.ds-switch__hint`, the hidden `.ds-switch__input` and the `.ds-switch__track`, which takes `--fill-accent` when on (`is-on` by class).
- `.ds-table`: a data table in a flush panel body with `.ds-table__wrap`. The head row takes `--fill-bar`; `is-numeric`, `is-centre`; `.ds-table__sub` a second line; `.ds-table__who`; `is-own` marks the reader's row.
- `.ds-list`: ruled rows: `.ds-list__item` with an optional `.ds-list__date`, a `.ds-list__body` (`.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__text`, `.ds-list__meta`) and perhaps a badge. `.ds-list--tight` is the one-line form of the home page, title left and date right.
- `.ds-panel`: a box inside the gold hairline: `.ds-panel__title` (may hold a badge at the right), `.ds-panel__body` (`--flush`), `.ds-panel__text`, `.ds-panel__foot`.
- `.ds-stat`: one figure, `.ds-stat__value` in the serif at display size over a tracked `.ds-stat__label`, centred, with a gold rule at its left; four sit in a `.ds-stats` row.
- `.ds-grid`: four `.ds-grid__cell` across (`--two`, `--three`), tinted and edged by `--color-fill-1`; `.ds-grid__cell--2`, `--3`, `--4`. A region cell starts with a small `.ds-scene`, then a badge, `.ds-grid__title`, `.ds-grid__text` and `.ds-grid__more`; `.ds-grid__icon` is available.
- `.ds-tabs`: a row of pills; `.ds-tabs__tab`, `is-current` in `--fill-accent`; `.ds-tabs__count` a number.
- `.ds-badge`: a pill label in `--fill-accent`; `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning`, `.ds-badge--danger`, `.ds-badge--quiet`, `.ds-badge--new`. `.ds-badges` is a row; `.ds-dot` with `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger` a lamp.
- `.ds-item`: an item name in its rarity colour: plain, `--uncommon`, `--rare`, `--epic`, `--legendary`, `--set`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: the skill window: a trigger wrapping a `.ds-tooltip__tip` shown on hover or focus (`is-open`, `.ds-tooltip--end`), with `.ds-tooltip__name` and `.ds-tooltip__line` rows (`--bonus`, `--muted`, `--lore`). `.ds-tooltip--card` writes the window into the page, as the selected skill's description.
- `.ds-progress`: a thin track with a `.ds-progress__bar`; width by `--10` to `--100`, colour by `--health`, `--xp`, `--rep`, `--danger`; `.ds-progress__label` above. Used for a hero's traits, the festival and exploration.
- `.ds-avatar`: a round crest (inline SVG) on a `--color-surface-strong` disc; `.ds-avatar--small`, `.ds-avatar--large`; `--1` to `--4` colour it.
- `.ds-accordion`: `<details>` sections: `.ds-accordion__item`, `.ds-accordion__summary`, `.ds-accordion__meta`, `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-menu`: the drop-down under a navigation item: `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__item`, shown on hover or focus (`is-open`); `.ds-menu__caret`.
- `.ds-columns`: a flexible column beside a 340px one. `.ds-sidebar`: a box of the narrow column: `.ds-sidebar__title`, a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` rows with a `.ds-sidebar__link` and `.ds-sidebar__meta`.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with `.ds-notice__title` and `.ds-notice__text`; `.ds-notice--error`. `.ds-notices` stacks several.
- `.ds-pagination`: round `.ds-pagination__link`s; `is-current` in `--fill-accent`, `is-disabled` flat.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a small tracked trail: `.ds-breadcrumb__link`, `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` (a slash), `.ds-breadcrumb__current`; it inherits the colour of the scene it lies on.
- `.ds-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__title` on `--fill-bar` with `.ds-dialog__close`, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__text`, `.ds-dialog__actions`; `.ds-dialog__backdrop` behind it.
- `.ds-empty`: an empty slot: `.ds-empty__icon`, `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text`, one secondary button.
- `.ds-band`: the closing call in `--fill-inverse`, centred, with two faint rings behind it: `.ds-band__title`, `.ds-band__text` and one large button. At most one per page.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing strip in `--fill-bar-alt`, centred: the brand, `.ds-footer__links` of `.ds-footer__link` and a `.ds-footer__note`.
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the example site; only the one named in the address shows, the home view when none is.
- `.ds-heading` (`--centre`), `.ds-rule`, `.ds-stack` (`--tight`), `.ds-split` (`--wide`), `.ds-row` (`--between`), `.ds-muted`, `.ds-icon`: helpers.

## Never

- `border-width <= 2px`: lines are hairlines; only the focus ring is 2px.
- `font-size >= 13px`: labels and meta text are the smallest text.
- `font-size <= 84px`: the hero title is the largest text.
- `font-weight <= 700`: titles are regular weight; bold belongs to labels and names in running text.
- `font-families <= 3`: a high-contrast serif, a soft sans and a monospace for code.
- `line-height >= 1.6`: body text is set open.
- `letter-spacing <= 3px`: small capitals are tracked 1px to 3px, titles hardly at all.
- `underlined-links <= 5%`: links are told apart by colour; only a hovered link is underlined.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 80px`: the longest shadow is the dialog's.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves by itself; only hovers ease.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. A new part of the home page is a `.ds-chapter` of the sheet with a numbered head; nothing floats on the page ground and no text is written on it. A box inside a chapter is a `.ds-panel` or a `.ds-sidebar`; a picture is a `.ds-scene` with its six spans and, for another light, a variant built from one fill colour; a new hero is a `.ds-figure` variant: one fill colour, one `clip-path` silhouette and one telling prop, never a face. Set titles in the serif at regular weight and everything small in tracked capitals of the sans. Keep the rule for text on fills and keep the fill and status colours as ink. Do not add images, heavy borders, dark slabs, a second accent or movement.
