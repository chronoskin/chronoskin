Version 3.2: the Lantern Isles

A fourth region, a new hero and a festival of fourteen days.

The update arrives on 14 October after a maintenance of about five hours. Everyone who has finished the second chapter can sail east at once; the world page shows the way.

What is new

  • The Lantern Isles: nine islands, four story quests and forty lamps.
  • The Lamplighter, a frost support who shields her friends.
  • Lantern week, with a staff to keep for seven sign-ins.

A gift for the wait

Enter the code LANTERN-3200 on the redeem page before 21 October for sixty lamp oil and three travel rations.
