A moth for every hearth

Three mana, two attack, four health, and a card in hand if a spell came first.

Lantern Moth is the card most decks of the fair start from. How to play it

  • Hold it until you can play a one-cost spell in the same turn.
  • Trade it into small minions; four health survives most of them.
  • Do not keep it in a slow opening hand against Wardens.

For deck builders

Its code is EMB-041. Two copies fit any Stoker or Gambler list; Moth Tempo is built around it.
