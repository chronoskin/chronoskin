What the league adds

An ember travels with you from the first map to the last.

Every pack of monsters you kill feeds the ember, and every minute you spend standing still lets it cool. Bring it to a forge and it can be spent on one item.

The forge

A forge stands in one map of each act. What it gives depends on the heat you arrive with:

  • Warm: one modifier of your item is rolled again.
  • Bright: one modifier is raised to its best value.
  • White: the item gains a modifier found nowhere else.

For those who script their loot filters

Forged items carry the tag Forged True, so a filter can show them in their own colour.
