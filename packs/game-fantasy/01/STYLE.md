# Game fantasy portal (carved stone, gilded frames)

## Summary

The home portal of an online role-playing game as such sites looked in the early years, 2004 to 2012, of an era that runs from 2004 to 2026: a fixed sheet on dark stone, every box a gilded game window with corner caps, ornate capitals over small Verdana, bevelled metal plates for buttons and a news column beside realm status and event boxes. It was the common look of official game sites and large fan sites of those years, built for a 1024px screen and drawn then with sliced images. Here stone, gilding and plates are all gradients, shadows and pseudo-elements fed by tokens.

## Layout

- Fixed centred column: `--size-page` is 980px (`.ds-page__wrap`). The page stone shows left and right of it and below the footer.
- Order of the home page: the banner (`--size-banner`, 92px, brand left, player count and account links right), the strip of navigation plates, then the sheet in `--color-canvas` between two frame-metal edges: the hero with the featured video across the full width, and below it two columns, the news panel (flexible, about 640px) and a column of side boxes (`--size-aside`, 290px) on the right, `--space-5` apart. The footer plate closes the sheet. Banner, navigation and footer are written once and stay on every view.
- Inner pages keep the banner, the plates and the footer; the hero is left out. They start with the breadcrumb on the sheet, then `.ds-page-header` (title and one line on the left, one or two plates on the right, a frame-metal rule under it), then the working parts: either the two columns again (the article with its accordion beside related boxes), or full-width blocks (notices, the `.ds-stats` strip, `.ds-tabs` standing on the framed table), or a `.ds-split` (the form beside a dialog and a small panel).
- Views: `home` (hero and video, latest news with pagination, realm status, world event bars, screenshot of the day, the closing band), `news` (one article, patch notes by calling in an accordion, related links and boxes), `guide` (the four callings as grid cells, the screenshot carousel, first steps and an empty video slot), `realms` (notices, figures, tabs by region and the realm table with a tooltip on a full realm), `account` (the account form with its buttons, the dialog that confirms closing the account, free-play progress).
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 2px (between a label and its bar), `--space-2` 4px (cell padding, small gaps), `--space-3` 8px (row padding, between buttons), `--space-4` 12px (panel padding, list gaps), `--space-5` 16px (between blocks and columns, sheet padding), `--space-6` 24px (hero padding, large button sides), `--space-7` 36px (reserved for wide ribbons).
- Control heights: `--size-control-small` 20px, `--size-control` 26px, `--size-control-large` 40px. `--size-date` 44px is the date plate of a news post, `--size-play` 48px the round play plate.

## Typography and colour roles

- Two families and a monospace. `--font-heading` and `--font-ui` are an inscriptional serif (Cinzel where installed, else Palatino Linotype, Book Antiqua, Georgia), always in capitals with 1px tracking (`--heading-transform`, `--heading-tracking`), for headings, navigation, buttons, table heads and labels. `--font-body` is Verdana for everything read. The references used commercial inscriptional and book faces; Cinzel and Palatino are the closest free stacks.
- Sizes: `--text-base` 12px at `--line-body` 1.5; `--text-small` 10px for meta, badges and footer; `--text-ui` 13px bold; `--text-large` 15px for the hero lead and the large button; `--text-h3` 14px, `--text-h2` 17px, `--text-h1` 24px; `--text-display` 34px with 2px tracking for the hero title only.
- Palette: near-black stone (`--color-page`), warm black sheet and slabs, bronze banner with pale gold lettering (`--color-bar`), dark blue steel for plates of the navigation, the hero and the footer (`--color-bar-alt`), parchment text, gold headings, a red primary plate and an arcane blue accent that also lights hovered plates and dialogs (`--color-focus` in `--shadow-control-hover` and `--shadow-dialog`).
- Surface: 2px gilded frames with a black line and a second thin gold line inside (`--shadow-panel`), almost square corners (2px and 3px), stone drawn as a cracked flagstone pattern over a vignette (`--fill-page`) and a fine speckle on slabs (`--fill-panel`), brushed bronze and hatched steel bars, plates split at half height into a lit and a dark half.
- The era's rule for text on fills, kept by every layout and every token set: on `--color-canvas` and on the three surfaces (`--color-surface`, `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong`, and `--fill-panel`) text is `--color-text`, `--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading`, `--color-heading-alt` and the link colours; on `--fill-bar` only `--color-bar-text`; on `--fill-bar-alt` only `--color-bar-alt-text`; on `--fill-inverse` only `--color-inverse-text`; on `--fill-accent` only `--color-accent-text`; on `--fill-button` `--color-button-text`, on `--fill-button-secondary` `--color-button-secondary-text`; in a form well `--color-input-text`; on `--color-notice` `--color-notice-text` and on `--color-danger-surface` `--color-danger`.
- No text is written straight on `--color-page`: the page only shows as stone around the slabs.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4`, `--color-success`, `--color-warning`, `--color-danger` and `--color-accent-alt` are inks: every palette of the era keeps them readable on its surfaces. They colour item names by rarity (`.ds-item`: common `--color-text`, uncommon `--color-success`, rare `--color-fill-1`, epic `--color-fill-2`, legendary `--color-fill-3`, set `--color-fill-4`), class crests, status labels and bars. They are never used as a fill under text; a grid cell is the surface tinted by 24% of its fill colour, and a status badge is the surface tinted by 16% of its status colour with the colour itself as ink and border.
- `--color-border-strong` is the metal of every frame (gold, brass or steel); `--color-border` the ordinary rule; `--color-border-muted` the faint rule between rows. `--color-accent-alt` is the small stone set in each frame corner, the "new" marker and the edge of a learned or current cell; it is never a fill under text.
- `--color-accent` (through `--fill-accent`) marks the current navigation plate, the current tab, the current page number, the default badge and the default bar of a progress bar.
- The destructive button is the secondary plate lettered and edged in `--color-danger`, because the primary plate of a palette may itself be red.
- Hero blocks are filled with `--fill-bar-alt` under a glow of `--color-accent` and written in `--color-bar-alt-text`; the banner and table heads take `--fill-bar`; the footer takes `--fill-bar-alt`. `--fill-inverse` with `--color-inverse-text` is the closing band of the home page (`.ds-band`): a strip of the opposite material, parchment on a dark set and dark stone on a light one.
- One-off values built in `components.css` from tokens: the faceted corner caps of `.ds-frame` (the frame metal against the same colour mixed 45% toward `--color-shadow`), button and plate edges (the fill colour mixed 60% toward `--color-shadow`), the 24% and 16% tints named above, alternate rows and wells as `--color-surface-alt` at 70% over the panel fill, `50%` radius for lamps and the round play plate, and `clip-path` polygons for ribbons, pennants, standards and the drawn landscape.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`; paints the stone. `.ds-page__wrap` is the 980px column, `.ds-page__body` the sheet, `.ds-page__inner` the stack of a view, `.ds-page__columns` the news column and the 290px column, `.ds-page__main` and `.ds-page__aside` their stacks.
- `.ds-nav`: the banner and the strip under it. `.ds-nav__banner` holds the `.ds-brand` with `.ds-nav__tagline`, and `.ds-nav__account` with `.ds-nav__count` and `.ds-nav__action` links; `.ds-nav__links` is the strip of `.ds-nav__link` plates, the current one in `--fill-accent` (`is-current`, or in the specimen one selector per view); `.ds-nav__end` pushes the account plate to the right. One item is a `.ds-menu`.
- `.ds-brand`: the mark and name at the left of the banner. `.ds-brand__mark` is a 56px crest tile dressed as the primary plate (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, the frame-metal border, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`); `.ds-brand__name` the name in the heading face at h1 size in `--color-bar-text`. Both are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-hero`: a frame in two columns: `.ds-hero__copy` with a `.ds-ribbon`, `.ds-hero__title`, `.ds-hero__lead`, `.ds-hero__actions` (one large plate, one secondary) and `.ds-hero__note`; and the featured `.ds-video`. Once, at the top of the home page.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page: `.ds-page-header__title`, `.ds-page-header__text`, `.ds-page-header__actions` on the right, a frame-metal rule below.
- `.ds-ribbon`: a short label on `--fill-accent` with notched ends.
- `.ds-video`: a framed 16 to 9 `.ds-scene` with a round `.ds-scene__play` plate and a `.ds-video__caption`. `.ds-scene` is a landscape drawn from `--color-bar-alt` (`.ds-scene__far`, `.ds-scene__near`; `--2` and `--3` change the sky): the stand-in for a screenshot or video still.
- `.ds-carousel`: one framed screenshot with `.ds-carousel__arrow` plates (`--next` on the right) over the `.ds-carousel__stage`, and a `.ds-carousel__foot` with the caption and diamond `.ds-carousel__dot`s, `is-current` lit.
- `.ds-list__date`: the date plate beside a news post, `.ds-list__month` over the day.
- `.ds-sidebar`: a framed side box: `.ds-sidebar__title` plate in `--fill-bar`, `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` rows (a lamp, a `.ds-sidebar__link`, a `.ds-sidebar__meta`), `.ds-sidebar__foot`.
- `.ds-footer`: the closing plate in `--fill-bar-alt`: centred `.ds-footer__links` of `.ds-footer__link` and a `.ds-footer__note`.
- Period components carried: `.ds-menu`, `.ds-tooltip`, `.ds-accordion`, `.ds-progress`, `.ds-carousel`. `.ds-avatar` is defined but this portal shows no portraits; a switch did not exist on these sites.
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the example site, a `<section>` with an id; only the one named in the address shows, and `.ds-view--home` shows when none is named. It is always a plain block; an inner wrapper (`.ds-page__inner`) does the layout.
- `.ds-frame`: the game window every box is built from. `--fill-panel` inside a border of `--border-width-strong` in `--color-border-strong`, with `--shadow-panel` for its inner lines and lip, and two pseudo-elements that set a faceted cap and a small `--color-accent-alt` stone on each corner (`--size-corner`, 8px). Add it to a panel, a table wrapper, a side box, the hero. `.ds-frame--plain` leaves the corner caps off for a lighter box inside another frame or a strip of figures.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2 (ruled underneath), h3 (in `--color-heading-alt`), paragraphs, lists, `code`, links. `.ds-prose__lead` is the opening line in the heading face; `.ds-prose__quote` a quoted passage with a `cite` line.
- `.ds-link`: text link; states `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` for meta and tool links. `.ds-links` is a wrapping row of links.
- `.ds-button`: a bevelled metal plate, 26px high, with a lit top edge and a dark lip from `--shadow-control`. `.ds-button--secondary` is the second metal; `.ds-button--danger` the destructive plate (secondary metal, danger ink and edge); `.ds-button--large` the 40px call in the heading face ("Play free", "Apply to join"); `.ds-button--small` 20px. States `is-hover` (takes `--shadow-control-hover`, which is where a set's glow shows), `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttons` is a row.
- `.ds-form`: rows (`.ds-form__row`) of a right-aligned `.ds-form__label` (130px, in the UI face) and a `.ds-form__field`. `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__select` and `.ds-form__textarea` are wells sunk with `--shadow-control-pressed`; `.ds-form__input--short` is a 140px well; `is-error` with `.ds-form__error` under it; `.ds-form__hint`; `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox`; `.ds-form__actions`, where `.ds-form__actions-end` pushes the destructive plate to the right. `.ds-form--search` is one well and one plate in a line.
- `.ds-table`: a data table, always inside a `.ds-frame`. Head cells take `--fill-bar`; rows are ruled with `--color-border-muted` and every second row is tinted; `is-numeric` right-aligns a column, `is-centre` centres it; `.ds-table__group` is a category row in `--color-surface-strong`; `.ds-table__sub` a small second line in a cell; `.ds-table__who` a crest beside a name.
- `.ds-list`: ruled rows. `.ds-list__item` holds a `.ds-list__body` with `.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__text` and `.ds-list__meta`, and may end in a badge. `.ds-list--tight` is the dense form for side boxes and item lists.
- `.ds-panel`: a box with a `.ds-panel__title` and a `.ds-panel__body` (`--flush` for a table or list that runs to the edges), `.ds-panel__text`, `.ds-panel__foot`. Use it together with `.ds-frame`.
- `.ds-stat`: one figure: `.ds-stat__value` (h1 size, heading face) over `.ds-stat__label` (small, UI face). Three to five sit in a `.ds-stats` row, one framed strip divided by rules.
- `.ds-grid`: four `.ds-grid__cell` blocks across (`.ds-grid--two` for two); the base cell is tinted and edged with `--color-fill-1`, `.ds-grid__cell--2`, `--3`, `--4` with the others. Inside: `.ds-grid__icon` (a crest in the fill colour), `.ds-grid__title`, `.ds-grid__text`, `.ds-grid__more` or a badge.
- `.ds-tabs`: plates standing on the top edge of the frame that follows them; `.ds-tabs__tab`, the `is-current` one taller and in `--fill-accent`; `.ds-tabs__count` for a number.
- `.ds-badge`: a small label in `--fill-accent`. Status variants `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning`, `.ds-badge--danger` (ink on a tint of itself), `.ds-badge--quiet` (a rank or a plain tag), `.ds-badge--new` (outlined in `--color-accent-alt`). `.ds-badges` is a row; `.ds-dot` with `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger` is a status lamp.
- `.ds-item`: an item name in its rarity colour: plain for common, `--uncommon`, `--rare`, `--epic`, `--legendary`, `--set`. Always bold, never underlined.
- `.ds-tooltip`: the item window, the era's signature. The trigger wraps a `.ds-tooltip__tip`, a small frame with `--shadow-dialog` that shows on hover or focus (`is-open` shows it by class; `.ds-tooltip--end` hangs it from the right edge). Inside: `.ds-tooltip__name` (with a `.ds-item` rarity class), `.ds-tooltip__line` rows, `--bonus` in `--color-success`, `--muted`, `--lore` in italics. `.ds-tooltip--card` writes the same window into the page.
- `.ds-progress`: a sunk track with a `.ds-progress__bar`; width by `.ds-progress--10`, `--25`, `--40`, `--60`, `--75`, `--90`, `--100`; colour by `--health` (`--color-success`), `--xp` (`--color-fill-2`), `--rep` (`--color-warning`), `--danger`. `.ds-progress__label` is the line above it, name left and figure right.
- `.ds-avatar`: a portrait as a framed crest: an inline SVG class crest on a `--color-surface-strong` tile with the frame border. `.ds-avatar--small` (26px), default 36px, `.ds-avatar--large` (72px); `.ds-avatar--1` to `--4` colour the crest with a fill colour.
- `.ds-accordion`: `<details>` sections; `.ds-accordion__item`, `.ds-accordion__summary` with a small plate that reads + or -, `.ds-accordion__meta` at its right, `.ds-accordion__body`.
- `.ds-menu`: the drop-down under a navigation item: a `.ds-menu__list` of `.ds-menu__item` links in a small frame, shown on hover or focus of the item (`is-open` by class). `.ds-menu__caret` is the small arrow in the item.
- `.ds-notice`: a ruled message with `.ds-notice__title` and `.ds-notice__text`; `.ds-notice--error` for errors. `.ds-notices` stacks several.
- `.ds-pagination`: small secondary plates, `.ds-pagination__link`; `is-current` in `--fill-accent`, `is-disabled` flat.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a small trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__link`, `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` (a double chevron) and `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a small game window: `.ds-dialog__title` plate in `--fill-bar` with a square `.ds-dialog__close`, `.ds-dialog__body` with `.ds-dialog__text`, `.ds-dialog__actions` centred. `.ds-dialog__backdrop` is the dimmed area behind it where the view has room for one.
- `.ds-empty`: an empty slot sunk into the sheet: `.ds-empty__icon`, `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one secondary plate.
- `.ds-band`: the closing call of the home page, a strip in `--fill-inverse`: `.ds-band__title` and `.ds-band__text` on the left, one primary plate on the right. At most one per page.
- `.ds-heading`: a section title followed by a fading rule (`--centre` puts a rule on both sides); `.ds-rule` a rule with a diamond in its middle.
- `.ds-stack`, `.ds-split` (`--wide` 3 to 2, `--even` equal heights), `.ds-row` (`--between`), `.ds-muted`, `.ds-icon`: small layout helpers.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 3px`: frames and plates are cut square; only lamps and the play plate are round.
- `border-width <= 2px`: the frame metal is 2px, every rule 1px.
- `font-size <= 34px`: the hero title is the largest text.
- `font-size >= 10px`: meta text is 10px Verdana and nothing is smaller.
- `line-height <= 1.5`: body text is set tight.
- `font-families <= 3`: an inscriptional serif, Verdana and a monospace.
- `letter-spacing <= 2px`: capitals are tracked 1px, the hero title 2px.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 20px`: shadows are lips and short glows, not soft floating cards.
- `transition = none`: nothing on these pages eased; a plate changed its fill at once.
- `animation = none`: movement belonged to the game, not to its site.
- `content-width <= 980px`: the page is a fixed column made for a 1024px screen.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. Anything that stands as a box is a `.ds-frame`: do not draw a new border, add the class. A raised thing (a plate, a title, a crest) takes a `--fill-*` gradient, an edge mixed toward `--color-shadow` and `--shadow-control`; a sunk thing (a well, a track, an empty slot) takes `--fill-input` or the tinted alternate surface and `--shadow-control-pressed`. Keep the era's rule for text on fills from the section above: bar text on bars, surface text on surfaces, and the rarity and status colours as ink only. A new banner or ribbon is cut with `clip-path` from a bar or accent fill. Do not add images, soft cards without a frame, transitions, or a colour that is not a token.
