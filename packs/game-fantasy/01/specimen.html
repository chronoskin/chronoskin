Patch 3.2: the Ember Gate

The gate at the end of the ash plains has been shut since the first chapter. Tonight it opens on every realm.

Realms go down at six for the update and return at nine. The client fetches the patch by itself; if it stalls, start it once more or type /patch retry in the launcher console.

What is new

  • The Cinder Reach, a zone for levels 58 to 60 with forty quests.
  • Warbands: groups of up to twelve that share one supply chest.
  • A second mount slot for every character over level 40.

Before you log in

Empty your mail. Letters older than thirty days are returned to their senders when the realms come back.

Changes by calling

Warden 4 changes
  • Shield Wall now lasts 10 seconds, up from 8.
  • Taunt can no longer miss creatures of your own level.
Arcanist 6 changes

Frost spells cost a tenth less to cast.

Ranger 3 changes

Pets keep their names when they are stabled.

Mender 9 changes

Every healing spell was looked at again.

Game guide

Four callings, one world. Pick the one that suits how you like to fight.

Play free

  • Warden

    Plate, shield and a long temper. Stands at the front and keeps the others alive by being hit first.

    Warden changes in 3.2

  • Arcanist

    Frost, flame and a thin robe. Great harm from far away, and very little patience for being touched.

    Arcanist changes in 3.2

  • Ranger

    A bow, a trap and an animal that does half the work. Travels alone better than any other calling.

    Ranger changes in 3.2

  • Mender

    Herbs, wards and a calm voice. Every warband wants two and can usually find one.

    Mender changes in 3.2
