A lamp for long evenings

Bright enough to read by, quiet enough to forget.

The shade turns through a half circle and the stem leans over a chair or a bed. The light unit is model CS-ARC-2 and is the same one used in the Dome pendant.

In the box

  • Stem, base and shade, in three parts
  • Light unit and a two metre cable
  • One hex key, which is all you need

Care

Wipe the shade with a dry cloth. Do not use polish on the oak.
