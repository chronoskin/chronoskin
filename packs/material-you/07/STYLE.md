# Tonal home control, 2025

## Summary

An "at a glance" home control screen in the tonal language of 2021 to 2026, in a dark scheme: no rail and no drawer, only a plain top row and a floating pill of destinations at the foot of the window, with the content between them as large shaped tiles laid on the page tone. Every colour is a tone of one indigo key colour, with an amber tertiary for the third tile and for notices; corners run from 14 to 36px, buttons, stat tiles, tabs and the status strip are full pills, and only the floating bar, menus and dialogs cast a shadow. Headings are a wide geometric sans (Lexend) at medium weight up to 68px over a neutral grotesque (Inter) at 16px.

## Layout

- Designed at 1440px; `--size-page` is 1440px and the page does not stretch beyond it. One column with `--space-6` at the window edges. From the top: the top row of `.ds-nav` (`--size-bar` 72px, on the page tone), the open view, `.ds-footer`, and `--size-dock-space` 104px kept free for the floating bar.
- `.ds-nav` holds two things: `.ds-nav__inner`, the top row with `.ds-brand`, the `.ds-menu` that switches homes and, at the right, `.ds-nav__tools` (an icon button with a `.ds-tooltip`, the `.ds-avatar`); and `.ds-nav__dock`, the floating bar on `--fill-bar`, a pill centred at the foot of the window (it sticks `--space-4` above the edge) with the destinations and, at its end, the round floating action button. At 1440px a destination is an icon beside its label in a 56px pill; the current one is filled with the secondary container.
- Home view, straight on the page tone: `.ds-status` (the secondary bar, one pill), then `.ds-glance` (the unboxed `.ds-hero` at 5 parts, the `.ds-carousel` of rooms at 7), `.ds-stats` (four pill tiles) `--space-5` under it, `.ds-filter` (section title, chips, a link), the `.ds-grid` of eight device tiles in four columns, and `.ds-columns` with the activity `.ds-panel` at 3 parts and a `.ds-stack` (the energy card and a `.ds-snackbar`) at 2.
- Spacing: `--space-1` 4px (between tabs, between the two parts of a progress track, accordion rows), `--space-2` 8px (between buttons, chips, carousel slides, dock padding), `--space-3` 12px (between tiles and stat pills), `--space-4` 16px (list row padding, field gap, between cards in a stack), `--space-5` 24px (tile and card padding, between columns), `--space-6` 32px (window edge, between hero and carousel, sheet top), `--space-7` 40px (sheet sides, above a section), `--space-8` 56px (unused reserve of the scale).
- Heights: buttons, avatars and icon discs `--size-control` 40px; large buttons and fields `--size-field` 56px; dock items and the floating action button `--size-fab` 56px; chips and the switch `--size-chip` 32px; list and accordion rows `--size-row` 72px; table rows, menu items and the status strip `--size-row-dense` 52px; device tiles at least `--size-tile` 168px; the carousel `--size-slide` 296px; the progress track `--size-track` 16px. `--size-measure` 680px caps running text, `--size-dialog` is 400px, `--size-menu` 248px.
- Inner pages (every view but the home view) have no status strip, hero or carousel. The top row and the floating bar stay; the content sits on one `.ds-main` sheet (`--color-canvas`, `--radius-page`, padding `--space-6` by `--space-7`) that opens with `.ds-breadcrumb` (where the view is below another), then `.ds-page-header`, then `.ds-tabs` and the working components in `.ds-columns` (3 parts and 2), or `.ds-layout` (the `.ds-sidebar` at 1 part, the content at 3).
- Views: `home` is the glance screen. `room` is one room: the device table under tabs with pagination, beside the climate card and who is in the room. `routines` lists the routines with their switches, an empty state for away routines, the delete dialog and further reading. `add` is the form for a new device with its notices, a step progress and the pairing guide. `settings` has the section list, the accordion of setting groups, the people and the hub storage.
- Below 1000px the glance, columns and settings layout become one column and the grid and stats two columns. Below 640px the section list of the settings view is one strip that scrolls sideways and fades at the right, the window edge is `--space-3`, the menu shows only its icon (`.ds-menu__label` is hidden) and its list opens across the full width of the top row, the tooltip button is dropped, the status strip drops its `.ds-status__action`, the stats stack, `.ds-nav__dock` stays a floating bar at the foot of the window and shows each `.ds-nav__link` as an icon above a 13px label with `--radius-page` corners, the current one keeping its filled pill, the third carousel slide is dropped and `.ds-table__extra` cells are hidden. Nothing scrolls sideways at 390px.

## Typography and colour roles

- Two families: `--font-heading` (Lexend) for titles, figures and the brand name; `--font-body` and `--font-ui` are the same Inter stack. `--font-mono` (IBM Plex Mono) is for code only. Lexend and Inter stand in for the proprietary geometric sans of the period.
- Sizes: `--text-base` 16px at `--line-body` 1.5; `--text-small` 13px (badges, field labels, list meta, phone dock labels); `--text-ui` 15px (buttons, chips, tabs, dock, table cells, second lines); `--text-large` 22px (hero lead, brand name); `--text-display` 68px at `--line-display` 1.02 with `--display-tracking` -2px (hero title, the carousel figure); `--text-h1` 42px, `--text-h2` 30px, `--text-h3` 20px at `--line-heading` 1.15 with `--heading-tracking` -0.5px.
- Weights: headings 500, display and figures 600, `--weight-ui` 500, `--weight-bold` 600. Nothing is heavier than 600. Labels are sentence case.
- Links are not underlined at rest; only the snackbar action is underlined.
- The era's rule for text on fills holds: on `--fill-page`, `--color-canvas`, the three surface tones and all four `--color-fill-*` blocks, text is `--color-text`, titles `--color-heading`, captions `--color-text-muted`. On `--fill-bar` text is `--color-bar-text`; on `--fill-bar-alt` `--color-bar-alt-text`; on `--fill-inverse` `--color-inverse-text`; on `--fill-accent` `--color-accent-text`; on `--fill-button` `--color-button-text`; on `--fill-button-secondary` `--color-button-secondary-text`; on `--color-notice` `--color-notice-text`; on `--color-danger-surface` the body is `--color-text` and the title and icon `--color-danger`. A solid block of `--color-danger`, `--color-success`, `--color-warning` or `--color-accent-alt` carries `--color-canvas` as its text colour.
- Tonal roles: `--fill-page` is behind everything and the hero sits on it unboxed. `--fill-bar` is the floating bar and the table head; `--fill-bar-alt` the status strip. `--color-canvas` is the sheet of an inner view and the icon disc on a tile. `--fill-panel` (`--color-surface`) fills cards, accordion rows and a device tile that is off; `--color-surface-alt` the stat pills, the tab track, the open carousel slide, menus and dialogs; `--color-surface-strong` tonal cards and the off switch. `--fill-accent` is the disc in a stat pill, the accent card, the floating action button and the default badge. `--fill-button-secondary` is every selected state: the current destination, tab, chip, menu item and sidebar row, and the rest of a progress track. `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` colour the device tiles that are on and the waiting carousel slides.
- `--color-link` is the primary colour: links, outlined and text button labels, the current carousel dot. `--color-heading-alt` is the hero kicker and the sidebar title. `--color-border` outlines chips and the table, `--color-border-strong` the outlined button, the off switch and the waiting carousel dots, `--color-border-muted` divides list and table rows.
- Shape: buttons, badges, tabs, the status strip, stat pills, the dock and pagination use `--radius-pill`; fields, chips, menu items and the snackbar `--radius-control`; cards, notices, the table and menus `--radius-panel`; device tiles, carousel slides, the sheet, dialogs and empty states `--radius-page`. A hovered tile tightens to `--radius-panel`.
- Elevation is tone. `--shadow-panel` is on cards and tiles (`none`), `--shadow-control` on buttons (`none`), `--shadow-control-hover` on hovered buttons, `--shadow-control-pressed` on the current tab and chip, `--shadow-dialog` on the floating bar, menus and dialogs. `--shadow-text` is on the hero title and the carousel figure (`none`). `--backdrop-blur` blurs behind the dialog scrim (`0px`).
- One-off values built with `color-mix()` or `calc()`: the 8% or 10% wash of the label colour on hovered tonal, text and icon buttons, chips, tabs, menu and dock items; the 22% status tints of the badges; the tick in the switch thumb, sized from `--size-thumb`; half of `--radius-control` for the tooltip and the inner corners of the accordion.

## Components

- `.ds-page` on `<body>`: the one-column shell on `--fill-page`.
- `.ds-nav`: the header. `.ds-nav__inner` is the top row; `.ds-nav__tools` its right end. `.ds-nav__dock` is the floating bar on `--fill-bar` holding `.ds-nav__links` of `.ds-nav__link` items (icon and label) and one `.ds-button--fab`. The current item (`is-current`, or the rule for the open view) is filled with `--fill-button-secondary`.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, one link. `.ds-brand__mark` is a round tile dressed like the filled button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, `--radius-pill`, `--shadow-control`, a transparent `--border-width` border) holding the mark as inline SVG; `.ds-brand__name` is the name in the heading font. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` drop-down. `.ds-menu__button` is its tonal pill (`.ds-menu__label` is the text, hidden on a phone); `.ds-menu__list` is a `--color-surface-alt` sheet with `--radius-panel` and `--shadow-dialog` holding `.ds-menu__item` rows; `is-current` is on the secondary container. Use it for switching between a few things of one kind.
- `.ds-tooltip`: wraps a trigger; `.ds-tooltip__text` is a small label on `--fill-inverse` shown under it on hover or focus (`is-hover` shows it statically).
- `.ds-avatar`: a disc with initials on `--color-fill-3`; `--2` and `--4` pick other fills. `.ds-avatars` overlaps several; `.ds-people` sets a stack beside a line of text.
- `.ds-status`: the secondary bar, one pill on `--fill-bar-alt` with an icon, `.ds-status__text` and an outlined `.ds-status__action`.
- `.ds-glance`: the row of hero and carousel. `.ds-hero`: the greeting, unboxed on the page tone, with `.ds-hero__kicker`, `.ds-hero__title`, `.ds-hero__lead` and a `.ds-buttons` group of large buttons.
- `.ds-carousel`: `.ds-carousel__track` holds `.ds-carousel__slide` items, the `is-current` one wide on `--color-surface-alt` with `.ds-carousel__head`, `.ds-carousel__foot`, `.ds-carousel__value` and `.ds-carousel__text`, the others as narrow shaped ends in a fill (`--3`, `--4`) with an icon and a `.ds-carousel__name`. `.ds-carousel__nav` under it holds `.ds-carousel__dots` (`.ds-carousel__dot`, the current one long) and `.ds-carousel__arrows`.
- `.ds-stat`: one summary figure, a pill with a `.ds-stat__icon` disc, `.ds-stat__value` and `.ds-stat__label`. Four sit in a `.ds-stats` row.
- `.ds-filter`: a section title, `.ds-chips` and a link at the right. `.ds-chip` is an outlined 32px chip; `is-current` fills it.
- `.ds-grid`: device tiles. A `.ds-grid__cell` (`--2`, `--3`, `--4` pick the fill; `is-off` puts it on `--fill-panel`) has a `.ds-grid__head` with a `.ds-grid__icon` disc and a `.ds-switch` or badge, then `.ds-grid__title` and `.ds-grid__text`.
- `.ds-switch`: a checkbox drawn as a pill track; the thumb is small and outline-coloured when off, larger with a tick on a `--fill-button` track when on.
- `.ds-progress`: a 16px track in two parts with a gap, `.ds-progress__bar` in `--fill-button` and `.ds-progress__rest` in the secondary container with a stop dot at its end. `--25`, `--40`, `--60`, `--75` set the share (50% without); `--warning` colours the bar. `.ds-progress__label` is the line above it. `.ds-figure` is a large number above a progress or in a card.
- `.ds-tabs`: a pill track on `--color-surface-alt` with `.ds-tabs__tab` pills; `is-current` is on the secondary container.
- `.ds-accordion`: a group of `<details>`; each `.ds-accordion__item` is a tonal row with `.ds-accordion__summary` (a title and a `.ds-accordion__hint` line, a chevron at the right) and `.ds-accordion__body`. Only the outer corners of the group are large.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the section list of the settings view, unboxed: `.ds-sidebar__title` and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of 56px pill rows `.ds-sidebar__link`; `is-current` is on the secondary container. `.ds-layout` places it beside the content.
- `.ds-main`: the sheet of an inner view. `.ds-section` (`--first` for less space above) with `.ds-section__head`, `.ds-section__title` and `.ds-section__label` titles a block; `.ds-columns` (`--even`) puts two blocks side by side and `.ds-stack` piles cards.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner view. `.ds-page-header__body` holds `.ds-page-header__title` (`--text-h1`) and `.ds-page-header__text`; `.ds-page-header__actions` sits at the right with one filled button at most.
- `.ds-prose`: long-form text; styles h1 to h3, paragraphs, lists, `strong`, inline `code` and links. `.ds-prose__lead` is the larger opening paragraph.
- `.ds-link`: inline link with `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`; `--quiet` is text-coloured, `--more` carries a chevron. `.ds-links` lays several out.
- `.ds-button`: the filled pill button. `--secondary` is tonal, `--outlined` outlined with a link-coloured label, `--text` has no container, `--danger` is filled with `--color-danger` (with `--text` a danger-coloured text button), `--large` is the 56px form, `--icon` a round icon button, `--fab` the floating action button in `--fill-accent`, `--on-inverse` the button on an inverted block. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. Group with `.ds-buttons`. `.ds-icon` (`--small`) is a stroked inline SVG.
- `.ds-form`: stacked fields. `.ds-form__field` wraps a `.ds-form__label` above a `.ds-form__control` (`--area` for a textarea; inside `.ds-form__select` for a select); `.ds-form__row` puts two side by side. `is-invalid` plus `.ds-form__error` shows an error, `.ds-form__hint` is help text. `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox` is a checkbox row; `.ds-form__check--between` with `.ds-form__check-text` and a `.ds-switch` is a setting row. `.ds-form__actions` holds the buttons.
- `.ds-table`: inside `.ds-table-wrap`, an outlined rounded container. The head row is on `--fill-bar`; `.ds-table__num` right-aligns numbers, `.ds-table__name` sets a code name, `.ds-table__extra` marks cells a phone can do without; `tr.is-current` is tinted.
- `.ds-list`: rows of `.ds-list__item` with a `.ds-list__icon` disc (`--1`, `--3`, `--4`) or an avatar, `.ds-list__body` with `.ds-list__title` and `.ds-list__text`, and `.ds-list__meta`, a badge or a switch at the right. `--cards` makes every row its own card; `is-current` puts one on the secondary container.
- `.ds-panel`: a filled card with `.ds-panel__title`, `.ds-panel__body` and optional `.ds-panel__head` and `.ds-panel__actions`. `--outlined`, `--tonal` and `--accent` as in the era's other layouts.
- `.ds-badge`: a small pill in `--fill-accent`; `--new` is the solid tertiary colour, `--count` a solid danger count, `--success`, `--warning` and `--danger` are tonal status labels with a dot. Group with `.ds-badges`.
- `.ds-notice`: a tonal card with a leading icon, `.ds-notice__body` and `.ds-notice__title`; `--error` is on `--color-danger-surface`. Group with `.ds-notices`.
- `.ds-pagination`: round `.ds-pagination__link` items; `is-current` is filled like the primary button, `is-disabled` is grey.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a muted trail of `.ds-breadcrumb__item` with `.ds-breadcrumb__link`, separated by drawn chevrons; the last carries `is-current`.
- `.ds-dialog`: a `--color-surface-alt` sheet with `--radius-page` corners, `.ds-dialog__icon`, `.ds-dialog__title`, `.ds-dialog__body` and text buttons in `.ds-dialog__actions`, on `.ds-overlay`, the scrim.
- `.ds-empty`: a centred block with `.ds-empty__mark`, `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one button.
- `.ds-snackbar`: a short message on `--fill-inverse` with `.ds-snackbar__action`.
- `.ds-footer`: one quiet line under the content, ruled above, with `.ds-footer__legal` and `.ds-footer__links` of `.ds-footer__link`.

## Never

- `gradient-fills = 0%`: every fill is one flat tone.
- `text-shadow = none`: no text has a shadow.
- `uppercase-text = 0%`: labels are sentence case.
- `border-width <= 3px`: outlines are 1px; the switch track and the focus line are 3px.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 10px`: only the floating bar, menus and dialogs are raised, softly.
- `font-weight <= 600`: emphasis is semibold at most.
- `font-weight >= 400`: no thin text.
- `font-size >= 13px`: the smallest labels are 13px.
- `font-size <= 68px`: the hero title is the largest text.
- `font-families <= 3`: a heading face, a text face and the mono for code.
- `line-height <= 1.6`: body text is 16px on a 24px line.
- `underlined-links <= 10%`: links are marked by colour.
- `border-radius <= 36px`: tiles stop at 36px; anything rounder is a full pill.
- `row-gap <= 12px`: list cards are 8px apart and table rows touch.
- `block-gap <= 40px`: blocks are 12 to 40px apart.
- `transition != none`: controls ease between states.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen. Something to glance at is a tile: a `.ds-grid__cell` in a fill when it is on or active, on `--fill-panel` when it is not, with `--radius-page`. Anything that holds longer content is a `.ds-panel`. Anything pressed is a `.ds-button` variant and stays a full pill; a selected state is the secondary container on a pill. New destinations go into the floating bar, never into a rail or a second bar; keep it to five items and one floating action button. Raise something by moving it to the next surface tone, not by adding a shadow. Use tokens only, take heights and gaps from the `--size-*` and `--space-*` scale, and follow the rule for text on fills in "Typography and colour roles". Never add a gradient, an uppercase label, a square corner on a container or a second filled button beside the first.
