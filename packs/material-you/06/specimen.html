How the week works

The week is the home screen. Everything with a date lands on it without being asked.

Each day is a column of cards. A card is an event, a list or a reminder, and the tag today pins one to the top. Lists without a date wait in the tray below.

Moving things

Press and hold a card, then drag it to another day. Shared cards move for everyone.

  • Drag to the edge to reach next week
  • Drop on the tray to remove the date
  • Shake, or use undo, to put it back

On a watch

The watch shows today only. Turn the crown for tomorrow.

Does the week start on MondayOr Sunday, as you like

Change it in the settings; shared lists follow each person's own choice.

What happens to last weekIt folds away

Unfinished things move forward and the rest is kept in the archive.

Can two people edit at onceYes, card by card

The last change to a card wins and the other is kept in its history.
