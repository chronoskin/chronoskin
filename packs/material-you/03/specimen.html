Choosing a button

Pick the kind by how much the action matters on this screen, not by how it looks.

A screen has one filled button at most. Tonal buttons support it, outlined buttons step back, and text buttons sit inside cards and dialogs. Every kind uses the class ds-button and is described in the token table.

Emphasis

  • Filled for the action that finishes the task
  • Tonal for the next most likely action
  • Outlined and text for everything else

Labels

Start with a verb and keep to three words. Sentence case, no full stop.
