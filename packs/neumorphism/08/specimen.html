Care and returns

How to hang, set and clean a clock, and how to send one back.

Start a return

Looking after a clock

A quartz movement asks for one battery a year and a dry wall. Everything else is habit.

Hanging and setting

Use the screw in the box and hang the clock on the keyhole, not on the rim. Set the time with the wheel at the back; never push the hands.

  • Wall clocks take one AA cell, alarm clocks one AAA.
  • The movement is marked CS-30 sweep on its back plate.
  • Keep the case out of steam and direct sun.

Cleaning

Dust the rim with a dry cloth. The face is pressed card and does not like water.

Sending a clock back

You have 30 days. Start the return from your order, print the label and use the box it came in.

My clock runs slow. What now?

Change the cell first: a weak one slows the hands long before it stops them.

Can I order other hands?

Do you repair old clocks?

Only our own, at any age. Write to us with the number on the back plate.
