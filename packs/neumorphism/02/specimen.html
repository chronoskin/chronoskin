Scenes

New schedule

How scenes work

A scene is a list of settings that are applied together, in order.

Each step names one device and one value, such as blinds: closed. A scene can hold up to twelve steps and runs from a key, a schedule or a sensor.

When a step fails

The scene carries on with the next step and reports the device that did not answer.

What is tried again

  • Lamps and plugs, twice within a minute.
  • Blinds, once after thirty seconds.
  • Locks are never tried again without you.

Running a scene from a schedule

A schedule can start a scene in place of a single device. Open Schedules, choose a time and pick the scene as its device.

Morning

Can two scenes run at once?
No. Starting a scene ends the one before it, and its devices keep the values they had.
What if I change a lamp by hand?
The scene leaves that lamp alone until it is started again.
Who can edit a scene?
Everybody who lives in the flat. Guests can start scenes and cannot change them.
