One surface, four ideas

Everything in the kit is the page itself, pushed out or pressed in.

Raised Cards and keys stand out of the page on a pair of shadows.
Pressed Fields, tracks and wells sink in, with the same pair turned inside.
Shaped A slow gradient makes a key domed or a field hollow.
Readable Text and status colours keep their contrast on every surface.

Version 2.4

These pages describe the release of 3 March. Notes

Two colours in, a whole kit out

You choose the surface and the accent. The kit works out the light side and the dark side of every shadow from the surface.

Set --surface and --accent on the root element. The dark side is the surface with 15% less light, the light side is white, and both are used at the same distance and blur.

Choosing a surface

A surface that is too pale leaves no room for the light side and one that is too deep hides the dark side. Stay in the middle.

What works

  • Greys and tinted greys between 85% and 92% lightness.
  • One accent with enough contrast to carry white text.
  • Text in a dark tone of the surface's own hue.

Checking the result

Every component page has a contrast report. Read dark surfaces before you invert a theme, and see which plans include ready-made themes.
