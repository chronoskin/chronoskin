House rules

What every member agrees to when booking a room.

Book a room

How booking works

A room is held for the person who booked it until ten minutes past the hour.

Members book in whole hours, up to two hours a day. A booking is kept under its own reference, such as R4-1430, which is printed on the door display.

Checking in

Tap the door display when you arrive. If nobody has checked in ten minutes after the start, the room is released and offered to the waiting list.

Who is asked first

  • Whoever has waited longest for that floor.
  • Then anyone waiting for a room with the same instrument.
  • Then the room is shown as free to everyone.

Giving a room back

If you finish early, tap the display again and choose Done. The remaining time goes back on the board at once, and it does not count against your two hours. See which rooms are free or book the next one.
