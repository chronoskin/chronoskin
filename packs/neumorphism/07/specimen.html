Getting around the player

Everything that plays goes through one queue. The disc on the first page always shows its head.

The queue

Starting an album or a playlist replaces the queue; adding a single track puts it after the one that plays. With autoplay on, similar tracks follow when the queue runs out.

  • Press Space to pause and to go on.
  • Press Shift and an arrow to skip a track.
  • Press L to add the playing track to your liked list.

Offline

Saved albums play without a network. A track that is not on the device is skipped and marked in the list, and the download starts again on the next connection.

Why did a track skip?

It was not on the device and there was no network. It is marked with a red dot in the queue.

Can two people listen at once?

On the family plan, yes: each listener has a queue of their own.

Where do imported files go?

Into the library, under Albums, with the tags the files carry.
