# Neumorphism player

## Summary

Neumorphism, or soft UI, is the interface style of 2019 to 2021 in which the page and everything on it share one pale colour and shape comes only from a pair of blurred shadows, a light one towards the top left and a dark one towards the bottom right. Its most drawn showpiece after the smart home panel was the music player: a large round disc, round transport keys, a pressed progress track and a queue. This pack is that player as a desktop app on a blush surface with plum text and one raspberry accent: a raised navigation column at the left, the playing track on one deep rounded card, and soft, very round panels in a heavy geometric display face over a plain humanist sans.

## Layout

- Frame: `.ds-app` is a centred grid `--size-page` (1240px) wide with `--space-5` (24px) padding: the `.ds-app__side` column, `--size-side` (248px), and `.ds-app__main`, which takes the rest (960px), `--space-6` (32px) apart. The page does not stretch beyond it.
- Navigation: `.ds-nav` is vertical, a raised panel at the top of the side column: the brand, the account `.ds-menu`, then one labelled key with an icon per section. The current section is pressed in. Under it, in the same column and on every page, stands the `.ds-sidebar` panel with the playlists and the download meter.
- Home page: one `.ds-page__stack` at 32px gaps in the main column: the `.ds-hero` card across the full width (disc at the left, text, progress and `.ds-keys` at the right); the `.ds-stat` row on the bare page; one `.ds-page__row`, three to two, with the `.ds-list` queue beside a stack of the `.ds-carousel` and a `.ds-panel`; a `.ds-page__title` and the `.ds-grid` of four stations.
- Inner pages keep the side column and have no hero. `--space-2` below the top of the frame come the `.ds-breadcrumb` and, 12px under it, the `.ds-page-header`: title at `--text-h1` with one muted line at the left, at most two buttons at the right, on the bare page. A list page then has the `.ds-tabs` left-aligned and `.ds-page__row--aside`: the working column (notices, table, pagination) and a `--size-aside` (312px) column of small panels. A detail page uses `.ds-page__row` (three to two): the list at the left, the dialog and a panel at the right. A settings page uses `.ds-page__row--even`: the form in a panel beside the `.ds-accordion` and a panel. A text page uses `.ds-page__row`: prose in a panel beside the accordion. Keep the columns of a row about equally long.
- Views. The specimen is one site with five views, each `<section class="ds-view" id="...">` inside `.ds-app__main`; the side column and the footer stand outside them and one view shows at a time. `listen` is the home view: the playing track, the week's figures, the queue beside the mixes and the friends who listen, and tonight's stations. `library` is the list page: tabs, notices, the table of albums with its pagination, and at the right the download ring and the empty state of podcasts. `playlist` is the detail page: the tracks of one playlist beside the dialog that deletes it and the people it is shared with. `settings` is the form page: the profile form beside the playback switches and the account buttons. `help` is the text page: prose beside the questions. The navigation presses in the link of the current view, by one `:has()` rule per view next to `.ds-nav__link.is-current`.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (dot to text, tab track padding), `--space-2` 8px (label to control, gaps between navigation keys), `--space-3` 12px (icon to text, row padding of the queue), `--space-4` 16px (control side padding, gaps between keys and buttons), `--space-5` 24px (panel padding, form field gap), `--space-6` 32px (the gap between every pair of raised panels), `--space-7` 48px (hero padding, gap above the footer), `--space-8` 64px (reserved for a page that needs a wider break).
- Raised panels need room for their shadows: never place two closer than `--space-5`, and never put a raised panel inside another. Inside a panel use wells, rules, round keys and avatars.
- `--size-rule` (1px) is the weight of the rules between rows; it is a layout token because the rules stay when a surface part sets `--border-width` to 0.
- Below 900px the frame is one column: the navigation panel becomes a block with the brand, the account menu and the section keys in a wrapping row, the playlists panel moves under the views and its links wrap into a row, every `.ds-page__row` stacks and the grid has two columns. Below 560px the padding drops to `--space-4`, the hero stacks with the disc above the text and takes `--text-h1`, the stats stack, form rows stack, the tabs fill the width and scroll inside their track, and table columns marked `.ds-table__opt` are hidden, so nothing scrolls sideways. On a phone (560px and below) the bar keeps the brand and the account (the avatar loses its name, `.ds-avatar__text`, and its menu list opens from the right edge) and `.ds-nav__list` becomes a raised dock of five equal keys fixed to the foot of the window, each `.ds-nav__link` an icon (`.ds-nav__icon`) above a small label, the current one pressed in; the counts `.ds-nav__count` are hidden.

## Typography and colour roles

- Two families: `--font-body` and `--font-ui` are a humanist sans stack (Lato first), `--font-heading` a geometric display stack (Raleway, then Avenir Next) used for the brand, titles and figures. `--font-mono` is for code only.
- Sizes: `--text-base` 15px body, `--text-ui` 14px for buttons, inputs, navigation, table cells and notices, `--text-small` 12.5px for meta text, labels and badges, `--text-large` 17px for lead text, `--text-h3` 16px, `--text-h2` 21px (also stat figures), `--text-h1` 28px, `--text-display` 46px for the hero title only.
- Weights: 400 body, 700 for headings, controls, labels and links, 800 for the hero title. Line height 1.55 for body, 1.25 for headings and controls.
- Uppercase appears only on table head cells and `.ds-sidebar__heading`, written in the component. The hero title is tracked in by 0.5px (`--display-tracking`); nothing else is tracked.
- One surface colour: `--color-page`, `--color-canvas`, `--color-surface`, `--color-bar`, `--color-button-secondary` and `--color-input` are the same blush (238 226 229). `--color-surface-alt` is one step lighter, for hovered rows and menu items; `--color-surface-strong` one step darker, for inline code, quiet and status badges, the unfilled part of a ring and carousel dots.
- The relief has two colours: `--color-shadow` is the dark side and `--color-border-muted` the light side. Every shadow token is built from these two. `--color-border-muted` is not used for anything else.
- Text is `--color-text` (plum) on the surface, `--color-text-muted` for meta and lead text, `--color-heading-alt` for panel titles, form labels and accordion heads.
- Each text token goes with its own fill and no other: `--color-bar-text` on `--fill-bar` (navigation panel, table head), `--color-bar-alt-text` on `--fill-bar-alt` (footer), `--color-inverse-text` on `--fill-inverse` (tooltip, code block), `--color-accent-text` on `--fill-accent` (badge, current page, the disc label, the playing row's cover, carousel art, accent avatar), `--color-button-text` on `--fill-button` (primary button, the play key, brand mark), `--color-button-secondary-text` on `--fill-button-secondary` (secondary button, round keys, pagination keys, current tab, avatar), `--color-input-text` on `--fill-input` (controls, account well, tab track, stat icons, list covers, carousel, empty state, ring face), `--color-notice-text` on `--color-notice`, `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`.
- Links in running text are `--color-link` at weight 700 with no underline; underline appears on hover. Navigation, titles and table links are `--color-link-quiet`.
- `--color-accent` is the one saturated colour: default badge, progress bar and ring, the thumb of a switch that is on, checked checkbox, current page, the label of the disc. `--color-accent-alt` only marks "new" as a dot. `--color-button` is the dark plum, so the primary button and the play key are the darkest things on the page.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` colour the discs of the grid stations and nothing else; no text is set on them.
- Status colours appear as an 8px dot (`--color-success`, `--color-warning`, `--color-danger`); `--color-danger` is also the text of errors and of the destructive button.
- One-off values with no token: circles use `border-radius: 50%`; the tab track takes `--radius-control` plus 4px; inline code uses a third of `--radius-control`; small wells cap their radius with `min()` over a `--space-*` token so that a very round surface part does not turn them into discs; placeholders are `--color-input-text` at opacity 0.55; the ring is a `conic-gradient()` of `--color-accent` and `--color-surface-strong`.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`; paints the surface colour and sets the base type. `.ds-app` is the frame, `.ds-app__side` the navigation column, `.ds-app__main` the column of the views. `.ds-page__stack` is a vertical stack, `.ds-page__row` two columns three to two, `.ds-page__row--aside` a working column and a 312px column, `.ds-page__row--even` two halves. `.ds-page__title` is a heading on the bare page over a row of the home view, with an optional link at its right. `.ds-view` is one screen of the specimen (see Layout); an application that gives each page its own document does not need it.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name at the top of the navigation panel, linking to the home page: `.ds-brand__mark` is a small raised key (`--size-brand`, 36px) dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text`, the hairline from `--border-width`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark as inline SVG painted with `currentColor`; `.ds-brand__name` is the name in the heading font at `--text-h3`, in `--color-bar-text`. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders: replace them with the installing project's own name and logo and keep the key.
- `.ds-nav`: the raised navigation panel. It holds the `.ds-brand`, the `.ds-menu` and the `.ds-nav__list` of `.ds-nav__link` keys, each with a `.ds-nav__icon` and an optional `.ds-nav__count`; the current item is `is-current` and pressed in; `is-hover` raises a link.
- `.ds-menu`: a drop-down, here the account menu under the brand. A `<details>` whose `.ds-menu__toggle` summary is a sunk well (it holds a `.ds-avatar` and a chevron) and whose `.ds-menu__list` is a raised sheet laid over what follows; `.ds-menu__link` is one entry, `is-hover` lightens it, `.ds-menu__link--danger` is the leaving entry.
- `.ds-hero`: the page introduction, one deep card with the radius of `--radius-page`: `.ds-hero__art` is a raised disc holding a sunk `.ds-hero__well` and the accent `.ds-hero__label`; `.ds-hero__text` holds `.ds-hero__eyebrow` (a badge and a muted line), `.ds-hero__title` at the display size, `.ds-hero__lead`, a `.ds-progress` with its legend, and `.ds-hero__actions` with the `.ds-keys` and the main button. Use it once, on the home page.
- `.ds-keys`: a row of round keys, `.ds-keys__key` (48px), in the secondary button's dress; `.ds-keys__key--primary` (64px) is the main one, in the primary button's dress. `is-hover` lifts a key, `is-active` sinks it, `is-on` keeps a toggle key sunk. Wrap each in a `.ds-tooltip`.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page, used instead of the hero: `.ds-page-header__text` holds `.ds-page-header__title` and one line of `.ds-page-header__lead`; `.ds-page-header__actions` sits at the right with at most one primary button. No fill or shadow of its own. Put the breadcrumb directly above it.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1 to h3, paragraphs, lists, inline `code`. `.ds-prose__lead` for a lead paragraph, `.ds-prose__code` for a dark code block. Put prose inside a `.ds-panel`.
- `.ds-link`: link in running text; states `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` for undecorated links in tables and titles. `.ds-link__sample` lays out a row of links.
- `.ds-button`: primary, 40px high, raised by `--shadow-control`; `is-hover` lifts it, `is-active` sinks it. `.ds-button--secondary` is the same key in the surface colour. `.ds-button--danger` is the destructive action: a surface-coloured key with `--color-danger` text; use it once, last in a group or as the confirming button of a dialog. `.ds-button--large` (52px) and `.ds-button--block` (full width) exist. `disabled` or `is-disabled` is flat, with no shadow. `.ds-button__row` spaces a group. One primary button per group.
- `.ds-form`: vertical form inside a panel. `.ds-form__field` wraps a `.ds-form__label` and a control: `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__textarea`, `.ds-form__select` (inside `.ds-form__select-wrap` with a `.ds-form__chevron` icon), `.ds-form__range` (sunk track, raised accent thumb). `.ds-form__check` is a label row holding a `.ds-form__checkbox` (raised square, accent when checked). An invalid input takes `is-error`, which replaces its well with a 2px danger ring, and is followed by `.ds-form__error`; `.ds-form__hint` is help text; `.ds-form__actions` holds the buttons; `.ds-form__row` puts two fields side by side. Text controls are wells: they use `--shadow-control-pressed`.
- `.ds-switch`: the on/off control, a `<label>` holding `.ds-switch__input` (the checkbox, invisible over the whole label), `.ds-switch__track` (a sunk pill with a raised thumb) and `.ds-switch__label`, which may hold a `.ds-switch__hint` line. When on, the thumb moves right and takes the accent; the track stays a well. `.ds-switch--end` puts the track at the right end of a full-width row, for settings lists.
- `.ds-table`: data table as one raised sheet: small uppercase head, rows divided by a 1px rule, no stripes. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns a numeric column, `.ds-table__meta` is muted text, `.ds-table__wide` gives the name column 38%, `.ds-table__opt` marks a column that is dropped on a phone, `is-hover` on a row lightens it.
- `.ds-list`: the queue, one raised sheet with rules between rows. `.ds-list__head` is its title row, with a switch or a count at the right. `.ds-list__item` holds a `.ds-list__cover` (a sunk square with the track number), a `.ds-list__body` (`.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__meta`) and a `.ds-list__time`. The playing row is `is-current`: its cover is raised and filled with the accent. `.ds-list__dot` is a status dot; `--off` turns it red, `--new` takes the second accent.
- `.ds-panel`: the basic raised card. `.ds-panel__title` is the title line (a badge may stand at its right), `.ds-panel__body` the padded content, `.ds-panel__text` a muted paragraph, `.ds-panel__facts` a list of labels and values, `.ds-panel__rows` a stack of `.ds-panel__row` lines (a person and a `.ds-panel__note`, or a switch).
- `.ds-grid`: four station cards in a row, each a raised `.ds-grid__cell` (a link) with a sunk `.ds-grid__art` in which a `.ds-grid__chip` disc stands, coloured by `--1` to `--4` from `--color-fill-*`; under it `.ds-grid__title` and `.ds-grid__text`. `is-hover` lifts a card.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of three summary figures on the bare page; each `.ds-stat__item` is a single figure: a round sunk `.ds-stat__icon`, then `.ds-stat__value` (21px, tabular figures) over `.ds-stat__label`.
- `.ds-tabs`: a sunk track holding `.ds-tabs__tab` segments; the one with `is-current` is raised out of the track. Use it for view switches, left-aligned under the page header, not for site navigation.
- `.ds-badge`: pill for counts and states, filled with the accent. `.ds-badge--quiet` is a flat pill in `--color-surface-strong` with `--color-text`. Status labels are `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning` and `.ds-badge--danger`: the same flat pill with a leading 8px dot in the status colour. Never colour the whole pill.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the raised panel under the navigation: `.ds-sidebar__heading` (small uppercase), `.ds-sidebar__list`, `.ds-sidebar__link` with a `.ds-sidebar__count`, pressed in when `is-current`; `.ds-sidebar__foot` is a ruled foot for a meter.
- `.ds-progress`: how far something is. The bar is a pressed track 8px high with a `.ds-progress__bar` in the accent; the level is a class on the track, `.ds-progress--25`, `--40`, `--60`, `--75` or `--90`. `.ds-progress__legend` is the line of two labels over it. `.ds-progress--ring` with the same level classes is a raised ring (120px) around a sunk `.ds-progress__face` holding a `.ds-progress__figure` and a `.ds-progress__caption`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a hint for an icon key. Wrap the trigger and a `.ds-tooltip__tip` in it; the tip is a small inverted label above the trigger, shown on hover and keyboard focus, or with `is-open`.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person. `.ds-avatar__pic` is a raised disc (36px) with initials in the secondary button's dress; `.ds-avatar--accent` fills it with the accent, for the signed-in listener. `.ds-avatar__text` holds `.ds-avatar__name` and `.ds-avatar__meta` beside it.
- `.ds-accordion`: one raised sheet of `<details>` sections, `.ds-accordion__item`, divided by rules: `.ds-accordion__head` is the summary with a round `.ds-accordion__chevron` key that is pressed in and turned while the section is open; `.ds-accordion__body` holds text or a column of switches. Use it for grouped settings and for questions.
- `.ds-carousel`: a well with one slide showing: `.ds-carousel__track` scrolls sideways with scroll-snap, each `.ds-carousel__slide` holds a raised accent `.ds-carousel__art` tile and a `.ds-carousel__body` (`.ds-carousel__title`, `.ds-carousel__text`, a link); `.ds-carousel__dots` under it marks the current slide with `is-current`.
- `.ds-notice`: information notice sunk into the surface, with `.ds-notice__icon` and `.ds-notice__title`; `.ds-notice--error` has a pale red well and `--color-danger` text. `.ds-notice__stack` stacks several.
- `.ds-pagination`: row of raised 40px keys, `.ds-pagination__link`; the current page is `is-current`, flat and filled with the accent.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: small muted trail above the page header of an inner page, `.ds-breadcrumb__item` separated by slashes, `.ds-breadcrumb__link` for the linked levels.
- `.ds-dialog`: a tinted sheet (`--color-overlay`) with a centred `.ds-dialog__box`, 440px wide, raised by the deepest shadow: `.ds-dialog__title`, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__actions` right-aligned.
- `.ds-empty`: empty state as a sunk well: a raised `.ds-empty__icon` disc, `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one primary button.
- `.ds-footer`: a rounded strip in `--fill-bar-alt` under the views, as wide as the main column: `.ds-footer__inner`, `.ds-footer__links`, `.ds-footer__link`.

## Never

- `border-width <= 2px`: there are no outlines; the only lines are 1px rules between rows and a 2px focus or error ring.
- `border-radius <= 44px`: controls are 18px, panels 30px, the hero card and the dialog 44px; only pills and discs go higher.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 36px`: controls blur 10px, panels 20px, the dialog, the disc and a lifted card 36px.
- `text-shadow = none`: text is flat; only boxes have relief.
- `gradient-fills <= 20%`: only accent fills and the ring are gradients; panels, keys and the page are flat.
- `font-size <= 46px`: the hero title is the largest text.
- `font-size >= 12px`: meta text and badges are the smallest text.
- `font-weight <= 800`: only the hero title is extra bold.
- `font-families <= 3`: a sans for text, a display sans for titles and one monospace.
- `line-height <= 1.6`: body text is set at 1.55.
- `underlined-links <= 10%`: links are marked by colour and weight; underline is a hover state.
- `uppercase-text <= 10%`: uppercase is for table heads and the sidebar heading only.
- `row-gap <= 16px`: rows of a table or list touch and are divided by a rule; rows of people or switches inside a panel sit 16px apart.
- `block-gap <= 48px`: raised panels sit 32px apart; 48px above the footer is the largest gap.
- `content-width <= 1240px`: everything stays in the centred frame.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen. Decide first whether it is raised or sunk: a container that holds content is raised and starts from `.ds-panel` (fill, radius and `--shadow-panel` together); something the user fills in or that shows a state is sunk and starts from `.ds-form__input` (`--fill-input`, `--color-input-text`, `--shadow-control-pressed`); something the user presses starts from `.ds-button--secondary`, round like `.ds-keys__key` when it carries only an icon. Keep the fill equal to the surface behind it, since the shadows only read on the shared colour, and never draw a shadow in a raw colour: use the shadow tokens as they are. Use tokens only; for a size with no token use `calc()` over `--space-*` or `--size-*`. Keep each text token on the fill it belongs to, and keep the accent to one small element per group.
