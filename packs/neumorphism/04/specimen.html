How the weekly plan is scored

Each planned session is worth its planned minutes, and the ring shows the share you have done.

A session counts once it is at least eighty percent as long as planned. Sessions are matched by day and kind, with a key such as thu-run.

Moved and missed sessions

A session you move inside the week keeps its value. One that is not done by Sunday night counts as missed.

What never counts

  • Walks shorter than ten minutes.
  • Sessions entered by hand without a duration.
  • Anything logged on a rest day.
