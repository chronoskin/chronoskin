# Neumorphism tiles

## Summary

Neumorphism, or soft UI, is the interface style of 2019 to 2021 in which the page and everything on it share one pale colour and shape comes only from a pair of blurred shadows, light towards the top left and dark towards the bottom right. This pack is its overview screen, the kind drawn for fitness, banking and habit apps of those years: a page built entirely from large raised tiles in rows of changing proportions, each holding one thing, a ring gauge, a set of bar meters, a small week chart or a short list. It follows the component-library form of the style: a pale blue-grey surface, near-black text in a plain grotesque at 16px with light weights, one bright blue accent, small 8px corners on controls and shallow wells.

## Layout

- One centred column, `--size-page` (1200px) with `--space-5` (24px) side padding. Inside it the page is a stack of tile rows, and every gap, between rows and between tiles, is `--space-6` (32px). There is no page sidebar and nothing sits on the bare page except the navigation row and the head of an inner page.
- Navigation: `.ds-nav` is a row `--size-nav` (64px) high on the bare page: the brand at the left, a raised capsule of links with icons in the middle, and a secondary button and the account disc at the right. The current link is a pill filled with the accent.
- A row of tiles is `.ds-bento`; the modifier sets the proportions: none for two equal tiles, `.ds-bento--lead` 2 to 1, `.ds-bento--three` 5 to 4 to 3, `.ds-bento--text` 7 to 5, `.ds-bento--form` 1 to 2; `.ds-bento--start` lets the columns keep their own heights. Tiles in a row stretch to the same height. `.ds-stat` (three tiles) and `.ds-grid` (four tiles) are rows of their own.
- Home page, top to bottom: `.ds-bento--lead` with the `.ds-hero` tile (text left, the `.ds-ring` right) beside a panel of `.ds-progress` bars; the `.ds-stat` row; the `.ds-grid` row; `.ds-bento--three` with the `.ds-list` tile, a stack of notices and a note panel, and the `.ds-sidebar` tile.
- Inner pages have no hero. On the bare page come the `.ds-breadcrumb`, the `.ds-page-header` (title at `--text-h1` left, actions right) and, on a list page, the `.ds-tabs` capsule, in one block; then one row of two columns that keep their own heights, `.ds-bento--text` with `.ds-bento--start`: the main tile or a `.ds-page__stack` (table, pagination, a meter tile; the form; the prose) in the wide left column and a `.ds-page__stack` of smaller tiles (dialog, list, empty state, notes) in the right. `.ds-bento--form` (1 to 2) is the same with a narrow form on the left. A wide form sets pairs of short fields side by side with `.ds-form__row`.
- Views. The specimen is one site with four views, each `<section class="ds-view" id="...">` inside `.ds-page__inner`; the navigation row and the footer stand outside them and one view shows at a time. `today` is the home view: the hero tile beside today's meters, the stat row, the week charts, then the session list, notices with the coach's note, and the plans sidebar. `sessions` is the list page: tabs, the table with pagination and the week's meters on the left, the dialog that deletes a session and the coming days on the right. `log` is the form page: the form tile on the left, small tiles and a notice on the right. `plan` is the text page: how the plan is scored, beside the empty state for races and two small tiles. The capsule fills the link of the current view with the accent, by one `:has()` rule per view next to `.ds-nav__link.is-current`; `log` marks Sessions.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 4px (between links in a capsule, dot to text), `--space-2` 8px (label to control, capsule padding, between chart bars), `--space-3` 12px (ring rim, chart padding, icon to text), `--space-4` 16px (between buttons, ring to figure in a stat tile, row padding), `--space-5` 24px (tile padding, between meters, between form fields), `--space-6` 32px (every gutter, hero tile padding), `--space-7` 48px (dialog backdrop padding), `--space-8` 64px (above the footer).
- Other sizes: `--size-ring` 208px and `--size-ring-small` 72px, `--size-bars` 80px (height of a week chart), `--size-day` 48px (the day well of a list row), `--size-brand` 36px, `--size-dialog` 440px.
- A tile holds wells, rules, flat rows and controls, never another raised panel. The ring is the one raised thing inside a tile, and it is a control-sized knob (`--shadow-control`).
- `--size-rule` (1px) is the weight of the rules between table and list rows; it stays when a surface part sets `--border-width` to 0.
- Below 900px every row stacks, the grid drops to two columns, and the navigation becomes two lines: brand and tools, then the capsule across the full width. Below 560px the side padding drops to `--space-4`, the gutters to `--space-5`, the hero title takes `--text-h1`, form rows stack, and table columns marked `.ds-table__opt` are hidden so that the table never scrolls sideways. On a phone (560px and below) the capsule `.ds-nav__list` stays a full-width row of centred `.ds-nav__link` items under the brand and `.ds-nav__tools`, the current one filled with the accent, and tabs wrap and centre. In `.ds-nav__tools` a `.ds-menu` loses its caret (`.ds-menu__caret`) and its toggle shrinks to the avatar or icon.

## Typography and colour roles

- One family for everything: `--font-body`, `--font-heading` and `--font-ui` are Roboto, then Segoe UI, Oxygen, Ubuntu and Helvetica Neue; `--font-mono` is for code only.
- Sizes: `--text-base` 16px, `--text-ui` 14px (buttons, inputs, navigation, table cells), `--text-small` 12px (meta, labels, badges, ring captions), `--text-large` 20px (lead, large buttons), `--text-h3` 16px, `--text-h2` 20px (tile figures), `--text-h1` 34px (page title, the figure in the large ring), `--text-display` 60px for the hero title only.
- Weights are light: `--weight-body` and `--weight-display` 400, `--weight-bold`, `--weight-heading` and `--weight-ui` 500. `--line-body` 1.5, `--line-heading` 1.25, `--line-display` 1.1.
- No tracking. Uppercase appears only on table head cells, written in the component.
- One surface: `--color-page`, `--color-canvas`, `--color-surface`, `--color-bar`, `--color-input` and `--color-button-secondary` are the same pale blue-grey (228 235 245). `--color-surface-alt` (237 242 249) is a step lighter, for hovered rows and links and for notices; `--color-surface-strong` (210 219 235) a step darker, for inline code, quiet and status badges, disabled controls, the unused part of a ring and unlit chart bars. The footer uses the same darker step (`--color-bar-alt`).
- The relief pair: `--color-shadow` (190 200 228) is the dark side and `--color-border-muted` (white) the light side of every shadow. `--color-border-muted` is used for nothing else.
- Text is near-black (`--color-text`, `--color-heading` 16 17 18) with dark grey (`--color-text-muted`, `--color-heading-alt` 70 74 82) for meta text, tile titles and labels.
- Each text token goes with its own fill: `--color-bar-text` on `--fill-bar` (navigation capsule, table head), `--color-bar-alt-text` on `--fill-bar-alt` (footer), `--color-inverse-text` on `--fill-inverse` (code block), `--color-accent-text` on `--fill-accent` (current navigation link, count badge, current page), `--color-button-text` on `--fill-button` (primary button, brand key), `--color-button-secondary-text` on `--fill-button-secondary` (secondary button, account disc, pagination keys), `--color-input-text` on `--fill-input` (controls, ring faces, day wells, the current tab, empty state), `--color-notice-text` on `--color-notice`, `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface`, `--color-text` on `--color-surface-alt` and `--color-surface-strong`.
- `--color-accent` and `--color-button` are one bright blue (41 121 255) with white text: ring arcs, meter bars, slider thumbs, switches that are on, the current navigation link. `--color-link` is the deeper blue 41 98 255. `--color-accent-alt` (teal, 0 119 170) only marks "new" or "planned" as a dot.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` (blue, green, rose, slate) colour the bars of the four week charts and nothing else; no text is set on them.
- Status: `--color-success` 102 153 0, `--color-warning` 214 118 0, `--color-danger` 198 40 72, as 8px dots and as error text and rings.
- Surface: `--border-width` is 0; `--border-width-strong` (2px) is the focus and error ring. `--shadow-control` is 3px offset with 6px blur, `--shadow-panel` and `--shadow-control-hover` 6px and 12px, `--shadow-dialog` 9px and 18px with its light side at 30%; `--shadow-control-pressed` is a shallow 2px and 5px inset. Corners: `--radius-control` and `--radius-panel` 8px, `--radius-page` 24px (every tile, the dialog), `--radius-pill` 24px (capsules, badges). Tiles are convex: `--fill-panel` is a 145 degree gradient from the surface colour mixed 45% with the light colour to the surface mixed 30% with the shadow colour, so every tile catches the light at its top left corner; keys, wells and bars stay flat. `--shadow-text` is `none`. Transitions run 0.3s.
- One-off values with no token: discs use `border-radius: 50%`; a ring arc is a `conic-gradient()` from `--color-accent` to `--color-surface-strong` with the reading as the percentage stop; chart bars have a `--space-1` radius; inline code uses half of `--radius-control`; placeholders are `--color-input-text` at opacity 0.55.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`; paints the surface and sets the base type. `.ds-page__inner` is the centred column; `.ds-page__stack` stacks tile rows, or tiles inside one cell of a row, 32px apart; `.ds-page__section` stacks an inner page the same way. `.ds-view` is one screen of the specimen (see Layout).
- `.ds-bento`: one row of tiles with 32px gutters; `--lead`, `--three`, `--text` and `--form` set the proportions as listed under Layout.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, at the left of the navigation row, linking to the home page: `.ds-brand__mark` is a small raised key (`--size-brand`, 36px) dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text` for the mark, the hairline from `--border-width`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) holding the mark as inline SVG painted with `currentColor`; `.ds-brand__name` is the name in the heading font at `--text-h3`, in `--color-heading` because it stands on the bare page. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders: replace them with the installing project's own name and logo and keep the key.
- `.ds-nav`: the top row. `.ds-nav__inner` holds the brand, the raised capsule `.ds-nav__list` of `.ds-nav__link` items (each with a 16px `.ds-nav__glyph` icon) and `.ds-nav__tools` with one button and the account `.ds-menu`. The current link (`is-current`) is filled with the accent; `is-hover` tints a link.
- `.ds-hero`: the lead tile of the home page: on the left a `.ds-hero__date` line, `.ds-hero__title` at the display size, `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__actions`; on the right one large `.ds-ring`. Once per page.
- `.ds-ring`: a gauge: a raised knob holding `.ds-ring__arc` (68% by default; `--25`, `--50` and `--90` set other readings) and the sunk `.ds-ring__face` with a `.ds-ring__figure` and, in the large size, a `.ds-ring__caption`. `.ds-ring--small` (72px) carries only a two-digit figure and stands at the left of a stat tile.
- `.ds-progress`: a stack of `.ds-progress__row` items, each a `.ds-progress__label` (name left, reading in a `span` right) over a sunk `.ds-progress__track` with an accent `.ds-progress__bar` (50% wide by default; `--30`, `--65`, `--85`). `.ds-progress--fill` spreads the rows over the height of a stretched tile.
- `.ds-page-header`: head of an inner page instead of the hero: `.ds-page-header__text` with `.ds-page-header__title` and one line of `.ds-page-header__lead`; `.ds-page-header__actions` at the right with at most one primary button. Breadcrumb directly above, tabs directly below.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1 to h3, paragraphs, lists, inline `code`; `.ds-prose__lead` for a lead paragraph, `.ds-prose__code` for a dark code block. Put prose inside a `.ds-panel`.
- `.ds-link`: link in running text; states `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` for undecorated links in tables and titles. `.ds-link__sample` lays out a row of links.
- `.ds-button`: primary, blue, 40px high, raised; `is-hover` lifts it, `is-active` sinks it. `.ds-button--secondary` is the same key in the surface colour. `.ds-button--danger` is the destructive action, a surface-coloured key with `--color-danger` text, used once and last in a group. `.ds-button--large` (52px) for the hero. `disabled` or `is-disabled` is flat. `.ds-button__row` spaces a group.
- `.ds-form`: a vertical stack of `.ds-form__field` (a `.ds-form__label` and a control): `.ds-form__input`, `.ds-form__textarea`, `.ds-form__select` (in `.ds-form__select-wrap` with `.ds-form__chevron`), `.ds-form__range`. `.ds-form__check` is a label row with a `.ds-form__checkbox` or a `.ds-switch`. `is-error` on an input gives a 2px danger ring and is followed by `.ds-form__error`; `.ds-form__hint` is help text; `.ds-form__actions` holds the buttons. Text controls are wells: `--shadow-control-pressed`. On an inner page the form is the narrow left tile.
- `.ds-table`: one raised sheet with a small uppercase head and 1px rules between rows. `.ds-table__num` right-aligns numbers, `.ds-table__meta` is muted, `is-hover` on a row lightens it.
- `.ds-list`: a tile of rows divided by rules. Each `.ds-list__item` starts with a sunk `.ds-list__day` well holding a day number, then `.ds-list__body` with `.ds-list__title` and a `.ds-list__meta` line led by a `.ds-list__dot` (green done, `--off` a problem, `--new` planned), then a `.ds-list__value` at the right.
- `.ds-panel`: the basic tile, with `--radius-page`: `.ds-panel__title` (a badge may sit at its right) and `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__body--grow` lets the body fill a stretched tile.
- `.ds-grid`: a row of four tiles. Each `.ds-grid__cell` (`--1` to `--4` pick the chart colour) holds a `.ds-grid__label`, a `.ds-grid__value`, a sunk `.ds-grid__bars` chart of seven `.ds-grid__bar` items (20% high by default; `--2` to `--5` are 40% to 100%) and a `.ds-grid__meta` line.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of three summary tiles; each `.ds-stat__item` is one figure, a small ring at the left and `.ds-stat__text` at the right with a `.ds-stat__label` over a `.ds-stat__value` and its `.ds-stat__meta` line.
- `.ds-tabs`: a raised capsule holding `.ds-tabs__tab` items; the current one (`is-current`) is a well pressed into it. Left-aligned under the page header as the view switch.
- `.ds-badge`: pill for counts, filled with the accent (`--color-accent-text` on `--fill-accent`). `.ds-badge--quiet` is a flat pill in `--color-surface-strong` with `--color-text`, for neutral counts; it has no shadow, because a well is too deep for a pill this small. Status labels are `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning` and `.ds-badge--danger`: the same flat pill with a leading 8px dot in the status colour. Never colour the whole pill.
- `.ds-sidebar`: a tile of links in the narrow cell of a row: `.ds-sidebar__heading`, `.ds-sidebar__list`, `.ds-sidebar__link` with an optional count badge at the right; the current link is pressed in.
- `.ds-notice`: a message sunk into the surface, with `.ds-notice__icon` and `.ds-notice__title`; `.ds-notice--error` takes `--color-danger`. `.ds-notice__stack` stacks several.
- `.ds-pagination`: raised 40px keys, `.ds-pagination__link`, left-aligned under the table; the current page is flat and filled with the accent.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: small muted trail, `.ds-breadcrumb__item` separated by slashes, `.ds-breadcrumb__link` for linked levels.
- `.ds-dialog`: a tinted sheet (`--color-overlay`) with a centred `.ds-dialog__box`, at most 440px wide, raised by `--shadow-dialog`: `.ds-dialog__title`, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__actions` right-aligned.
- `.ds-empty`: a sunk well with a raised `.ds-empty__icon` disc, `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and one primary button.
- `.ds-footer`: full-width flat band in `--fill-bar-alt`: one line with the site name at the left and `.ds-footer__links` with `.ds-footer__link` at the right, inside `.ds-footer__inner`.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a hint on hover or focus. Wrap the trigger and follow it with `.ds-tooltip__tip` (`role="tooltip"`), a small label in `--fill-inverse`; `--below` drops it under the trigger, `--end` lines it up with the trigger's right edge. Use it for an icon key or a short badge whose meaning needs a word; never force it open.
- `.ds-avatar`: a person as initials on a raised disc (`.ds-avatar__pic`, `--size-avatar`, `--shadow-control`), with an optional `.ds-avatar__text` holding `.ds-avatar__name` and `.ds-avatar__meta`. Use it wherever a person is named.
- `.ds-accordion`: one raised sheet of `<details class="ds-accordion__item">` sections; `.ds-accordion__head` is the summary with a round `.ds-accordion__chevron` that is pressed while open, `.ds-accordion__body` the answer. Use it for questions and grouped settings in a side column.
- `.ds-menu`: the account menu, a `<details>`: `.ds-menu__toggle` is a sunk well holding the `.ds-avatar` and a `.ds-menu__caret`, `.ds-menu__list` the raised list of `.ds-menu__link` items (`--danger` for signing out) that opens under it.

## Never

- `border-width <= 2px`: nothing is outlined; 1px rules and the 2px ring are the only lines.
- `border-radius <= 24px`: controls and wells 8px, tiles and the dialog 24px; only capsules and discs go higher.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 18px`: tiles blur 12px and the dialog 18px; the relief is crisp, not foggy.
- `text-shadow = none`: text is flat.
- `gradient-fills <= 15%`: only the tiles are convex; keys, wells, bars and badges are one flat colour each.
- `font-size <= 60px`: the hero title is the largest text.
- `font-size >= 12px`: meta text and badges are the smallest text.
- `font-weight <= 500`: headings are medium, never bold.
- `font-families <= 2`: one sans and one monospace.
- `line-height <= 1.55`: body text is set at 1.5.
- `letter-spacing = 0`: no tracking on any text.
- `underlined-links <= 10%`: underline is a hover state.
- `uppercase-text <= 5%`: uppercase is for table heads only.
- `row-gap <= 4px`: rows of a table or list touch and are divided by a rule.
- `block-gap <= 64px`: every gutter is 32px; 64px above the footer is the largest gap.
- `content-width <= 1200px`: everything stays in the centred column.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen. A new piece of the overview is a new tile: start from `.ds-panel` (fill, `--radius-page` and `--shadow-panel` together), give it one subject, and place it in a `.ds-bento` row whose tiles add up to the full width; never leave a tile alone on a row beside empty space, and never put a raised tile inside a tile. Inside a tile, a reading is a `.ds-ring`, a `.ds-progress` or a sunk chart well; something to fill in starts from `.ds-form__input`; something to press starts from `.ds-button--secondary`. Keep every fill equal to the surface behind it, use each shadow token alone as the whole `box-shadow` value (a token may be `none`), and write outlines as `var(--border-width) var(--border-style)` with a border colour token. Use tokens only; for a size with no token use `calc()` over `--space-*` or `--size-*`. Keep each text token on the fill it belongs to and the accent to rings, bars and the one current item.
