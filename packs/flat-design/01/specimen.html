Questions about pricing

The four we are asked most.

Can I change plan later?

Yes, in either direction and at any time. The new price starts with the next invoice we send you.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing, unless you choose a plan. Your invoices stay readable and can be exported for a year.

Do you take a share of payments?

No. Card processors charge their own fee; chronoskin adds nothing on top of the monthly price.

Is there a contract?

There is not. Plans are paid month by month and stop on the day you cancel.
