Borrowing, the short version

Everything on the shelves can go home with you for three weeks.

A card lets you take up to twelve titles at a time from any branch and return them to any other.

Holds and renewals

Asked at the desk

How long can I keep a title?

Three weeks. You can renew it twice from the loans page, unless another reader is waiting.

Can I return it to another branch?

Yes, to any of the six, or to the box at Central, which is open all night.

What does a hold cost?

Nothing. It waits six days at the desk of the branch you choose.
