Sending guide

How chronoskin delivers a letter and what the figures mean.

Write a campaign

What happens when you press send

A letter leaves in batches of two hundred, so a large list takes a few minutes.

Each reader gets their own copy. chronoskin notes when it is delivered, opened and clicked, and nothing else.

What the figures count

  • Delivered: the reader's mail server accepted it
  • Opened: the pictures in the letter were loaded
  • Clicked: at least one link was followed

Adding a reader's name

Write {first_name} anywhere in the subject or the message and it is replaced for each reader.

Why is a letter counted as bounced?

The reader's mail server refused it: the address no longer exists or the mailbox is full. After three bounces the address is set aside.

Can I stop a letter after pressing send?

For as long as it is scheduled, yes. Once the first batch has left, the rest follows.

What does a reader see when they unsubscribe?

One page with one button. They are off the list at once and you see them in the report.

How many readers can a list have?

As many as the plan allows. The bar under your name in the rail shows how much of it is used.
