The speaker

TO Tomas OkaforType designer

He draws type for timetables, receipts and other places where figures matter more than words.

All speakers

What the talk is about

Most of what people read on a screen is numbers: prices, times, counts. Hardly any typeface is drawn with them first.

The talk walks through one family of figures from sketch to release and shows where lining, old-style and tabular forms each earn their place in an interface.

What you will take home

  • When a column of figures needs tabular forms and when it does not
  • How to check a face for figures before you license it
  • A short list of open families that get it right

For people who write CSS

One declaration does most of the work: font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums. The second half of the talk is about the cases it misses.
