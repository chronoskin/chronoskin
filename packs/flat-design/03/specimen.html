Taught in a room, not a feed

Every course meets live, with a tutor who can see what your hands are doing.

Classes are capped at twelve. You get the notes the same evening and a recording for thirty days, so a missed week is not a lost one.

What a course includes

  • Four to six sessions of ninety minutes
  • Materials, or a list of what to bring
  • One piece of finished work to take home

For course organisers

Timetables are published as a plain feed. Request /feeds/term.ics to add every session to a shared calendar.

What if I miss a session?

The notes reach you the same evening and the recording stays up for thirty days. Tutors keep ten minutes before the next class for catching up.

Do I need my own tools?

No. Tools and materials are on the bench when you arrive, unless the course page lists something to bring.

Can I move to another term?

Up to a week before the first session, once, at no cost.

Labels on the timetable

Every course carries one or two of these, so you can see at a glance whether there is room.
