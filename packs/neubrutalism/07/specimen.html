Help for makers

How a title gets from your stapler onto a shelf.

Open a shelf

Selling on chronoskin

Anyone who makes printed things in small runs can open a shelf. You set the price, we list it, and 85% of every sale goes straight to you.

Before you list

Photograph the cover flat and in daylight. Give each title a short code such as NBA-04 so that orders are easy to pick.

  • Runs of 20 to 500 copies
  • Printed, stapled, sewn or folded by you or a local press
  • Nothing that is only a download

When an order comes in

You get a note with the address and any message. Pack it within two working days and mark it sent. See how postage is worked out.

Asked most

When do I get paid?

Every Monday, for everything marked sent the week before.

Can I sell seconds?

Yes, marked as seconds and priced lower.

Who pays for postage?

The reader does, unless the order is over 40.

What if a parcel goes missing?

We refund the reader and you keep your share.
