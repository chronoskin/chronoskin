Your first invoice

An invoice is a client, a list of lines and a due date. Everything else is optional, and most of it can wait until somebody has paid.

What a good invoice has

Keep each line to what the client received. Set the term in the field named due_in_days and leave the rest at its default.

  • One line per piece of work, in plain words
  • A due date that is a real date, not "on receipt"
  • Bank details where nobody has to look for them

After it is paid

Mark it as paid, send a one-line thank you, and reuse the invoice as next month's template.
