Open show settings

Moving a feed

A feed is the address podcast apps ask for new episodes. Move it carefully and no listener notices; move it carelessly and they all have to follow again.

Before you change the address

Set the old address to forward for at least a month. In the feed file this is the line new-feed-url, and chronoskin writes it for you.

  • Keep the episode numbers exactly as they were
  • Publish nothing on the day you move
  • Check one app on a phone, not only the website

If plays drop afterwards

Give it a week. Apps fetch on their own schedule, and a quiet weekend is not a broken feed.
