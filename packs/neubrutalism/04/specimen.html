Your first timetable

A timetable is a list of classes, each with a room, a teacher and a number of places. Everything else can wait until somebody has booked.

What a class needs

Give it a name people would say out loud. Set the size in the field named places and leave the rest as it is.

  • A start time and a length, not a vague "evening"
  • A size you can teach well, not the size of the room
  • One line on what to bring

After the first week

Look at who came, thank the regulars, and copy the week forward. See copying a week for the short way.
