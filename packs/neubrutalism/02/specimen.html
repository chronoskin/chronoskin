Choosing a box

A box is the right size when the thing inside cannot move and you did not pay to post air. Most shops need three sizes and use one of them nine times in ten.

Measure the product, not the shelf

Add two fingers of padding on every side, then round up to the nearest stock size. Store the result in box_code so the rate table can use it.

  • Heavy things go in small boxes
  • Fragile things get a box inside a box
  • Flat things travel in card envelopes, never in bags

When to reuse a box

Reuse any box that still stands square. Cover every old label before the new one goes on.

Three stock sizes

Small is 20 by 15 by 10, medium 30 by 20 by 15, large 40 by 30 by 25. Sizes are in centimetres.
