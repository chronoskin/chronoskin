Mend your bike night

Saturday 18 October, 18:30, at the old dairy.

Back to October Book a place
Doors at 18:15.

The yard gate is the green one on Mill Lane, not the front door.

What happens

Bring a bike with something wrong with it. Priya and two volunteers show you how to fix it yourself: punctures, brakes that squeal, gears that skip. Tools and tea are on the bench.

Children are welcome with an adult. Parts are sold at cost from the box by the door.

Hosting on chronoskin

Anyone who lives or works between the canal and the park can pin an event. It does not have to be big: six people around a table counts.

Before you pin it

Check the room is free and pick a day. Give the event a short tag such as bike-night so that regulars can follow it.

  • Say what it costs, even when the answer is nothing
  • Say whether children and dogs can come
  • Say where the door is

On the night

Open the list on your phone and tick people in. Unclaimed places go to the waiting list after fifteen minutes. See how a booking looks to a guest.

Asked a lot

Does it cost anything to post?

No. The board is paid for by the street fund.

Can I charge for tickets?

Yes, at the door. We do not take payments.

What if I have to cancel?

Mark it cancelled and everyone booked gets a message.

Who reads my event over?

One of nine volunteer neighbours, in turn.

Still stuck?

Come to the street plan meeting on Monday and ask in person.
