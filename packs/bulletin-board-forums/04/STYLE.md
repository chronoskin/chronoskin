# Bulletin board forum, portal and thread view, 2002 to 2007

## Summary

This is the forum of a gaming or hobby community between about 2003 and 2007, when the board software grew a portal front page: a link bar across the very top, a banner with a search box, and three columns in which a menu, a log-in box and a poll stand beside the site news. Behind the front page the forums are plain full-width sheets, and a thread is drawn the way most engines of the time drew it, each post a ruled block with the poster's name, rank, avatar and counts in a panel on the left and the message on the right. The skin is the dark one such communities preferred: charcoal cells on a near-black sheet over a deep blue-green page, ivory Verdana, khaki headings and buttons, a glossy petrol bar, square corners and no motion.

## Layout

- The sheet `.ds-page__frame` is `--size-page` (92%) wide and centred, with a 1px `--color-border-strong` outline; the page colour shows as a band on both sides. `.ds-page` has `--space-5` (12px) above and below. Designed at 1024px. Never let the sheet touch the viewport edge and never narrow it below 90%.
- Order from the top, all inside the sheet: the link bar `.ds-nav__menu` (primary bar fill, items divided by 1px rules), the banner `.ds-nav__head` on the canvas (brand and tagline on the left, the search form on the right, at least `--size-banner`, 62px, tall), the visitor strip `.ds-nav__sub` (secondary bar), then `.ds-page__body`, the footer band, and the small print `.ds-page__legal` on the canvas. Nothing with text sits on the page colour.
- The front page is `.ds-portal`, three columns: `--size-side` (172px), fluid, `--size-side`, `--space-5` apart, top-aligned. Left: `.ds-sidebar` with the menu, log-in and who-is-online blocks. Centre `.ds-portal__main`: the welcome block `.ds-hero`, then the news `.ds-list`. Right `.ds-portal__side`: `.ds-panel` blocks (poll, top posters, calendar). An optional `.ds-notice` runs across the full width above the columns. Under the columns, at full width: the latest-topics `.ds-table`, the `.ds-grid` of forum sections and the one-line `.ds-stat` strip. The three columns should end near each other: add or drop a side block or a news item instead of leaving one column short.
- Inner pages (a forum, a thread, the reply form) keep the link bar, banner, visitor strip and footer and drop the three columns and the hero: the view is one full-width column. From the top: `.ds-breadcrumb` (an open line with a home icon on a faint rule), `.ds-page-header` (a bordered `--color-surface-alt` box: title and one meta line on the left, one or two buttons on the right), `.ds-tabs` for the filters of a forum or the views of a thread, a `.ds-toolbar` line with tool links on the left and `.ds-pagination` on the right, then the working block. For a forum that is the topic `.ds-table` with `.ds-table__group` rows, closed by a `.ds-toolbar` holding the `.ds-legend` and the buttons. For a thread it is `.ds-thread`: a two-cell head (`Author`, `Message`) and one `.ds-post` per message, `--space-3` apart, a confirmation `.ds-dialog` directly under the post it concerns, then a `.ds-toolbar` with the topic buttons and the page numbers again. Tables and the reply `.ds-form` follow at full width; an error `.ds-notice` sits between the page header and the form.
- Inner pages with a menu of their own (the control panel, help) keep the breadcrumb and page header at full width and then use `.ds-portal ds-portal--two`: the `.ds-sidebar` menu in the `--size-side` column and the working blocks in `.ds-portal__main` beside it. There is no right column on these pages.
- Views. The specimen is a six-screen example site; the link bar has one item per view and marks the current one. Each view is a `.ds-view` section whose `.ds-view__body` stacks its blocks `--space-5` apart. `portal` (home) is the portal front page: notice, the three columns with menu, account box and who is online, the welcome block and site news, poll and side panels, then the latest topics, the forum sections and the statistics strip. `board` is one forum: filter tabs, page numbers, the topic table with pinned and ordinary groups, the icon legend and the new-topic button. `topic` is a thread: three posts, the delete confirmation that a post's Delete tool opens, the topic buttons and the attachment list. `post` is the reply form as it came back with an attachment error, over a review of the latest posts. `panel` is the member's control panel: the menu column, an empty messenger inbox and the tracked topics. `help` is the help page: the list of help topics and the staff box beside the house rules as long text and a line of related links.
- A post is a grid of `--size-poster` (160px) plus the rest, three rows with 1px gaps: name cell and date strip, poster panel and message, status cell and tool strip. The avatar is `--size-avatar` (64px) square.
- Table columns: status `--size-status` (28px), title (fluid), each count `--size-count` (62px), last post `--size-last` (160px). Rows are at least `--size-row` (32px); bars and title strips `--size-bar` (24px).
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 1px, `--space-2` 3px, `--space-3` 5px, `--space-4` 8px, `--space-5` 12px, `--space-6` 18px.
  - `--space-1`: the gap between cells inside every bordered block, vertical padding of buttons and inputs.
  - `--space-2`: vertical padding of bars, strips and table cells; gap between tabs and between post tools.
  - `--space-3`: gap between posts, between inline items, padding of small panel bodies.
  - `--space-4`: horizontal padding of cells and strips, padding of post panels and the page header, gap between buttons.
  - `--space-5`: page and body padding and the gap between neighbouring blocks and columns. This is the only block gap.
  - `--space-6`: list indent, padding of the empty state.
- Form rows are a `--size-label` (22%) label cell and a field cell; text inputs are `--size-field` (260px) unless `--wide`; the banner search input is `--size-search` (150px). A dialog is `--size-dialog` (52%) wide, centred in the flow. Poll and rank meters are `--size-meter` (9px) high.

## Typography and colour roles

- `--font-body` and `--font-heading` are the same Verdana stack; `--font-ui` puts Tahoma first for navigation, tabs, buttons and inputs; `--font-mono` (Courier New) is for inline code. All are system fonts, as in the references.
- Sizes: `--text-small` 10px for meta, counts captions, strips, side blocks and table cells; `--text-base`, `--text-ui`, `--text-h2` and `--text-h3` 11px for running text, post bodies, controls and block titles; `--text-large` and `--text-h1` 13px bold for news titles, poster names, the thread title and the hero title; `--text-display` 18px for the brand name only. Nothing is larger than 18px.
- `--line-body` is 1.4 in running text; headings and bars use `normal`. Headings are bold, never uppercase, no tracking.
- Links carry no underline at rest (`--link-decoration` and `--link-decoration-quiet` are `none`) and gain one on hover. `--color-link` (pale blue) is for links in text and meta; `--color-link-quiet` (ivory, always bold where it is a title) for topic, news and file titles, poster names, breadcrumb and menu links; hover is `--color-link-hover`.
- Surfaces: `--color-page` outside the sheet, `--color-canvas` the sheet and, through `--fill-panel`, the dark line in the 1px gaps. `--color-surface` is the main cell (message, news item, table cell), `--color-surface-alt` the poster panel, count and status cells, form labels, the page header and tool strips, `--color-surface-strong` the title strips of side blocks and panels, group rows of a table, the name and date row of a post and the form action row. Text on the first two is `--color-text`; on the strong surface it is `--color-heading` or `--color-heading-alt`.
- Bars: `--fill-bar` (the petrol bar with a lighter top and darker foot) with `--color-bar-text` and `--shadow-text` for the link bar, table heads, the thread head, the hero, news, grid, form, dialog and empty-state titles and the current tab. `--fill-bar-alt` with `--color-bar-alt-text` for the visitor strip, the current link-bar item and quiet badges. `--fill-inverse` with `--color-inverse-text` for the footer band.
- Borders: every block has a 1px `--color-border` outline; `--color-border-strong` outlines the sheet, buttons, the dialog and the rule under the tabs; `--color-border-muted` rules rows inside a box and under the breadcrumb.
- Markers: `--color-accent` for new-post icons, rank pips, poll bars, menu bullets, the notice edge and the badge, with `--color-accent-text` on it; `--color-accent-alt` for an emphasised word; `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface` for errors; Status badges are a `color-mix()` of 30% of the status colour (`--color-success`, `--color-warning`, `--color-danger`) over `--color-surface`, with `--color-text` and a 1px outline in the status colour, so they read under every palette. The destructive button is the error pair: `--color-danger` text on `--color-danger-surface` with a `--color-danger` outline.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` are four close charcoal tones for grid cells; the style has no bright block colours.
- One-off values outside the tokens: the footer separator is drawn at 0.6 opacity; icon and tile sizes are `calc()` multiples of `--size-icon` and `--text-display`.

## Components

- `ds-progress`: a quota or poll bar, a native `<progress>` with a label above (private-message quota).
- `ds-accordion`: a collapsible `<details>` entry for a forum jump or an FAQ answer.
- `ds-avatar`: the square picture of a member, placed under or beside the name.
- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`. Everything goes in `.ds-page__frame`; blocks go in `.ds-page__body`; `.ds-page__legal` is the small print at the foot of the sheet. `.ds-portal` with `.ds-portal__main` and `.ds-portal__side` makes the three columns; `.ds-portal--two` keeps only the side column and the main one, for an inner page with a menu. `.ds-toolbar` is a line with tools or buttons left and pagination right. `.ds-legend` is the key to the topic icons at the foot of a topic list: `.ds-legend__item` entries in muted text, each led by a `.ds-legend__icon` in the accent colour (`is-read` for the dimmed one).
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the specimen's example site; only the one named in the address shows, the `.ds-view--home` one when none is named. `.ds-view__body` inside it is the column of blocks. An installing project with real pages does not need these two classes. `.ds-icon` sizes an inline SVG icon, `.ds-sprite` hides the symbol sheet, `.ds-block` puts a span on its own line.
- `.ds-nav`: the site header. `.ds-nav__menu` is the link bar of `.ds-nav__item` cells holding `.ds-nav__link`; the current one takes `is-current` (the secondary bar fill with its text colour); in the specimen the same dress is applied by one `:has()` rule per view. `.ds-nav__head` is the banner with `.ds-nav__brand` (the `.ds-brand` with a `.ds-nav__tagline` under it) and `.ds-nav__search` (label, a `.ds-form__input--search` and a secondary button). `.ds-nav__sub` is the visitor strip with `.ds-nav__sublink` links.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name at the left of the banner, linking to the portal; one per page. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small square tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button` over `--color-button`, a `--border-width` outline in `--color-border-strong`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`) with the mark drawn in `--color-button-text`; it is 1.5 times `--text-display`, so it follows the type set. `.ds-brand__name` is the name in `--font-heading` at `--text-display` and `--weight-display`, with `--display-tracking` and `--heading-transform`, coloured `--color-heading` for the canvas banner it sits on. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo: replace both, keep the tile.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: an open line: `.ds-breadcrumb__icon`, bold `.ds-breadcrumb__link` items, `.ds-breadcrumb__sep`, `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-hero`: the welcome block at the head of the centre column, a bordered box like every other: `.ds-hero__title` bar, `.ds-hero__body` with a `.ds-hero__mark` icon, the `.ds-hero__lead` and the `.ds-hero__action` line (one primary button and a text link), and a `.ds-hero__foot` strip of totals. Never a full-width banner.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page, used instead of `.ds-hero`. `.ds-page-header__title` (13px bold heading colour), `.ds-page-header__desc` (one 10px line), `.ds-page-header__action` (primary button, optionally a secondary one).
- `.ds-prose`: long text such as rules: h1, h2, h3, p, ul, strong, code, hr, in a bordered `--color-surface` box.
- `.ds-link`: every link in text. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` for names and tools, `.ds-link--strong` for bold.
- `.ds-button`: the primary action. `.ds-button--secondary` for the lesser action, `.ds-button--danger` for a destructive one (delete, ban), error text on the error surface with an error outline, once per confirmation beside a secondary button. States `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`, `is-disabled` or `disabled`. Group with `.ds-buttons`.
- `.ds-form`: a bordered block of rows. `.ds-form__title` bar, `.ds-form__row` with `.ds-form__label` (plus `.ds-form__hint`) and `.ds-form__field`; controls take `.ds-form__input` (`--wide`, `--auto`, `--search`, `is-invalid`, `is-disabled`), errors `.ds-form__error`, checkboxes `.ds-form__check` and `.ds-form__checkbox`, buttons in `.ds-form__actions`.
- `.ds-table`: topic lists, file lists and any data. `th` is the primary bar (`.ds-table__head--left` for text columns); cells `.ds-table__status` (`is-read`), `.ds-table__num`, `.ds-table__last`; `.ds-table__group` is a strong row between groups; inside the main cell `.ds-table__title` and `.ds-table__meta`.
- `.ds-thread`: the stack of posts, opened by `.ds-thread__head` with two `.ds-thread__label` cells. `.ds-post` is one message: `.ds-post__name` (with the `.ds-post__author` link) and `.ds-post__meta` in the top row; `.ds-post__panel` (`.ds-post__rank`, `.ds-post__pips` of `.ds-post__pip` squares, `is-off` for an empty one, `.ds-post__avatar`, `.ds-post__facts` of `.ds-post__fact`) beside `.ds-post__body` (`.ds-post__text` paragraphs, an optional `.ds-quote`, `.ds-post__sig` pinned to the foot); `.ds-post__status` beside `.ds-post__tools`, whose `.ds-post__toolset` groups hold small `.ds-post__tool` buttons. The panel is always on the left.
- `.ds-quote`: a quoted message inside a post: `.ds-quote__label` over a bordered `.ds-quote__body`.
- `.ds-list`: the news feed. `.ds-list__head` bar, then `.ds-list__item` (`is-alt` for the alternate one) with `.ds-list__title`, `.ds-list__meta`, `.ds-list__excerpt` and a right-aligned `.ds-list__aside` of comment and read-more links.
- `.ds-panel`: a titled side box. `.ds-panel__title` (`--bar` for the primary bar), `.ds-panel__body` (`--alt`), `.ds-panel__line` paragraphs, or `.ds-panel__rows` of `.ds-panel__row` (name left, figure right). `.ds-poll` inside a body shows results: `.ds-poll__question`, `.ds-poll__option` with a `.ds-poll__track` and a `.ds-poll__fill` (`--46`, `--31`, `--17`, `--6` set the width).
- `.ds-sidebar`: the left column of `.ds-sidebar__block` boxes: `.ds-sidebar__title`, then a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` links each led by a `.ds-sidebar__bullet` (`is-current` for the page you are on), or a `.ds-sidebar__body` for a small form or text with `.ds-sidebar__label`, and an optional `.ds-sidebar__foot`.
- `.ds-grid`: four equal cells with 1px gaps in one bordered block under a `.ds-grid__title` bar; `.ds-grid__cell` (`--2`, `--3`, `--4`) holds a `.ds-grid__icon`, a `.ds-grid__name` and `.ds-grid__meta` lines.
- `.ds-stat`: `.ds-stat` is the row, one bordered strip: a `.ds-stat__title` cell at the left, then equal `.ds-stat__item` cells, each a bold `.ds-stat__value` beside its `.ds-stat__label` on one line. Never separate cards.
- `.ds-tabs`: square `.ds-tabs__tab` items standing on a 1px strong rule; `is-current` takes the primary bar fill.
- `.ds-badge`: a small square label. `.ds-badge--quiet` in the secondary bar colours; The status variants `.ds-badge--success` (solved, online, approved), `.ds-badge--warning` (pending, awaiting) and `.ds-badge--danger` (locked, banned, overdue) are a pale tint of the status colour with body text and a 1px outline in that colour. `.ds-new` is bold accent-alt text for a remark.
- `.ds-notice`: one line with a 2px accent edge on the left. `.ds-notice__title` is the bold lead-in; `.ds-notice--error` is the error box with a danger outline.
- `.ds-pagination`: one bordered strip of cells: `.ds-pagination__label`, then `.ds-pagination__link` cells; `is-current` for the current page.
- `.ds-dialog`: a confirmation box in the page flow with a strong outline: `.ds-dialog__title` bar, `.ds-dialog__body` with `.ds-dialog__text` and `.ds-dialog__actions`; `.ds-dialog__backdrop` is a flat band behind it. It never floats over the page.
- `.ds-empty`: a bordered block with one centred message (`.ds-empty__body`, `.ds-empty__text`) and one button, under an optional `.ds-empty__title` bar (primary bar fill) that names the list that is empty.
- `.ds-footer`: the inverse band closing the sheet: a `.ds-footer__row` of `.ds-footer__link` items with `.ds-footer__sep` between them on the left, a `.ds-footer__note` on the right.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: every box is square in all references.
- `box-shadow = none`: no period reference has a box shadow.
- `transition = none`: states switch at once.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves.
- `font-size <= 18px`: 18px is the largest text measured, and only the brand name uses it.
- `font-size >= 10px`: 10px is the smallest size this pack uses.
- `font-weight <= 700`: bold is the heaviest weight.
- `font-families <= 3`: Verdana, Tahoma for controls, a monospace for code.
- `border-width <= 2px`: borders are 1px; 2px only on the notice edge.
- `letter-spacing <= 0px`: no tracking anywhere.
- `uppercase-text <= 0%`: nothing is transformed to uppercase.
- `underlined-links <= 20%`: links have no underline until hovered; between 0% and 8% in the dark and neutral references.
- `gradient-fills <= 15%`: the gloss is on primary bars and the primary button only.
- `row-gap <= 1px`: rows touch or are separated by a 1px line.
- `block-gap <= 20px`: neighbouring blocks are 12px apart.
- `content-width >= 80%`: the sheet takes 92% of the viewport; never a narrow column.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen: a new side box from `.ds-sidebar__block` or `.ds-panel`, a new feed from `.ds-list`, a new kind of post from `.ds-post`, a new data view from `.ds-table`. Build it as a 1px `--color-border` outline on `--fill-panel`, with cells in the three surface colours separated by `--space-1`, and a title in `--fill-bar` or `--color-surface-strong`. Put heading-colour text on the strong surface, body text on the other two, and never place text on the page colour. Use tokens only: no raw colours or lengths, no new radius, shadow or transition, no font size outside the type tokens, and `--space-5` as the gap to the next block.
