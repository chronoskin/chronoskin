Quote (brindle, 19 Nov 2005, 05:48 PM)
I lose the ford by turn nine every time. Two towers are not enough and a third leaves me with no economy.

Towers are the wrong answer there. The ford is three tiles wide, so a rush has to come through in single file, and one line of pikes on your own bank holds it for the price of a single tower.

Put the pikes one tile back from the water, not on it. Cavalry that has crossed loses its charge bonus, and your archers on the hill get two free volleys while it reforms.

"Never fight uphill, never fight downstream." Map pack 14, slot 3.

House rules and posting guide

Everyone who posts here agrees to the rules below. They are short. Read them once and you will rarely meet a moderator.

Before you post

Search first; most openings have a long topic already. Name the map and the number of players in the title, and say which patch you are playing.

  • One topic per question, in one forum only.
  • Attach replays as files, not as links to other sites.
  • Wrap build orders in [code] tags so the columns stay aligned.

Signatures

Signatures are limited to three lines of text and no images.
