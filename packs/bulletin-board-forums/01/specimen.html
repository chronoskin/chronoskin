Bench supply hums at low load

Started by quietfuse on 14 Mar 2005. 31 replies, 1,204 views. New

Post reply
14 Mar 2005, 06:02 PMPost 1 of 32
quietfuse Regular Posts: 412 Joined: Aug 2003

I have a twin 0 to 30 V bench supply from the early seventies on the bench. Above about half an amp it is quiet. Below that the transformer hums loudly enough to hear across the room, and there is 40 mV of ripple on the output.

Rectifier diodes test fine. The reservoir capacitor is original and reads 3,900 uF against a marked 4,700. Is that enough to explain it, or should I be looking at the regulator?

One fault at a time.

m.teague Moderator Posts: 6,105 Joined: Feb 2002
quietfuse wrote: The reservoir capacitor is original and reads 3,900 uF against a marked 4,700.

Capacitance is only half of it. Measure the series resistance as well; a thirty year old electrolytic can read nearly full value and still be useless. I would replace it before touching anything else.

Hum that gets worse at light load usually points at the regulator hunting, not the transformer. Check the small compensation capacitor across the error amplifier while you are in there.

Put the model number in the title, please.

Same supply here, same fault. On mine it was the minimum load resistor across the output, which had gone open. Without it the series pass transistor cannot regulate down at low current and the loop sings at mains frequency.

It is a 2 W wirewound part under the rear terminal strip. Discharge the reservoir before you reach in.

Board rules and posting guide

Everyone who posts here agrees to the rules below. They are short. Read them once and you will rarely need a moderator.

Before you post

Search first; most common faults have a long topic already. Put the make and model in the title, and say what you have measured so far. A good first post saves three replies asking for the basics.

  • One topic per fault, in one board only.
  • Attach schematics as links, not as inline scans.
  • Mains work: say whether the set is isolated before you ask for measurements.
  • Wrap part numbers in [code] tags so they stay readable.

Signatures

Signatures are limited to three lines of text and no images. Set yours in the control panel.

Moderation

Moderators may edit a title to add a model number, move a topic to the right board, or lock one that has run its course. Disagree by private message, not in the topic.
