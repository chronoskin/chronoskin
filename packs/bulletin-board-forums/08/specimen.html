Autumn meetup

Friday 18 and Saturday 19 November 2005. Organised by tessa. You are signed up.

Two days of skins and talk

The autumn meetup is the one time of year the board meets in a room. Friday is for talks, Saturday for the workshop and the dinner. Thirty places, of which four are left.

Programme

  • Friday 14:00: doors, then three short talks on template edits.
  • Saturday 10:00: skin workshop. Bring a board you can change.
  • Saturday 19:00: dinner, paid on the evening.

What to bring

A laptop with a local copy of your board. The workshop files are in meetup/workshop.zip in the file archive.

Related: all meetups, last year's notes (visited), travel thread (hover), room sharing (active).
