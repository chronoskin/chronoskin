Round 8: a win on the last flag

Posted by mirelle on 12 Mar 2006 at 23:40. 412 views, 8 comments.

Add a comment

chronoskin 2, Grey Harbour 1

Nobody expected an easy night and nobody got one. Grey Harbour had not lost on Twin keeps all season, and for eighteen minutes it looked as if they would not start now.

Map one: Twin keeps

We went a flag down inside three minutes. Halvard moved to the upper gallery, the base held, and mirelle levelled with a run through the water tunnel. Overtime went our way after a dropped flag on their ramp.

Map two: Saltworks

Saltworks is a runner's map and theirs is quicker than ours. They took it 3 to 1. The decider was played straight after on the home map.

What worked

  • Two on defence at all times, even when chasing a goal.
  • Calling the flag carrier's route, not only the position.
  • Binding say_team drop calls to one key.

The decider

One all with ten seconds left, and a capture with four on the clock. The demo is in the downloads section for anyone who does not believe it.
