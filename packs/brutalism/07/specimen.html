Help and rules

How the board works, what may go on it, and what the marks beside a notice mean.

Post a notice

The board is a wall with pins in it

Anyone may read it. Anyone with an address in town may pin a notice to it. The board keeps no pictures on its front page, sells nothing itself and shows no advertising other than the notices, which are all advertising.

What may go up

Things you own, rooms you may let, work you can offer or need done, what you have lost and what you have found. Write the headline the way you would say it over a fence.

What may not

  • Animals for sale, and anything that needs a licence to sell.
  • The same notice in two sections.
  • A price with words in it. The price box takes 180 and not 180 ono.

A notice that breaks a rule comes down and its owner is told which one.

Asked most weeks

Why did my notice come down?

Thirty days passed, or somebody reported it. You were sent a line saying which. A notice that timed out can be pinned up once more from "my notices".

Can I change the price after pinning?

Yes, downwards, as often as you like. The notice is marked "price dropped" for two days.

Who sees my address?

Nobody. Replies are passed on by the board until you answer one yourself.

How does a notice get pinned at the top?

Four are picked by hand each Monday. It cannot be bought.
