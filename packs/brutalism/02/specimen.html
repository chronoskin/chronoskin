Ask: which glue survives a dishwasher?

54 points, sent by marit 7 hours ago, tagged question. 86 comments.

Send a link

The handle of a bread knife, beech, split along the tang. Epoxy lasted a month. The knife goes in the machine every day and nobody in this house is going to start washing it by hand.

What has held for you, for years and not weeks?

Rules

How the front page is sorted

Written by the volunteers, last changed 2017-01-09.

Every link starts with one point from the person who sent it. Readers add points; time takes them away. The whole rule is short enough to read in the source of this page, and nothing else decides the order.

What counts

  • One point for each reader who found the link useful.
  • The age of the link in hours, plus two.
  • Nothing else: no names, no tags, no payment.

The arithmetic

The score is points / (hours + 2) ^ 1.6, worked out again every five minutes. A link held by a volunteer keeps its points but is left out of the list.

Marks you will see

Unread replies 12, a tag joinery, a fresh link new, and the state of a link: listed held removed

What gets a link removed

A shop's own offers. A page about a page. A title rewritten to argue. The same address twice in thirty days.

Why was my link held?

A volunteer reads the first link of every new member, and any link from an address nobody has sent before. It takes a day at most.

Can I send my own page?

Once, and say that it is yours. After that let somebody else find it.

Who are the volunteers?

Four readers who have been here since 2014. They remove links; they do not rank them.
