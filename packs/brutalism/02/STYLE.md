# Web brutalism, link list

## Summary

The reader-sorted link list of web brutalism, as kept between 2014 and 2019 by small communities that wanted a front page to be nothing but titles: one filled line of navigation, then numbered two-line rows in small grey Helvetica in a column that keeps a margin of empty window at both sides, with one pale blue line of navigation, dark blue links and a red word here and there. Nothing is rounded, shadowed, graded or animated, there are no pictures and no cards; structure comes from the numbers, from dotted hairlines and from grey 11px meta lines under blue titles. It was the look of link aggregators, bookmark logs and the comment sites built by programmers for themselves.

## Layout

- The page is a sheet: `.ds-page__frame` is `--size-page` (85% of the window) wide, centred, filled with `--color-canvas`, and starts `--space-3` (8px) below the top of the window. `--fill-page` is `--color-surface-alt` (#f7f7f7), so the white sheet stands on a faintly grey page and its margins left and right are seen. The sheet has no border and no maximum width.
- Order inside the sheet: `.ds-nav` (one filled line, closed by a hairline in `--color-border-strong`), `.ds-page__body` (padding `--space-4` 12px), `.ds-footer`. There is no wordmark above the line and no second navigation row: the brand (a small square mark and the bold site name) opens the line and the account links sit at its right end. The header, the sheet's padding and the footer are written once; the views change between them.
- The home page opens with `.ds-hero` across the sheet (headline and lead at the left, the two buttons stacked in a `--size-sidebar` column at the right, a hairline below). Under it `.ds-page__columns` holds `.ds-page__main` and, `--space-6` (40px) to its right, `.ds-sidebar` at `--size-sidebar` (220px). The sidebar has no rule and no fill; it is told from the list by its smaller type.
- The main column is the list: `.ds-list` rows numbered down the page and grouped under `.ds-list__day` lines, then `.ds-pagination` indented to the titles. Rows are `--space-3` (8px) apart and have no rules between them; only the day lines and block captions (`.ds-page__label`) carry a hairline.
- Blocks below the list are `.ds-page__section`, `--space-5` (20px) apart, each under a small bold `.ds-page__label`. Short blocks go side by side in `.ds-page__pair` (two equal columns, 40px apart) so the sheet is never half empty. `.ds-page__rule` is a hairline across the sheet between unrelated parts.
- Inner pages (one discussion, the sending form, a member's page, the tags, the rules) keep the same line of navigation and the same footer, in one column: no hero and no sidebar. `.ds-page__body` opens with the `.ds-breadcrumb` in 11px, then `.ds-page-header` (18px title, a grey line of description, one button at the right, a hairline below), then the `.ds-stats` line when there is one, then `.ds-tabs` with the table or list in `.ds-tabs__body`. Everything else goes in a `.ds-page__pair`: the main thing (the comments, the form, the running text, a table) in the left half and the short blocks that belong to it (a dialog, a panel, a small list under a `.ds-page__label`) in the right half, so that the halves end near each other.
- Views. The specimen is one small site with six views, one showing at a time. `front` is the home page: hero, the numbered list by day with the sidebar, pagination. `thread` is one question and its comments, with the confirmation for deleting a comment and the box for adding one. `send` is the form for sending a link, under the two messages it can answer with, beside the boxed advice. `member` is a member's page: figures, tabs over the table of links sent, latest comments. `tags` is the four busiest tags as blocks, all tags as a table, and the empty state of a tag with no links. `rules` is running text with the marks, a boxed list and further links beside it. A view is `<section class="ds-view" id="...">` holding one `.ds-page__body`; links between views are plain `href="#id"`.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 2px is the vertical padding of the navigation line, buttons, inputs and tabs and the gap between a title and its meta line; `--space-2` 4px is the padding of tags, table cells and the gap between tabs; `--space-3` 8px is the page margin, the gap between rows and between buttons, and the side padding of the navigation line; `--space-4` 12px is the padding of the sheet, panels, notices and grid cells, the gap between paragraphs and between navigation links; `--space-5` 20px is the gap between blocks; `--space-6` 40px is the gap between columns.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-rank` 36px is the number column of the list, `--size-label` 96px the label column of a form, `--size-field` 380px a text input, `--size-num` 96px a numeric table column, `--size-dialog` 400px a dialog.
- Below 800px the sheet takes the whole window, the columns and pairs stack, the page header puts its button under the text, form labels sit above their fields, the grid is two columns, the figures of `.ds-stats` are set apart by space with no `|` (a wrapped line never opens with one) and table cells marked `.ds-table__extra` are left out.
- On a phone (800px and below) there is no menu button: `.ds-nav__list` stays a wrapping line of links with a `--space-1` by `--space-3` gap, and `.ds-nav__item--end`, the account `.ds-menu`, loses its push to the right and follows the links. The account drop-down opens as it does on a wide screen.

## Typography and colour roles

- White on white: `--color-page`, `--color-canvas` and `--color-surface` are #ffffff; `--color-surface-alt` #f7f7f7 is alternate table rows, `--color-surface-strong` #efefef the current tab and page number, tags and inline code.
- `--color-bar` #ddeeff with `--color-bar-text` #333333 is the pale blue line of navigation, table heads and dialog titles. `--color-bar-alt` (footer) is white with #555555 text. `--color-inverse` #1111aa with white text marks the current navigation item.
- Text is dark grey, not black: `--color-text` #444444, `--color-heading` #333333, `--color-text-muted` #808080 for meta lines, counts and captions. `--color-heading-alt` #ff0000 is the red of sidebar and panel titles.
- `--color-link` and `--color-link-quiet` are #1111aa, so a list title, a sidebar link and a link in a meta line are all dark blue and always told from the grey text and counts beside them; `--color-link-visited` is #800080; hover and active are #ff0000.
- Rules are grey: `--color-border` #aaaaaa under day lines, captions and tabs and around panels; `--color-border-muted` #dddddd between table rows. `--color-border-strong` #8899cc is the blue-grey hairline under the navigation, hero and page header, above each grid column and the footer and around notices and dialogs.
- `--color-accent` #ddffaa with `--color-accent-text` #333333 is the count badge and, as `--color-button`, the pale green primary button; `--color-accent-alt` #ff0000 is only a mark (the asterisk of "new").
- Fills are the three pale tints and a grey: `--color-fill-1` #ddeeff, `--color-fill-2` #ddffaa, `--color-fill-3` #ffffaa, `--color-fill-4` #f7f7f7; `--color-notice` is #ffffaa. `--color-text` is readable on all of them. The secondary button, inputs and `--color-danger-surface` are white.
- Status: `--color-danger` #cc0000, `--color-success` #2a7a00, `--color-warning` #9a5b00. The references have a red but no green or amber text; these two are derived, dark enough to read on white.
- One family: `--font-body`, `--font-heading` and `--font-ui` are Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif; `--font-mono` is Monaco, "Courier New", monospace, for inline code only.
- Small and even: `--text-base` and `--text-ui` 13px with `--line-body` 1.4 is titles, running text, navigation and controls; `--text-small` 11px is meta lines, site names, tags, captions, the sidebar, the breadcrumb and the footer; `--text-large` 16px is the hero's lead and the figures of the stats line. Headings are bold and barely larger than the text: `--text-h1` 18px (page header, article title), `--text-h2` 16px, `--text-h3` 13px; `--text-display` 28px at regular weight (`--weight-display` 400) is the hero headline and the largest text on a page.
- Links in running text are underlined (`--link-decoration` underline) and must stay so, because other palettes of the era give them the colour of the text. List titles, navigation, tabs, sidebar, meta, breadcrumb and footer links are bare (`--link-decoration-quiet` none) and gain the underline on hover. Current navigation items, tabs and page numbers are `<span>`, not links.
- Nothing is uppercased or tracked; `--weight-ui` is 400, so only the brand name and headings are bold.
- Hairlines only: `--border-width` and `--border-width-strong` are both 1px and `--border-style` is dotted, so every rule and box is a 1px dotted line and emphasis comes from the colour of the strong border, not its weight. Buttons, inputs and the brand mark follow it too: their edge in `--color-input-border` is `--border-width` in `--border-style`, a dotted hairline here. Their fills, not their edges, tell them from the text around them.
- All radii are 0, all shadows `none`, every fill is its plain palette colour; `--focus-ring` is a 1px solid outline; `--transition` none.
- Each text token is used only on its own fill: `--color-bar-text` on `--fill-bar`, `--color-bar-alt-text` on `--fill-bar-alt`, `--color-inverse-text` on `--fill-inverse`, `--color-accent-text` on `--fill-accent`, `--color-button-text` on `--fill-button`, `--color-notice-text` on `--color-notice`, `--color-danger` on `--color-danger-surface` or the sheet, `--color-text` and the muted and heading colours on the canvas, the surfaces and the four fills. Keep to this so that other palettes work with the same structure.

## Components

- `.ds-page` on `<body>`: page colour, 8px top margin and base type. Each page is one `.ds-page__frame` (the sheet; `--last` drops the gap below it) holding the navigation, `.ds-page__body` and the footer. `.ds-page__columns` with `.ds-page__main` first is the home page's list and sidebar; `.ds-page__pair` sets two blocks side by side; `.ds-page__section` is a block (`--last` drops its bottom gap), `.ds-page__label` its small bold caption on a hairline, `.ds-page__rule` a hairline across the sheet.
- `.ds-nav`: the one filled line at the top of the sheet. The `.ds-brand` comes first; `.ds-nav__list` holds `.ds-nav__item` entries, each one `.ds-nav__link`, separated by 12px of space and nothing else. `.ds-nav__item--end` pushes itself and the items after it to the right end (account, log out). The current page is an inverted block: the stylesheet marks the link whose `href` names the view that is showing (one selector per view, and the home link when the address names none); on a page of its own, give the link `is-current`.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, a link to the front page, the first thing on the navigation line. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small plain ruled square (1.2 times `--text-ui`, about 16px, so the line stays one line tall), dressed like the push button: `--fill-button`, the same hairline edge in `--color-input-border`, the mark drawn in `--color-button-text` with square line caps; no radius and no shadow beyond what the tokens give. `.ds-brand__name` is bold 13px in `--color-bar-text`, because it stands on `--fill-bar`. The name `chronoskin` and the mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: an 11px line of `.ds-breadcrumb__link` items with `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` (`/`) between them, ending in `.ds-breadcrumb__current`, the first thing in the body of an inner page.
- `.ds-hero`: the introduction of the home page. `.ds-hero__copy` holds `.ds-hero__title` (28px, regular weight) and one `.ds-hero__lead` paragraph at 16px; `.ds-hero__actions` at the right stacks one primary and one secondary button, both as wide as the sidebar, over a grey `.ds-hero__note`. No fill, no box, no centring; a hairline closes it.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page, instead of the hero: `.ds-page-header__main` with `.ds-page-header__title` (18px) and one grey `.ds-page-header__text` line; `.ds-page-header__action` at the right holds one button. A hairline closes it.
- `.ds-stat`: one figure: a bold 16px `.ds-stat__value` followed on the same line by a grey `.ds-stat__label`. Three to five sit in a `.ds-stats` line, cut by `|` that the stylesheet draws. A line of text, never boxes.
- `.ds-prose`: running text, at most 84 characters wide: h1, h2, h3, paragraphs, square-bulleted and numbered lists, `code` in 11px monospace on `--color-surface-strong`, `blockquote` behind a hairline in grey. `.ds-prose__meta` is the grey 11px line under a title.
- `.ds-link`: text link with `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` is for meta and tool links; `.ds-link--strong` is bold. `.ds-links` lays links out in a wrapping row.
- `.ds-button`: a flat square push button, 13px, with a hairline edge in `--border-style`; a `<button>`, or an `<a>` when it only leads to another page. `.ds-button--secondary` is the same on the secondary fill. `.ds-button--danger` is the destructive action: `--color-danger` text and edge on `--color-danger-surface`. `disabled` or `is-disabled` greys it; `is-hover` underlines the label; `is-active` takes the strong border colour. Group with `.ds-buttons`.
- `.ds-form`: a form with no box: `.ds-form__title`, rows (`.ds-form__row`) of a grey lowercase `.ds-form__label` and a `.ds-form__field`. Controls take `.ds-form__input` (`--wide` fills the field, for textareas; `--auto` for selects); `is-invalid` plus `.ds-form__error` marks a validation error; `.ds-form__hint` is help text; `.ds-form__check` with a native `.ds-form__checkbox`; `.ds-form__actions` lines the buttons up with the fields (`--flush` when the form has no label column).
- `.ds-table`: a data table with no upright rules: bold `th` on the bar fill, rows on 1px `--color-border-muted` hairlines, `.ds-table__row--alt` for alternate rows, `.ds-table__num` for right-aligned numeric columns, `.ds-table__group` for a group row, `.ds-table__sub` for a grey 11px note inside a cell, `.ds-table__extra` on the cells of a column that a narrow window leaves out.
- `.ds-list`: the numbered link list, an `<ol>`. `.ds-list__day` is a bold 11px date line on a hairline that opens a group. Each `.ds-list__item` has a right-aligned grey `.ds-list__rank` and a `.ds-list__body`: first line the `.ds-list__title` link at body size and weight, a grey `.ds-list__site` in brackets and any tags; an optional `.ds-list__excerpt`; then the grey `.ds-list__meta` line with quiet links cut by `|`. No rules between rows. Comments use the same rows without a rank: the meta line first, the text under it, and `.ds-list__item--reply` to hang a reply one number column in.
- `.ds-panel`: a 1px hairline box with a bold `.ds-panel__title` as its first line (no title strip) and a `.ds-panel__body`. Use it for an aside next to a form; the list itself is never boxed.
- `.ds-grid`: four columns of `.ds-grid__cell`, 12px apart, each hanging from a hairline in `--color-border-strong` (`--2`, `--3`, `--4` select the other fills), with `.ds-grid__title`, `.ds-grid__text` and a grey `.ds-grid__meta` line. No side or bottom borders.
- `.ds-tabs`: a line of `.ds-tabs__item`, each holding a `.ds-tabs__tab` link in the quiet link colour, standing on a hairline. The current one is a `<span class="ds-tabs__tab is-current">`: bold text on a `--color-surface-strong` block. Content goes in `.ds-tabs__body`.
- `.ds-badge`: a count: bold 11px on the accent fill, square. `.ds-badge--alt` is a word with an asterisk in `--color-accent-alt` before it ("new"); `.ds-badge--tag` is a word in a hairline box on `--color-surface-strong`. Status labels are `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning` and `.ds-badge--danger`: a lowercase word in the status colour behind a small square that the stylesheet draws. No pill, no icon.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the narrow right column of the home page, in 11px. Each `.ds-sidebar__block` has a `.ds-sidebar__title` on a hairline and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__row` lines: a `.ds-sidebar__link` at the left and an optional `.ds-sidebar__count` at the right. `.ds-sidebar__text` is a grey paragraph; `.ds-sidebar__search` is an input with a button.
- `.ds-notice`: a sentence in a hairline box, starting with a bold `.ds-notice__title`. `.ds-notice--error` uses the danger colour for text and box. Stack several in `.ds-notices`.
- `.ds-pagination`: a line of `.ds-pagination__link` items, indented to the list titles: newer, numbers, older. The current page is a `<span>` with `is-current`: bold on a `--color-surface-strong` block. `is-disabled` is grey; `--first` drops the left padding of the first word.
- `.ds-dialog`: a hairline box in the strong border colour, `--size-dialog` wide, with a bold `.ds-dialog__title` on the bar fill, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__text` and right-aligned `.ds-dialog__actions` (cancel first, the action last). It sits centred on `.ds-dialog__backdrop`, a flat block of `--color-overlay` in the page flow. No shadow, no dimming of the page.
- `.ds-empty`: a grey centred `.ds-empty__text` sentence and one secondary button between two hairlines.
- `.ds-footer`: centred 11px lines under a hairline in the strong border colour: `.ds-footer__links` of `.ds-footer__item` entries cut by `|`, each a `.ds-footer__link`, then a `.ds-footer__text` sentence.
- `.ds-menu`: a `<details>` in the bar whose `<summary class="ds-menu__button">` keeps the browser's own triangle and drops `.ds-menu__list`, a ruled box of `.ds-menu__link` items on `--color-surface`; `.ds-menu--right` opens toward the left. Use it for the signed-in member's own links.
- `.ds-accordion`: questions and answers between rules. Each `<details class="ds-accordion__item">` has a `<summary class="ds-accordion__title">` that keeps the browser's own triangle and a `.ds-accordion__body`. Use it for a short list of questions, never for navigation.
- `.ds-tooltip`: a boxed `?` beside a form label (give it `tabindex="0"`) holding `.ds-tooltip__text`, an inverted hard-edged note shown under it on hover or focus, or with `is-open`. Use it for one sentence of help on a field.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: every box and control is square.
- `box-shadow = none`: nothing casts a shadow.
- `text-shadow = none`: text is flat.
- `gradient-fills <= 0%`: every fill is one flat colour.
- `letter-spacing <= 0px`: no text is tracked.
- `transition = none`: states change instantly.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves.
- `border-width <= 1px`: every line is a hairline.
- `font-size <= 28px`: the hero headline is 28px; titles are 18px.
- `font-size >= 11px`: meta text stops at 11px.
- `font-families <= 2`: Helvetica and a monospace.
- `line-height >= 1.3`: small text is set a little open, at 1.4.
- `line-height <= 1.5`: and no looser.
- `uppercase-text <= 0%`: nothing is uppercased.
- `underlined-links <= 40%`: only links inside running text are underlined.
- `content-width <= 86%`: the sheet leaves a margin of page colour at both sides.
- `row-gap <= 12px`: rows of the list sit close; there is no air between them.
- `block-gap <= 40px`: blocks are a hairline or 20px apart.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen: a row type copies `.ds-list__item` (a number or date at the left, a title line, a grey 11px meta line, 8px to the next row, no rule), a block gets a `.ds-page__label` caption on a hairline, a boxed aside copies `.ds-panel`, a line of choices copies `.ds-tabs` (bare words, the current one bold on `--color-surface-strong`), a status copies `.ds-badge--success`. Use only the tokens in `tokens.css` and the `--size-*` and `--space-*` tokens in `components.css`, and pair each text token with its own fill as listed above. Keep links in running text on `--link-decoration`, since their colour may equal the text colour. When in doubt do less: a grey line of text before a label, a hairline before a box, and no radius, shadow, gradient, icon or transition at all.
