2017-03-14 21:42

The rain gauge finally agrees with the bucket

After three weeks of drift the tipping gauge now reads within two percent of the plain bucket beside it. The fault was a spider, not the electronics, which is the kind of answer the calibration notes never prepare you for.

Total for the month so far is 61.4 mm, most of it in two nights. The marsh path was under water on the ninth.

What changed

Nothing clever. The funnel has a mesh over it and the reed switch was moved a little closer to the magnet.

  • Mesh over the funnel, 2 mm holes.
  • Switch moved 1 mm closer to the magnet.
  • Bucket emptied and compared every morning for a week.

Reading the log file

Each tip is one line with a timestamp. Run tips --since 2017-03-01 to count them, then multiply by 0.2 to get millimetres.

A wind vane made from a baking tray

2017-03-09 18:10, filed under instruments. Comments are open until 2017-04-08.

All entries

The shop vane lasted one winter. This one is a baking tray cut along the diagonal, a bicycle spoke and the bearing out of a dead hard disk, and it has now pointed the right way for eleven days running.

It turns in less wind than the old one did. That is the bearing, not the tray: the tray is only there because it was flat and already ruined.

If it looks home-made, that is because it is. The wind does not mind.

Readings from it go into the same table as everything else, eight compass points and no more, because that is as well as anyone can read a tray from a kitchen window.

About this log

Who keeps it, how it is made and what you may do with it.

Who writes this

Ines Varga keeps the instruments on a marsh she can see from her kitchen and answers mail slowly. 12 entries this month, filed under instruments tides. Comments on an entry are open for thirty days, closing in the last week and closed after that.

How it is made

Every entry is a text file. A script of forty lines turns the files into pages and copies them to a server that costs less than the batteries in the rain gauge. There is no database, so there is nothing to back up except the files, and they are in the archive for anyone to take.

  1. Write the entry in a text editor.
  2. Run make log.
  3. Read it once on the real page, then leave it alone.

What you may do with it

Copy what you like. The readings are facts and belong to nobody; the entries are mine, and you may quote them at any length if you say where they came from.

What it does not do

Does it count me?

No. It does not count you, follow you or remember you.

What is kept when I comment?

Your name beside the comment, and only if you tick the box. The address is read once and thrown away.

May I have the readings as a file?

Yes. Every month is a plain text file, and so is every year since 2014.
