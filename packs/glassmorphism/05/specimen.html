What counts as a room?

Anything members book as one place: a ceramics studio, a darkroom, the yard with the forge. Machines inside a room are free on every paid plan.

Is the pilot free?

Yes. Nobody pays until the spring opening, and pilot workshops keep their first room free afterwards.

Can we change plan later?

At any time. The new price counts from the next month.

Recurring bookings for classes

A class that meets every Tuesday no longer needs twelve bookings.

Pick a slot, choose Repeat weekly and an end date. The whole series is checked against opening hours and fair-use limits before it is saved, and each date can still be moved on its own. In an export a series carries the field series_id.

What happens on a clash

If one date of the series is already taken, the rest is booked and you are put on the waitlist for that date. The Workshop plan allows twenty series at a time.

Also in this release

  • The week view starts on the day your workshop opens.
  • Waitlist offers now say how long the slot is.
  • Statements round to the quarter hour, not the minute.
