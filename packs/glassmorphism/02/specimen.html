Guide

How the hub chooses which room gets heat, and when a schedule steps aside.

See the schedules

How the house decides

Each room asks for heat on its own, and the boiler answers the room that is furthest from its target.

A room has a target and a comfort band of half a degree around it. Inside the band the valve rests. The hub stores each change with the field set_by, so you can tell a schedule from a hand on the dial.

Schedules

A schedule is a list of times and targets for one room. Scenes sit on top: while a scene is on, its targets win.

When a schedule is skipped

  • A window in the room has been open for five minutes.
  • Someone changed the dial in the last two hours.
  • Frost guard is holding the whole house.

Why is a room colder than its target?

The boiler answers the room that is furthest from its target first. The others follow within the hour.

What does an open window do?

Heating in that room stops after five minutes and starts again when the window closes.

Can a scene run on a timer?

Yes. A scene can end by itself after one to twelve hours.
