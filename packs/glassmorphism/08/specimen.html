Setup guide

What each step does and what you can leave for later.

Back to setup

From sign-in to a working board

Setup has four screens. Only the first is required; the rest fill in sensible defaults if you skip them.

What is saved, and when

Each step is saved when you press Continue. Leaving half way keeps what you entered for seven days under your email address.

What you can skip

  • Profile: your name is taken from your email address.
  • Workspace: you get the blank template.
  • Team: you can invite people at any time.

Coming back to a step

The numbered steps at the top are links. To reopen one from elsewhere, add #profile or #team to the setup address.

Is there anything to pay?

No. Setup never asks for card details, and a workspace of up to ten people stays free.

Can two people own a workspace?

Yes. Give a second person the owner role in step 4.

How do I bring a spreadsheet?

Choose any template, then use Import on its first board.

What if I picked the wrong template?

Clear the sample rows and apply another from workspace settings.
