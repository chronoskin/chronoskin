Guide

How a reading gets from the gauge to this workspace, and when it raises an alert.

Set a threshold

How readings are collected

Each station measures on its own clock and sends what it has when the link is up.

A reading is a level, a flow and a temperature taken in the same second. Stations keep seven days of readings on board, so a short outage loses nothing. Each one is stored with the field taken_at, not the time it arrived.

Thresholds

A threshold belongs to one station and one measure. When a reading crosses it, the people on the list are told once, and again each hour if repeating is on. Thresholds are kept on the alerts page.

What counts as a crossing

  • Two readings in a row beyond the threshold.
  • A single reading more than ten percent beyond it.
  • A station that has been silent for thirty minutes, as 08 Reed bank is now.

Why did an alert arrive late?

A station that loses its link sends its readings when the link returns. The alert carries the time of the reading.

Can two people get the same alert?

Yes. Add a second threshold with the same level and another address.

How long are readings kept?

Seven days on the station and two years in the workspace.
