Reading the forecast

What the figures mean and how far ahead to trust them.

Back to today

Figures, ranges and chances

A forecast is a set of likely outcomes. chronoskin shows the middle of that set and tells you how wide it is.

Chance of rain

The percentage under each hour is the share of model runs in which at least 0.2 mm fell at your place during that hour. It says nothing about how hard it rains; the amount is shown in the week table.

What the temperatures are

  • The large figure is the air temperature at 2 m, in the shade.
  • Feels like adds wind and humidity to that figure.
  • High and low run from 06:00 to 06:00, not from midnight.

Sharing a reading

Every hour has an address of its own. Add ?at=17:00 to a place to open it at that hour.

Why did the forecast change since this morning?

New model runs arrive four times a day. The evening rain moved two hours earlier in the 09:00 run.

How far ahead is a figure reliable?

Three days for temperature, about two for rain. After that, read the range.

Where do the readings come from?

From the nearest station to each place. If it falls silent, the model fills in.
