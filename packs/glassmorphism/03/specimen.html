Reminders

Notes that go out on their own when an invoice is late.

See open invoices

How reminders work

A reminder is an ordinary message from you, sent on the day you would have remembered to write it.

Each invoice has a due date and a grace period of three days. After that the first reminder goes out. Every message is stored with the field sent_as, so a reply reaches your own inbox.

Stopping a run

A run ends the moment a payment is matched. You can also pause it by hand for one client or for one invoice.

A reminder is held back when

  • the client has written to you in the last two days,
  • part of the amount has already arrived,
  • the invoice is marked as disputed.

Tone of the notes

First notes are short and friendly. Second notes name the amount and the day it was due. Nothing is sent at weekends.
Can I stop one reminder?

Yes. Pause the run on the invoice and the next note waits until you start it again.

Who is the sender?

You are. Replies come to your own inbox.

What if the client has paid?

The run ends the moment the payment is matched.

No disputed invoices

When a client questions an amount, mark the invoice and it will wait here.
