Hello!

I teach mathematics by day and look at the sky by night.

I have kept this page since March 1997. It began as one screen of text and a picture of the Moon, and it has grown a room at a time. Everything here is written by hand in a text editor and saved as index.html.

The observatory

The observatory is a garden shed whose roof rolls back on four wheels from a lawn mower. Inside are:

  • a six-inch reflector on a wooden mount;
  • a red torch and a folding chair;
  • a tin of biscuits for cold nights.
