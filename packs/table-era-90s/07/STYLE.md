# Table-era web board, 1998

## Summary

A message board as hobby sites and software houses ran them between 1996 and 2000: the whole page is one bevelled 740px table centred in an 800px window, with a row of raised picture keys under the name and the boards listed below as a grid of bevelled cells. Running text is the browser's 16px Times, titles are set in bold Courier, and every label, count and date is 11px or 12px Arial; links are underlined in the page's own dark blue, on a pale grey sheet over a deep blue ground with sky blue bars and silver strips. It was the look of the first web boards, built from the browser's own table borders and form controls, until skinned forum software replaced it after 2000.

## Layout

- The page is fixed, not fluid, and centred: `--size-page` is 740px. `.ds-page__frame` is the sheet, a bordered table with `--space-3` (6px) of padding; nothing is wider than it and nothing stretches with the window.
- The specimen is one example site, a message board for amateur observers, of five views. `.ds-nav` and `.ds-footer` are written once, outside the views; each view is a `.ds-view` section and one shows at a time.
- Order from the top: `.ds-nav`, then the view, then the `.ds-footer`. The `.ds-nav` is three rows: `.ds-nav__top` (the `.ds-brand` at the left, a two-line tagline, the small search form at the right), `.ds-nav__links` (a tray holding five raised keys, each a small drawing and a label, sharing the full width) and `.ds-nav__status` (one inverted line: who you are at the left, the date at the right). There is no rail, no side navigation and no tab row in the header.
- The home view, `.ds-page__main`, is a stack of full-width blocks with `--space-5` (14px) between them: the `.ds-hero` welcome box, the `.ds-stat` strip, the board index (a `.ds-table` with category rows across it), and at the foot `.ds-page__columns`, three fifths for the `.ds-list` of latest messages under a `.ds-heading` and two fifths for `.ds-page__side`, a `.ds-panel` over the two-by-two `.ds-grid`.
- Inner pages (a board, a topic, the message form, a help page) keep the `.ds-nav` and the `.ds-footer` and drop the welcome box and the strip of figures. `.ds-page__inner` is one column at the full width. It opens with `.ds-page__head`, which stacks with `--space-2` between them the `.ds-breadcrumb`, the `.ds-page-header` (a filled strip: title and one small line at the left, one button at the right) and, when the page has several listings, the `.ds-tabs` strip. Below that come the working components with `--space-5` between them: the `.ds-table` with its key and `.ds-pagination`, or the `.ds-posts` of a topic, or `.ds-page__split`, which sets the form column (`.ds-page__block`) beside a 190px `.ds-sidebar`. Only the form page has a side column.
- Views: `boards` is the front page (welcome box, figures, board index, latest messages, who is reading, four coloured cells). `topics` is one board: the listings as a strip, the table of topics with its status key, the page numbers and the empty state of a board that has no polls. `thread` is one topic, message after message, with its page numbers and the reply button. `post` is the message form with its notices, the posting rules in the sidebar and the message window that discarding a message opens. `rules` is a page of running text, including the line that shows the link states, with a boxed panel of further help. The key of the view that is showing is held down; the first key is held down when the address names no view.
- Blocks are separated by bevelled outlines, title strips and flat fills, never by cards with shadows or wide gutters. Text is left-aligned; only the keys, the figures, the four cells, the empty state and the footer line are centred.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 2px is the spacing between table cells, keys and posts and the padding of strips; `--space-2` 4px is cell padding, the gap between a drawing and its label and between the lines of the inner-page head; `--space-3` 6px is the padding of the sheet, the side padding of cells and strips and the gap between buttons; `--space-4` 10px is the gap between boxes in a side column and the padding of the welcome box; `--space-5` 14px is the gap between neighbouring blocks and between columns; `--space-6` 30px is the list indent and the width of a short input.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-author` 130px author cell of a message, `--size-search` 120px search field, `--size-icon` 16px drawing in a key or in front of a board, `--size-label` 110px form label column, `--size-field` 240px text input, `--size-area` 360px textarea, `--size-check` 13px checkbox, `--size-dialog` 340px message window.

## Typography and colour roles

- Three faces, the two a browser fell back on and the one its controls used. `--font-body` (Times New Roman, Times) sets running text, board and topic titles, message text and member names. `--font-heading` and `--font-mono` (Courier New) set the name, page titles, category rows, panel titles and code. `--font-ui` (Arial, Helvetica) sets keys, table heads, counts, dates, descriptions, forms, buttons, notices and all small print. The references used Times and Arial as installed system fonts; Courier is the fixed face their plain-text boards were read in.
- Sizes: `--text-base` 16px; `--text-small` 12px for descriptions, counts, dates and small print; `--text-ui` 11px for keys, table heads and controls; `--text-large` and `--text-h2` 18px for figures, section headings and the large button; `--text-h1` 22px for a page title; `--text-h3` 16px bold; `--text-display` 28px for the name only. Line height is `normal` for text and 1.1 for headings. Bold is 700.
- No uppercase transform and no letter spacing. Status words are typed in capitals.
- Links: `--color-link` (the deep blue of the page ground) and always underlined, including keys, titles and quiet links. `--color-link-visited` is purple, `--color-link-active` red, `--color-link-hover` equals the link colour because the era had no hover effect. `--color-link-quiet` (pure blue) is for links to a member's own page.
- Text is `--color-text` black on every light fill: `--color-canvas`, `--color-surface`, `--color-surface-alt`, `--color-surface-strong` and the four `--color-fill-*`. `--color-text-muted` grey is for dates, signatures and the folder of a board with nothing new. `--color-heading` is black; `--color-heading-alt` (deep blue) is the name, section headings, sidebar titles, h3 and the folder of a board with new messages.
- Which text sits on which fill: `--color-bar-text` on `--fill-bar`, `--color-bar-alt-text` on `--fill-bar-alt` and on `--color-warning`, `--color-inverse-text` on `--fill-inverse`, `--color-accent-text` on `--fill-accent`, `--color-success` and `--color-danger`. Links keep `--color-link` on bars and fills, so a palette's bars must carry it; only on the inverted status line do links take `--color-inverse-text`.
- Fills: `--fill-bar` (sky blue) is the keys and every table head. `--fill-bar-alt` (silver) is category rows and panel title strips, and its colour borders notices. `--fill-inverse` (deep blue, white text) is the status line, the current choice of the `.ds-tabs` strip and the title bar of the message window. `--color-surface-alt` is the page header strip, alternate rows, author cells, the figure strip, the sidebar and the empty state; `--color-surface-strong` is the key tray, the `.ds-tabs` strip, the footer line and the current page number. `--color-fill-1` (pale yellow) is the welcome box; all four fills colour the grid cells.
- `--color-accent` (red) is the solid badge; `--color-accent-alt` (orange) is the "Hot!" word. `--color-success` (green) and `--color-warning` (orange) are flat fills for status labels. `--color-danger` is error text, the error notice border and the label of a destructive button.
- Borders: every outline and cell border is `--border-width` (2px) in `--border-style` (`outset`), the raised edge a browser drew round a table with a border: `--color-border` for boxes and strips, `--color-border-muted` for cells and rules inside a box, `--color-border-strong` for the sheet, the heading underline and the message window. Radius is 0. The sheet and panels take `--shadow-panel`, the welcome box and the message window `--shadow-dialog`, so another surface set changes the frames of the whole page.
- Not expressible by a token, written literally in `components.css`: keys, buttons, selects and the mark's tile have `border-style: outset` and inputs, checkboxes, pressed buttons and the held-down key `inset`, at `--border-width-strong` (3px; the checkbox at `--border-width`) in `--color-border-muted` and `--color-input-border`.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`; the patterned ground, body type. `.ds-page__frame` is the 740px bordered sheet in `--color-canvas`. `.ds-page__main` is the home stack; `.ds-page__columns` its two columns at the foot and `.ds-page__side` the right one. On inner pages `.ds-page__inner` is the single column, `.ds-page__head` stacks path, page header and strip, and `.ds-page__split` sets `.ds-page__block` beside the sidebar.
- `.ds-nav`: header. `.ds-nav__top` holds the `.ds-brand`, `.ds-nav__tagline` and the `.ds-nav__search` form with its `.ds-nav__field`; `.ds-nav__links` is the tray of `.ds-nav__item` / `.ds-nav__link` keys, each with a `.ds-nav__icon` drawing (inline SVG painted with `currentColor`); the key of the view that is showing is held down on `--color-surface` (`components.css` has one selector per view for this, and `is-current` does the same by hand). `.ds-nav__status` is the inverted line under the keys; its links are `.ds-nav__status-link`.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, at the left of `.ds-nav__top`. `.ds-brand__mark` is a square tile dressed like the button (`--fill-button` over `--color-button`, `--color-button-text` for the drawing, the `outset` bevel in `--color-border-muted`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`), so every palette and surface reskins it; it holds the mark as inline SVG painted with `currentColor`, drawn with square line caps, a 3 unit stroke and crisp edges. `.ds-brand__name` is the name in the heading family at `--text-display` in `--color-heading-alt`. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-hero`: the welcome box, the era's page introduction: a bordered box on `--color-fill-1` with `.ds-hero__title` (22px) and `.ds-hero__lead` at the left and `.ds-hero__action` at the right, a large button over a secondary one and a `.ds-hero__hint` line. Never a tall banner.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page, used instead of `.ds-hero`: a bordered strip on `--color-surface-alt`. `.ds-page-header__text` holds `.ds-page-header__title` and `.ds-page-header__lead` (one 12px line); `.ds-page-header__action` holds one button at the right.
- `.ds-prose`: running text on help pages; styles `h1`, `h2`, `h3`, `p`, `ul`, `ol`, `strong`, `code`; `.ds-prose__lead` for an 18px lead line.
- `.ds-link`: every link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` for links to a member's own page, `.ds-link--strong` for bold links. `.ds-links` wraps a line of links.
- `.ds-button`: grey bevelled button with an 11px label, on a `<button>` or, when it leads to another page, on a link. `.ds-button--secondary` is a darker grey for the lesser action, `.ds-button--danger` is the destructive action (discard, delete): the same bevel on `--color-surface` with a bold label in `--color-danger`, never a red button. `.ds-button--large` uses 18px. States `is-hover`, `is-pressed` (bevel inverts), `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttons` lays out a row.
- `.ds-form`: two-column form, right-aligned bold `.ds-form__label` and `.ds-form__field`. Controls: `.ds-form__input` (`--short` for 30px fields), `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea` (fixed-width face), `.ds-form__check`. `.ds-form__hint` for small help, `.ds-form__error` for a bold message in the danger colour under the field (the control itself does not change), `.ds-form__actions` for the button row.
- `.ds-table`: a grid of bevelled cells with 2px spacing. `.ds-table__head` (`--fill-bar`, 11px bold, `--num` right-aligned), `.ds-table__cell` with `--num` and `--meta` (12px), `--group` (a category row across the table on `--fill-bar-alt`) and `--flag` (the narrow first cell holding a `.ds-table__flag` folder; `is-new` colours it). `.ds-table__title` is the bold linked title, `.ds-table__desc` the 12px line under it, `.ds-table__row--alt` a row on `--color-surface-alt`, `.ds-table__caption` the 12px line beneath.
- `.ds-stat`: summary figures as one bordered strip on `--color-surface-alt`, divided by rules. `.ds-stat` is the row; each `.ds-stat__item` sets a `.ds-stat__label` (12px) and a `.ds-stat__value` (18px bold Courier) on one line. Three to five items; never large numerals or separate cards.
- `.ds-list`: ruled list of messages; `.ds-list__item` holds a linked subject and a `.ds-list__meta` line (grey 12px: who, where, when); `.ds-list__more` is the bold small link under the list.
- `.ds-posts`: a topic. `.ds-posts__head` is the bar over it; each `.ds-post` sets `.ds-post__author` (`.ds-post__name` and three small lines on `--color-surface-alt`) beside `.ds-post__body` (`.ds-post__meta` line, `.ds-post__text` paragraphs, `.ds-post__sig`).
- `.ds-panel`: a bordered box: `.ds-panel__title` strip on `--fill-bar-alt`, then `.ds-panel__body`; `.ds-panel__note` is a 12px line in it.
- `.ds-grid`: two-by-two grid of flat coloured, bordered cells with 2px spacing: `.ds-grid__cell` and `--2`, `--3`, `--4` for the four fills, `.ds-grid__title`, `.ds-grid__text`, all centred.
- `.ds-tabs`: the era's tabs, a "Show:" strip on `--color-surface-strong`: `.ds-tabs__label`, then `.ds-tabs__item` / `.ds-tabs__tab` links; the current one, `is-current`, is a solid `--fill-inverse` block.
- `.ds-badge`: small solid red label. `.ds-badge--text` is a bold coloured word beside a link; `.ds-badge--count` is a grey count. Status labels are the same solid block with the word typed in capitals: `.ds-badge--success` (open), `.ds-badge--warning` (pinned), `.ds-badge--danger` (locked). `.ds-badges` is the key line under a table.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the bordered box on `--color-surface-alt` beside the form: sections of `.ds-sidebar__title` over `.ds-sidebar__list` / `.ds-sidebar__item`; `.ds-sidebar__code` sets a few characters in the fixed-width face.
- `.ds-notice`: 12px box on `--color-notice` bordered in the `--color-bar-alt` colour; `.ds-notice__label` bold lead word, `.ds-notice--error` in the danger colour. `.ds-notices` stacks several.
- `.ds-pagination`: one left-aligned line: `.ds-pagination__label`, then small bordered `.ds-pagination__link` boxes; current `is-current` on `--color-surface-strong`, `is-disabled`.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: 12px path separated by double angle marks; `.ds-breadcrumb__item`, last one `is-current` and bold.
- `.ds-dialog`: a 340px message window in the page flow: border in `--color-border-strong`, `.ds-dialog__title` bar on `--fill-inverse`, `.ds-dialog__body` and centred `.ds-dialog__actions`. No backdrop.
- `.ds-empty`: bordered block on `--color-surface-alt` with `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and a button, centred.
- `.ds-footer`: `.ds-footer__links`, a "Jump to:" strip on `--color-surface-strong` (`.ds-footer__label`, bar-separated `.ds-footer__item`), then the centred `.ds-footer__legal` line.
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the example site; only the one named in the address shows, and `.ds-view--home` shows when none is named. It has no box of its own.
- `.ds-heading`: section title in the heading face over a rule in `--color-border-strong`.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: no reference has a rounded corner.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 0px`: the only shadow is a 1px outline; nothing is blurred.
- `text-shadow = none`: no reference has a text shadow.
- `gradient-fills <= 15%`: only keys, table heads, title strips, inverted blocks and the badge carry the two-tone band; every other fill is flat.
- `border-width <= 3px`: table borders are 2px and control bevels 3px.
- `font-size <= 28px`: the name is the largest text; headings stop at 22px.
- `font-size >= 11px`: labels on keys and controls are 11px, nothing smaller.
- `font-weight >= 400`: only regular and bold were measured.
- `font-families <= 3`: Times, Courier and Arial.
- `underlined-links >= 90%`: links are always underlined; only the name, a current item and a link dressed as a button lose the underline.
- `letter-spacing = 0`: no tracking anywhere.
- `uppercase-text <= 0%`: no text-transform; capitals are typed.
- `row-gap <= 8px`: table rows and list lines sit close.
- `block-gap <= 16px`: neighbouring blocks are at most 14px apart.
- `content-width <= 740px`: the page is a fixed 740px sheet.
- `transition = none`: nothing animates on hover.
- `animation = none`: no CSS animation.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. A new listing is a `.ds-table` of bevelled cells under a `--fill-bar` head, with a category row where it has groups; a new box is a `.ds-panel` with a silver title strip; a new choice of listings is a `.ds-tabs` strip; a new action is a bevelled `.ds-button`, or a key in the `.ds-nav__links` tray when it belongs to the whole site. Keep running text at 16px Times with 11px and 12px Arial for everything a board prints about a message, keep links underlined, keep every block at the full width of the sheet except on the form page, and never add radius, blur, hover effects, a side rail or more whitespace than `--space-5`.
