Rules and help

Short, and the same on every board. Last changed 2 November 1998.

Ask a Moderator

Rules of the chronoskin boards

The boards are run by volunteers in their spare evenings. Three rules keep them readable.

The three rules

  1. Post in the board that fits. A telescope for sale goes in the Trading Post, not under Deep Sky.
  2. Give a report its place, date and time in Universal Time, so that others can compare.
  3. Disagree with the observation, never with the observer.

Questions people ask

How do I quote another message?

Begin the line with a greater-than sign, like this: > the Moon will be out of the way. The board sets quoted lines in the fixed-width face.

What do the link colours mean?

A topic you have not opened, a topic you have read, a link under the pointer, a link as you click it, a member's own page and a pinned topic.

Why was my topic locked?

A moderator locks a topic when the question is answered in the help pages or when it has left its subject. Nothing is deleted: a locked topic can still be read and searched.
