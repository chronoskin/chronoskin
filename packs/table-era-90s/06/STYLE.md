# Table-era software company site, 1998

## Summary

The site of a software company or an institution as built between 1996 and 2000: a fixed 740px page with the name and a jump menu at the top, one row of equal navigation cells under a heavy rule, a product band, and link lists set in equal columns with a hairline under every link. Text is small Verdana, 11px with 10px small print, under regular-weight Georgia headings, on one saturated gold ground with white boxes, black rules and black inverted blocks; links are dark navy and underlined. It was the look of company, product and department sites in the years when a site was a brochure with a download page, before wider stylesheet layouts replaced it after 2000.

## Layout

- The page is fixed, not fluid, and centred: `--size-page` is 740px, and `.ds-page__frame` carries a `--space-3` (6px) margin above and `--space-4` (10px) at the sides inside it. Nothing is wider than 740px and nothing stretches with the window.
- The specimen is one example site, a software company, of five views. `.ds-nav` and `.ds-footer` are written once, outside the views; each view is a `.ds-view` section and one shows at a time.
- Order from the top: `.ds-nav`, then the view, then the `.ds-footer`. The `.ds-nav` is three rows: `.ds-nav__top` (the `.ds-brand` at the left, a 10px tagline beside it, the `.ds-nav__jump` menu at the right), the `.ds-nav__links` row of equal cells across the full width under a heavy rule, and the `.ds-nav__strip`, one line of small print with a notice at the left and the date at the right.
- The home view, `.ds-page__main`, is a stack of full-width bands with `--space-5` (16px) between them and no side column: the `.ds-hero` (text at the left, the product's box at the right), the `.ds-grid` of four product boxes, `.ds-page__columns` (two thirds for the `.ds-list` of news under a `.ds-heading`, one third for `.ds-page__side`, a stack of `.ds-panel` boxes), and the `.ds-index`, three equal columns of ruled links.
- Inner pages (downloads, a support answer, a form, the company page) keep the `.ds-nav` and the `.ds-footer` and drop the bands. `.ds-page__split` sets a `--size-side` (150px) `.ds-sidebar` at the left, a column of plain ruled links that belongs to that section, behind a vertical hairline, and the page itself, `.ds-page__inner`, at the right after a `--space-5` gutter. `.ds-page__inner` opens with `.ds-page__head`: the small `.ds-breadcrumb`, the `.ds-page-header` (title and one line at the left, one button at the right, a hairline under it) and the `.ds-tabs` row when the page has parts of its own. Below come `.ds-stat` when the page has figures, the `.ds-table` with its `.ds-pagination`, or running text with a `.ds-panel` of related links, or the `.ds-notices`, the `.ds-form` and a message box, with `--space-5` between blocks.
- Views: `home` is the front page (product band, product boxes, news and boxes, link columns). `downloads` is one page of files: the platforms as tabs, the table of files with its status key, and the page links. `support` is one answer from the knowledge base as running text, with the line that shows the link states and a panel of related answers. `register` is the registration form with its notices and the message box that clearing the form opens. `company` is the page about the firm: the row of figures, the staff directory table and the empty state of the jobs list. The cell of the view that is showing takes `--fill-bar` and its label loses the underline; the home cell is marked when the address names no view.
- Blocks are separated by hairlines, heavy rules and outlines, never by cards with shadows or wide gutters. Everything is left-aligned; only the navigation cells and the product's box are centred.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 2px is the padding of strips, tabs and ruled links; `--space-2` 4px is cell padding and the gap between a title and its text; `--space-3` 6px is the page margin above, box padding, the gap between buttons and form rows; `--space-4` 10px is the side margin of the sheet, the gap between product boxes, paragraph spacing and the padding of the product band; `--space-5` 16px is the gutter between columns and the gap between neighbouring blocks; `--space-6` 24px is the list indent and the inset of a news text under its title.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-side` 150px link column, `--size-box` 150px product box, `--size-jump` 130px jump menu, `--size-label` 130px form label column, `--size-field` 220px text input, `--size-short` 44px short input, `--size-area` 320px textarea, `--size-check` 13px checkbox, `--size-dialog` 340px message box.

## Typography and colour roles

- A sans-serif for everything read and a serif for titles. `--font-body` and `--font-ui` (Verdana, Geneva, Arial) set running text, links, tables, navigation, forms, buttons and small print. `--font-heading` (Georgia, Times New Roman) sets the name, page and section titles, panel and column titles, news titles and figures, at regular weight: `--weight-heading` and `--weight-display` are 400. `--font-mono` (Courier New) is for code and textareas. The references drew their serif titles as pictures; Georgia is the nearest face that shipped with browsers of the time.
- Sizes: `--text-base` and `--text-ui` 11px; `--text-small` 10px for the tagline, the strip, the path, captions, dates and the footer; `--text-h3` 12px bold sans-serif; `--text-large` 14px for panel, sidebar, product and news titles; `--text-h2` 17px for section and column titles; `--text-h1` 22px for a page title and the product line; `--text-display` 30px for the name only. `--line-body` is 1.35 and `--line-heading` 1.15. Bold is 700; navigation, buttons and labels are bold (`--weight-ui` 700).
- No uppercase transform and no letter spacing; capitals in labels are typed.
- Links: `--color-link` `#000068` (dark navy) and always underlined, navigation cells and titles included (all three decoration tokens are `underline`). `--color-link-visited` `#680060`, `--color-link-active` `#d00000`; `--color-link-hover` equals the link colour because the era had no hover effect.
- The ground is gold: `--color-page` and `--color-canvas` are both `#ffcc00`, so the whole window is one colour and text sits straight on it. `--color-surface` white is boxes and table cells; `--color-surface-alt` `#fff8e8` (cream) is alternate rows, resting tabs and the empty state; `--color-surface-strong` `#e8b808`.
- Text is black; `--color-text-muted` `#585858` is for dates and counts. `--color-heading` is black; `--color-heading-alt` `#683008` (brown) is the name, h3 and the figures.
- Fills: `--color-bar` `#e8b808` (deep gold, black text, navy links) is the product band, the table head and the current navigation cell. `--color-bar-alt` `#ffe888` (pale gold, black text) is the navigation row and every panel title strip. `--color-inverse` black with white text is the product's box, the current tab, the dialog title bar and the footer strip; links placed on it take its text colour. `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` (white, pale gold, deep gold, cream) fill the product boxes and carry black text and navy links; `--color-fill-2` is also the closing row of a table.
- `--color-accent` black with `--color-accent-text` gold is the solid "NEW" badge; `--color-accent-alt` `#d00000` is the "New!" word. `--color-danger` `#900000` is error text, the error notice border and a fill under gold text; `--color-success` `#006600` is a fill under gold text; `--color-warning` `#ff5000` is a fill under black text (`--color-bar-alt-text`). Notices sit on `#ffffd0`; the error notice is dark red on white.
- Controls belong to the page: `--color-button` white and `--color-button-secondary` pale gold with black text, bevelled in `--color-border-muted` `#e8b808`; white fields with black text in a `--color-input-border` `#a89018` bevel. Borders: `--color-border` `#a89018` (dark gold) for hairlines, `--color-border-strong` black for heavy rules and the dialog. `--color-shadow` is the dark gold.
- Surface: `--border-width` is 1px, `--border-width-strong` 3px and `--border-style` `solid`: hairlines and heavy rules, nothing between. Boxes stand under a heavy rule: `--shadow-panel` is a 3px edge in `--color-border-strong` along the top of the sheet and of every panel, with no blur; `--shadow-dialog` adds a 4px offset edge in `--color-border`. Bars are pinstriped: `--fill-bar`, `--fill-inverse` and `--fill-accent` repeat three columns of the palette colour and one of a slightly darker (for the dark fills, lighter) mix of it, and `--fill-bar-alt` is hatched the same way on the diagonal. `--fill-page` paints a 72px band of a darker mix of the page colour down the left edge of the window, closed by a 2px lighter line, the tiled left border of the period. Radius is 0; `--shadow-control` and `--shadow-text` are `none`; every other fill is the flat palette colour; `--focus-ring` is `1px solid`; `--transition` is `none`.
- Not expressible by a token, written literally in `components.css`: the bevel of buttons, selects and the brand tile is `border-style: outset` and of inputs, checkboxes and pressed buttons `inset`, drawn in `--color-border-muted` and `--color-input-border`. The hairline grid of a table is the table's own `--color-border` background showing through `--border-width` of cell spacing, so it follows the border width and colour but not the border style.

## Components

- `.ds-page`: on `<body>`; page fill and body type. `.ds-page__frame` is the centred 740px sheet with its margin inside it; it takes `--shadow-panel`, so a surface set that outlines its boxes also outlines the sheet. `.ds-page__main` is the home stack of bands; `.ds-page__columns` its one uneven row and `.ds-page__side` the stack of boxes in it; `.ds-page__split` is the inner-page grid of link column and page, `.ds-page__inner` the page column, `.ds-page__head` the stack of path, page header and tabs.
- `.ds-nav`: the header. `.ds-nav__top` holds the `.ds-brand`, the `.ds-nav__tagline` and `.ds-nav__jump` (a label, a select and a button). `.ds-nav__links` is the row of equal cells in `--fill-bar-alt` under a `--border-width-strong` rule, `.ds-nav__item` / `.ds-nav__link`, bold underlined links with hairlines between; the cell of the view that is showing takes `--fill-bar` and `--color-bar-text` and loses the underline (`components.css` has one selector per view for this, and `is-current` does the same by hand). `.ds-nav__strip` is the line of small print under the cells. A link to a section that has no view of its own carries `.ds-nav__link--elsewhere`: it opens the nearest fitting view and is never marked as the view that is showing.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, at the left of `.ds-nav__top`. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small square tile dressed like the button (`--fill-button` over `--color-button`, `--color-button-text` for the drawing, the `outset` bevel in `--color-border-muted`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`), so every palette and surface reskins it; it holds the mark as inline SVG painted with `currentColor`, drawn with flat line caps and a 2.2 unit stroke. `.ds-brand__name` is the name in the heading family at `--text-display` and `--weight-display`, in `--color-heading-alt`. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo.
- `.ds-hero`: the product band, the era's page introduction: a band in `--fill-bar` inside a hairline, with a small `.ds-hero__hint` label, the `.ds-hero__title` (22px), `.ds-hero__lead` and `.ds-hero__action` (the main button, a second button and a small line) at the left, and `.ds-hero__box` at the right: a block in `--fill-inverse` standing for the product's box, with `.ds-hero__box-name` and `.ds-hero__box-note`. Never a full-height banner and never a centred headline.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page, used instead of `.ds-hero`. `.ds-page-header__text` holds `.ds-page-header__title` (22px) and `.ds-page-header__lead` (one line); `.ds-page-header__action` holds one button, bottom-aligned at the right. A hairline closes it.
- `.ds-prose`: running text; styles `h1`, `h2`, `h3` (bold sans-serif in `--color-heading-alt`), `p`, `ul`, `ol`, `strong`, `code`; `.ds-prose__lead` for a 14px lead line.
- `.ds-link`: every link. States `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` is a small link, `.ds-link--strong` a bold one. `.ds-links` wraps a line of links.
- `.ds-button`: bevelled key with an 11px bold label, on a `<button>` or, when it leads to another page, on a link. `.ds-button--secondary` is the lesser action in `--fill-button-secondary`, `.ds-button--danger` is the destructive action: the same bevel on `--color-surface` with the label in `--color-danger`, never a red button. `.ds-button--large` uses 14px. States `is-hover`, `is-pressed` (bevel inverts), `is-focus`, `is-disabled`. `.ds-buttons` lays out a row.
- `.ds-form`: two-column form, right-aligned bold `.ds-form__label` and `.ds-form__field`. Controls: `.ds-form__input` (`--short` for a 44px field), `.ds-form__select`, `.ds-form__textarea`, `.ds-form__check`. `.ds-form__hint` for 10px help, `.ds-form__error` for a bold message in the danger colour under the field (the control itself does not change), `.ds-form__actions` for the button row. A select is a bevelled key in `--fill-button`; a checkbox is a small inset field in `--color-input` whose tick is a block of `--color-input-text`.
- `.ds-table`: a hairline grid under a heavy rule: the table is filled with `--color-border` and its cells stand `--border-width` apart. `.ds-table__head` (`--fill-bar`, bold, left-aligned; `--num` right-aligned), `.ds-table__cell` with `--num`, `--up`, `--down` and `--group` (a full-width closing row on `--color-fill-2`), `.ds-table__row--alt` for cream rows, `.ds-table__desc` for the 10px line under a linked name, `.ds-table__caption` for the left-aligned 10px lines beneath.
- `.ds-stat`: the row of figures: equal cells between a heavy rule above and a hairline below, hairlines between them. Each `.ds-stat__item` is a `.ds-stat__value` (22px serif in `--color-heading-alt`) over a 10px `.ds-stat__label`. Three to five items; never separate cards.
- `.ds-list`: news items without bullets: `.ds-list__item` holds a `.ds-list__title` (a 14px serif link) and under it `.ds-list__text`, set in `--space-6` from the left, ending in a `.ds-list__meta` date; `.ds-list__more` is the right-aligned small bold link.
- `.ds-index`: three equal columns of links, the site's contents at the foot of the home page: each column a `.ds-index__title` on a heavy rule over a `.ds-index__list` of `.ds-index__item`, every one closed by a hairline. `.ds-index__list` is also used inside a panel for a short list of related pages.
- `.ds-panel`: an outlined white box: `.ds-panel__title` strip in `--fill-bar-alt` with an optional small `.ds-panel__tool` link at the right, then `.ds-panel__body` with `.ds-panel__text` and `.ds-panel__form`, a one-field form (`.ds-panel__field`) with its button on one line.
- `.ds-grid`: one row of four product boxes with `--space-4` between them: `.ds-grid__cell` and `--2`, `--3`, `--4` for the four fills, each a hairline box under a heavy top rule holding a `.ds-grid__title` link (14px serif), a 10px `.ds-grid__text` and a bold 10px `.ds-grid__price` line.
- `.ds-tabs`: a joined row of outlined cells standing on a heavy rule, for the parts of an inner page; `.ds-tabs__item`, `.ds-tabs__tab` (an underlined link on `--color-surface-alt`); the current one, `is-current`, is inverted: `--fill-inverse`, `--color-inverse-text`, bold, not underlined.
- `.ds-badge`: small solid label in `--fill-accent`. `.ds-badge--text` is a bold word in `--color-accent-alt` beside a link; `.ds-badge--count` is a muted count in brackets. Status labels are the same solid block in a flat status colour, with the word typed in capitals: `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning`, `.ds-badge--danger`. `.ds-badges` is the key line that explains them under a table.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the column of plain links at the left of an inner page, behind a vertical hairline, with no fill. Sections of `.ds-sidebar__title` (14px serif on a heavy rule), `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item`, each closed by a hairline; the page that is showing is `is-current`, bold and not a link; `.ds-sidebar__note` is a 10px line and `.ds-sidebar__more` a right-aligned small bold link.
- `.ds-notice`: a hairline box on `--color-notice` with a heavy left edge in `--color-border-strong`; `.ds-notice__label` bold lead words, `.ds-notice--error` in the danger colour on `--color-danger-surface` with danger borders. `.ds-notices` stacks several.
- `.ds-pagination`: one line under a table: `.ds-pagination__label` (the count) at the left, then at the right previous, numbered `.ds-pagination__link` and next; the current page, `is-current`, is bold in a hairline box; `is-disabled`.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a 10px path separated by slashes; `.ds-breadcrumb__item`, last one `is-current` and bold.
- `.ds-dialog`: a 340px box in the page flow, left-aligned under the form that opens it, with a `--color-border-strong` outline, a `.ds-dialog__title` bar in `--fill-inverse`, `.ds-dialog__body` and right-aligned `.ds-dialog__actions`. No backdrop.
- `.ds-empty`: a hairline block on `--color-surface-alt` with `.ds-empty__title`, `.ds-empty__text` and a button, left-aligned.
- `.ds-footer`: `.ds-footer__links`, a strip in `--fill-inverse` of small `.ds-footer__item` links separated by bars, in the strip's text colour; then `.ds-footer__legal`, one 10px line with the copyright at the left and the webmaster at the right.
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the example site; only the one named in the address shows, and `.ds-view--home` shows when none is named. It has no box of its own.
- `.ds-rule`: a hairline; `.ds-rule--strong` the heavy rule. `.ds-heading`: a 17px serif section title on a hairline.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: no reference has a rounded corner.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 0px`: the only shadows are hard edges; nothing is blurred.
- `text-shadow = none`: no reference sets a text shadow in its markup.
- `gradient-fills <= 12%`: only bars, title strips, inverted blocks and the badge are pinstriped; every other fill is flat.
- `border-width <= 3px`: hairlines are 1px; heavy rules and bevels are 3px.
- `font-size <= 30px`: the name is the largest text; headings stop at 22px.
- `font-size >= 10px`: small print is 10px, nothing smaller.
- `font-weight >= 400`: only regular and bold were measured.
- `font-families <= 3`: Verdana, Georgia and Courier.
- `underlined-links >= 90%`: the references underline their link text; only the name, a current item and a link dressed as a button lose the underline.
- `letter-spacing = 0`: no reference tracks its text.
- `uppercase-text <= 0%`: no reference uses text-transform; capitals are typed.
- `row-gap <= 8px`: ruled links and table rows sit close.
- `block-gap <= 16px`: neighbouring blocks are at most 16px apart.
- `content-width <= 740px`: the page is a fixed 740px sheet.
- `transition = none`: nothing in the references changes on hover.
- `animation = none`: no reference has a CSS animation.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen and use tokens only. A new set of links is a column of `.ds-index` or a section of the `.ds-sidebar`, each link on its own hairline; a new box is a `.ds-panel` with a pale title strip; anything tabular is a `.ds-table` hairline grid under a heavy rule; a new band on the home page spans the full 740px. Keep text at 11px with 10px small print and serif titles at regular weight, keep links underlined, open blocks with a heavy rule and close rows with hairlines, keep the home page free of side columns and inner pages to the link column plus one page column, and never add radius, blur, hover effects, centred headlines or more whitespace than `--space-5`.
