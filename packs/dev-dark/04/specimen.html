Streaming query plans

v4.2.0New13 Mar 2025, 46 commits

The plan arrives while the query runs, and the planner samples your data instead of assuming its shape.

Follow releases Upgrade guide

What changed in 4.2

Until now a plan was something you asked for after a query had been slow. From this release it is a stream you can watch.

Set plan.stream on a session and every step reports its row count and timing as it finishes. The driver exposes it as an iterator, so no polling is needed.

Added

  • Streaming plans over the wire protocol, version 7 and later
  • Sampled histograms for every indexed column, refreshed nightly
  • A --explain-live flag in the command line client

Changed defaults

The sample rate defaults to 1 percent. Tables under 10,000 rows are read in full.
