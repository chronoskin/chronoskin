Questions

Before you sign up

  • What counts as a build minute?Billing, updated 4 Mar 2025

    Time a runner spends on your commands, rounded up to the second. Waiting in the queue and restoring the cache are free.

  • Who needs a seat?Seats, updated 18 Feb 2025

    Anyone who starts builds or changes settings. People who only read logs through a shared link do not.

  • What happens when I pass my minutes?Limits, updated 18 Feb 2025

    On Free, builds wait for the next month. On a paid plan they keep running at $0.004 a minute, up to the cap you set.

How you are billed

Seats are charged ahead for the year. Minutes are charged behind, month by month.

The estimate above uses the numbers from the calculator. The real figure comes from usage.minutes at the end of each month and is never more than your cap.

Changing seats

  • A seat added mid-year is charged for the months that remain
  • A seat removed becomes credit on the next invoice
  • Invited people cost nothing until they accept

Refunds

Cancel within 30 days of the first invoice and the whole amount is returned.
