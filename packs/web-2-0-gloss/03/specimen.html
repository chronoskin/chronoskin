Sending in a film

Any crew can send a film of twenty minutes or less. It is watched by two volunteers first, and nearly everything is accepted; see the house rules for the few exceptions.

What to send

One file, with the sound mixed in. Do not add our name to the titles; the film is yours and stays yours.

Before you send

  • Name everyone who worked on it.
  • Check that the music is yours to use.
  • Write two sentences about the film.

Every crew page has a feed; add /feed to its address.

After it is accepted

The film gets a page of its own with the crew named on it, and it joins the list on your crew page. You can withdraw it at any time; the page stays, the film stops playing.

How long until my film shows?

Two volunteers watch it within a week. You get a message either way.

Can I replace the file later?

Yes, from your crew page. The comments and the count stay.

The player stops halfway

Pause it for a minute and let the grey bar run ahead of the red one.
