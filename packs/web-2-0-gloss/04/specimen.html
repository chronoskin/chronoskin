Sharing an album

Every album has one address. Anyone you send it to can look without signing up, and only people you invite can add to it. You can change this at any time on the photos tab.

Who sees what

A photo is as open as its album unless you say otherwise. Hidden photos stay in the count but show to nobody else.

Three things to know

  • Labels are shared: a word you add is a word everybody can search.
  • Full-size files are kept for thirty days on a free account.
  • An album feed lives at the album address with /feed on the end.
