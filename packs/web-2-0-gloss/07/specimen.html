No. One name, so that somebody ticks it.

Lists and to-dos

A list is a heading with to-dos under it. A to-do is one line that somebody can tick. That is the whole idea, and the rest of chronoskin is there to keep those lines short, owned and dated.

Writing a good to-do

Start with a verb and name one thing. If it needs the word "and", it is two to-dos. Give it to one person; a to-do for everyone is a to-do for nobody.

  • A day is optional, but only dated to-dos show on the calendar.
  • Notes are for what the next person needs to know, not for the history.
  • Hours are logged when you tick, or later from the list.

Filing from the quick box

The box on the Today page files into your Inbox. Begin the line with a list name and a colon, as in Office move: ring the movers, and it goes straight to that list. Add !fri at the end to give it Friday.

When a list is finished

  1. Tick or delete what is left.
  2. Choose Archive the done. The list leaves the left pane.
  3. Find it again under Lists, in the archive, for as long as you keep the account.
