Questions people ask before signing up

What happens after thirty days?

We ask for a card. If you give none, the account drops to Solo and nothing is lost.

Can I change plans later?

Up or down, at any time, from the plan page. The price changes from the next month.

Do my customers see any of this?

No. They write to your usual address and get an answer from it.

How do I leave?

Two clicks, and you can take every letter with you as one file. More answers

Start with Team. If a month shows you need less, move down and pay less from then on.

Help

Plain answers about plans, paying and leaving, written by the two people who run chronoskin.

Sign up

Plans and paying

Every account starts with thirty free days on the plan you picked. Nothing is charged until that month is over, and we write to you three days before it ends.

Changing plans

Move up or down from the plan page. Moving down never deletes mail; mailboxes over the new limit stop receiving until you pick which ones to keep.

What counts as a mailbox

  • One address your customers write to.
  • Any number of people can answer from it.
  • Addresses that only forward do not count.

Leaving

Close the account from the plan page. For thirty days after, every letter can be fetched as one file from /export on your account address.
