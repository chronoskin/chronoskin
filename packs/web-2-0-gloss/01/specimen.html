Help

Short answers about adding, keeping and labelling links, written by the people who run the place.

Add a link

How the front page works

Anyone can add a link. Readers keep what they find useful, and links kept by enough people in a short time move to the front page. Nothing is chosen by an editor.

Adding a link

Give the address of the page, a plain title and two or three sentences that say what a reader will find there. Do not copy the first paragraph; write your own.

Before you add

  • Search first, the link may already be waiting.
  • Point to the original page, not to a page that quotes it.
  • Pick labels a stranger would search for.

Keeping a link

Keeping puts a link in your own list and counts as one reader for it. A link needs a second reader before it leaves the waiting list, and it can be removed again from your list at any time.

Feeds exist for every label: put /feed on the end of a label address to get one.
