Quarter rests

Card 4 of 10. Which sign means one beat of silence?

All lessons
Card 4 of 103 right, 0 wrong

One beat of silence

It sits in the middle of the staff and lasts as long as a quarter note.

Two beats of silence

A small hat that sits on the middle line.

Four beats of silence

The same hat, hanging under the line.

About rests

A rest is silence that is counted. In 4/4 time a quarter rest takes one of the four beats.

How to count it

Say the number in your head and keep your hands still.

Easy to mix up

  • The half rest sits on the line.
  • The whole rest hangs under it.
  • The quarter rest looks like a small bolt.
