Brown butter gnocchi

Crisp outside, soft inside, with sage and a squeeze of lemon. 25 minutes for two.

Save to my box Start cooking
  • Method
  • Notes
  • Photos
3

Brown the butter

Melt the butter over a medium heat and keep it moving. It foams, goes quiet, then turns the colour of hazelnuts.

Set 8 minutes
4

Fry the gnocchi

Tip the gnocchi into the butter in one layer and leave them alone until the undersides are gold.

Set 5 minutes
5

Sage and lemon

Add the sage for the last minute, then the lemon off the heat.

Set 1 minute

Cook's notes

Shop gnocchi are fine here. What matters is a dry pan and patience with the butter.

The card is filed as PASTA-112. It was tested with salted butter; with unsalted, add a pinch of salt with the sage.

What can go wrong

Butter goes from brown to burnt in half a minute. Take the pan off the heat when in doubt.

Swaps that work

  • Thyme for sage, in the same amount.
  • A spoon of capers instead of the lemon.
  • Walnuts on top when there is no cheese.

Serve with cucumber, yoghurt and dill, or find another side in your box.
