Adding a device

Anything that speaks to the hub can join the house: lamps, plugs, valves, locks and speakers.

Plug the device in and hold its button for five seconds, until it blinks. The hub lists it under a short code such as LAMP-07, and you give it a name and a room.

When it does not show up

Most devices that stay hidden are too far from the hub or were paired to another house before.

Three things to try

  • Bring the device within five metres of the hub.
  • Hold the button for fifteen seconds to clear the old house.
  • Check that the hub's own light is steady, not blinking.

Once it is in, switch it from its room or put it in a routine.

Does the house work without the internet?

Yes. Switches, scenes and routines run on the hub. Only the view from outside the house needs a connection.

Who can unlock the front door?

Only people you invited as residents. Guests see the lights and nothing else.

How is the energy figure measured?

Plugs and valves report what they draw. Devices that cannot report are estimated from their hours on.
