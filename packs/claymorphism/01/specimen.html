Is real money in the jars?

No. The jars keep count of money you hold for your child. You pay it out when a jar is spent.

Can two homes share a child's jars?

Yes, on the Family plan. Each home fills on its own day.

What happens when a month ends?

Nothing moves. Jars carry over until they are spent or emptied.

How do we stop?

From the family page, on any day. The jars stay readable for a year.

How filling works

On jar day the pocket money arrives in one lump and waits to be shared out between the jars.

You set the amount and the day once. Each fill is kept under its own reference, such as SAT-09, so that both homes see the same Saturday.

Who decides the split

Your child does, inside the limits you set. A limit is a smallest or a largest share for one jar.

A common set of limits

  • At least a tenth goes to the giving jar.
  • At most half goes to the spending jar.
  • The rest is free to move between goals.

When a jar is spent

Tap the jar and choose Spend. You hand over the money, and the jar starts again from zero. See how many jars each plan has or make the first one.
