First ride in three steps

1

Wake the dock

Press the round key next to a bike with a green light and type the five figures of your code.

2

Pull, then check

Pull the bike back by the saddle. Squeeze both brakes and spin the pedals once before you set off.

3

Push in until green

At any dock, push the front wheel in hard. The ride ends when the light turns green, not before.

What a ride costs

You pay for the minutes between pulling a bike out and the green light at the next dock, and nothing else.

The first unlock of a day is free. A ride is kept under a reference such as R-48213, which you find in the app and on the receipt, and which we will ask for if a charge looks wrong.

How the minutes are counted

The clock starts when the lock opens and stops when a dock shows green. A dock that blinks red has not taken the bike, so the ride runs on.

Three things that stop the clock

  • A green light on the dock.
  • A full dock, reported from the app: you get 15 free minutes.
  • A broken bike, reported within 2 minutes of unlocking.

Prices at a glance

The same prices apply at every dock. For the small print see fees and how to avoid them, or ask us about a charge.

Check a charge yourself

  1. Open the rideIn the app, tap Rides and pick the day. Each ride shows its two docks and its minutes.
  2. Take off the free minutesA day pass gives 30 and a year pass 45, on every ride.
  3. Multiply what is leftMinutes left times the price of a minute, plus the unlock if you have no pass.

Before you write

My ride did not end

Push the bike in again until the light is green. If it stays red, report a full dock from the app and the clock stops.

I was charged twice

One of the two is a hold that your bank lets go within three days.

The code does not work

A code is good for ten minutes. Ask for a new one in the app.
