Calendar sync

Meetings on the contact, first calls in your calendar.

Your diary and your customer list, in step

Connect a calendar once. From then on every meeting with a contact is on their page, and every call you book from chronoskin is in your diary.

What it does

The app reads the guests of each event and matches them to contacts by address. A match files the meeting under Activity; a guest it does not know is offered as a new contact.

Set-up

  • Choose which calendars to watch: private ones are never read.
  • Pick a default length for calls booked from a deal.
  • Decide whether declined meetings are filed or dropped.

For developers

Events arrive on the calendar.event.matched hook, so your own app can react to a meeting as well.
