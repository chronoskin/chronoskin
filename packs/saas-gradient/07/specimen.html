What the API does

Six resources. Each has its own page in the reference.

Shipments

Create, change and cancel a parcel on its way from you to a customer.

POST /v3/shipments

Labels

A label as PDF or ZPL, in the size your printer takes.

GET /v3/labels/:id

Tracking

Every scan as an event, in the same words for every carrier.

GET /v3/trackers/:id

Rates

Prices for a parcel from all carriers, before you buy anything.

POST /v3/rates

Addresses

Check and tidy an address, so a parcel is not sent to nowhere.

POST /v3/addresses

Webhooks

We call your server when something changes, and retry for three days.

POST /v3/webhooks

Create a shipment

Buys a label from the cheapest carrier that can meet the service you ask for, and returns the shipment with its label and price.

Before you call

Use a test key while you build. A test shipment gets a real-looking label marked SAMPLE and is never collected.

Units

  • Weights are whole grams: weight=1200 is 1.2 kg.
  • Sizes are whole millimetres, longest side first.
  • Money is in the smallest unit of your account's currency.

Test mode is free. Labels made with a test key cost nothing and cannot be posted.

Fractions of a gram are refused. Since 2019-03-11 the call fails with weight_not_whole.

Errors

400 address_not_found

No carrier knows the postcode. Check it with the Addresses resource first; the reply suggests the nearest match.

402 balance_too_low

The account cannot pay for the label. Top up, or switch on automatic top-ups in the dashboard.

429 too_many_requests

More than 100 calls in a second. Wait for the time given in the Retry-After header.
