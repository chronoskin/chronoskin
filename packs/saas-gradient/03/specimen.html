Booking limits on each plan

Every plan counts the same three things each month. When you reach a limit, clients can still book; you are asked to move up a plan.

What is counted

A booking counts when it is made, not when the visit happens. A cancelled booking still counts; a booking you move does not count twice.

Seeing where you stand

Open Account and choose Usage. The same figures are returned by the widget as usage.bookings if you show them on your own site.

Bookings this month410 of 500

If you go over

  • Nothing is switched off in the month you go over.
  • You get one email at 80% and one at 100%.
  • Text reminders stop at the limit; email reminders carry on.
