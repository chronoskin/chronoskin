# Linklog, 2005

## Summary

A linklog as kept between 2003 and 2007 beside or instead of a weblog: several short dated items a day, each a link with one line of remark, a quotation or a note, filed under tags. The page is a wide parchment sheet hanging from the top of the window, the menu as small tabs on its top edge, the brand (mark and site name) under them, a dark tag bar across, and then day blocks ruled down the left beside a weighted tag index. Text is 13px Georgia with small tracked capitals in Trebuchet for every label; rules are dotted, corners square, and the sheet and its boxes carry a soft glow of shadow.

## Layout

- The page is fixed, not fluid: `.ds-page__frame` is `--size-page` (880px) wide, centred, `--color-canvas` on `--fill-page`, flush with the top of the window and open there, with a 1px `--color-border` line down each side and along the foot and `--shadow-panel` around it. It never stretches.
- Order from the top: `.ds-nav` (the `.ds-nav__menu` tabs hanging at the top right, the `.ds-nav__head` with the brand left and a two-line tagline right, then `.ds-nav__tags`, the `--fill-bar` tag bar across the sheet), the view, and `.ds-footer`, the inverted strip. The menu is never a bar of its own and never sits beside the name.
- The home view is the `.ds-hero` (one tinted line across the sheet), then `.ds-page__columns`: the stream (`.ds-page__main`) on the left and `.ds-sidebar` at `--size-side` (230px) on the right, `--space-7` apart, and last the `.ds-grid`, four tinted cells across the whole sheet directly above the footer.
- The stream is a stack of `.ds-day` blocks `--space-6` apart: a `.ds-day__date` line (small bold tracked capitals with the day's count at the right) over a `.ds-list` that is ruled down its left side with a `--border-width-strong` line. Items are divided by 1px rules, never by cards. Other blocks of a column are `.ds-page__section`, closed by a 1px rule with `--space-6` above and below; `.ds-page__label` is the small tracked caption over a block.
- Inner pages keep the sheet, the tabs, the name, the tag bar and the footer. The hero and the closing grid are not used. A `.ds-breadcrumb` line sits under the tag bar, inside the sheet's padding. A page about one thing (an item, the about page) keeps both columns: the main column opens with `.ds-page-header` (18px title, one 11px grey line, one button at the right, a 3px `--color-border-muted` rule), then the text, related links, the `.ds-comments` thread and the form last; the sidebar carries lists that belong to that page. A page that is an index (the tags) drops the sidebar and uses `.ds-page__wide`: page header, `.ds-stats`, `.ds-tabs` with the panel in `.ds-tabs__body`, the table, `.ds-pagination`. Tabs stand on their own rule under the page header.
- Views: the specimen is one linklog with four views. `links` is the front page: three days of items, the index column and the four counts. `item` is one link with its remark written out, related links, comments, the delete dialog, the notices that answer a posted comment and the comment form. `tags` is the tag index: figures, tabs over the weighted cloud, the table of the busiest tags, pagination and an empty tag. `about` is the keeper panel, what gets in, and the form for suggesting a link. The menu links to links, tags and about; items are reached from titles, comment counts and sidebar lists.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 1px is the tracking of labels (doubled or tripled for titles and dates) and the padding of sidebar rows; `--space-2` 2px is the gap between tabs and between a line and its meta line; `--space-3` 5px is the padding of buttons, tabs, table cells and the tag bar; `--space-4` 8px is the padding of a list row, the inset of a day block and of notices; `--space-5` 12px is the gap between paragraphs and the padding of panels, the hero and the footer; `--space-6` 18px is the gap between blocks and day blocks; `--space-7` 28px is the side padding of the sheet and the gap between the columns.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-kind` 52px is the column that names an item's kind, `--size-label` 100px the label column of a form, `--size-field` 250px a text input, `--size-num` 80px a numeric table column, `--size-dialog` 380px a dialog.

## Typography and colour roles

- `--font-body` and `--font-heading` are Georgia (then Times), both at normal weight (`--weight-heading` 400); `--font-ui` is "Trebuchet MS", used small, in capitals (`--ui-transform` uppercase) and tracked for the menu, the tag bar, buttons, tabs, every label and the footer. `--font-mono` (Courier New) is only for code. All are system fonts; the references' body Times is kept as the fallback.
- `--text-base` 13px with `--line-body` 1.6 is running text and item lines. `--text-small` 11px is meta lines, the sidebar, tables and notices; `--text-ui` 10px is all Trebuchet labels. `--text-large` 15px is the hero's lead, a quoted line and the third weight of a tag.
- `--text-display` 27px is the site name and the counts in the closing grid, `--text-h1` 18px a page title and the heaviest tags, `--text-h2` 15px a sub-heading, `--text-h3` 13px bold Trebuchet a minor heading. Headings are never uppercased. Labels are tracked 1px to 3px, written `var(--space-1)` and multiples of it because the type part has no token for the tracking of labels.
- `--color-text` #333333 is body text; `--color-text-muted` #999999 is meta lines, kinds and captions. `--color-heading` #cc6600, the orange, is the site name, titles and day dates; `--color-heading-alt` #003366, the navy, is sidebar and panel titles, quoted lines and stat figures.
- `--color-link` #5588aa, not underlined, turning `--color-link-hover` #cc6600 and underlined on hover; visited #4477aa; active #003366. An item's title link is bold. `--color-link-quiet` #999999 is tag and tool links in meta lines.
- `--color-bar` #003366 with white text is the tag bar, the current menu tab and dialog titles; `--color-bar-alt` #114477 with `--color-bar-alt-text` #99ccff is the idle menu tabs; `--color-inverse` #002244 with `--color-inverse-text` #99ccff is the footer.
- Surfaces: `--color-surface` and `--color-canvas` #f8f0e0; `--color-surface-alt` #f0e8e0 is the stats line, forms, alternate rows and the keeper's reply; `--color-surface-strong` #e0d8c0 is panel titles, idle tabs and the current page number.
- Text on fills follows the era's layouts: body and heading colours on the canvas, surfaces and `--color-fill-*` tints; `--color-bar-text` only on `--fill-bar`; `--color-bar-alt-text` only on `--fill-bar-alt`; `--color-inverse-text` only on `--fill-inverse`; `--color-accent-text` only on `--fill-accent`; `--color-button-text` on the button fill.
- `--color-fill-1` to `--color-fill-4` (#f0e8e0, #e0d8c0, #d8c8b8, #ffffff) are the four cells of the closing grid; fill-1 is also the hero. `--color-accent` #cc6600 with white text is the count badge and list bullets; `--color-accent-alt` #5588aa is the "new" word.
- Borders: `--color-border` #d0c0b0 for ordinary rules, `--color-border-muted` #e0d8c0 for the heavy left rule of a day block and the rule under a page header or table head, `--color-border-strong` #cc6600 for the left edge of the stats line and of a quotation and the outline of notices. `--border-width-strong` is 3px.
- Status colours are used only as the text and 1px outline of status labels and for a destructive button: `--color-success` #538620, `--color-warning` #bb5500, `--color-danger` #cc0000.
- Surface: every line is drawn in `--border-style` (dotted here). `--shadow-panel` is a soft even glow (10px blur, no offset) around the sheet, panels and the boxed sidebar block; `--shadow-dialog` is the same at 16px. `--fill-bar`, `--fill-bar-alt` and `--fill-inverse` darken towards the bottom, `--fill-page` towards the sides of the window; buttons, inputs and panels are flat. A pressed button takes an inset shade. Focus is a 1px solid outline. Hover, visited, active and focus colours were not measurable on static captures and are taken from the same palette.

## Components

- `.ds-page` on `<body>`: page fill and base type. Everything goes in `.ds-page__frame`. `.ds-page__columns` holds `.ds-page__main` and `.ds-sidebar`; `.ds-page__wide` is the body of a view without a sidebar; `.ds-page__section` is a block closed by a rule (`--last` drops it); `.ds-page__label` is its caption.
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the example site. The specimen holds its views side by side in the markup and shows one at a time: the view named in the address, or the `.ds-view--home` view when none is named. In a real project each view is a page of its own; drop the class there.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, at the left of the header under the tabs; a link to the home page. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small square tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button`, `--color-button-text` for the drawing, a 1px `--color-input-border` outline that is 2px along the bottom, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`), 1.3 times `--text-display` on a side and never less than twice `--text-base`; it holds the mark as inline SVG painted with `currentColor`. `.ds-brand__name` is the name in `--font-heading` at `--text-display` in `--color-heading`. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo; keep the classes and swap the text and the drawing.
- `.ds-nav`: the header, written once above the views. `.ds-nav__menu` is the row of `.ds-nav__link` tabs hanging from the top edge at the right, filled with `--fill-bar-alt`; the current one takes `--fill-bar` and bold `--color-bar-text`: `is-current` on a real page, and in the specimen one selector per view that picks the link by its `href` (a single item counts as the links). `.ds-nav__head` holds the `.ds-brand` and `.ds-nav__tagline`. `.ds-nav__tags` is the tag bar: a `.ds-nav__tags-label` and `.ds-nav__tag` links on `--fill-bar`.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: a line of small tracked capitals under the tag bar: `.ds-breadcrumb__link` items, `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` (`/`), ending in `.ds-breadcrumb__current`. No strip, no fill.
- `.ds-hero`: the front page's introduction, once: a `--color-fill-1` line across the sheet with a rule below, `.ds-hero__text` (`.ds-hero__title` at 18px and a `.ds-hero__lead` at 15px) on the left and `.ds-hero__actions` (one button over one text link) at the right. Not a banner: no large type, no picture.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page, never together with the hero: `.ds-page-header__main` holds the `.ds-page-header__title` and one `.ds-page-header__text` line; `.ds-page-header__action` at the right holds one button. A 3px `--color-border-muted` rule closes it.
- `.ds-stat`: one summary figure, set as words in a sentence: a `.ds-stat__value` (18px Georgia, navy) followed on the same line by its `.ds-stat__label` (10px tracked capitals, grey). Three to five follow each other in `.ds-stats`, one `--color-surface-alt` line with a 3px `--color-border-strong` left edge. Do not stack the label under the number and do not box the figures.
- `.ds-day`: one day of the log: `.ds-day__date` (the date in small bold tracked capitals, the day's count as a `.ds-badge` at the right) over one `.ds-list`.
- `.ds-list`: the items of a day, ruled down the left. Each `.ds-list__item` has a `.ds-list__kind` (link, quote, note, in grey capitals) and a `.ds-list__body`. A link is one `.ds-list__excerpt` paragraph that opens with the bold `.ds-list__title` link and continues with the remark; a quotation is a `.ds-list__quote` (15px, navy); a note is a plain `.ds-list__excerpt`. Each ends with a `.ds-list__meta` line: time, source, comments, tags.
- `.ds-tags`: the weighted tag index, a wrapping run of `.ds-tags__tag` links whose size follows their use: plain is 11px, `--2` 13px, `--3` 15px, `--4` 18px in the heading face. Use it in the sidebar and in a panel; four weights are enough.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2 (orange), h3 (bold Trebuchet, navy), paragraphs, square-bulleted lists with orange markers, `code`, and `blockquote` with a 1px `--color-border-strong` left rule. `.ds-post__meta` is the small grey line under it.
- `.ds-comment`: one comment, inside a `.ds-comments` list: a `.ds-comment__text` paragraph over a `.ds-comment__meta` line, divided by 1px rules. `.ds-comment--author` tints the keeper's own reply.
- `.ds-link`: text link with `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` is the grey meta link; `.ds-link--strong` is bold. `.ds-links` lays links out in a wrapping row.
- `.ds-button`: a small button lettered in bold tracked capitals, `--fill-button` with a 1px `--color-input-border` outline that is 2px along the bottom. `.ds-button--secondary` is paler and not bold. `.ds-button--danger` is the destructive action: the secondary button lettered and underscored in `--color-danger`, never filled red. `disabled` or `is-disabled` fades it. Group with `.ds-buttons`.
- `.ds-form`: a `--color-surface-alt` block under a 3px rule: `.ds-form__title`, rows (`.ds-form__row`) of a bold capital `.ds-form__label` and a `.ds-form__field`. Controls take `.ds-form__input` (`--wide` full width, `--auto` for selects); `is-invalid` plus `.ds-form__error` marks a validation error; `.ds-form__hint` is help text; `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox` for checkboxes; `.ds-form__actions` starts the buttons under the fields and keeps them on one row.
- `.ds-table`: data table at 11px with open sides: a head row of grey tracked capitals on a 3px rule, rows divided by 1px rules, `.ds-table__row--alt` for alternate rows, `.ds-table__num` for right-aligned numeric columns, `.ds-table__group` for a group row. The head is not a bar.
- `.ds-panel`: a 1px outlined box with the soft glow, a `--color-surface-strong` `.ds-panel__title` in tracked capitals and a `.ds-panel__body`. Use it for the tag cloud or one boxed note.
- `.ds-grid`: four `.ds-grid__cell` blocks across the whole sheet, joined edge to edge and divided by 1px rules, each a flat tint (`--2`, `--3`, `--4` select the other fills) with a large `.ds-grid__count`, a `.ds-grid__title` in tracked capitals and a `.ds-grid__text` line. It closes the front page above the footer; do not put gaps between the cells.
- `.ds-tabs`: a row of small boxed `.ds-tabs__tab` links in tracked capitals standing on a 1px rule; `is-current` takes the sheet's colour and joins the content below, which goes in `.ds-tabs__body`.
- `.ds-badge`: small square orange count. `.ds-badge--alt` is a bare coloured word ("new"); `.ds-badge--tag` is a grey outlined tag. Status labels are `.ds-badge--success`, `.ds-badge--warning` and `.ds-badge--danger`: a square label with a 1px outline and bold word, both in the status colour.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the index column at 11px. Each `.ds-sidebar__block` has a navy `.ds-sidebar__title` in tracked capitals on a 1px rule and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` rows, the `.ds-sidebar__link` at the left and an optional `.ds-sidebar__count` at the right edge; `.ds-sidebar__list--two` sets a link list in two columns; `.ds-sidebar__text` is a paragraph; `.ds-sidebar__search` is an input with a button. `.ds-sidebar__block--box` boxes one block with `--fill-panel` and the glow.
- `.ds-notice`: one-line message on `--color-notice` with a 1px `--color-border-strong` outline, starting with a bold `.ds-notice__title`. `.ds-notice--error` uses the danger colour for text and outline. Stack several in `.ds-notices`.
- `.ds-pagination`: centred row of small 1px boxed `.ds-pagination__link` items. `is-current` is `--color-surface-strong` with a `--color-border-strong` outline; `is-disabled` is faded.
- `.ds-dialog`: a 1px outlined box with `--shadow-dialog`, a `--fill-bar` `.ds-dialog__title`, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__text` and right-aligned `.ds-dialog__actions`. It sits in the page flow on `.ds-dialog__backdrop` (`--color-overlay`). No dimmed overlay.
- `.ds-empty`: a centred grey `.ds-empty__text` and one secondary button between two 1px rules; no box.
- `.ds-footer`: the inverted strip closing the sheet, in tracked capitals: one `.ds-footer__row` with a line of text left and `.ds-footer__links` of underlined `.ds-footer__link` items right.
- `ds-avatar`: a small square with initials beside a commenter name.
- `ds-accordion`: a sidebar list that opens and closes, a details element whose summary holds the block title.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: every box and control is square.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 16px`: the sheet and its boxes glow softly; nothing casts a deep shadow.
- `text-shadow = none`: text is flat, including on the bars.
- `font-size <= 27px`: the largest text is the site name; titles are 18px.
- `font-size >= 9px`: the smallest text measured is 9px; the pack itself stops at 10px.
- `font-weight <= 700`: bold is the heaviest weight; titles are not bold.
- `font-families <= 3`: Georgia, Trebuchet MS for labels and a monospace for code.
- `line-height <= 1.7`: body line height is 1.4 to 1.6 in the references.
- `line-height >= 1.3`: text is never set solid.
- `border-width <= 3px`: rules are 1px, button feet 2px, heavy rules 3px.
- `letter-spacing <= 3px`: only small capitals are tracked.
- `uppercase-text <= 25%`: capitals are for labels, never for running text or titles.
- `underlined-links <= 15%`: links are told by colour; only footer links are underlined at rest.
- `gradient-fills <= 6%`: only the bars, the footer strip and the page are shaded; tints and controls are flat.
- `block-gap <= 30px`: blocks sit one rule apart.
- `content-width <= 900px`: a fixed centred sheet; the page does not stretch with the window.
- `transition = none`: states change instantly.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen: a new kind of item copies `.ds-list__item` (its kind in the `--size-kind` column, a 1px rule below), a new group of items copies `.ds-day` (a tracked date line over a block ruled down the left), a new boxed block copies `.ds-panel`, a new line of figures copies `.ds-stats`, a new band across the sheet copies `.ds-grid` (flat tints joined edge to edge). Every label is `--font-ui` at `--text-ui`, in `--ui-transform` and tracked; every title is unbolded `--font-heading`. Use only the tokens in `tokens.css` and the `--size-*` and `--space-*` tokens in `components.css`; if a size is missing, take the nearest step of the scale. Keep text at 10px to 27px, corners square, fills flat, and separate things with a rule before reaching for a box.
