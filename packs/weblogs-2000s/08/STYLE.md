# Planet of weblogs, 2005

## Summary

A planet as communities ran them between 2003 and 2007: one page, written again by a script every half hour, that gathers the newest entries of several dozen weblogs into a single river. The sheet stretches with the window on a dark, finely lined page; a filled bar carries the brand (mark and site name) and a lowercase menu, a thin strip under it says when the feeds were last fetched, and below them the entries run down a wide column, each under the strip of the weblog it came from with its writer's small tile, beside a tinted column of subscriptions. Text is 12px Tahoma under bold Palatino headings in black and maroon on white, with honey-coloured strips, square corners, 1px lines, thick 5px rules and fine double frames in place of shadows.

## Layout

- The page is fluid: `.ds-page__frame` fills the window less `--space-6` at each side, never narrower than `--size-min` (760px) and never wider than `--size-page` (1100px), centred beyond that. It is `--color-canvas` on `--fill-page`, outlined with a 1px `--color-border` line and stands `--space-5` clear of the top of the window. No text is ever placed on the page outside the sheet.
- Order from the top, all inside the sheet: `.ds-nav` (the `.ds-nav__bar` in `--fill-bar` with the `.ds-brand` at the left and the `.ds-nav__menu` standing on its lower edge at the right; then the `.ds-nav__strip` in `--fill-bar-alt` with the time of the last round left and the feed links right), the view, and the `.ds-footer`. There is one bar only and the menu is inside it.
- The home view opens with two full-width bands: the `.ds-hero` (one tinted line of title and lead with the action at the right) and the `.ds-stats` line of counters. Under them `.ds-page__columns` holds the river (`.ds-page__main`, fluid) at the left and `.ds-sidebar` at `--size-side` (232px) at the right. There is no gap between the two: the sidebar is a tinted column with a 1px line down its left side and runs the full height of the view, and each column carries its own padding (`--space-6`).
- The river is a single stack: a `.ds-day` line (the date on a thick pale rule), then one `.ds-post` per entry, `--space-6` apart, then blocks (`.ds-page__section`) that open with a `.ds-page__label`. Entries are not boxed: the `.ds-post__head` strip marks where each begins.
- Inner pages keep the sheet, the bar, the strip and the footer. The hero and the counters are not used. A `.ds-breadcrumb` line sits directly under the strip, across the sheet. A page about one thing (the form, the about page) keeps `.ds-page__columns` with the tinted sidebar: the main column opens with `.ds-page-header` (20px title, one muted line, one button at the right, a thick pale rule below), then notices and the form, or the text, links and a panel. A page that is a listing (the weblogs) drops the sidebar and uses `.ds-page__wide`: page header, `.ds-tabs` with the table in `.ds-tabs__body`, `.ds-pagination` with the summary counts at its far end, then `.ds-page__halves` for the two blocks that belong under the table. Tabs stand on their own rule under the page header, never in the bar.
- Views: the specimen is one planet with four views. `river` is the front page: the band that says what is gathered, the counters, two entries in full, the short entries of the day, the four channels, and the column of subscriptions, feeds and the newest member. `weblogs` is every feed that is followed: tabs by channel over the table, pagination with the state counts, the dialog that takes your own weblog out, and an empty channel. `add` is the form that proposes a feed, under the notices that answer it. `about` is what a planet is: the text, links to take away and the panel on the keepers. The menu links to all four; titles and subscriptions lead to the list of weblogs.
- Spacing scale: `--space-1` 1px is the padding of sidebar rows and the gap between a title and its meta line; `--space-2` 3px is the padding of strips, buttons and inputs; `--space-3` 5px is the padding of table cells, list rows, tabs and notices and the gap between buttons; `--space-4` 9px is the padding of panels, grid cells and the dialog, the inset of an entry and the gap between paragraphs; `--space-5` 13px is the padding of the bar, the hero, the form and the sidebar's sides; `--space-6` 18px is the side padding of the sheet's columns and the gap between entries and blocks; `--space-7` 26px is list indent, the foot of a column and the gap between counters.
- Fixed sizes: `--size-face` 64px is a writer's tile, `--size-time` 72px the time column of the short list, `--size-label` 116px the label column of a form, `--size-field` 280px a text input, `--size-num` 76px a numeric table column, `--size-dialog` 380px a dialog.

## Typography and colour roles

- `--font-body` and `--font-ui` are Tahoma, Geneva, DejaVu Sans. `--font-heading` is "Palatino Linotype", "Book Antiqua", Palatino, used for the site name, entry and page titles, the day line, sidebar titles, counters and channel names. `--font-mono` (Lucida Console) is only for code. All are system fonts, so nothing was substituted.
- `--text-base` 12px with `--line-body` 1.55 is entry text; `--text-small` 11px is strips, meta lines, tables, the sidebar, the breadcrumb and the footer; `--text-ui` 11px is the menu, buttons, inputs, tabs and captions, bold and lowercased by `--ui-transform`; `--text-large` 14px is only the hero's lead line.
- `--text-display` 32px bold Palatino is the site name and the largest text on the page. `--text-h1` 20px is an entry title, a page title and the hero title; `--text-h2` 15px is the day line, sidebar titles, counters and channel names; `--text-h3` 12px bold Tahoma is a minor heading. Headings are never uppercased or tracked.
- `--color-text` #181818 is body text, `--color-text-muted` #686868 times, captions and meta lines, `--color-heading` #000000 titles, `--color-heading-alt` #680000 the day line, sidebar titles, counters, sub-headings in prose, list markers and code.
- `--color-link` #a60000, underlined in running text (`--link-decoration` underline) and plain on hover (`--link-decoration-hover` none), when it turns `--color-link-hover` #000000. Titles of rows, sidebar links and the breadcrumb are links without underline (`--link-decoration-quiet` none) that gain one on hover; the underline there is written out, because the hover token of this type set removes it. Visited is #787878, active #cc0000; `--color-link-quiet` #686868 is for category and permalink links.
- `--color-bar` #680000 with `--fill-bar` (a 3px line of the accent colour along its lower edge) fills the top bar, table heads and dialog titles; text on it, including the site name, is `--color-bar-text` #f8d088. `--color-bar-alt` #f8d088 with `--color-bar-alt-text` #303030 is the strip under the bar, the current menu item and panel titles. `--color-inverse` #181818 with `--color-inverse-text` is the footer only.
- Surfaces: `--color-canvas` and `--color-surface` are white; `--color-surface-alt` #f0f0f0 is the sidebar column, alternate rows, the form and the empty state; `--color-surface-strong` #d8d8d8 is the strip that heads an entry and table group rows.
- Fills: `--color-fill-1` is the hero band and the first channel cell; `--color-fill-2`, `--color-fill-3` and `--color-fill-4` are the other cells and the writers' tiles. Text on them is `--color-text` and `--color-heading`, as on a surface. `--color-accent` #f8d088 with `--color-accent-text` is feed badges, counts and the current page number; `--color-accent-alt` #cc0000 is the bare "new here" word.
- Borders: `--color-border` #b8b8b8 for the sheet, boxes and rules under captions, `--color-border-muted` #e8e8e8 between rows and for the thick pale rules, `--color-border-strong` #680000 for the 5px rules that mark an entry's strip, a channel cell, a notice and the form, for the line under sidebar titles and the current tab, and for the dialog. `--border-width-strong` is 5px. Every ordinary line is drawn in `--border-style`.
- Buttons are flat: `--color-button` #f8d088 with `--color-button-text`, outlined 1px in a darker mix of the two (`color-mix()`, because the palette has no token for a button's edge); the secondary button is `--color-button-secondary` with `--color-button-secondary-text` at `--weight-ui`. Inputs are `--fill-input` with a 1px `--color-input-border` line. Control outlines are written `solid` in `components.css` so that a dotted or grooved surface set does not break them.
- Nothing casts a soft shadow. `--shadow-panel` is a fine double frame (1px of the sheet's colour, then 1px of `--color-border`) around panels and writers' tiles; `--shadow-dialog` is the same with a 3px `--color-border-strong` band.
- Status colours: `--color-success` #007000, `--color-warning` #a56a00 and `--color-danger` #cc0000 are a small square and a bold word in the status colour, the lettering and outline of a destructive button and of an error notice, never a fill.
- Hover, visited, active and focus colours were not measurable on static captures; they are taken from other measured colours of the same palette. Focus is a 1px dashed `--color-focus` outline.

## Components

- `.ds-page` on `<body>`: page fill and base type. Everything goes in `.ds-page__frame`. `.ds-page__columns` holds `.ds-page__main` and the sidebar; `.ds-page__wide` is a view without the sidebar; `.ds-page__halves` sets two blocks side by side. `.ds-page__section` is a block with `--space-6` under it (`--last` drops the gap); `.ds-page__label` is the small bold caption on a hairline over a block.
- `.ds-view`: one screen of the example site. The specimen holds its views side by side in the markup and shows one at a time: the view named in the address, or the `.ds-view--home` view when none is named. In a real project each view is a page of its own; drop the class there.
- `.ds-brand`: the site's mark and name, at the left of the top bar; a link to the home page. `.ds-brand__mark` is a small square tile dressed like the primary button (`--fill-button` over `--color-button`, `--color-button-text` for the drawing, the same mixed 1px edge, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-control`), as large as `--text-display` on a side and never less than 2.2 times `--text-base`, so every palette and surface reskins it; it holds the mark as inline SVG painted with `currentColor`. `.ds-brand__name` is the name in `--font-heading` at `--text-display` with `--weight-display` and `--display-tracking`, in `--color-bar-text` because it stands on the bar. The name `chronoskin` and its mark are placeholders for the installing project's own name and logo; keep the classes and swap the text and the drawing.
- `.ds-nav`: site header, written once above the views. `.ds-nav__bar` holds the `.ds-brand` and `.ds-nav__menu`, a row of `.ds-nav__link` words in `--color-bar-text` on the lower edge of the bar; the current one is a tab in `--fill-bar-alt`, the colour of the strip below: `is-current` on a real page, and in the specimen one selector per view that picks the link by its `href`. `.ds-nav__strip` is the thin line under the bar with `.ds-nav__strip-link`.
- `.ds-breadcrumb`: one 11px line under the strip, ruled below: `.ds-breadcrumb__link` items, `.ds-breadcrumb__sep` (`/`), ending in `.ds-breadcrumb__current`.
- `.ds-hero`: the front page's introduction, once, as a tinted band across the sheet: `.ds-hero__main` holds `.ds-hero__title` (20px) and `.ds-hero__lead` (14px); `.ds-hero__actions` at the right stacks one button over one text link. It is one or two lines high, not a banner.
- `.ds-page-header`: the head of an inner page, used instead of the hero and never with it: `.ds-page-header__main` holds the `.ds-page-header__title` and one `.ds-page-header__text` line; `.ds-page-header__action` at the right holds one button or one text link. A 5px pale rule closes it.
- `.ds-stat`: one counter on a line: a `.ds-stat__value` (15px bold Palatino, `--color-heading-alt`) followed on the same line by its `.ds-stat__label` (11px muted). Three to five sit in a `.ds-stats` band under the hero, ruled below, `--space-7` apart. They are words on a line, not boxes.
- `.ds-day`: the date that opens a day of the river, on a thick pale rule.
- `.ds-post`: one gathered entry. `.ds-post__head` is the strip that names where it came from: `.ds-post__source` (the weblog, bold) with the writer after it, and `.ds-post__time` at the right. `.ds-post__body` holds a `.ds-face`, a `.ds-prose` whose h1 carries a `.ds-prose__title` link, and `.ds-post__meta` on a faint rule (category, comments at the weblog, permalink separated by `|`).
- `.ds-face`: the writer's tile, hung at the right of an entry: a `.ds-face__tile` square with initials (`--size-face`) and the first name under it. `.ds-face--2` and `.ds-face--3` choose another fill. In a real project the tile holds a small portrait.
- `.ds-prose`: running text: h1, h2, h3, paragraphs, lists with maroon markers, `code`, `blockquote` with a thick pale rule at the left.
- `.ds-link`: text link with `is-visited`, `is-hover`, `is-active`, `is-focus`. `.ds-link--quiet` is the grey meta link; `.ds-link--strong` is bold. `.ds-links` lays links out in a wrapping row.
- `.ds-button`: flat filled button with a darker 1px edge, bold lowercase Tahoma. `.ds-button--secondary` is the grey button for the second choice. `.ds-button--danger` is the destructive action (withdraw, stop following): the grey button lettered and outlined in `--color-danger`, never filled with it. `disabled` or `is-disabled` greys it; `is-hover` lightens the face and adds a 1px ring (`--shadow-control-hover`); `is-active` turns the ring inward. Group with `.ds-buttons`.
- `.ds-form`: a tinted block under a 5px `--color-border-strong` rule with `.ds-form__title` and rows (`.ds-form__row`) of a bold right-aligned `.ds-form__label` and a `.ds-form__field`. Controls take `.ds-form__input` (`--wide` full width, `--auto` for selects); `is-invalid` plus `.ds-form__error` marks a validation error; `.ds-form__hint` is help text; `.ds-form__check` with `.ds-form__checkbox` for checkboxes; `.ds-form__actions` aligns the buttons with the fields and wraps.
- `.ds-table`: data table at 11px without an outline: `--fill-bar` head row, rows divided by `--color-border-muted`, `.ds-table__row--alt` for alternate rows, `.ds-table__num` for right-aligned numeric columns, `.ds-table__group` for a group row, `.ds-table__title` for the bold link that names a row and `.ds-table__sub` for the muted line under it.
- `.ds-list`: the short entries: each `.ds-list__item` is a `.ds-list__time` at the left and, beside it, a bold `.ds-list__title` link over a `.ds-list__meta` line (and an optional `.ds-list__excerpt`), rows divided by faint rules.
- `.ds-panel`: 1px outlined box in a fine double frame with a `.ds-panel__title` strip in `--fill-bar-alt` and a `.ds-panel__body` of `.ds-panel__text` paragraphs. For the newest member in the sidebar or an aside in the text.
- `.ds-grid`: four `.ds-grid__cell` boxes across under a 5px top rule, tinted with the four fills (`--2`, `--3`, `--4`), each with a `.ds-grid__title` and a `.ds-grid__text`. For channels and section indexes.
- `.ds-tabs`: a row of `.ds-tabs__tab` words on a 1px rule, `--space-6` apart, each with an optional `.ds-tabs__count`; `is-current` is in the heading colour with a 5px `--color-border-strong` line under it. The content goes in `.ds-tabs__body`.
- `.ds-badge`: small square label on the accent fill, for feed formats and counts. `.ds-badge--alt` is a bare coloured word; `.ds-badge--tag` is a quiet outlined label for a channel or a lesser feed format. Status labels are `.ds-badge--success` (fetched), `.ds-badge--warning` (slow) and `.ds-badge--danger` (failing): a small square and a bold word, both in the status colour, without a box.
- `.ds-sidebar`: the tinted right column at 11px. Each `.ds-sidebar__block` has a Palatino `.ds-sidebar__title` on a `--color-border-strong` hairline and a `.ds-sidebar__list` of `.ds-sidebar__item` rows: a `.ds-sidebar__link` at the left and an optional `.ds-sidebar__count` (a number or how long ago) at the right. `.ds-sidebar__text` is a paragraph; `.ds-sidebar__search` is an input with a button; `.ds-sidebar__feeds` is a wrapping row of badges.
- `.ds-notice`: one-line message in `--color-notice` with a 1px outline and a 5px `--color-border-strong` rule at the left, starting with a bold `.ds-notice__title`. `.ds-notice--error` uses the danger colour for text and lines on `--color-danger-surface`. Stack several in `.ds-notices`.
- `.ds-pagination`: a row of underlined `.ds-pagination__link` words and numbers at the left: earlier, numbers, later. `is-current` stands on the accent fill; `is-disabled` is faded. `.ds-pagination__summary` at the far end holds counts as badges.
- `.ds-dialog`: a white box outlined in `--color-border-strong` with a `--fill-bar` `.ds-dialog__title`, `.ds-dialog__body`, `.ds-dialog__text` and right-aligned `.ds-dialog__actions`, in the double frame of `--shadow-dialog`. It sits in the page flow on `.ds-dialog__backdrop` (the overlay colour).
- `.ds-empty`: tinted outlined box with a centred `.ds-empty__text` and one secondary button.
- `.ds-footer`: the inverted band that closes the sheet: one `.ds-footer__row` with a line of text left and `.ds-footer__links` of underlined `.ds-footer__link` items right, and a fainter `.ds-footer__note` line that says how the page was made.
- `ds-accordion`: a sidebar list that opens and closes, a details element whose summary holds the block title.
- `ds-tooltip`: a hint shown on hover or focus of a link; the tip is a child with class ds-tooltip__tip.

## Never

- `border-radius <= 0px`: every box is square.
- `box-shadow-blur <= 0px`: frames are hard lines; nothing has a soft shadow.
- `text-shadow = none`: text is flat, including on the bar.
- `font-size <= 32px`: the site name is the largest text; entry titles are 20px.
- `font-size >= 11px`: strips, meta lines and the sidebar are 11px and nothing is smaller.
- `font-families <= 3`: Tahoma, Palatino for headings and a monospace for code.
- `line-height <= 1.6`: entry text is set at 1.55.
- `line-height >= 1.4`: text is small but never set solid.
- `border-width <= 5px`: lines are 1px; emphasis rules are 5px.
- `uppercase-text <= 0%`: the menu and captions are lowercase; nothing is set in capitals.
- `gradient-fills <= 10%`: only the bar, table heads and the footer carry their accent line; everything else is flat.
- `block-gap <= 26px`: entries and blocks sit 18px apart.
- `content-width <= 1100px`: the sheet stretches with the window but stops at 1100px.
- `transition = none`: states change instantly.
- `animation = none`: nothing moves.

## Extending

Derive a new component from the nearest one in the specimen: a new kind of entry copies `.ds-post` (a `--color-surface-strong` strip with a 5px `--color-border-strong` rule at its left, the text inset by `--space-4`), a new boxed block copies `.ds-panel`, a new row type copies `.ds-list__item` (faint rule below, `--space-3` padding), a new sidebar block copies `.ds-sidebar__block` with its title on a hairline, a new tinted box copies `.ds-grid__cell`. Use only the tokens in `tokens.css` and the `--size-*` and `--space-*` tokens in `components.css`; if a size is missing, take the nearest step of the scale rather than a new number. Text on a fill uses the token paired with it: `--color-bar-text` on `--fill-bar`, `--color-bar-alt-text` on `--fill-bar-alt`, `--color-accent-text` on `--fill-accent`, `--color-inverse-text` on `--fill-inverse`, and `--color-text` or `--color-heading` on surfaces and the four fills. Keep the sheet fluid, keep entries unboxed, and mark emphasis with a thick rule rather than a fill.
