Hold a slot, then confirm it

A hold reserves a time for a short while without booking it. Use one whenever something has to happen between choosing a time and committing to it, such as a payment.

Create the hold

Send the calendar and the instant you want to POST /v4/holds. The answer carries an id and the moment the hold runs out, ten minutes later unless you ask for less.

const hold = await chronoskin.holds.create({
  calendar: "studio",
  start: "2025-03-14T09:30:00Z",
  minutes: 45,
});

What can go wrong

  • The time was taken a moment ago: the answer is 409 with the next free slot.
  • The calendar is closed that day: the answer is 422.
  • A test key can hold at most five slots at once.

Confirm or release

When the payment succeeds, confirm the hold and it becomes a booking. If it fails, release it at once so the time is free again; do not wait for it to run out. The holds reference lists every field.

A hold is not a booking

It is not sent to the customer's calendar and no webhook fires until you confirm it.

Do not extend a hold in a loop

A hold can be extended once. A second attempt answers 409 and the slot is released.

Questions about holds

How long can a hold last?

Between one and thirty minutes. Ten is the default, and one extension of the same length is allowed.

Does a hold count against my rate limit?

Creating one is a request like any other. Holding it costs nothing.

Can two holds overlap?

Only when the calendar allows more than one booking at a time, such as a class with twelve places.

Parameters of a new hold

POST/holds
calendar, required

The calendar's short name or id. The key must have the bookings scope for it.

start, required

An instant in UTC. A local time without a zone is refused.

minutes

Length of the slot. Defaults to the calendar's own slot length.

expires_in

How long the hold lasts, in minutes, from 1 to 30.
