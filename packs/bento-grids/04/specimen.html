Guide

How a board is put together

Budgets, entries and the small parts every tile is made of.

Add entry

One subject per tile

If a tile needs a second title, it is two tiles.

Budgets

A budget is a sum set aside for one purpose and one month. It is never topped up silently: moving money in leaves a line in the ledger.

Adding an entry

Type the amount and pick a budget, or run chronoskin add 64 materials from the terminal. The bank feed fills in the rest.

What an entry keeps

  • The amount, the day and who logged it
  • The budget it was taken from
  • A photo of the receipt, when there is one

Questions

Can an entry sit in two budgets?

No. Split it into two entries, one for each.

Who can close a month?

Whoever opened the board, and anyone they name.

What does the bank feed fill in?

The day, the amount and the payee. The budget is still yours to choose.
