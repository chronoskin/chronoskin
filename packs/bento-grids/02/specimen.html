How a shelf works

The short guide, and the pieces a shelf is built from.

Start a shelf

Shelves

A shelf is a short list of books you mean to finish. It holds five at most, so adding a sixth means deciding which one goes back.

Logging a page

Type the page you stopped on, or run chronoskin log 212 from the desktop app. The streak counter moves when the number does.

What a shelf keeps

  • The page you stopped on, per book
  • Every quote you clipped, with its page
  • The day you started and the day you finished

Questions

Why only five books on a shelf?

Because a sixth is usually the one that stops the other five.

Can a shelf be shared?

Yes. Everyone on it sees the page counts, and notes stay private unless you say otherwise.

What happens to a finished book?

It moves to the library with its notes and the two dates.
