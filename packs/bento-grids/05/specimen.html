How a project runs

The same four steps?A wordmark takes six weeks, a text face about six months., whatever the size.

  • A twenty minute call and a written brief
  • Three directions on paper within two weeks
  • One direction drawn out and tested in use
  • Files, a short guide and a month of support

A voice for the morning paper

The paper had used the same borrowed grotesque since 1994. It was tidy and it was nobody's in particular, which is a problem for a paper named after its town.

Starting from the masthead

The old masthead had a square g and a flat-topped t. Both stayed, and the rest of the alphabet was drawn to keep them company. The first proofs were set at 8.5 point, the size of the body text.

What had to hold

  • Six columns at 8.5 point on thin newsprint
  • Headlines that fit one more word than before
  • Figures that line up in the tide tables

Into use

The family went to print in February. The italic follows this autumn; you can ask for a trial in the meantime.
