Release rooms

A room is a page that exists for one release. It opens when a branch is cut and closes when the deploy is confirmed healthy.

Opening a room

Run chronoskin room open or press the button on the board. The room copies the checklist from your workspace template.

What a room holds

  • The checks that must pass before shipping
  • A running log of every deploy attempt
  • The rollback note, written before it is needed

Closing a room

When the deploy has been healthy for ten minutes the room closes by itself. The log stays readable from the releases page for a year.

Questions

Can two rooms be open for one service?

No. A second branch waits in the queue until the first room closes.

Who can press the ship button?

Owners at any time, members once every check is green.

What happens to a room after a rollback?

It stays open with the rollback note pinned to the top of its log.

Labels

Pills mark state, squares count. Green, amber and red are status only.
