How billing works

The guide to invoices, credit notes and the labels you will meet on every screen.

New invoice

Invoices

An invoice is a numbered request for payment. Once sent it is never edited; a mistake is fixed with a credit note that points back to it.

Numbering

Numbers run in one sequence per year. Set the first with chronoskin number 0001 or in ledger settings.

What a client sees

  • Your details and theirs, with both tax numbers
  • Each line with its quantity, rate and tax
  • The due date and how to pay

Credit notes

Voiding an invoice writes a credit note for the same amount and tells the client. Both stay in the ledger with their numbers.

Questions

Can a sent invoice be changed?

No. Void it, and the credit note and the corrected invoice go out together.

When does the first reminder go?

Three days before the due date, unless reminders are off for that client.

Is the tax rate set per client?

Per client, with one default for everyone else.
